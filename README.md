# Video Strategy To Grow D.J. Brand

A unified four-pillar video content strategy combining four series -- **AI Agent Minute**, **Agent Tip of the Day**, **The Playbook**, and **Inside the Industry** -- to build D.J. Paris's social media presence and recruit real estate agents to Kale Realty through the Keeping It Real Podcast brand.

**Brand goals (priority order):**

1. Kale Realty recruiting in Chicago
2. NAR relationship deepening
3. Keeping It Real Podcast subscriber growth
4. National thought leader reach

---

## The Schedule

| Day | Series | Pillar | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | AI Agent Minute | 1 | AI prompt to start the week |
| **Tuesday** | Agent Tip of the Day | 2 | Podcast guest wisdom |
| **Wednesday** | **The Playbook** (NEW) | **3** | One tactical play for one specific scenario |
| **Thursday** | Agent Tip of the Day | 2 | Podcast guest wisdom |
| **Friday** | AI Agent Minute | 1 | AI prompt for the weekend |
| **Saturday** | **Inside the Industry** (NEW) | **4** | Insider access, synthesis, or reactive news |
| _Sunday_ | _Off_ | - | |

Total: 6 posts per week. Same volume as before, redistributed across four pillars.

Reactive Inside the Industry posts (Type C / News Flash) can override any weekday slot when major industry news breaks. The displaced script reschedules to the next available slot of its original series.

## The Four Series

### AI Agent Minute (100 scripts) — Pillar 1

**Format:** D.J. Paris talking to camera, ~60 seconds
**Formula:** Do X, Not Y, To Get Z
**Focus:** Every script teaches agents how to use a specific AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity) for a real estate task
**Cadence:** Monday and Friday (2x/week)

Scripts: [`scripts/ai-agent-minute/`](scripts/ai-agent-minute/)
Rankings: [`rankings/ai-agent-minute-rankings.md`](rankings/ai-agent-minute-rankings.md)

### Agent Tip of the Day (62 scripts) — Pillar 2

**Format:** D.J. Paris presenting guest wisdom from Keeping It Real podcast episodes, ~60 seconds
**Focus:** Real-world tactics, mindset shifts, and systems from top-producing agents
**Source:** Extracted from 700+ podcast episodes, quality-gated (scores 7-10 out of 10)
**Cadence:** Tuesday and Thursday (2x/week)

Scripts: [`scripts/agent-tip-of-the-day/`](scripts/agent-tip-of-the-day/)
Rankings: [`rankings/agent-tip-rankings.md`](rankings/agent-tip-rankings.md)

### The Playbook (3 pilot scripts) — Pillar 3

**Format:** D.J. Paris talking to camera, ~45 seconds
**Formula:** Scenario → Don't → Do (exact phrase) → Why → Try this
**Brandable phrase:** Every video starts with "Here's the play."
**Focus:** One tactical play for one specific real estate scenario, every Wednesday
**Reference model:** Jefferson Fisher (trial lawyer, ultra-specific communication techniques)
**Cadence:** Wednesday (1x/week, pilot phase)

Scripts: [`scripts/the-playbook/`](scripts/the-playbook/)
Format guide: [`docs/the-playbook-format.md`](docs/the-playbook-format.md)

### Inside the Industry (12 scripts) — Pillar 4

**Format:** D.J. Paris talking to camera, 45-75 seconds
**Three sub-types:**

- **Type A: Access** (`IA-`) — "I just talked to [specific person]. Here's what I noticed."
- **Type B: Synthesis** (`IS-`) — "After 700 interviews, here's what I've learned."
- **Type C: Reactive** (`NF-`) — "[Major news] just dropped. Here's what it means."

**Focus:** Insider access, sharp opinions, news reactions with a Chicago angle
**Reference models:** Steven Bartlett (Diary of a CEO), Ryan Serhant
**Cadence:** Saturday (1x/week, fixed) plus reactive Type C posts when news breaks

Scripts: [`scripts/inside-the-industry/`](scripts/inside-the-industry/)
Series playbook: [`docs/inside-the-industry-playbook.md`](docs/inside-the-industry-playbook.md)

## After the Tips Run Out (Week 21+ at original cadence, Week 31+ at new cadence)

The original 6-day schedule had Agent Tip running 3x/week, which exhausted at Week 21. Under the new 4-pillar schedule, Agent Tip runs 2x/week and exhausts around Week 31 (~mid-November 2026).

Three options at exhaustion:

1. **Pull from the bench.** 38 additional evaluated podcast scripts (scored but not published) plus 595 clip-worthy moments already extracted from the podcast archive.

2. **Generate new tips.** The Keeping It Real content system has a complete pipeline (clip hunter → script writer → kill judge) that can process any of the 700+ podcast episodes into new scripts.

3. **Reallocate the slots to The Playbook or Inside the Industry.** Both pillars are expected to grow rapidly and could absorb additional weekly slots.

**Recommendation:** Start evaluating Option 1 by August 2026 (6 weeks before exhaustion).

## Build System

All generated content (scripts, calendar, rankings) for the original two series (AI Agent Minute and Agent Tip of the Day) is produced by `build.py`:

```bash
python3 build.py
```

This reads source data from the `keeping-it-real-content-system` repo and generates everything. Re-run if scripts are added, reordered, or the start date changes.

**Note:** The Playbook and Inside the Industry scripts are currently hand-authored and live in `scripts/the-playbook/` and `scripts/inside-the-industry/`. They are not yet integrated into `build.py`. A future build system update will incorporate them.

**Dependencies:** Python 3 standard library only. macOS required (uses `textutil` for RTF conversion).
