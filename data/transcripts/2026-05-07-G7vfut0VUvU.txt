My brokerage loses $20,000 a year on purpose. I came up with a policy and no other brokerage in Chicago has copied it yet and I do it again tomorrow. So, here it is. Our agents pay $54 a month and that covers support, training, tech, helps us keep the lights on and pay our staff. But, the moment one of our agents starts volunteering at the local, state, or national level, we pay that fee for them. The entire time they're giving back, it's on us. There's like two reasons why we do this. One, agents who volunteer become better agents. They pick up skills you can't learn in a classroom and they build relationships with other realtors who also want to give back. Two, it's a branding play. I want more Kale agents volunteering than any other brokerage in Chicago. About 30 of our agents take us up on it. That's the 20 grand. Real money for a business our size and I'd sign up to do it again tomorrow. If you run a brokerage, the associations need volunteers more than ever and owners, you can afford this. If you're an agent, get involved whether your brokerage helps you or not, you will be a better agent. You'll feel good about contributing and I bet your production goes up.
