4% That's how many buyers find their home through an open house or a yard sign. NAR has been saying this for years. Most agents are still running them to find buyers. So, NAR's 2025 profile of home buyers and sellers show that 52% found it online. 4% found it through a yard sign or an open house. 3% started their search at an open house. So, open houses are a showing tool for buyers who already found the home online. Not a buyer acquisition tool. Now, Inman said the same thing this week. Stop investing in the wrong outcome. So, there's three plays this week. One, stop pitching open houses to sellers as &gt;&gt; &gt;&gt; we'll find the buyer. Pitch them as we'll get great feedback on the price and meet your neighbors. Different outcomes, same Sunday. Two, &gt;&gt; reallocate Sunday open house prep time towards listing optimization. Better photos, sharper descriptions, smarter MLS upload timing. Three, track every open house. Who came, what they said, who lives nearby. The relationships are the actual ROI. Open houses sell only 4% of homes. They build the next four years of your business. Run them for that.
