Stop using AI to write your emails. I teach AI to real estate agents and your clients can spot yours in the first line. M-Dash is, I hope this email finds you well. In today's market, I wanted to reach out. Every one of those is a signal a human didn't write the email. Your client reads it, knows a robot wrote it, and the lead goes cold before they reply. You think it sounds professional, your client thinks you're lazy, and it's kind of insulting. So, here's what you do now. Before you send your next email, read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it on the phone, cut it, then send.
