Dual agency cost sellers one and a half billion dollars. Off-market listings cost them another 1.3 billion. Same problem, but a different mechanism. So, Zillow released both numbers on May 14th in the same study. 15 million transactions analyzed from 2023 to 2025. The dual agency penalty showed up every year. The off MLS penalty showed up every year. Two structural seller costs hiding in plain sight. Why? Because both restrict the buyer pool. Dual agency limits the buyer to one agent's roster. Off MLS limits it to whoever's in the private network. Narrower pool, lower prices, the seller absorbs the gap. So, here's two questions to ask before your next listing presentation. One, will you market this listing on the MLS or in a private listing network only? If you pitch private exclusives, the Zillow data is now your seller's counter argument. Have a real reason that beats 1.3 billion in documented losses. Two, will you represent the buyer side if your buyer wants to write? If yes, the cost to your seller is $2,165. &gt;&gt; &gt;&gt; Have a real reason that beats that number, too. Now, when the agent narrows the buyer pool, the seller pays. The data just put price tags on both ways it happened.
