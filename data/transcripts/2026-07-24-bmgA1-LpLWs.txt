I use software that scores which agents are most likely to leave the brokerage. It's called Corded and it's never recruited a single agent for me. By the way, I still use it every day and I love Corded. But that same kind of tool points at sellers now. Re-evaluate scores your own database on who's likely to move in in the next 6 to 12 months. But here's the part that should stop you. Every agent in your market can buy the same list. The signal isn't the advantage, the call is. And there's an easy way to blow it. Our rule at Kale is that you never open with the signal. Telling somebody an algorithm flagged them is creepy and it ends the call. On the listing side, don't say my data says you might be selling. Say you've owned a place a long time. Two homes on your block just closed this quarter. Want to know what they sold for? So here's what you do now. Pull 10 owners from your database who've been in the same house a long time. Call one today with what sold on their block. No listing ask. The software hands you the name, it'll never make the call.
