Kansas City agent grew her business 74% a year for 5 years. She spent nearly nothing on lead generation. Her tactic is the one most agents quit using. Rachel Kilmer Inman profiled her this morning. No paid leads, no Zillow spend. Her business is built on what the industry calls pop-bys. A small gift hand-delivered to a past client with no ask attached. Coffee on the way to a showing, a pumpkin on the porch in October, a school supply pack in August. The reason isn't the gift, it's the doorstep. So most agents quit pop-bys when CRM automation showed up. The reason they worked is the reason they're rare now. It's effort. Showing up at a doorstep is the one marketing move you can't fake with an email blast. Rachel's 74% didn't come from a tool, it came from showing up. So here's what you do now. Make a list of your 10 favorite past clients. Pick one cheap seasonal item this weekend, block 4 hours next Saturday, hand-deliver each one with a 30-second hello, then do it again in 3 months. That's the whole system. No spend, just the doorstep.
