I have interviewed hundreds of the top 1% real estate agents on my podcast. Here's something I've noticed that surprised me every single time. When I asked top producers, these are agents doing 25, 50, 100 million dollars a year in volume, what do they still do themselves that they probably shouldn't do? The answer is almost always the same. They still answer their own phone. Not a call center, not an assistant, not that weird AI robot voice thing. They pick up the phone. Agents at every other production level have outsourced it. The top 1% do not. The gap is not a lack of awareness. Every top producer knows they could delegate. It's not a time problem. They're busier than anyone. It's a belief that answering the phone is beneath a certain level of success. Top producers do not agree. They think the call is where the relationship lives and the relationship is the entire business. One of my guests put it this way, "I don't know what deal is in that call until I pick it up." So, the question is not whether you can afford to answer your own phone, it's whether you can afford not to, especially when the top 6% still do. Tomorrow morning, pick up one call you'd normally skip. See what happens.
