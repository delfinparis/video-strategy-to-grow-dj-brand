I just got out of a closed-door meeting with senior leadership at National Association of Realtors and they just told me what the next big policy battle on Capitol Hill is. Every listing agent should be paying attention. About six of us in the country were in the room. A Q1 strategic plan readout. We went through everything the advocacy team was working on, the housing supply legislation, the broker engagement work, the new consumer ad campaign that's already driven 15,000 leads to realtor.com. But this is the one that's coming next and in their own words is capital gains policy reform. Now, NAR didn't get too specific about exactly which piece of the tax code and I don't want to speculate, but anyone who's been in real estate long enough knows what's at stake. The primary residence exclusion that hasn't been raised since 1997, investor capital gains and the 1031 rules. Any one of those moving would change who lists, who holds, and who 1031s. Now, this is the kind of policy story that hits agent pipelines 12 months from now without ever showing up on agent feeds today. NAR is signaling now. The smart move is to start tracking it now. The next NAR policy fight isn't about commissions, it's about the long-term owners who would list tomorrow if the tax math worked. Watch this one closely.
