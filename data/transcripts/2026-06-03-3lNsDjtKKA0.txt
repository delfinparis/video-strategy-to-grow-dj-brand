realtor.com just launched an AI that compares any two homes, runs your affordability math, and maps your daily commute free. Agents started panicking this morning when the tool was released. It's called Real Assist. It's built on Google Maps AI. Rolling out as of today, their CEO says it makes buyers more informed, and he's right. The buyer touring your listing already ran the numbers before they called you anyway. So, here's the part nobody is saying out loud. That tool didn't automate your job. It automated the part of the job that you were never worth a commission for anyway. Pulling comps was never the value. It just felt like it. So, this week, three things. One, stop selling information. The buyer has it now for free. Two, sell the thing the AI can't. In Chicago, that's knowing which block floods every spring and which is about to get reassessed. Three, when a client shows up with AI research, say good. Then tell them what the data left out. realtor.com didn't automate your job. They automated your busy work. What's left is the part worth paying for, your experience, your guidance, your empathy, your support. AI can't solve for that.
