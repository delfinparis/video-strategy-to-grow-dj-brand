81% Now, that is how often your listing presentation has no competition. So, NAR's profile of home buyers and sellers has been showing this for years. Most recently in 2024, 81% of sellers contacted only one agent before deciding who would sell their home. So, the 2025 report confirmed the pattern. 2/3 of sellers found their agent through a referral or a past relationship. So, most sellers, they're not comparison shopping, they are confirming. So, here's the catch. The listing presentation as a sales tool was built for a world where sellers actually shopped agents. They don't do that anymore. By the time you are sitting in their living room with the binder, the decision is functionally already made. Your job at the kitchen table is not to win the listing, it's not to lose it. One, stop optimizing your listing presentation. Start optimizing what gets you in the door. The decision happens before the meeting when there. &gt;&gt; &gt;&gt; Two, audit where your listings actually came from in the last 12 months. If most are referrals or sphere, and NAR says roughly 2/3 are, your real pipeline is your network, not your slides. Three, spend the time you would have spent reformatting your CMA on staying visible to the people who already know you. That's where the listing decision gets made. Listing presentations do not really matter. The work that wins listings happens months earlier. The contest is not the presentation, it's everything that happens before it.
