Realtors, stop posting. You're just listed and you're just sold. I know your broker told you to do this. Posting is fine, but what you're posting is the problem. There's a study on why and here's the fix. So, when one of these flops, you blame your photos or you decide to post more often, but that's not it. Your just sold post dies because it's about you and nobody scrolling was ever watching you. So, two psychologists, Gilovich and Savitsky, called it the spotlight effect. We're sure everyone's watching us, but they are not. Everyone's running their own spotlight scanning for one thing. Does this actually help me? Your sale answers a question that nobody asked. So, give them their own spotlight. Take the last thing a client asked you at 9:00 at night and answer that. Like you're talking to a person cuz it's not about you, it's about them. Do you want your dentist posting I just cleaned another mouth? No, you want them posting about how you can avoid developing cavities. Post solutions to problems. Before your next just sold, write down the last question a client asked you. Post that instead and see who shows up in your inbox.
