## IG Personal — needs context before alarm

|                         | Apr 11 last-30 (~Mar 12-Apr 10) | Apr 28 last-30 (~Mar 28-Apr 27) | Δ |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------:|--------------------------------:|---:|
| Views                   |                          16,304 |                           5,842 | **−64%** |
| Accounts reached        |                           6,758 |                           2,538 | **−62%** |
| Interactions            |                             583 |                             145 | **−75%** |
| Accounts engaged        |                             544 |                              68 | **−87%** |
| Profile visits          |                             325 |                             119 | **−63%** |

**On its face:** these numbers cross the 90-day reversal trigger (>30% reach drop sustained).

**Why it's not yet decisional:** the 30-day window ending Apr 27 catches ~3 weeks of pre-pivot content + ~1 week of pivot content. The pre-pivot window ending Apr 10 caught the *peak* of the Mar 13/14/16/24/30 viral posts (the "Top 4 posts" called out in the baseline memory all came from that 18-day window). The current window has lost those peaks and not yet accumulated post-pivot peaks.

**Methodology limit:** IG Personal's native Professional Dashboard does not support custom date ranges — it's locked to 7 / 28 / 30 / 90-day rolling windows. There is no way to isolate "Apr 21-27 only" on this surface from native Insights. The cleanest decisional read will come at the **2026-05-21 snapshot**, when the rolling-30-day window will be ~100% post-pivot content (Apr 21 onwards). At that point any drop is real, not a transition artifact.

**Interim signal worth weighting:** profile activity is 119 visits on the current 30-day window. Pre-pivot's 325 visits was inflated by the Mar 13/14 viral peaks driving profile clicks. The "no-viral baseline" of profile visits looks like ~120-150/30d, which is what we're seeing now — and it's not zero. So part of the -63% profile-visits decline is "we lost the March peaks," not "the new content fails."
