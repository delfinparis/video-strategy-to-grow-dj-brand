## Headline correction: the "viral" numbers were paid

Two personal-IG posts that looked like organic breakouts were **Zillow ad pushes**, and the matching LinkedIn spike was **pushed by NAR** (D.J. confirmed 2026-06-22):
- "Judge ordered Chicago's MLS to turn Zillow back on" (May 22): 1,148,385 views / 593,449 reach — Zillow-promoted

## Is it working? (honest read)

- **Reliable engine, not a rocket.** Every post organically reaches a few hundred to low-thousands per surface. Solid base, not taking off.
- **Reach growing? Can't confirm.** One window only. Need a prior 30d organic pull to compute a trend. This snapshot IS that prior baseline going forward.
- **Hooked? Sampling, not bingeing.** FB avg watch time 4-13s on ~60s videos; 1-minute views near zero (except 43k-listings 24, I-defended-Zillow 36). IG 3-sec view rate swings 2.6%–54.7% — hook lands on hard-number openers (Connecticut 51%, capital-gains 31%), dies on soft/promo. Saves+shares mostly 0-1 = not yet resonant enough to redistribute.
