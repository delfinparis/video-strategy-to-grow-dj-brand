## 1. Your first year is supposed to be brutal. -- SLOTTED WEDNESDAY

**Section:** Career and identity | **Heat:** 4.5 | **Hook family:** 4 (System Indictment) |
**Hook shape:** 4, cohort address + indictment + promise

**Who profits:** brokerages whose model depends on churn (a body that lasts eleven months and
quits still paid a desk fee, a franchise fee, maybe a referral back to the brand).

**The turn:** the difficulty isn't a rite of passage, it's an unmanaged onboarding with no
mentor, no 90-day plan, and no accountability.

**Hook (spoken):** "If this is your first year, you're not failing. Your onboarding is. Sixty
two percent of new agents made under ten thousand dollars last year, and most of them never
got handed an actual plan."

**The swap:** Get the 90-day plan in writing this week: which five people you call every day,
which three systems you have to learn, and who checks on you weekly. If your brokerage doesn't
have one, write it yourself tonight and start Monday. Five names a day, no exceptions.

**Loop-back:** "You didn't fail your first year. Nobody built you one."

**Receipt:** NAR, 2025 Member Profile of Real Estate Agents: 62 percent of new agents earned
under $10,000 in their first year. Named source, current year, one number, said once.

## 2. You need a bigger CRM / more leads. -- SLOTTED MONDAY

**Section:** Practice | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 9 (Swap / List) | **Hook shape:** 1,
imperative + harm

**Who profits:** lead vendors, who get paid whether the lead closes or not.

**The turn:** the database an agent already owns sits unworked while they shop for a bigger one.

**Hook (spoken):** "Stop paying for more leads. One agent grew her business seventy four
percent a year for five years working the client list she already had, untouched, for free."

**The swap:** This Saturday, pull your past client list, pick your top ten, and hand-deliver
something small to each one this week. Coffee, a fall treat, no ask attached. Do it again in
three months.

**Loop-back:** "You don't have a leads problem. You have an untouched list problem, and it's
sitting in your phone right now."

**Receipt:** Inman, 2026-06-21 ("How This Agent Grew Her Business 74% YOY, Spending Next To
Nothing"): one Kansas City agent averaged 74 percent year-over-year growth for five years with
almost no paid lead generation. The agent's name is in the Data Source below but stays out of
the script per the lane's never-name-an-individual-agent rule.

**Section:** Tech and AI | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 8 (Cohort Callout, reversal variant)
| **Hook shape:** 3, cohort reversal

**Who profits:** doom content, and the vendors selling the cure for it.

**The turn:** AI replaces the parts of the job nobody was paying for anyway.

**Hook (spoken):** "For everyone convinced AI is about to replace you, you're half right. It's

## 5. Post as often as you can. -- SLOTTED FRIDAY

**Section:** Marketing and social | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 7 (Forbidden) | **Hook
shape:** 5, paradox verdict

**Who profits:** content-mill services selling volume.

**The turn:** past a threshold the extra posts compete with each other, and consistency beats
frequency.

**Hook (spoken):** "Posting every single day is why fewer people are seeing what you post."

**The swap:** Pick four days a week you can actually hold for six months, and stop touching it
past that. Consistency is the only lever here that compounds.

**Loop-back:** "You didn't need more posts. You needed the same four days, every week, for six
months."

**Receipt:** Buffer, 2026 Social Media Study: accounts posting consistently (20 of 26 weeks)
earned roughly 450 percent more engagement per post than sporadic posters. Already verified
in-repo via `docs/myths-that-dont-move-the-needle.md` #3.
