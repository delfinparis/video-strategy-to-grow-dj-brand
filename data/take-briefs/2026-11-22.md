## 1. "Your first year is supposed to be brutal." — the only Wednesday-eligible option

**Section:** Career and identity | **Slot: Wed** | **Heat: 4** | **Hook family: 4 (System Indictment)** | **Shape: 4 (cohort address + indictment + promise)**

**Who profits:** brokerages whose model depends on churn.

**The turn:** the difficulty is not a rite of passage, it is an unmanaged onboarding.

**Hook (spoken):** "If you're in your first year right now: half the agents who closed one deal in 2022 closed zero deals in 2023. That's not you failing. That's a brokerage that never built you a plan."

**The receipt:** 49% of agents who first closed in 2022 closed nothing in 2023. Relitix via Inman, published 2026-05-14 ("New agents say how they're surviving," inman.com/2026/05/14). The Relitix/Inman number passed the 2026-09-10 batch receipt audit as confirmed.

**The swap:** Write your own 90-day plan tonight. Five target activities a week, logged daily, reviewed every Friday against last week's numbers. Don't wait for a brokerage that has no incentive to hand you one.

**Loop-back:** "Your first year isn't brutal because you're bad at this. It's brutal because nobody built you a plan, so build your own."

## 2. "Stack hashtags for reach." — Monday candidate

**Section:** Marketing and social | **Slot: Mon or Fri** | **Heat: 4** | **Hook family: 9 (Swap / List)** | **Shape: 1 (imperative + harm)**

**Who profits:** nobody, it's inherited folklore.

**The turn:** the tag stack is doing nothing the first line of the caption couldn't do better.

**Hook (spoken):** "Stop stacking ten hashtags under every post. You are the one shrinking your own reach."

**The receipt:** On Instagram, posts with hashtags got 31 to 32 percent fewer views and 33 percent fewer interactions than posts without. Metricool 2026 Instagram Study, 24 million-plus posts.

**The swap:** Drop to zero or one tag. Put your real search phrase, the thing a buyer would actually type, in the first line of the caption instead.

**Loop-back:** "The hashtags were never the discovery tool. The first line of your caption always was."

**Why this is the natural Monday pick:** the swap is the whole payload, which is the Family 9 spine, and the imperative shape is the default for that slot.
