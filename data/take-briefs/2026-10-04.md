## 1. Your first year is supposed to be brutal. -- WED

**Section:** Career and identity | **Heat:** 4.5 | **Hook family:** 4 (System Indictment) | **Hook shape:** 4 (cohort address + indictment + promise)

**Who profits:** brokerages whose model depends on churn -- replacing a quitting first-year costs less than actually training one.

**Hook (spoken):** "If you're a real estate agent in your first year, your brokerage isn't trying to help you survive it. It's trying to find out if you'll quit."

**Who profits (spoken):** "Every manager who calls that normal runs a model that gets cheaper every month you go unpaid. Replacing you costs less than training you."

**The receipt:** Only 15 percent of agents nationally have two years or less in the business right now, the lowest share NAR has ever recorded, down from 18 percent the year before. **Source: NAR 2026 Member Profile**, released June 25, 2026 (based on 2025 data). [HousingWire coverage](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/nar-2026-member-profile-experience/).

**The swap:** "If you're new, stop waiting on floor time and company leads. This week, text 20 people you already know: I just got my license, keep me in mind."

**Loop-back:** "Your first year isn't brutal because the business is hard. It's brutal because nobody built you an onboarding, and calling that a rite of passage is how they get away with it."

**Receipt note on this stat:** it's a real, current, named-source figure, but it measures membership composition at a point in time, not a flow attrition rate. Don't let the script claim "85 percent fail" or any derived number -- the honest claim is the 15 percent itself, stated plainly, with the argument built on top of it, not folded into it.

## 2. Stack hashtags for reach. -- MON

**Section:** Marketing and social | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 9 (Swap / List) | **Hook shape:** 1 (imperative + harm)

**Who profits:** nobody, it's inherited folklore from a platform that stopped counting hashtags as search years ago.

**Hook (spoken):** "Stop stacking hashtags on your posts. It's hurting your reach, not helping it."

**Who profits (spoken):** "Nobody taught you that on purpose. It's a leftover habit from a platform that stopped treating hashtags as search a long time ago."

**The receipt:** Posts with hashtags got roughly 31 to 32 percent fewer views and 33 percent fewer interactions than posts without them, across 24 million-plus Instagram posts. **Source: Metricool, 2026** (already in-repo, [myths-that-dont-move-the-needle.md](../../docs/myths-that-dont-move-the-needle.md) #1). Checked, holds up, no caveat flagged.

**The swap:** "Drop the tag block. Put one real search phrase in your first line instead, the kind of thing an agent would actually type into the search bar."

**Loop-back:** "The hashtags were never the discovery tool. The caption always was. The tags were just hiding it."

## 3. You need a bigger CRM / more leads. -- MON

**Section:** Practice | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 9 (Swap / List) | **Hook shape:** 6 (diagnosed loss)

**Who profits:** lead vendors, who get paid whether the lead converts or not.

**Hook (spoken):** "You know why your last deal went to someone else? It wasn't because you didn't have enough leads. It's because you stopped calling the ones you already had."

**Who profits (spoken):** "The vendor selling you the next batch needs you to believe the problem is supply, not follow-up."

**The receipt:** A Kansas City agent grew her business 74 percent a year for five years and spent almost nothing on paid leads, working her existing sphere instead. **Source: Inman, June 21, 2026** ("How This Agent Grew Her Business 74% YOY, Spending Next To Nothing"). Already confirmed in-repo via `scripts/carousels/EVERGREEN-002-pop-by-system-74-percent.md`.

**The swap:** "Pull your last 10 closed clients this week. Hand-deliver something small to four of them. No pitch, just a hello."

**Loop-back:** "You don't need a bigger CRM. You need to open the one sitting on your phone right now."

**Build-pass note:** this exact figure already ran as `NF-052` and the `EVERGREEN-002` carousel (2026-07-08) and was drafted once before for this same cow in the 2026-09-27 brief (never filmed, never logged -- it's clean to use). ## 4. AI is going to replace agents. -- FRI

**Section:** Tech and AI | **Heat:** 4.3 | **Hook family:** 8 (Cohort Callout) | **Hook shape:** 3 (cohort reversal)

**Who profits:** doom content, and the vendors selling the cure for the fear they're also selling.

**Hook (spoken):** "For those of you panicking that AI is going to replace you, you're wrong. It's going to replace the busywork you were charging full commission to avoid doing well."

**Who profits (spoken):** "Doom content profits from the fear. So does every vendor selling you the tool that fixes it."

**The receipt:** Three in four agents who use AI are using it to write listing descriptions, the single most common use case, ahead of social posts (56 percent) and emails (52 percent). **Source: NAR 2026 REALTORS® Technology Survey**, fielded June 2026. NAR's own deputy chief economist, Jessica Lautz, frames the freed-up time as going toward "the guidance, negotiation and steady advice that clients count on" -- the part AI isn't touching.

**The swap:** "Let it write the first draft of your next listing description. Spend the time you got back on the phone call you've been putting off."

**Loop-back:** "AI isn't coming for your job. It's coming for the part of your job you never wanted anyway."
