## 1. Stop stacking hashtags on your posts. — Mon

**Section:** Marketing and social | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 9 (Swap/List) | **Shape:** 1, imperative + harm

**Hook (spoken):** "Stop stacking hashtags on your posts. You're trading away a third of your views for nothing."

**Who profits:** Nobody. It's inherited folklore, not a product anyone sells you.

**The swap:** Drop the tag stack entirely. Put one real search phrase, the neighborhood or price point a buyer would actually type, in the first line of the caption instead.

**Loop-back:** "The algorithm was never blind to hashtags. It was reading eleven of them as a tell that you had nothing else to say."

**Receipt:** Metricool, 2026 study of 24M+ Instagram posts -- posts *with* hashtags got roughly 31-32% fewer views and roughly 33% fewer interactions than posts without. Correlational, not causal, but the direction is consistent. In-repo at [`docs/myths-that-dont-move-the-needle.md`](../../docs/myths-that-dont-move-the-needle.md) #1, cross-checked today, no caveat, no re-verification needed.

**Hook (spoken):** "If you're a realtor in your first year, your brokerage budgeted for you to make eight thousand dollars and called that normal. That's not the market. That's an onboarding nobody built."

**Who profits:** Brokerages whose recruiting and retention model depends on churn -- a revolving door of new licensees costs them nothing and the fee checks still clear.

**The swap:** This week, ask your broker for the actual 90-day plan they will run with you, in writing: who calls you, how often, on what schedule. If there isn't one, that's the answer to why this year feels brutal.

This is new sourcing, not an in-repo figure: the bank entry carried no number at all (its "evidence: in-repo" note pointed only to a swipe-file hook line), and I did **not** use the "87% of agents fail" or "71% sold zero homes" figures -- `docs/recruiting-objections.md` line 101 flags both as unverified industry legend that should never be attributed to NAR.
