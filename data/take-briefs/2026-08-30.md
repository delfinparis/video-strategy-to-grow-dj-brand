## SELECTED

### MONDAY -- "You need a bigger CRM / more leads."

**Section:** Practice | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 9 (Swap / List) | **Hook shape:** 1
(Imperative + harm)

**Hook (spoken):** "Stop buying leads. Lead vendors get paid the second you swipe your card,
whether that lead ever calls you back or not."

**Who profits / turn:** Lead vendors profit from the sale, not the result. The database an agent
already owns -- past clients, people who already said yes once -- sits unworked while the agent
pays to compete for a stranger.

**The receipt:** One Kansas City agent grew her business 74% a year for five years without buying
a single lead. Source: Inman, "How This Agent Grew Her Business 74% YOY, Spending Next To
Nothing," June 21, 2026.

**The swap:** "Pull ten names from your own phone, people who already hired you, and hand-deliver
something small to each one this Saturday. No pitch, no 'know anyone selling.' Just contact."

**Loop-back:** "You don't have a lead problem. You have an untouched database, and buying more
leads just means paying to keep ignoring it."

### WEDNESDAY -- "Your first year is supposed to be brutal." (the week's hardest swing)

**Section:** Career and identity | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 4 (System Indictment) | **Hook
shape:** 4 (Cohort address + indictment + promise)

**Hook (spoken):** "If this is your first year, your brokerage budgeted for you to make eight
thousand dollars and called it normal. Here's why that's not your fault."

**Who profits / turn:** Brokerages whose model depends on churn. A brutal first year isn't a rite
of passage, it's an onboarding nobody built, because the split gets collected whether or not the
agent makes it to year two.

**The receipt:** Agents with two years or less in the business earned a median gross income of
$8,000 last year. Source: National Association of Realtors, 2026 Member Profile, published June
25, 2026.

**The swap:** "Ask your broker for one specific thing this week: a 90-day plan with a name
attached to it who checks in with you, not a manual you were handed and never opened."

**Loop-back:** "Your first year was never supposed to be brutal. It was supposed to be managed,
and somebody decided it was cheaper not to."

### FRIDAY -- "Post as often as you can."

**Section:** Marketing and social | **Heat:** 4 | **Hook family:** 7 (Forbidden) | **Hook shape:**
2 (Verdict + cost)

**Hook (spoken):** "Posting every single day isn't discipline. It's the reason fewer people are
seeing you."

**Who profits / turn:** Content-mill services that sell volume, because volume is the only thing
they can measure. Past a threshold, an agent's own posts start competing with each other for the
same feed.

**The receipt:** Accounts that posted consistently (roughly 20 of 26 weeks) earned about 450% more
engagement per post than sporadic posters. Source: Buffer, 2026 State of Social Media study.

**The swap:** "Pick one weekly cadence you can actually hold for the next twenty weeks, and stop
grading the week by how many times you posted."

**Loop-back:** "You didn't need to post more. You needed to still be posting in six months, which
is the one thing volume never bought you."
