# Share Your Why — Mentor Cheat Sheet

**Kale Mentors** • Send it to D.J., he does the rest

You record a quick, raw clip about being a mentor. D.J. edits it into a polished video you can post to attract clients and good people. That's the whole deal. Takes about 5 minutes.

---

## 1. Turn on highest quality FIRST (do this once)
This is the most important step. Film it low-res and it can't be fixed later.

- **iPhone:** Settings → **Camera** → **Record Video** → choose **4K at 60 fps** (or 4K at 30). High Efficiency format is fine.
- **Android:** Open the **Camera** app → tap **Settings** (gear) → **Video resolution / quality** → set to the **highest** option (**4K / UHD**, or **FHD 1080p 60fps** if 4K isn't there). Set it for the front camera too.

## 2. Set up your phone (30 seconds)
- **Go vertical.** Hold the phone up and down, not sideways.
- **Eye level.** Prop it against a coffee cup, a laptop, or a shelf so the camera is at eye height, not looking up your nose.
- **Face a window or a lamp.** Light in front of you, never behind you.
- **Quiet room.** No TV, no music, no hallway noise.
- **Clean the lens.** A quick wipe with your shirt. Smudges kill quality.

## 3. Hit record and answer these (pick 2–3)
Just talk like you're telling a friend. No script needed. Speak from the heart.
- Why did you become a mentor?
- What's a win you've had with one of your mentees?
- What's your favorite part of being a mentor?
- What surprised you most about mentoring?
- What would you tell an agent thinking about becoming a mentor?

**Keep it under 60 seconds. One take is perfect. Mess-ups are fine — D.J. edits those out.**

## 4. Quick filming tips
- Look **at the lens**, not at yourself on the screen.
- Start with energy. Smile before you talk.
- Real beats polished. Don't overthink it.

## 5. Send it to D.J. — use a LINK (don't text the raw file)
Texting the video shrinks the quality and wastes that 4K you just shot. Send a link instead.

**iPhone (Photos app)**
1. Open **Photos**, find your video.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow ↑).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Copy iCloud Link."** Wait a few seconds while it prepares.
4. Paste that link in a text to D.J.

**Android (Google Photos)**
1. Open **Google Photos**, tap your video.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Tap **"Create link."**
4. Tap **Copy**, then paste the link in a text to D.J.

*Both methods keep the full original quality. Texting the file does not.*
