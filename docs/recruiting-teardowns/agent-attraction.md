# Agent Attraction — Master Recruiting Technique Library

**Source:** 58-video YouTube playlist + the "Complete Agent Attraction Blueprint" PDF, all by **agentattraction.io** (founders "Jastej/Jaz/Prav"). Transcribed and deduped June 2026.
**What this is:** Every distinct recruiting technique they teach, deduped across all videos, each tagged with the videos that cover it, then mapped to Kale's world (Confirm / Steal / Reject).

> **Read this first — the model mismatch.** Agent Attraction sells brokers/team leaders a done-for-you paid-ads recruiting system. A large share of their economics assumes **revenue-share / downline brokerages (eXp, Real, LPT)** and **charging agents to join**. Kale is **flat-fee, no downline**. So their *attraction mechanics* (positioning, funnel, ads, offers, scripts) are highly transferable; their *monetization model* (downline overrides, $3–10K paid-to-join products) mostly is not. Tags below make the split explicit.

## 1. Core philosophy — Attract, don't chase

- **The Chasing Trap.** Cold calls, DMs, Indeed/ZipRecruiter, mass email, coffee meets = "chasing," which signals "I need you more than you need me" and repels producers. Flip it: run value so agents come to you pre-sold. *"The more you chase, the more they run."* — v01,04,05,06,08,09,11,13,14,18,19,20,22,23,25,26,28,32,34,35,36,38,39,40,41,43,44,45,47,48,52,53,54,56
- **Status flip / "status delta."** Teaching value first reframes you from "needs agents" to "can help me" — you become mentor/authority, they show up "arms uncrossed." — v13,14,18,41,42,29
- **Dating / bar / fishing metaphors.** Whoever initiates loses leverage; drop the right *bait* and the fish swim to you. — v13,18,20,22,28,34,37
- **Only ~20% of agents are in-market at any time** ("white-hot list"), and it rotates every few months. Inbound ads make that 20% self-identify instead of you chasing a moving target. — v04

**Kale map:** ✅ **CONFIRMS** the whole joinkale problem-chooser + webinar approach (value-first, agent-as-hero). This is the same thesis as the [[storybrand]] restructure and the [[project_recruiting_objection_map]] wedge. Nothing to change — it's reassurance you're on the right road.

## 2. Positioning & the offer (their #1 lever)

- **"The offer is everything" — not ads, not funnel.** Whether a producer joins is the offer, not splits/brand/market. Most brokers have "a brochure, not an offer" — no reason to act *now*. — v08,10,21,26,42,46,50,56
- **Rolex in a brown paper bag / $120 steak / diamond in a velvet box.** Brokers have real value but package it badly. Fix packaging & positioning, don't change the model. The VSL is the "display case" / "movie trailer." — v04,08,09,28,32,36,37,43,45,47
- **Lead with transformation, not features.** "Great culture, competitive splits, great tech, supportive team" = the *wrong* pitch (everyone says it). Sell the outcome: get them from X to Y deals, predictable pipeline, off the roller coaster. — v04,07,08,09,33,34,35,36,38,39,41,42,43,45,46,47,50,52,56
- **Brokerage = a product; agents = your customers.** Until you can say in one sentence what changes in an agent's income/life, "you have a place to park a license, not a product." — v04,06,50,51,52,56 (their "Brokerage as a Product / BaaP" system)
- **Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi 4 levers):** maximize Dream Outcome + Perceived Likelihood (proof) + Speed (30/60/90-day win), minimize Effort/Sacrifice (done-for-you). Tighter 3-part version: **specific promise + risk-reversal/guarantee + speed.** — v08,09,21,26
- **Offer as a filter / avatar call-out.** Write copy for ONE avatar so the offer *selects* who responds (and Meta reads the page to target lookalikes). Two profitable avatars: (a) busy agent stuck at ~1 deal/mo → offer *appointments* (not leads); (b) capper/team leader → offer wealth/freedom/empire. — v46,49,50,51,54,56
- **Boutique > small.** Reframe small size as "boutique, exclusive, high-touch, curated": same-day callbacks, meet in 72h, know their numbers. Big franchises "can't move fast or make agents feel special — that's your opening." — v09
- **Name your messy coaching into a system.** Package the hidden value you already deliver into a named process with numbers/timelines. — v08

**Kale map:** ✅ **CONFIRMS + 🟡 STEAL.** "Sell transformation not features / lead with the agent's goals" is exactly the chooser design. **Steal specifically:** the **boutique-not-small** reframe (Kale's edge over Compass/KW), and **naming your support into a system** (turn Kale's actual support into a named, numbered program for the "Faster support"/"Coaching"/"Mentorship" pages — directly addresses the proof gaps in [[project_joinkale_storybrand_homepage]]). ⛔ **Watch:** "the offer is everything" can tempt over-promising — Kale's offer must stay backed by real proof points.

## 3. What producing agents actually want (their "12,000 conversations" data) — v07,09

This is the most directly useful video for Kale. Their five claimed findings:
1. **Not leads — a system.** Producers can already close; they lack *consistency*. Sell "predictable pipeline," not "leads dumped in your lap."
2. **Not splits — support.** "They'd rather make 80% of 20 deals than 90% of 15." Split-joiners leave for 1% more.
3. **Treated as a business owner, not an employee.** Partnership language: "My job is to help you build *your* business."
4. **They leave from neglect, not money.** Top application answer is "my broker doesn't return my calls / I feel like a number" — never "splits too low."
5. **Proof, not promises.** "Sarah joined doing 8 deals, last year closed 22" beats "our agents do well."

**Kale map:** ✅ **CONFIRMS hard.** This *is* your [[project_joinkale_storybrand_homepage]] research ("only 13% stay for pay; real drivers = belonging/support/mentorship") in their words. 🟡 **STEAL:** insight #4 (neglect) is a sharp recruiting-content angle — "the brokerage that actually picks up the phone." Insight #5 is the push you already flagged: **build the proof library** (named agent + numbers) before the Leads/Support/Mentorship pages ship.

## 4. The funnel architecture (the "24/7 Digital Recruiter")

Their flagship build, repeated in nearly every video:

- **3-page VSL funnel (the tested "control"):** (1) value-first **Video Sales Letter** + application below it, (2) Calendly self-booking, (3) thank-you/pre-frame page. Built in GoHighLevel; "spent $100K/mo and can't beat this control." — v05,06,09,10,11,13,18,20,22,23,25,26,28,29,32,34,36,37,39,41–47,50–56
- **VSL = value disguised as a pitch.** 5–20 min "how-to / mini-webinar": teach 3 steps to the outcome, show proof, tell your story, THEN bridge to the brokerage offer + CTA. Built from a client questionnaire whose answers become the script. — v02,05,06,09,23,29,33,47
  - Claimed stat (cite skeptically): *"VSLs increase conversion 40%→138%"* (sourced to funnel-vendor blogs). — v43,51,54
- **Application as filter + "ammunition."** Before booking, capture: current brokerage, production (last 12 mo), 1-yr GCI goal, #1 bottleneck, **urgency/timeline to switch.** Double duty: screens tire-kickers + arms the call. Copy can openly *disqualify* ("not for agents who haven't closed a deal a month"). — v05,06,09,10,11,23,25,26,49,51,52,54
- **Two-call process.** 15-min discovery call (fixed questions) → closing call that "bottom-lines" back to the discovery notes. Don't start in person — discovery on Zoom first. — v03,25,37
- **Pre-frame / warm-up page + show-up engineering.** Post-booking page with background, case studies, offer, and an objection-handling Q&A video. **Show-up boosters:** "add to phone calendar" button (native push notifications, +10–15% show-up), reminder video, reminder emails/texts. Cited "7–13 touchpoints to switch." — v10,13,18,23,25,26,36,37
- **Pre-appointment email sequence.** Agent Attraction sends **18 emails** before their own calls — story, case studies, the steps, the offer. "Shortening the cycle is *your* responsibility." — v36
- **Multi-channel speed-to-lead follow-up.** Phone captured with consent → call, double-dial, text, voicemail, email drip, social DM. **Manual call to everyone who didn't fully book** is a big lift (Brian → ~15 interviews/wk). Treat each lead like a Zillow internet lead: call *immediately*, don't wait on automations. — v02,04,09,16,19,25,26

**Kale map:** 🟡 **STEAL — this is the concrete build for the joinkale chooser.** Their modal→page→VSL→application→Calendly chain *is* the architecture you locked (chip → modal teaser → page → book-a-call). Specifically adopt: the **application that captures production + bottleneck + urgency** (feeds Close, closes your [[project_joinkale_meta_attribution_gap]]), the **pre-frame/show-up stack**, and **immediate manual follow-up**. ⛔ **Skip the GoHighLevel dependency** — joinkale is Squarespace + Close + Calendly; you don't need GHL. ⛔ Treat the "138% conversion" stat as marketing, not fact.

## 5. Traffic — paid ads, retargeting, AI creative

- **Meta (FB/IG) Leads campaigns, NOT job boards.** Producers live on FB/IG marketing themselves; Indeed/ZipRecruiter/WizeHire bring "anyone with a pulse." — v10,19,23,26,33,34,35,37,40,41,43,45,46,47
- **Their exact Meta setup (v19):** Leads objective + instant form (name/email/**phone required**, greeting OFF to cut friction); campaign budget; dynamic creative ON; placements = **feeds/stories/reels only** (kill in-stream & search); audience **>40k**, stack real-estate interests; put "real estate agent" keywords in copy so the algorithm learns; redirect form → VSL page.
- **License-qualifier as first form field** ("Are you a licensed agent in [market]?") — cleans data + trains targeting. — v25
- **Ad formula:** Promise + who-it's-for + what's-in-it-for-them + one CTA. **Hook = 80% of the battle** / first 3–15 sec drive ~47% of conversions (Meta 2024). Image ads ≈ ⅓ the CPC of video (lower-quality leads). — v04,05,23,26,33,38
- **Two ad types (v33):** *Direct Offer ad* (lead with a bold guarantee — when you have a real lead-gen USP) vs *Promise/Value ad* (lead with transformation + a named method — when your value is coaching/support).
- **Retargeting = the cheap second/third/fourth chance.** ~$1–5/day "ethical stalking" across FB/IG/YT/Google to anyone who engaged; serve testimonials, case studies, **a ~30-question FAQ objection-handling video.** ~70% cheaper, ~50% lower cost/conversion, aim ~6 impressions/day (7–10 touches to act). Upload lead CSV → custom audience. — v10,13,18,27,33,36,37,38,40
  - Server-side tracking ("Stape") to recover retargetable audience lost to iOS opt-out (8/10 → 3/10 → back to ~8/10). Expert-only. — v27
- **AI creative (v38):** Veo 3 for 3–15 sec hooks (AI avatars "in front of houses"); "Manus" + "nano banana" for image ads in ~60 sec. Meta wants 20–50 fresh creatives/mo.
- **The "pixel moat."** Claim: years of agent-data spend trained a Facebook pixel that hyper-targets agents; feed only *right-fit* application data back so Meta finds lookalike top producers. — v10,26,49,52,54
- **You don't have to be on camera / be a top producer.** Hire an actress (~$300) or delegate calls to a closer (Josh recruited 25 in 90 days). But several videos also say *put your own face on it* since agents partner with YOU. — v34,37,38,44,45,47,49

**Kale map:** 🟡 **STEAL — directly fixes your paid-ads gap.** The v19 Meta setup, the license-qualifier field, hook-first creative, and the **retargeting + FAQ-video** stack are exactly what your [[project_joinkale_meta_attribution_gap]] needs (optimize to *Lead* events, capture to Close, retarget warm). ⚠️ **For Kale, put DJ's own face on it** — your brand IS the differentiator (KIR podcast, NAR influencer); don't hire an actress. ⛔ Their "pixel moat" is a sales claim, not a capability you can buy.

## 6. The offer menu (their ~11 tested offers) — v10,21

Ordered aggressive → safe. All follow **benefit + guarantee + speed.** Useful as *structures*; the lead-gen guarantees mostly assume the broker provides leads (Zillow Flex / in-house marketer / white-label course).

1. Double your production in 12 mo, or we run your ads free until you do
2. Beat last year's income, or we double your lead flow
3. Beat last year's income or full refund **+ a $1,000 check**
4. First deal in 60 days or it's on us
5. 3 deals in 90 days or all three on us
6. First *listing* sold in 60 days or on us
7. Listing *appointments* (safer — doesn't promise a sale)
8. 10 appointments with buyers/sellers in 30 days (fulfilled via white-label self-gen course)
9. First appointment within 72 hours (speed/"Amazon Prime" play)
10. 20 exclusive leads/mo, not shared, or your first deal's on us (Zillow Flex partners)
11. We invest $1,000/mo marketing you for 90 days; <10 appts → we double the budget

**Their own offer to brokers (the meta-example):** *"10 appointments with producing agents on your calendar in 7 days or you don't pay — we keep working free until you hit it."* 16-week done-for-you engagement; market exclusivity (3 brokerages / 15-mi radius) as scarcity.

**Kale map:** 🟡 **STEAL the *structure* (benefit + guarantee + speed), not the lead guarantees.** Kale can't promise "20 exclusive leads" without a lead engine. But Kale *could* build honest, deliverable guarantees: e.g., a transparent fee/take-home guarantee, a "real human answers in X hours" support SLA, or a "first 30 days we set up your AI toolkit" onboarding sprint. ⛔ Skip the manufactured "3 per radius" scarcity unless it's literally true.

## 7. Monetization — ⛔ mostly REJECT for Kale

- **"Get paid to recruit" / onboarding-platform fee.** Charge agents $500–$15K up front (framed as paying for a *service/course*, not to "join"), reinvest into ads → "unlimited recruiting budget." Argument: paid agents value it + churn less; free = no-show (the "Friday-join/Monday-leave" guy). — v02,28,32,34,37,41,42,43,44,46,49,50,52,54
- **Digital-product / coaching flywheel ($1M without selling houses).** One funnel feeds 6 streams: $3–10K course + team production split + **revenue-share downline** + content + affiliate (CRM/IDX) + done-for-you ad services. Charging upfront "flips status." — v29,35,41
- **Ancillary revenue (the one transferable piece).** Profit from services agents use anyway (preferred lender, title/escrow JV) so you can offer attractive economics and still profit per agent. — v02
- **EBITDA / exit framing.** More agents + lower churn + low fixed cost (cheap CRM) → higher 3–6x EBITDA valuation → exit / loan / M&A (Compass–Anywhere $1.6B). — v21,23,26,34,35,38,48

**Kale map:** ⛔ **REJECT the core.** Charging agents to join and downline overrides **directly contradict Kale's flat-fee, "we removed the brand tax" wedge** ([[project_recruiting_objection_map]]). Importing a paid-to-join fee would blow up the positioning and feed objection #7 ("is this an MLM/catch?"). 🟡 **Steal only:** the **ancillary-revenue** logic (how Kale stays profitable at low fees is itself a recruiting *proof point* — "here's how we keep the lights on without taxing you"), and the **EBITDA/headcount** framing for DJ's own strategic thinking.

## 8. Retention / stickiness

- **"Recruiting doesn't grow a brokerage — retention does."** Make leaving "financially illogical" by being the white-label *engine* of the agent's business (leads, systems). Team leaders "won't leave a machine they didn't build." — v42,46,50,56
- **Agents who choose you for value (not splits) stay.** Split-joiners are bargain-hunters who churn. — v07,13,18,01
- **Pareto coaching.** Pour training into the top 20% (80% of revenue); let new agents *spectate* and imitate. — v03
- **White-label "Agent Accelerator" course** as both recruiting magnet and retention tool (agents produce → stay). — v18,22,24,32,34,35,37,39,46,47,50,52,53,58
- **Community / mastermind** as retention + a proof rolodex across markets. — v37,58

**Kale map:** ✅ **CONFIRMS the wedge** ("cheaper *plus* still here" — retention via real support, not lock-in). 🟡 **STEAL:** a Kale-branded "business-in-a-box" onboarding/training asset would serve the *Coaching/Mentorship/Social* chooser pages AND retention — but Kale gives it as *included support*, never a paid gate.

## 9. Rhetoric, objection-handling & scripts (lift for content + coldcall)

- "You don't have a recruiting problem, you have a system problem / an attraction problem." — v04,18
- "You don't have a lead problem, you have a conversion/follow-up problem." — v03,56
- Overhead Trap / "you have an overhead problem, not a work-ethic problem." — v13,14
- Blue-ocean: "99% of your market still cold-calls — you'll be the only one attracting." — v13,18
- "Stop being the top producer in your own office" (build a business, not a job). — v04,09
- 6 Bottlenecks & 4-link chain (Demand→Conversion→Retention→You) diagnostics. — v01,09
- Two-path / "6 months from now" future-pacing close. — v13,14,18,20,22
- Macro fear: "adapt or die," NAR settlement, lowest sales since 1995, 48% switching, 144k switched. — v34,38,48,54

- **Reference-refusal logic (v55):** won't hand over client references (selection bias + one bad ref = "marketing suicide"); redirects to Trustpilot + public YouTube interviews. (A tell worth noting.)
