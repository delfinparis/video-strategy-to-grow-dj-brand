## The one finding that drives the whole slate

The brand-as-consumer-magnet belief is a **myth, and it's already proven.** When buyers
and sellers pick an agent, "national brand" rates as *not important* (Baylor Keller Center
study, via Inman 2017), and roughly two in three sellers come through a referral or an
agent they have already worked with (NAR 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers).
Consumers pick the agent, not the brokerage.

But agents do not actually stay for the brand's consumer pull. They stay for culture,
mentorship, leads, accountability, and the fear of being abandoned. The best recent studies
(Mike DelPrete 2025; NAR Magazine 2026) rank culture and belonging and leadership *above*
splits and brand. And the number-one knock on cheap/100% firms is brutal and often true:
"you're on your own."

**The wedge for every Kale recruiting script:** we removed the brand tax AND we still
deliver the support the cheap firms strip out. Not "we're cheaper." Cheaper plus still here.
