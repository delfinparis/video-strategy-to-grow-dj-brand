## Findings

### CRITICAL - none

### HIGH - Hanna/Batton wording

**H1. "Hanna Holdings for $8.25 million" in the Pattern beat may confuse Batton vs. Tuccori.** The preceding sentences list Keller Williams and RE/MAX as Batton settlements. Hanna Holdings is different: it opted into Tuccori (not Batton). Script just names the amount without specifying the case, so it reads as though Hanna settled Batton too. Fix: add "in Tuccori" or "opting into Tuccori" to Hanna's line for accuracy.

### MEDIUM - Timing precision

**M1. "Exact same day" on Elliman/NAR opt-in is imprecise.** Sources place NAR on April 10. Elliman is described as filing "Thursday" which was April 9 or April 10 depending on reading. Script's "exact same day" might be correct but is hard to prove. Softened to "within hours of each other" or "the same settlement filing" - accurate and still punchy.
