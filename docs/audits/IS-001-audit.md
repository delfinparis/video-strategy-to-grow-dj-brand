## Findings

### CRITICAL - none

### HIGH - "800 deals" was an unsourced specific number

**H1. "Eight hundred deals where something almost went wrong" and variants appeared 4 times in the script and captions.** This is a specific number meant rhetorically to represent "a career's worth of deals" -- but per Rule 1, readers will take it literally. No named top producer in the archive has been confirmed at that exact volume. Softened to "a career's worth of deals" / "a career of reps" across all 4 instances (spoken script, LinkedIn caption, IG caption, X caption).

### MEDIUM - IS series standard compliance

**M1. IS scripts should name 2+ podcast guests supporting the pattern.** Original Data Source only referenced Garrett Maroon. Added Kristee Leonard (KIR 2026-01-22) and Karina Chavez (KIR 2025-12-09) as additional named guests whose interviews reinforce the "judgment-driven operational choice" pattern. All three were already verified in earlier audits.

### MEDIUM - WOW line + Data Source format

Both polished in this rewrite.

### LOW - Shareable Moment

Renamed from "Strongest Line" and updated to the stronger memorable line ("AI gives you leverage. Experience gives you judgment. One of those takes twenty years.").
