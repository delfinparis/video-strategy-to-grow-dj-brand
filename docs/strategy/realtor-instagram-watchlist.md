# Realtor Instagram Watchlist + Repurposing System

A curated watchlist of the best Instagram accounts that make content **for real estate
agents** (talking *to* agents with tips, news, and coaching -- not luxury-listing accounts
marketing *to* buyers and sellers), plus the system for watching them and turning their
ideas into D.J.'s own static/carousel posts without copying.

Built 2026-07-07. Sibling to the [Growth Intelligence Engine](growth-intelligence-engine.md):
that engine watches *creator-growth* sources (how to grow a channel); this watchlist watches
*realtor-content* sources (what to post to agents). The carousel format these feed is specified
in [`docs/series/carousel-standard.md`](../series/carousel-standard.md).

---

## Why this exists

D.J.'s socials are strong on 45-75s talking-head news commentary (Inside the Industry). The
gap is **static and carousel posts** -- the saveable, shareable format that accounts like
Coffee & Contracts and Jimmy Mackin use to rack up follows and saves. Those accounts have
already solved "what makes an agent stop, save, and send." This watchlist is the input side of
a repurposing loop: watch the best, extract the *idea and the format*, reload it with D.J.'s
industry-news authority and Chicago-recruiting angle, and ship it as his own.

**The goal for this format (set 2026-07-07): reach and saves.** These posts prioritize broad,
saveable agent-education content to grow follower count and authority, with recruiting as the
softer secondary. That reorders the usual repo priority for *this format only* -- everything
else stays recruiting-first. It also means the two content lanes run roughly evenly: **news
repurpose** (turn an Inside-the-Industry script into a carousel) and **evergreen tips** (net-new
saveable how-to carousels modeled on the accounts below).

---

## The anti-plagiarism rule (read first)

Repurpose the **idea and the structure**, never the words or the exact creative. The line:

- **Steal the format.** "One-tactic breakdown," "5 scripts for X," "POV meme," "here's what
  the data says" -- formats are not ownable. Use them freely.
- **Steal the angle prompt, not the post.** If Coffee & Contracts posts "3 captions that get
  saved," that is permission to build "3 captions that get saved -- for listing agents in a
  low-inventory market," in D.J.'s words, with D.J.'s examples.
- **Never lift copy, slides, or a specific joke verbatim.** No screenshot-and-reword. If you
  couldn't defend the post as your own original take to the person you took it from, it is too
  close.
- **Add the thing they can't.** D.J.'s unfair advantage is 700+ podcast episodes, NAR access,
  and a live Chicago brokerage view. Every repurposed idea should carry a layer only he has.

## How to watch them (two paths)

Instagram has **no free public feed/RSS and no free API**, so there is no zero-effort automation.
Pick based on how much D.J. wants to spend and touch.

### Path 1 -- Lean manual (recommended to start, $0)

The habit, ~20 minutes, once a week:

1. **Follow all verified accounts from a dedicated context** (D.J.'s brand IG, not a personal
   scroll) so the algorithm learns to surface them.
2. **Make one IG "Saved" collection** named `SWIPE`. During the week, when any watchlist post
   stops the thumb, save it to `SWIPE`. No judgment in the moment -- just capture.
3. **Weekly swipe review (Sunday or Monday):** open `SWIPE`, and for each saved post ask the
   three repurpose questions below. Log the keepers in a running idea doc. Clear the collection.

This is deliberately low-tech. The value is the *weekly extraction habit*, not the tooling.

### Path 2 -- Semi-automated (when volume justifies a small spend)

Turn a subset of the watchlist into RSS and route it through the existing engine:

1. Use **RSS.app** or self-hosted **RSS-Bridge** to generate a per-account Instagram feed
   (both convert a public profile to RSS; RSS.app is paid past a small free tier, RSS-Bridge is
   free but you host it and it breaks when IG changes markup).
2. Store the feed URLs in [`data/realtor-ig-watchlist.json`](../../data/realtor-ig-watchlist.json)
   (the `rss` field per account, blank until you generate it).
3. Extend `scripts/growth_digest.py` to read those feeds. It already does exactly this pattern
   for YouTube/podcasts: pull what's new in the window, hand the text to Claude, and score each
   item. The realtor version scores against the reach-and-saves goal and the anti-plagiarism
   rule instead of the recruiting-first rubric, and outputs "3 carousel ideas to build this
   week," each tagged **news-repurpose** or **evergreen**.

Path 2 is a phase-2 build. Do not block the content on it -- Path 1 produces ideas today.

**Reality check on both:** scraping IG at scale can trip ToS and rate limits. Keep the watch
list human-scale (30 accounts, weekly), and treat any automation as a convenience layer over the
manual habit, never a replacement for D.J.'s judgment on what fits the brand.

## The three repurpose questions (run on every saved post)

For each post in the weekly `SWIPE` review:

1. **What is the format?** (one-tactic breakdown / listicle / POV meme / data card / myth-bust /
   before-after / "what to say when..."). Name it. Formats are reusable; log which one.
2. **What is the transferable idea?** Strip the specifics. "Captions that get saved" ->
   "saveable captions." "Cold-call opener" -> "objection opener." The idea is the seed.
3. **What is D.J.'s unfair layer?** How does 700 episodes / NAR access / a Chicago brokerage
   view make his version sharper or more credible than the original? If there is no layer, skip
   it -- a generic tip in D.J.'s voice is not worth a slot.

A post that survives all three becomes a carousel brief. Build it through
[`docs/series/carousel-standard.md`](../series/carousel-standard.md).

## Scoring lens (reach and saves, for this format only)

When choosing which surviving ideas to actually build, rank by:

- **Save-worthiness.** Would an agent save this to act on later? Reference material, scripts,
  and checklists get saved. Opinions get liked and scrolled. Favor saveable.
- **Send-worthiness.** Would one agent DM this to another? Relatable truths and "this is us"
  moments get sent. Favor sendable.
- **On-brand-ness.** It still has to sound like D.J. -- industry insider, not generic coach. A
  tactic that only works with an engagement-ask CTA, a fabricated stat, em dashes, or AI-speak
  is out (same hard rules as everything in the repo; see
  [`editorial-standards.md`](../editorial-standards.md) and CLAUDE.md).
- **Recruiting-adjacency (secondary).** Given equal reach potential, prefer the idea that also
  makes a Chicago agent think "I want to be where this person is." Soft, never the lead.

## Cadence and stat sourcing (where this connects to the rest of the system)

- **How many per week:** 3 carousels/week once ramped (~2 evergreen tips + 1 news-repurpose),
  added *alongside* the daily videos, not in place of them, plus an optional weekly still-text-video
  A/B test for the first month. The full rationale, the researched sweet spot, and the weekly grid
  live in [`docs/series/carousel-standard.md`](../series/carousel-standard.md#cadence-how-many-per-week-and-how-to-pepper-them-in)
  and are slotted into [`schedule/master-calendar.md`](../../schedule/master-calendar.md).
- **Where the stats come from:** carousels pull numbers from the *same* vetted sources as the
  walk-and-talks and podcast promos -- the evergreen stat bank (`data/news-briefs/stat-bank.json`
  via `scripts/stat_bank.py`), the daily news brief, the Coffee Talk aired scripts
  (`coffeetalk-episode-registry`), and the KIR episode analysis
  (`keeping-it-real-content-system`) -- all under editorial-standards Rule 1. Detail in the
  Carousel Standard's stat-sourcing section.

  why, the same way the Growth Intelligence Engine retires dead feeds.

  route it through the recruiting/partnership lane, not just the content watch.
