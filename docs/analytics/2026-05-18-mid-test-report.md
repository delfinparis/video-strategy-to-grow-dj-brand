## TL;DR

1. **The pivot is working.** FB Personal views are up **+225%** over the prior 28-day window (29,673 vs 9,100). Total engagement on FB Personal is up **+68%** (1,494 vs ~890). No other input variable changed; the content pivot is the variable.
2. **FB Personal is the engine.** It produces ~20x the engagement-per-follower of IG Personal and ~50x of LI Personal. The personal-first hypothesis from the April 18 synthesis is *radically* correct — but for FB specifically, not all three personal surfaces.
3. **TT Biz, YT Biz, FB Biz all working as designed.** Median walk-and-talk views 774 / 537 / 273 respectively. FB Biz has a viral ceiling — NF-012 hit 28,720 views on that surface alone.
4. **The "contrarian reframe" formula travels across every surface.** NF-012 (RE/MAX-mad-at-wrong-people) broke out on 5 of 6 surfaces it was posted to. Pattern repeats with NF-009-MRED, NF-011-hud, NF-010-nar-budget. This is the playbook signature to replicate.
5. **LI Personal needs a format pivot.** Reels underperform 15–20x against essay variants of the *same* script. NF-011 essay format: 1,019 impressions. NF-011 reel format: ~52 impressions. Same script, same audience.
6. **The funnel-conversion layer is unproven.** IG Personal produced **2 external link taps** in 30 days against 9,439 views. If representative, the tapthis.co → pixel-retargeting flywheel is not yet being fed at the dashboard's green-light rate (≥30 tapthis.co clicks/week per [`recruiting-funnel-dashboard.md`](recruiting-funnel-dashboard.md)). This is the question the July 19 decision should hinge on, not the content-reach layer.
7. **The audience composition is the recruiting story.** FB Personal followers are 54.2% in the 35–54 age band — exactly the Kale recruiting target. 53/47 women/men matches the realtor population. This surface isn't just biggest — it's most-targeted.

## The "Contrarian Reframe" Formula — Cross-Platform Replication

The single most-replicated content pattern in the pivot window:

| Surface | NF-012 (RE/MAX-mad-at-wrong-people) | Surface median for context |
|---|---:|---:|
| **FB Biz** | **28,720 views** (235 likes, 156 comments, 586,471 sec view time) | 273 views |
| FB Personal | 2,541 views | 840 views |
| **LI Biz** | 2,258 impressions, **1,047 viewers, 222 clicks** | ~30 imp |
| TT Biz | 2,165 views (19 shares) | 774 views |
| YT Biz | 1,168 views (20 likes) | 537 views |
| IG Personal | 260 views | 212 views |
| IG Biz | not posted (gap) | n/a |

Five surfaces, one script, every result outperformed the surface median. The angle structure is replicable:

1. **Take an industry story the audience is mad about** (RE/MAX agents are mad at the buyer).
2. **Name a different villain than the obvious one** (the seller, not the buyer — RE/MAX corporate sold it).
3. **End on a decision the reader has to make** (stop being mad at Real; decide whether to keep paying franchise systems that can sell you).

Other walk-and-talks that show the same DNA and replicated similarly:
- **NF-009-MRED-private-listing** — 1,963 TT / 896 FB Personal / 332 YT — "Real estate just changed overnight" framing.
- **NF-011-hud (crime/schools)** — 2,352 TT (60 shares) / 896 FB Personal — "HUD said the data is fine. Your opinion of the data is not."
- **NF-010-nar-budget (1-in-5)** — 503 TT / 668 FB Personal / 137 YT — "NAR just decided. Nobody told them."
- **NF-010-chicago-arbitrage** — 1,953 TT / 1,369 FB Personal — "Chicago appreciated 7x faster than the rest of the country."

**Tactical implication:** for NF-026 onwards, score each candidate script against the three-criterion test before production. Scripts that meet all three are the high-leverage shots.

## Format-Surface Fit Findings

Three distinct surface preferences emerged that the Apr 18 synthesis predicted only directionally:

| Surface | What it rewards | What it punishes |
|---|---|---|
| FB Personal | Walk-and-talks with a clear voice ("Nobody wants to see your pet photos" at 601 views suggests personal hot-takes also land) | Throat-clearing, repost duplicates (multiple cases of paired posts with one tanking) |
| TT Biz | Tactical content with shareable data points; short hooks; immediate "send to friend" trigger | Long set-up, no number in the first 3 seconds |
| YT Biz Shorts | Search-shaped titles; how-to + named-source content; tutorial structure | Conversational meandering; no clear topic in the title |
| FB Biz | Anything FB Personal likes, but with viral lottery upside | Same as FB Personal |
| IG Personal (Reels) | Walk-and-talks land, but Posts ("What I'm Thinking This Week") outperform Reels on this account | Pure news flashes without a personal frame |
| IG Biz | Modest signal across all formats | Anything experimental — algorithm doesn't index it |
| LI Personal | **Long-form essays**, named-source explainers, podcast promos | Native-format reels (15–20x underperformance vs essay variants) |
| LI Biz | Long-form essays in the brand voice; minimal reach but high intent | Anything that requires viral discovery |

**The LI Personal essay finding is the most actionable mid-test format change.** NF-011 in essay format hit 1,019 impressions on LI Personal. The reel version of the same script hit ~52. Recommendation: continue producing all walk-and-talk scripts as native short-form video, but for LI Personal specifically, **publish the spoken script as a text-essay post** in addition to (or instead of) the reel. The 15-second LinkedIn rewrite of the same talking points should take 5–10 minutes and is likely to produce more strategy-relevant reach than the reel.

## What Didn't Work — Things to Stop

1. **Duplicate FB Biz publishes.** Multiple cases of the same script posted twice within minutes (NF-016: 49 v 307 views; NF-022: 438 v 748; NF-013-compass posted three times) suggest a scheduling-workflow glitch or a two-page crosspost that is throttling one of the pair. Worth diagnosing before NF-026+ to recover the ~20% of FB Biz reach being lost to this pattern.
2. **NF-012 was not posted on IG Biz.** Your single best-performing piece of content this month exists on every surface except one. If that gap is a workflow error, fixing the publishing checklist captures meaningful upside on that surface.
3. **NF-011-listing-presentations on YT Biz got 2 views.** Same script earned 273 on FB Biz, 242 on TT Biz, 109 on IG Biz. Two views on YT is not a normal range — it suggests the upload was delisted, unlisted, or misclassified. Worth checking in YT Studio before the next mid-test refresh.
4. **AIT-chatgpt was bottom-3 on IG Biz (73 views) but top-1 on YT Biz (1,181 views).** Same script, opposite outcomes. Pillar quality is not surface-portable. The AI Tip slot should be optimized differently per surface — search-shaped on YT, conversational on FB/IG, tactical with a sharable data point on TT.

## Recommendation for the June 2 Mid-Test Check-In

The June 2 check-in is two weeks out. Going into it, the right framing is:

**The content layer is unambiguously working. The funnel layer is unproven.**

The Apr 18 pivot decision documented in [`../strategy/2026-04-18-pillar-pivot-decision.md`](../strategy/2026-04-18-pillar-pivot-decision.md) set the test at "30 tapthis.co clicks/week" as the green-light leading indicator. We have:

- ✅ Content reach growing aggressively (FB Personal +225%, +9 posts beating the prior surface ceiling)
- ✅ The pillar mix is right (Inside the Industry confirmed as #1)
- ✅ The audience composition is the right composition for recruiting (54% in the 35–54 band on the strongest surface)
- ❓ Unknown whether reach is converting to tapthis.co clicks at the dashboard's required threshold
- ❓ Unknown whether the 5 pixel audiences are growing at the +50/+30/+10 targets

**Specific actions before June 2:**

1. **Pull a tapthis.co GA4 report** for Apr 20 – May 31. If it's tracking toward 30 clicks/week by June 2, the pivot is green-light. If it's flat, the question becomes "great reach, no conversion — why?"
2. **Check the 5 pixel audience sizes** in Meta / Google / LinkedIn / Reddit / TikTok Ads Manager dashboards. Even if tapthis.co clicks are below target, growing pixel audiences mean the retargeting flywheel is being seeded.
3. **Decide whether to add a "DM me 'KALE' for a 15-minute conversation" CTA test** on 2–3 high-performing FB Personal posts. This is a parallel conversion path that doesn't require a tapthis.co click. Worth one structured test before drawing conclusions about content-to-conversion mechanics.
4. **Re-publish the 3–5 top-performing reels as LinkedIn essays** to capture the format gap.

The 90-day decision on **July 19** should be made on the funnel-layer evidence, not the content-reach layer. The content-reach evidence is already conclusive.

## Open Questions

1. **Why didn't NF-012 replicate on IG?** Same algorithm family as FB (Meta), same audience, same caption. NF-012 hit 28,720 on FB Biz and was either not posted or capped at ~260 on IG Personal. Worth understanding before banking on the contrarian-reframe formula universally.
2. **Is the LI Biz "1,047 viewers + 222 clicks" on NF-012 a one-off, or does LinkedIn's audience consistently convert at higher click rates than other surfaces?** If it's a pattern, LI Biz might be the *highest-intent* surface even with the smallest median reach. That would change its role from "candidate to drop" (Apr 18 recommendation) to "candidate to invest in differently."
3. **What is the actual tapthis.co weekly click count for weeks 17–20?** Not in any of the platform exports — has to come from tapthis.co GA4. This is the load-bearing missing data point for the July 19 decision.
