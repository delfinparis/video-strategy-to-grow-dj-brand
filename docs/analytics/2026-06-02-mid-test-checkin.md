2. **A second breakout landed — and it answers May 18's Open Question #1.** NF-027 (the federal-judge / MLS-Zillow reel, posted May 22) did **1,148,356 views / 593,449 reach** on **IG Personal**. 6. **Clarification on the May 18 "duplicates" finding:** those are *technical double-posts* of one script (a scheduling glitch — NF-016 49 v 307, NF-022 438 v 748, NF-013 ×3), **not creative variants.** Each video is a unique story, script, and message. ## The two breakouts, side by side

Both fit the [contrarian-reframe formula](2026-05-18-mid-test-report.md) the May 18 report identified. What's instructive is how differently they distributed.

| | NF-012 RE/MAX-mad-at-wrong-people | NF-027 federal-judge-MLS-Zillow |
|---|---|---|
| FB Biz | **70,000** | 909 / 762 (two cuts) |
| IG Personal | 335 | **1,148,356** |
| TikTok Biz | 2,337 | 879 |
| YouTube Biz | 1,173 | 344 |
| LI Biz | 2,266 (222 clicks) | 148 |
| LI Personal | 2,266 | 6,769 |
| IG Biz | *not posted (gap)* | 79 |

**Why NF-027 went nuclear on IG specifically:** it was the longest reel in the set (101 sec), a thorough explainer of a high-stakes timely story, and it earned **112 saves + 74 shares + 50 new follows**. Saves and shares are the algorithm's strongest forward signals — that's the lever, not the hook. (Engagement *rate* was tiny — 605 likes ÷ 1.15M = 0.05% — so it was a reach explosion to non-followers, not a loyal-audience moment. Good for discovery and 50 follows.)

**The takeaway for production:** the formula reliably produces breakouts; you cannot reliably predict *which surface* will catch. Post every contrarian-reframe script to all short-form surfaces and let the lottery run — the cost of the losing surfaces is near zero, and the winning surface pays for everything.
