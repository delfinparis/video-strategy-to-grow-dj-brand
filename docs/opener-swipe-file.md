# Opener Swipe File

Ready-to-film first lines, mapped to the 9 hook families ([`hook-matrix-cheatsheet.md`](hook-matrix-cheatsheet.md)) and tagged with a suggested visual open ([`pattern-interrupt-cheatsheet.md`](pattern-interrupt-cheatsheet.md)). Pull one each morning. These are *openers* -- the spoken line one. Act 1 still owes a payoff promise (the 3-act spine), so most of these open a loop the video then closes.

**Rules baked in:** friction points outward at a belief/practice/system, never at the agent (you stand *next to* them). No engagement-asks. Numbers that carry weight are left as `[sourced #]` slots -- fill from a real, cited source per universal Rule 1. Don't repeat a `hook_family` or a `pattern_interrupt` two posts running.

Format: **line** *(heat / suggested visual move #)*

---

## 1. Mirror -- name their private behavior *(heat 2-3)*

- "You wrote the follow-up text. You read it twice. You deleted it." *(2 / move 4)*
- "You've got a folder of leads you tell yourself you'll call Monday. It's Thursday." *(2.5 / move 5, hold your phone)*
- "You saved three of my videos and did none of them. I'm not mad. I do it too." *(2 / move 2)*

## 2. Sacred Cow -- attack a sacred practice *(heat 4, default family)*

- "Open houses are the biggest waste of your Sunday in this business." *(4.5 / move 6, on-site)*
- "Your monthly market update email is training people to ignore you." *(4 / move 1)*
- "Farming a zip code with mailers in 2026 is setting money on fire slowly." *(4.5 / move 1)*

## 3. Defector -- credential vs. claim *(heat 3-4)*

- "I teach AI to agents. I'm about to talk you out of using it for this." *(3.5 / move 2)*
- "I run recruiting at a 300-agent brokerage. I'm going to tell you when NOT to switch." *(3.5 / move 6)*
- "I've interviewed 700 top producers. Most of their advice will hurt you." *(4 / move 3)*

## 4. System Indictment -- indict the system, defend them *(heat 4, default family)*

- "Your first year didn't fail because of you." *(4 / move 1)*
- "The reason you feel behind is that the whole model is built to keep you paying." *(4.5 / move 1)*
- "Nobody taught you lead follow-up because your brokerage makes more when you guess." *(4.5 / move 3)*

## 5. Confession -- "I was wrong about..." *(heat 2-3)*

- "I was wrong about open houses for nine years." *(2.5 / move 2)*
- "I told agents to post daily. I was half right and it cost people." *(2.5 / move 2)*
- "I used to think the flashy brokerage name mattered. Here's what changed my mind." *(2 / move 6)*

## 6. Named Stakes -- a real number, name, or moment *(heat 3-3.5, number must be sourced)*

- "`[sourced #]` more inquiries. From one text almost no agent sends." *(3 / move 5)*
- "A Greenwich agent did `[sourced $]` last year. His whole system fits on an index card." *(3.5 / move 5, hold a card)*
- "`[sourced $]`. That's what NAR just agreed to pay in the `[case]` settlement." *(3.5 / move 1)*

## 7. Forbidden -- the thing nobody tells them *(heat 3-4)*

- "Nobody at your brokerage will tell you this, so I will." *(3.5 / move 3)*
- "Here's the part of the commission conversation they train you to skip." *(3.5 / move 7)*
- "The reason your split 'improved' and your paycheck didn't. Let's do the math." *(4 / move 5)*

## 8. Cohort Callout -- name a pro cohort *(heat 3.5-4)*

- "New licensees. Your first year is being wasted on the wrong thing." *(4 / move 4)*
- "Agents who just left a big-box brokerage. You're about to make one expensive mistake." *(3.5 / move 4)*
- "Part-time agents. Everyone tells you to go full-time. Watch this before you do." *(3.5 / move 1)*

## 9. Swap / List -- "don't say X, say Y" *(heat 2-3.5, the save magnet)*

- "Stop telling sellers 'I'll get you the most money.' Say this instead." *(2.5 / move 7)*
- "Three things to delete from your listing presentation today." *(2.5 / move 7, count on fingers)*
- "Don't ask a lead 'are you still looking.' Ask them this one instead." *(3 / move 5, hold phone)*
- "When a buyer says 'we're just looking,' stop saying 'no problem.' Do this." *(3 / move 7)*

---

## How to use this each morning

1. Check yesterday's `hook_family` and `pattern_interrupt` in the last script. Pick a different family and a different move.
2. Grab a line that fits today's brief. Fill any `[sourced #]` slot from the news brief or a cited source (never guess a number).
3. Make sure the line *promises a payoff*, not just a stop. If it only stops the scroll, add the loop (see the 3-act spine, Act 1).
4. Log both `hook_family` and `pattern_interrupt` in frontmatter.

*Expand this file as lines prove out. When one hits, note the post in a comment next to it so the winners rise. Built 2026-07-07.*
