# Hook Matrix -- Filming Cheat Sheet

One-page glance for filming. Full doctrine: Rule 10 in [`editorial-standards.md`](editorial-standards.md).
Pick a family on purpose. Log it as `hook_family`. Don't repeat a family two posts in a row.

---

## The 9 families (first spoken line)

| # | Family | The move | Heat | Sample first line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Mirror** | name their private behavior | 2-3 | "You wrote the follow-up text. You read it twice. You deleted it." |
| 2 | **Sacred Cow** | attack a sacred *practice* | 4-5 | "Open houses are the biggest waste of your Sunday in this business." |
| 3 | **Defector** | credential vs. claim | 3-4 | "I teach AI to agents. I'm about to talk you out of using it." |
| 4 | **System Indictment** | indict the system, defend them | 4-5 | "Your first year didn't fail because of you." |
| 5 | **Confession** | "I was wrong about..." | 2-3 | "I was wrong about open houses for nine years." |
| 6 | **Named Stakes** | a real number/name/moment | 3-3.5 | "403 percent more inquiries. From one text almost no agent sends." |
| 7 | **Forbidden** | the thing nobody tells them | 3-4 | "Nobody at your brokerage will tell you this, so I will." |
| 8 | **Cohort Callout** | name a pro cohort | 3.5-4 | "New licensees. Your first year is being wasted on the wrong thing." |
| 9 | **Swap / List** | "don't say X, say Y" | 2-3.5 | "Stop telling sellers 'I'll get you the most money.' Say this instead." |

**Family 9 is the save magnet.** Three shapes: the Swap (don't say X, say Y), the Stop-Doing list, the Do/Don't in the room.

## The one rule that protects the brand

**Friction points OUTWARD** at a belief, tool, practice, or system. You stand *next to* the agent, never across from them.
- ✅ "Your CRM is a graveyard you pay monthly for." (attacks the tool + neglect)
- ❌ "Lazy agents are killing this business." (attacks the agent -- banned)

**3 gut checks before you film a hot one:** Is it true and would I defend it on a podcast? Does a good agent respect me *more* after this? Is the friction aimed at the system, not the person?

---

## Cadence

- Heat **4-5** (Sacred Cow, System Indictment) = **once a week max.**
- Everything else carries the daily load at heat **2-3.5.**
- Every **~2 weeks**: run one **emotional / identity** post instead of a tip (permission slip, why-this-work-matters). That's what earns follows.

## Power phrases (slot into any family)

- Pre-load: "Nobody's going to tell you this, so I will." / "I'll probably get angry DMs for this."
- 15-sec re-hook: "Here's where it gets worse." / "This is the step everyone skips."
- Close (no asks): "Do this before anything else today." / "Open your phone right now."

## After you post (first 60 min)

- Reply to every comment. Pin a value comment (the script / next step / the receipt), never "follow me."
