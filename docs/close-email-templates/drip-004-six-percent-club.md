# Drip 004: Top Producer Phone Habit Follow-Up

**Triggered by:** Engagement on IS-002 (The 6% Club: Top Producers Still Answer Their Own Phone)

**Send within:** 24-48 hours of the engagement
**Best for:** Top producers (anyone doing $10M+ in volume), team leaders who still work deals themselves, agents who visibly care about relationships

## Subject

Saw you engaged with the phone habit post

## Body

[Name], the 6% Club post struck a nerve this week, and I noticed you engaged with it. That tells me you're probably in the small group that already gets why answering the phone is the whole business, not a chore to delegate.

I run the Keeping It Real Podcast, and I've interviewed most of the people in Chicago who do this job the way you clearly do. I'd love to find 15 minutes for a conversation about what else the top producers I interview keep doing that the rest of the industry has outsourced. Think of it as pattern-sharing between practitioners.

Here's my direct link: [CALENDLY_LINK]

-- D.J. Paris

## Rationale

- The engagement itself qualifies the recipient as "already in the 6% Club." Flattery is earned, not manufactured.
- "Pattern-sharing between practitioners" is the key frame -- it positions D.J. as a peer observer, not a teacher or recruiter.
- "Most of the people in Chicago who do this job the way you clearly do" is a powerful implicit credential: D.J.'s network is exactly the network this person cares about.
- Zero recruit pitch. Zero mention of Kale. For a top producer, the value has to be the conversation itself, not an offer.
- Caveat: This template is only appropriate for agents whose profile signals top-producer status (LinkedIn listings count, mentions of volume, team size). For mid-producers, use drip-003 instead.
