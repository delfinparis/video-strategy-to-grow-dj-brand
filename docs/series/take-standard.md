# The Take — Series Standard

This document defines the per-series rules for **The Take**, the contrarian video lane added
2026-08-10. It builds on [`editorial-standards.md`](../editorial-standards.md), which is the
universal standard. Everything in the universal standard applies here. This document only adds
what is specific to The Take.

**If this document and the universal standard ever conflict, the universal standard wins.**

The candidate bank (the beliefs, who profits from each, and where the evidence lives) is
[`../../data/sacred-cows.md`](../../data/sacred-cows.md). This standard is how a chosen entry
becomes a finished video.

---

## What The Take is

A short-form video where D.J. names a thing agents are told to do, shows it does not work,
names who makes money from it staying true, and hands over the thing to do instead.

**Core promise to the viewer:** *You have been doing this because everyone does it. Here is
why it does not work, here is who profits from you believing it does, and here is what to do
on Monday instead.*

The shape D.J. asked for is **"realtors shouldn't do X, do Y instead."** That is the spine, but
it is not the hook -- if all three weekly takes open with the same swap construction the lane
reads formulaic inside a month (see **Hook rotation** below).

### Why this is not just a Value Giveaway

The "say this, not that" giveaway flavor is also a swap, and the two will be confused if the
line is not held. The difference is **beat 2**:

| | Value Giveaway ("say this, not that") | The Take |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Generate leads | Reach + authority |
| Gate | Gated on IG/FB | **Never gated, any platform** |
| Beat 2 | The better words | **Who profits from the wrong default** |
| Register | Helpful | Contrarian, heat 3.5-4 |
| Disagreement | None expected | **Required.** If nobody argues, it is a tip |

A take without the who-profits beat is a giveaway that forgot its gate. Send it back.

### Why it is not Broker Problems either

Broker Problems points at the viewer's *brokerage*. The Take points at a *practice, belief, or
incentive* anywhere in the business. There is overlap in the Brokerage economics section of the
bank; when a candidate is really about splits, fees, or support, run it as a Broker Problem
instead and keep the take slot for practice, marketing, prospecting, and money beliefs.

## Cadence and slotting

**3 per week: Mon, Wed, Fri.** Ungated on every platform. Video count goes from 12 to 15 per
week; the post total goes from 17 to 20.

**Each take video pairs with the take carousel already scheduled that day** (Mon/Wed/Fri 9:00am
routine, [`carousel-standard.md`](carousel-standard.md)). Both surfaces work the **same bank
entry**, which is the whole reason this cadence is affordable -- one verification pass covers
both. The division of labor:

| Surface | Job |
|---|---|
| **Video** | Makes the argument. The who-profits turn is spoken, and the friction lives here |
| **Carousel** | Hands over the receipt. The number, the source, and the swap laid out to be saved |

They do not use the same hook family. The video claims its family first (see rotation below);
the carousel picks a different one.

**Claiming discipline.** The video claims the week's three entries when D.J. films, and logs
them in the rotation table in the bank. The carousel routine reads that same table, so a claimed
entry is not re-picked. An entry that runs on video and carousel the same day counts as **one**
use against the 8-week no-repeat rule, not two.

---

## Heat: 3.5 default, exactly one 4 per week, never 5

This is the constraint the whole lane is built around. Rule 9.2 caps heat 4-5 at **one post per
week** across the entire publishing schedule, and the bank already reserved that slot for video.
Three takes a week does not get to break it.

| Slot | Heat | Hook family | Posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 3.5 | 9 (Swap / List) | Defensible contrarian. Names the wrong default, cites the source |
| **Wed** | **4** | 2 (Sacred Cow) or 4 (System Indictment) | The week's one friction slot. Names a practice or an incentive as wrong |
| **Fri** | 3-3.5 | 7 (Forbidden), 8 (Cohort Callout), or 5 (Confession) | Contrarian from a different angle |

**Wednesday is the only heat-4 video anywhere in the week.** If a News or Broker Problems script
wants heat 4 that week, one of them gives way -- the cap is one post, not one per series. The
`news_brief.py` hook-cadence banner reports whether the friction slot is still open; check it
before drafting.

**Heat 5 is banned in this lane, permanently.** Heat 5 means naming a person, a brokerage, a
coach, or a specific product as wrong. D.J. runs a 769-agent brokerage and is on the record in
trade press. Point at the practice and the incentive behind it, never at a named party. This is
not a cadence rule that can be spent; it is a floor.

**And the rule is "no identifiable target," not "no name typed."** Read
[`sacred-cows.md`](../../data/sacred-cows.md), the section *Specific about the practice,
unidentifiable as a party*, before drafting beat 1 or beat 2. A knowing wink is not a shield:
"we all know which brokerage does this" carries the same exposure as naming them, with less
punch. The opposite failure is more common and worse for this lane, which lives or dies on
disagreement: hedging into "some brokerages don't provide great value" is safe, says nothing,
and nobody argues with it. Go maximally specific about the *practice* while the target stays a
category. Ship test: could a viewer name ONE company with confidence? If yes, rewrite.

## The qualifying test (inherited from the bank)

A candidate is only a take if it clears all four. These are restated here because they decide
whether a script gets made:

1. **Is there a real number behind it?** Named source, publication year. If the only support is
   "everyone knows," it is an opinion. Kill it or find the number.
2. **Does D.J. actually believe it?** He does not float positions he would not defend on a
   podcast.
3. **Would a competent agent genuinely disagree?** If every agent already nods, it is a tip, not
   a take, and it will generate nothing. The disagreement is the engine.
4. **Does it stand with the agent?** Friction points outward at a belief, a practice, or a
   system. Never at the agent for having believed it.

Test 4 is the one that fails most often in drafting, and it is the difference between a comment
thread and a pile-on. The agent believed the wrong thing because someone with an incentive
taught it to them. That framing is not a softener bolted on at the end; it is beat 2.

## The five-beat format (mandatory)

Every take runs this arc. It is the *compressed* [Viral 3-Act Spine](viral-3-act-spine.md):
beat 2 is the single story turn, and it does not expand into a developed narrative.

```
1. THE COW      - The wrong default, named flat in the first spoken line. 0:00-0:03,
                  spoken, no warm-up. This is the scroll-stop.
2. WHO PROFITS  - The turn, and the reason this lane exists. Someone makes money from
                  this belief surviving. Name the incentive, not the company.
                  ("Who told you that? The listing agent who wanted Sunday covered.")
3. THE RECEIPT  - One real number, named source, year. The proof the take is not a mood.
4. THE SWAP     - Do Y instead. One thing, physical, at the agent's own business,
                  doable tomorrow. This is the half of the promise most takes shortchange.
5. LOOP-BACK    - Rule 9.1. The last line reframes THE COW so the hook hits harder on
                  rewatch. Not a summary.
```

Act mapping: THE COW + WHO PROFITS = Act 1 and the turn. THE RECEIPT + THE SWAP = Act 3 payoff.
LOOP-BACK closes the loop.

**Beat 4 is not optional and it is not "so stop doing that."** D.J.'s framing is *shouldn't do X,
**do Y instead**.* A take that lands the indictment and hands over nothing is a rant, and it is
the fastest way for this lane to cost more brand than it earns. If the swap is not concrete, the
take is not ready.

**Beat 2 is what makes it a take.** The bank records a `Profits:` line for every entry precisely
so this beat is never improvised. Where the honest answer is "nobody, it is inherited folklore,"
say that -- it is a legitimate and disarming version of the beat.

## Length

- **Target: 45-60 seconds** (~100-150 spoken words).
- **Hard cap: 75 seconds** (~185 spoken words), per universal Rule 7.
- Beats 2 and 3 are where these run long. The receipt is one number, said once. If the script is
  over, cut beat 3 to its shortest true form before touching beat 4.

---

## The close (extends Rule 4)

Ungated means the full "here's what you do now" close with **zero engagement asks**. No keyword,
no comment ask, no "save this," on any platform.

Three forms, one per script, varied across a run:

- **Action close** -- "Open your listing presentation tonight. Delete the third slide." Tied to
  the swap.
- **Watchpoint close** -- "Next open house you sit, count how many people come in with an agent
  already."
- **Series signal (Rule 10.5, informational)** -- "I do one of these every week." Allowed because
  it is informational, not directive. Never "follow for more." Cap at roughly 1 in 3.

The **loop-back is a separate discipline layered on the close**, not a fourth option. The close
gives the action; the final sentence reframes the hook.

**Comments come from Rule 10.4, never an ask.** The defensible line that recruits defenders is
the native mechanic here: a heat-3.5 take where the agents who already agree feel compelled to
back D.J. up against the ones who don't. "You tell me if I'm wrong" is allowed as stated, never
directed. **Someone reads the comments on every take** and flags Chicago agents who out
themselves into Close, the same operating rule Broker Problems runs on.

## Hook rotation

The swap construction ("stop X, do Y") is the spine of every script in this lane, which means it
cannot also be the hook every time. That is exactly the formulaic-AI pattern the
[AI tells field guide](../ai-tells-field-guide.md) flags.

- **Rotate the family by slot** per the heat table above. Log `hook_family` in frontmatter.
- **Never run the same family in consecutive takes**, and never the same family as the day's
  paired carousel.
- **Max one "it's not X, it's Y" construction per script**, and do not stack it with a
  Rule-of-Three.
- Pull the first line from [`../opener-swipe-file.md`](../opener-swipe-file.md) mapped to the
  chosen family, then sharpen it to the specific cow. Never write the hook cold.
- Pair with a **visual open** from [`../pattern-interrupt-cheatsheet.md`](../pattern-interrupt-cheatsheet.md),
  logged as `pattern_interrupt`, not repeated two posts in a row.

## Source rigor (extends Rule 1)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Re-verify at build time** | The bank is candidates, not scripts. A stat that was right in April may be wrong by August. Re-confirm before drafting, every time. |
| **Named source + year** | In the Data Source section and, where the cadence allows, spoken. No "studies show." |
| **One receipt per script** | Never stack two numbers. Beat 3 is one number said once. |
| **Never invent the counter-number** | If the number that would make the take work cannot be verified, the take does not ship. Bank entries carrying a `Flag:` say so explicitly. |
| **`evidence: research` entries are not ready** | An entry marked `research` in the bank has no verified number yet. Verify it first or pick another entry. |

The receipt is the difference between a take and a hot take. A take that loses its number in the
comments does more damage than the reach was worth.

---

## Never name

Not in the script, not in the captions, not in the on-screen text:

- A named brokerage, franchise, or team
- A named coach, coaching program, or trainer
- A named software product presented as the villain
- An identifiable individual agent

Name the **incentive**: "the brokerage that wants retention without earning it," "the vendor
selling speed-to-lead," "the coaching model that gets paid on seats." That is heat 4 and it is
defensible. The named version is heat 5 and it is not what this lane is for.

## AI Music Prompt (required)

Every take is a walk-and-talk and must end with an `## AI Music Prompt` section beneath
`## Data Source`, per [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) and [`../ai-music-prompts.md`](../ai-music-prompts.md).

- **Default preset:** the **Playbook / coaching** preset, tuned firmer. ~100-104 BPM,
  minor-leaning-but-hopeful, "confident, unhurried, slightly pointed." A take should not sound
  like a rant, and it should not sound like a coaching pep talk either.
- **Heat 4 (Wednesday):** drop to ~96 BPM and go more controlled, not more aggressive. Urgency in
  the music on top of friction in the script reads as anger.
- **300-character hard cap.** `[no vocals]` at the start, `no melodic hook, no vocal chops, no
  risers, no whooshes`, `unobtrusive background for voiceover`, `loopable`, BPM as a number.

## Social copy (extends Rule 5)

Five captions: LinkedIn, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook. No X block.

- **Zero em dashes**, zero AI-speak transitions, zero throat-clearing setups.
- **Hashtag caps** from [`../caption-and-hashtag-strategy.md`](../caption-and-hashtag-strategy.md):
  LinkedIn/IG/TikTok/YouTube 3-5, Facebook 2-3, realtor-first, one brand tag. Built fresh every
  time, never lifted from an older script.
- **No keyword, ever.** This lane is ungated on every platform including IG and FB. A take with a
  gate stapled to it reads as bait, which is the one thing that turns a defensible position into
  a pile-on.
- The LinkedIn caption carries the full argument including the receipt, because LinkedIn is where
  the who-profits beat gets argued in the replies and D.J. wants the source visible.

## The four passes still apply

Draft, stress-test, EP-polish, council review. Run silently, deliver only the final v3 with the
`## AI Music Prompt` and `## Council Review` blocks. Two additions specific to this lane, run
during the stress-test pass:

- **The rant test.** Strip the swap. Is what's left still worth posting? If the script only works
  because of the indictment, beat 4 is too thin.
- **The recruiting test** (Rule 10.3 guardrail 3). Read the hook as a good agent who might join
  Kale. Do they respect D.J. more or less? Less means kill it, regardless of projected reach.

## Anti-patterns

- **Indictment with no swap.** A rant. The most likely failure mode of this entire lane.
- **A take with no number.** Beat 3 empty means it is a mood, and the comments will find that out.
- **Naming a brokerage, coach, or product.** Heat 5. Banned outright, not rationed.
- **Blaming the agent for having believed it.** Test 4 of the qualifying test. "If you're still
  doing this, you're lazy" is not a take, it is an insult with a hook.
- **Three swaps in a week.** The spine repeats; the hook family must not.
- **Gating a take.** Never, on any platform.
- **Manufacturing the controversy.** Rule 10.3 guardrail 2. If D.J. would not defend it on a
  podcast, it does not run, no matter how well it would perform.
- **Reusing an entry inside 8 weeks**, or running the video and carousel on different entries the
  same day, which burns two bank entries for one day of content.
