# Pattern Interrupt -- Visual Open Cheat Sheet

The **spoken** hook stops the scroll ([`hook-matrix-cheatsheet.md`](hook-matrix-cheatsheet.md) is the first line). This sheet is the **visual** open that runs *underneath* the first spoken line -- the move the eye catches in the first second, before the caption even renders.

**The constraint this is built for:** D.J. shoots walk-and-talks on a selfie stick. One camera, one subject, motion. No B-roll rig, no second angle, no set changes. Every move below works one-handed, mid-walk. Pick one on purpose and log it as `pattern_interrupt` in frontmatter. **Don't repeat the same visual open two posts in a row** (same rule as `hook_family`).

Why it matters: captions.ai builds captions from audio, so the spoken hook still does the heavy lifting. The visual open is the *second* stop signal for the ~50% who catch the motion before they catch a word. It's free reach. It costs nothing but intent at film time.

---

## The 7 moves (all selfie-stick, all mid-walk)

| # | Move | How to film it | Pairs best with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The Stop** | Walk fast for one beat, then plant and lean into the lens on the hook word. Motion-to-freeze. | System Indictment, Sacred Cow |
| 2 | **The Push-In** | Start at arm's length, pull the phone toward your face on the key word. Manual zoom, no gear. | Confession, Named Stakes |
| 3 | **The Whip** | Point the camera at the *thing* (house, sign, block, ugly kitchen) for ~1s, then whip to your face. | Named Stakes, Forbidden |
| 4 | **Walk-Toward** | Camera held out, you walking straight *at* it, background sliding past. Not the side-profile stroll. | Cohort Callout, Mirror |
| 5 | **One Prop** | Hold up a single object on line one -- keys, coffee, a lockbox, a sold rider, your phone. | Named Stakes, Swap/List |
| 6 | **Location Cold-Open** | Start planted in front of something that doesn't look like a realtor video: a teardown, a $2M listing, a line at an open house. | Sacred Cow, Defector |
| 7 | **Gesture-On-Beat** | One sharp hand move synced to the first word: fingers up, point off-frame, palm-to-camera "stop." | Swap/List, Forbidden |

**Family 9 (Swap/List) tip:** moves 5 and 7 make the "don't say X, say Y" land -- hold up a hand or a prop as you flip it. Reinforces the save-magnet format visually.

---

## The one rule that keeps it authentic

The visual open serves the line. It never becomes a gimmick that outruns the point. If a stranger would think "why is he doing that," drop it. The move is a *stop signal*, not a magic trick.

- Motion and framing changes read as energy. Costume/prop stunts read as try-hard. Stay on the motion side.
- The location cold-open (#6) is the highest-value move for real estate: the property IS the B-roll you can't otherwise shoot. Use it whenever you're on-site.

---

## Rotation

Log `pattern_interrupt: [1-7]` in frontmatter next to `hook_family`. Two-post no-repeat rule on both. Over a week you should touch at least 4 different moves. If every open is The Stop, the feed flattens the same way it does when every hook is a Sacred Cow.
