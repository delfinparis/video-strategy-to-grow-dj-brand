**Goal:** turn the locked StoryBrand "problem-chooser" hero into a working funnel that captures every visitor into Close and fires a Meta "Lead" event, so paid ads finally have a measurable conversion.

**Status of inputs (all locked, June 2026):** hero headline, byline (769 agents), the stem "What I need most right now is ___", the 8 chips, the 8 modal video scripts, and the chip → modal → page architecture. See the StoryBrand homepage plan and [recruiting-objections.md](recruiting-objections.md). This doc adds the *funnel mechanics* — lifted from the [Agent Attraction teardown](recruiting-teardowns/agent-attraction.md) and adapted to Kale's flat-fee model.

> **Why this doc exists:** the chooser was designed as a messaging exercise. The 58-video Agent Attraction teardown showed the proven *plumbing* that makes a value-first recruiting funnel actually convert and track. This merges the two: your message + their mechanics, minus their paid-to-join model.

## The shared funnel (every chip runs this)

```
Chip tap
  → MODAL: 30-45s teaser video + 1 headline + "Learn more" button
  → PAGE (indexable): full reframe → 3-step plan → proof → CTA
  → CAPTURE: short application (not just a form) → books Calendly + writes Close lead + fires Meta "Lead" event
  → PRE-FRAME + SHOW-UP stack → the call
```

Three rules that make or break it:
1. **Different video** in the modal vs the page. Modal is the hook, page is the payoff.
2. **Capture is an application, not a contact form** (see below). This is the single highest-value steal from the teardown.
3. **Every step logs to Close** (chip tap, "Learn more" click, application, booking). That closes the [Meta attribution gap](../README.md) — ads can optimize to the Lead event instead of landing-page views.

## The capture application (the key mechanic)

Replace "name + email" with a 5-field application that doubles as a tire-kicker filter and call ammunition. Lifted straight from Agent Attraction's funnel, trimmed for Kale:

1. Are you a licensed agent in IL? (qualifier)
2. Roughly how many deals did you close in the last 12 months? (production)
3. What is your #1 goal for the next year?
4. What is the single biggest thing holding you back right now? (the bottleneck — this is the gold)
5. How soon would you move if it is the right fit? (urgency/timeline)

Then name + email + phone (with consent for follow-up). The application data writes to the Close lead so DJ or Ana walks into every call already knowing the agent's production, goal, bottleneck, and urgency. No blind calls.

## The 8 chip paths

Each: **teaser hook (spoken, modal)** → **reframe** → **page beat sheet** → **proof still owed**. Hooks honor [editorial-standards.md](editorial-standards.md): spoken, contractions, no em-dashes, straight quotes.

**1. Leads**
- Hook: "You don't have a leads problem. You have a follow-up problem."
- Reframe: more leads on a leaky pipeline just means more lost leads. Producers want a *system*, not leads dumped in their lap (teardown insight #1).
- Page: pain (leads you paid for, never called back) → reframe → 3-step (find, route, follow up) → proof → CTA.
- ⚠️ Proof owed: a named agent behind "found 2-3 ready clients in two weeks."

**2. Higher splits**
- Hook: "A bigger slice of not enough is still not enough."
- Reframe: "they'd rather make 80% of 20 deals than 90% of 15." Split is irrelevant if the brokerage helps you close more (teardown insight #2).
- Page: pain → reframe → take-home comparison → the wedge (brand tax removed AND support kept) → CTA.
- ⚠️ Proof owed: the real take-home calculator.

**3. Lower fees**
- Hook: "Cheap should never mean hidden."
- Reframe: not cheap, transparent and reachable.
- Page: pain → reframe → show the actual numbers ($400/sale capped $6,000/yr, $54/mo, $249/yr E&O) → CTA.
- ✅ Proof exists: joinkale.com/compare.

**4. Faster support**
- Hook: "A slow answer is a no answer."
- Reframe: producers leave from *neglect*, not money (teardown insight #4). The brokerage that picks up the phone wins.
- Page: pain (deal on the line, nobody answers) → reframe → name the support into a system (who answers, hours, response-time SLA) → CTA.
- ⚠️ Proof owed: who picks up, the hours, the real response time. (Naming it into a system is the steal here.)

**5. Coaching**
- Hook: "'Just prospect more' isn't coaching."
- Reframe: a real coach who trains you, not a pep talk.
- Page: pain → reframe → the named coaching program (cadence, who, format) → CTA.
- ⚠️ Proof owed: the coaching specifics.

**6. Mentorship**
- Hook: "You learn to close by watching someone close, not from a login."
- Reframe: a portal isn't a mentor.
- Page: pain → reframe → the mentor-shadow program → CTA.
- ⚠️ Proof owed (biggest risk): confirm a real mentor-shadow program exists before this page ships. Promise to new agents.

**7. AI that works**
- Hook: "You don't need another AI tip. You need AI that does the work."
- Reframe: tips create homework; you want output.
- Page: pain → reframe → pull from the "You're Doing AI 100% Wrong" webinar + toolkit → CTA.
- ✅ Proof exists: webinar + toolkit.

**8. Social Media help**
- Hook: "Your problem isn't posting. It's 'what do I even say?'"
- Reframe: you need the words, not a calendar.
- Page: pain → reframe → the system that hands you the content (the video-script engine) → CTA.
- ⚠️ Proof owed: what the social support actually is.

**+ Catch-all "Something else? Tell us →"**: 1-field box → writes straight to Close as a lead in the agent's own words. Highest-signal data; tells you the chip you're missing.

## Pre-frame + show-up stack (steal from teardown §4)

After someone books, don't go dark until the call:
- **Pre-frame page** after booking: who Kale is, 2-3 agent case studies, what the call covers.
- **Show-up boosters:** "add to phone calendar" button (native push reminders, claimed +10-15% show-up), one reminder video, reminder email + text.
- **Immediate manual follow-up** to anyone who applied but didn't book — call them like a fresh internet lead, don't wait on automations.

---

## Paid-ads loop (steal from teardown §5 — fixes the attribution gap)

This is the whole reason to build the chooser as a funnel:
- **Optimize the Meta campaign to the Lead event** (the application submit), not landing-page views. Today ads optimize to views and ~0 tracked leads reach Close.
- **License-qualifier as the first field** cleans data and trains targeting.
- **Hook-first creative:** first 3 seconds carry it. Put DJ's face on it (his brand is the differentiator — do NOT use a hired actress like Agent Attraction does).
- **Retargeting layer:** cheap retargeting to anyone who tapped a chip or watched a modal but didn't apply. Serve a short FAQ / objection-handling video (the "is Kale too cheap to be good?" objection from [recruiting-objections.md](recruiting-objections.md)).

## What we deliberately do NOT copy from Agent Attraction

- ⛔ **No paid-to-join / onboarding fee.** Contradicts Kale's "we removed the brand tax" wedge and feeds the "is this an MLM?" objection.
- ⛔ **No downline / rev-share framing.** Kale is flat-fee.
- ⛔ **No manufactured scarcity** ("3 brokerages per market," "28 spots left").
- ⛔ **No hired actor.** DJ's KIR/NAR-influencer brand is the asset.

## Build order (ship proof-ready first)

1. **Now:** wire the capture application → Close → Meta Lead event on the 2 proof-ready pages (**Fees, AI**) + the catch-all. Prove the loop end-to-end on real traffic.
2. **As proof lands:** build the other 6 pages in this order of risk — Support, Leads, Splits, Social, Coaching, **Mentorship last** (verify the program is real first).
3. **Then:** turn on retargeting + the FAQ objection video, and scale ad spend only after the Lead event is tracking cleanly.

**Single highest-value move:** the capture application feeding Close is both the messaging win (you finally ask agents their problem directly) and the attribution fix. Build that first.
