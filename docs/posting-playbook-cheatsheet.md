# Posting Playbook -- One-Page Cheat Sheet (2026)

Glance sheet for *when* to post, *how many* hashtags, and the caption shape. Full doctrine and the evidence live in [`caption-and-hashtag-strategy.md`](caption-and-hashtag-strategy.md). This sheet never overrides it or [`editorial-standards.md`](editorial-standards.md).

**The one thing to remember:** in 2026 every platform ranks by interest-graph recommendation, not chronological follower feeds. So timing and hashtags are hygiene, not levers. The hook, the story, and first-hour engagement velocity decide reach. Posting *daily* already wins the timing game. Don't agonize past the windows below.

## Per-platform quick card

Priority order is D.J.'s, from the stress test: LinkedIn is #1, Facebook #2 (74% Chicago, local recruiting), Instagram #3, TikTok secondary/national, YouTube #4. Times are audience-local (Central for the Chicago recruiting audience). Tue-Thu is the strongest band everywhere.

| Platform | Best window | Hashtags | Caption lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (#1) | Tue-Thu; **video 2-5pm**, text 10am-2pm. One post per ~12h max | **3-5** (never more; 5+ throttles) | Full setup + the real search phrase, plain words. Carries the long caption |
| **Facebook** (#2) | Evenings **5-8pm**; midday Tue/Wed | **2-3** | Same care as LinkedIn for the local audience. Keyword caption |
| **Instagram** (#3) | Mid-morning + **12-6pm** | **3-5** | Hook + one real keyword + the one move. Keep tight |
| **TikTok** (secondary) | Late afternoon into **5-9pm** | **3-5** keyword tags, skip #fyp | Search label + the hook |
| **YouTube Shorts** (#4) | Least time-sensitive; post when consistent | **3-5** (#shorts + 2-4 topical) | Title + description do the work, keyword-rich |
| **X** | Tue-Thu daytime | **1-2** | Hook + one keyword |

**Timing is worth ~20-30% of *immediate* reach, not a growth strategy.** A great video at an ok time beats a mediocre one at the "perfect" minute, every day.

## Hashtag rules (the short version)

- **Fewest that still categorize the post.** More is measurably worse on LinkedIn and Instagram.
- **Realtor-first.** Real estate tags lead; one brand tag (#KeepingItRealPodcast / #InsideTheIndustry) inside the cap is fine.
- **Drop the long tail.** Not #RealtorLife #RealEstateCoaching #SphereOfInfluence stacked. Pick the 1-2 most specific to the post.
- **No #fyp, no generic tags.** They do nothing for reach in 2026.

## Captions do the discovery work now (not hashtags)

- **Front-load one real search phrase** in the first line or two, in plain language the audience would actually type: "real estate lead generation," "buyer agency agreement," "Chicago real estate," "how agents get listings."
- **Weave the keyword into the hook,** don't bolt on a keyword-stuffed sentence.
- **Write for the send and for dwell, never for the comment.** "Would one agent DM this to another agent?" Sends and long dwells are the 2026 signals. No engagement-asks (banned by editorial standard *and* downranked).

## What NOT to do

- Don't chase the exact minute. Pick one slot per platform and hold it.
- Don't reuse the identical hashtag block on every video (YouTube especially can read it as automation).
- Don't add "comment below / tag an agent / share this." Ever.
