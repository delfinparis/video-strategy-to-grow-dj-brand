## Source facts (verified May 13, 2026)

These are the load-bearing claims behind every asset in this folder. Every other piece of content can be re-verified against this list.

- **Filing date.** Zillow filed in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois on Tuesday, May 12, 2026. (Multiple confirmations: Inman, Real Estate News, HousingWire, The Real Deal Chicago, RISMedia, Chicago Agent Magazine, Crain's Chicago Business.)
- **Named defendants.** Two: Midwest Real Estate Data (MRED) and Compass International Holdings. **MLS Grid is not a named defendant**, it is referenced in the complaint as a related entity controlled by MRED's CEO. (Confirmed across all 2026-05-12 trade coverage.)
- **MRED's threat (May 6).** MRED demanded Zillow reinstate Compass private listings in states outside MRED's territory and threatened to terminate Zillow's data access if it did not comply. MLS Grid issued a parallel termination threat the same day. (Inman, Real Estate News, Zillow's own news release.)
- **Compass terminated direct listing feed.** Compass terminated all of its direct listing feed agreements with Zillow nationwide on May 8, 2026. (Inman, RISMedia, HousingWire.)
- **MRED board composition.** Compass holds three of MRED's roughly thirteen board seats, equal to about 23 percent. Compass regional VP Fran Broude has served fourteen of the past sixteen years on the board. (Chicago Agent Magazine summary of the complaint, May 12, 2026.)
- **MRED CEO and MLS Grid.** Rebecca Jensen is MRED's CEO and chairs the MLS Grid board. (Inman, HousingWire.)
- **Compass Chicago market share.** Compass controls roughly 35 percent of sales in the Chicago market. (Chicago Agent Magazine, summarizing complaint.)
- **MRED nationwide expansion + subsidy.** In April 2026, MRED announced expansion of its Private Listing Network nationally. Compass became the first partner and agreed to subsidize part of the MRED membership cost for the first 100,000 Compass agents who join MRED nationally. Zillow's complaint argues this could triple MRED's size. (Inman, RISMedia.)
- **Prior Compass v. Zillow case.** Compass filed an antitrust suit against Zillow over Listing Access Standards in March 2026; a federal judge denied Compass's request for preliminary injunction in February 2026; Compass voluntarily dropped the suit on March 18, 2026 after Zillow relaxed enforcement. (CNN Business, Inman, Real Estate News.)
- **Banned listings under LAS.** Industry reporting indicates roughly 48 listings have been permanently banned from Zillow under its Listing Access Standards as of recent enforcement, with the majority being Compass listings. Sourced to Open Homes Photography's analysis; this figure should be requalified before any press use because the exact number changes over time.

## What this package does and does not do

This package gives D.J. Paris a complete pre-built response to one news event, across formats. It is not a system for monitoring future MRED, Compass, or NAR litigation. If this case settles or escalates, the assets in this folder need to be updated; they were produced May 13, 2026, the day after the complaint was filed.
