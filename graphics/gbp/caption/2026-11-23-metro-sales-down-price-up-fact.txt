Illinois REALTORS' own October report shows Chicago metro closed sales fell 10.4% year over year to 6,994, while the median sales price still rose 3.5% to $380,000. Inventory dropped too, down 5.3% to 15,399 homes. Fewer sales and higher prices at the same time usually means buyers who are ready are competing over a shrinking pool, not a market losing demand.
