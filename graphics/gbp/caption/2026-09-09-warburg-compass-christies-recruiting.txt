Warburg Realty, a New York brokerage with about 120 agents and over a hundred years of history, moved from Coldwell Banker Warburg to Warburg at Compass in July 2026, then to Christie's International Real Estate New York on September 8, 2026. All three brands sit under the same corporate parent, because Compass's acquisition of Anywhere Brands put Coldwell Banker and the Christie's International Real Estate license in the same portfolio. The "brand choice" many agents think they're making when they switch firms is sometimes already decided upstream. Before your next move, ask who actually owns the brand at the top of the chain.
