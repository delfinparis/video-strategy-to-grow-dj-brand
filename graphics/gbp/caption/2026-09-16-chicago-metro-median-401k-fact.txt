Illinois REALTORS' own Market Stats, compiled with DePaul University's Institute for Housing Studies, put the nine-county Chicago Metropolitan Area median sale price at $401,000 in July 2026, up from $375,500 in July 2025. Statewide, 13,503 homes sold in July, up 0.8 percent year over year, while the 22,363 homes available for sale were down 4.7 percent from a year earlier.
