Chicago City Council approved $66.2 million in subsidies this month to turn the closed Von Humboldt School into 103 apartments, 61 of them reserved for households earning 60 to 80 percent of the area median income, according to Block Club Chicago. Rents will run from $945 for a studio to $2,257 for a three-bedroom, with construction starting in early 2027 and units ready by June 2028.
