eXp and Kind Lending are winding down their Success Lending joint venture, the in-house mortgage platform eXp has used to recruit agents since 2021, after production fell from $658 million in 2024 to $526.5 million in 2025 and just $270 million year to date in 2026. eXp says it's now exploring a new venture with Newrez. If a brokerage is pitching you a captive lender as a reason to join, ask for the joint venture's funded-loan volume trend before you sign, not after. Learn more at joinkale.com.
