HousingWire reports that seller impersonation fraud attempts, a criminal posing as a property's legitimate owner to sell it out from under them, hit 59% of title firms in ALTA's latest study, up from 28% just two years ago. A deal that collapses at closing over a fraud attempt costs your client time and money and costs you the referral, which is exactly why a Kale Realty agent is trained to talk through title's verification steps before a seller ever asks.
