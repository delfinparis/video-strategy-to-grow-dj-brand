A new study from the Association of Real Estate License Law Officials, covering more than 10
million residential transactions from January 2024 through June 2026, found coming-soon listings
sold 1.55 percent above MLS-listing performance in the first half of this year, while private,
off-MLS listings sold below MLS performance both years studied. This is regulator data, not a
brokerage's own numbers. If you're weighing how to bring a listing to market, the independent
research backs the phased approach over going fully private. Learn more at joinkale.com.
