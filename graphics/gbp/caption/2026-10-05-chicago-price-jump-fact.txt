Illinois REALTORS' July 2026 report put the City of Chicago's median home price at $425,000, up 13.3% from $375,000 a year earlier, while the number of homes sold dipped 2.9% to 2,137 closings. Prices are still climbing on tight supply even as fewer homes change hands, a gap worth explaining to any buyer waiting for the market to soften before they act. Learn more at joinkale.com.
