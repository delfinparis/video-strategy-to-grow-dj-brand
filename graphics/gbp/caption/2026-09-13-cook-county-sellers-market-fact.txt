Redfin's August 2026 Cook County report shows home prices up 5.1 percent year over year, more than double the national 2.2 percent rate, while active listings fell 3.6 percent. Over 43 percent of homes sold above their asking price here, compared to just 24 percent nationally, and the typical home sold in 51 days. Where the national market is loosening, Cook County is tightening. If you are buying or selling here this fall, price the deal on the local numbers, not the national headlines.
