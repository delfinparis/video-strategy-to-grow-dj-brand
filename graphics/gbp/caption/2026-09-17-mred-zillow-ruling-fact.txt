On September 15, 2026, a federal judge in the Northern District of Illinois denied Zillow's request to force Midwest Real Estate Data, the Chicago area's own MLS, to restore its listing feed access, and ordered Zillow's claims against MRED into private arbitration instead, per HousingWire and RISMedia. If you list in the Chicago market, this is worth watching: it means MRED's current rules on how your listings can appear on outside portals stand for now, with no court order blocking them.
