Chicago Agent Magazine reports suburban Chicagoland detached home prices climbed 5% year over year to a median of $420,000 in October, even as closed sales fell 11.5% to 2,246. Attached homes told the same story: median price up 3% to $283,250, sales down 9.9%. Fewer buyers are closing, but the ones who do are paying more, which points to a market short on inventory, not short on demand.
