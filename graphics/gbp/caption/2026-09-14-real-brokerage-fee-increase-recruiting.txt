Effective September 1, 2026, The Real Brokerage raised its U.S. annual brokerage fee from $750 to $900 and its CBR per-transaction fee from $40 to $50, according to the company's own published fee schedule. The change applies whether or not Real's pending merger with RE/MAX closes. When a brokerage built around scale keeps raising the fees agents pay to be there, it is worth comparing that against a flat fee that does not move. Learn more at joinkale.com.
