NAR's Pending Home Sales Index fell to 66.0 in September, the lowest reading in the index's history, down 7.3% from August and 11.3% from a year earlier as mortgage rates climbed again. Every region fell, but the Midwest's 8.7% year-over-year drop was the smallest of the four, well behind the West's 15% decline. Buyers who are still out there right now have less competition than the national headlines suggest. Learn more at joinkale.com.
