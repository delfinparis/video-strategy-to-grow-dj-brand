United Real Estate Group says it has paid agents $1 billion in cumulative commissions since it was founded, with 96 percent of every commission going directly to the agent at closing, according to RISMedia. The company says that structure nets agents 21 to 45 percent more than a traditional 70/30 or 80/20 split model pays out over time. Kale Realty runs the same idea: flat fee, so what you close is what you keep. Learn more at joinkale.com.
