Sotheby's International Realty, now under Compass International Holdings, converted its two largest independently owned franchises, ONE Sotheby's International Realty in Florida and TTR Sotheby's International Realty in the Washington D.C. metro, into company-owned offices this week. Together the two firms closed more than $12.5 billion in sales in 2025. It is the second such move in two months, following a July acquisition of eight more Sotheby's franchise subsidiaries. When a franchisor keeps buying back its own strongest-performing offices instead of collecting royalties from them, it is worth asking what your own franchise fee is actually buying you.
