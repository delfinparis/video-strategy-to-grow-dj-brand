On November 3rd, voters in Massachusetts, California, and Florida decide ballot measures with real stakes for housing. Massachusetts Question 7 would cap minimum lot sizes for single-family homes at 5,000 square feet statewide. California Proposition 45 would set strict deadlines, up to 365 business days, for environmental review under CEQA. Florida Amendment 3 would raise the homestead exemption from $50,000 to $150,000 in 2027 and to $250,000 in 2028 (HousingWire, October 18, 2026). Policy like this moves the market your next home search happens in. Learn more at joinkale.com.
