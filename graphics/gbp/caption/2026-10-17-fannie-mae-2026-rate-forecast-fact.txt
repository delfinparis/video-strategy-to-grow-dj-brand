Fannie Mae's Economic and Strategic Research Group now projects 30-year mortgage rates will average 7.5 percent in 2026, up from the 6.7 percent it projected in September, a roughly 80-basis-point revision in one month. The firm also cut its 2027 forecast for single-family mortgage originations to $1.86 trillion, down from $2.27 trillion (HousingWire, October 16, 2026).
