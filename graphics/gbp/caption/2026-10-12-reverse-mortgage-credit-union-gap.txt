Ron Kamler, president and CEO of Alliance Reverse Mortgage, says credit unions originated only 81 of the nearly 36,000 reverse mortgages closed nationwide in the past year, and estimates 99.7 percent of credit unions have no in-house reverse mortgage specialist. If a senior client's first call is their own credit union, they're likely being told to look elsewhere anyway.
