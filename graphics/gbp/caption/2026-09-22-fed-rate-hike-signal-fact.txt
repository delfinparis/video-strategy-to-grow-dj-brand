The Fed's September meeting came in hawkish, with the committee formally projecting one more rate
hike before year end, Redfin's head of economics research Chen Zhao reported this week. New York
Fed President John Williams and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack are both on the calendar to
speak again this week, and the market will be parsing every word for how many more hikes are
coming. A buyer waiting for relief should hear the wait might get longer, not shorter. Learn more
at joinkale.com.
