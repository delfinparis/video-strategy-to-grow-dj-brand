Redfin's Chicago housing market data through August 2026 shows the median sale price at $426,255, up 9.3 percent year over year, with 7,541 homes sold, up 2.0 percent from last year. Homes are selling in a median of 49 days, three days faster than a year ago, and the typical home is going for about 1 percent over asking with roughly three offers on the table. If you are pricing a listing or writing an offer in Chicago this fall, the local numbers say sellers still have room to negotiate. Learn more at joinkale.com.
