A federal judge gave final approval on August 4 to a $28.5 million settlement, $20 million from Keller Williams and $8.5 million from RE/MAX, resolving claims that industry rules kept buyer-agent commissions artificially high. Both companies deny wrongdoing. It's the latest in a string of commission lawsuits against the big franchise brands.
