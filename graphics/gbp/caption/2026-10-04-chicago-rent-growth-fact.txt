Zillow's July 2026 Rent Report put Chicago's typical asking rent at $2,253, up 5.1% year over year, the fourth-fastest rent growth among major U.S. metros behind only San Francisco, San Jose, and Virginia Beach. For a tenant watching their renewal notice climb, that gap is the conversation starter: run the math on what a mortgage payment looks like against a rent that keeps resetting higher every twelve months.
