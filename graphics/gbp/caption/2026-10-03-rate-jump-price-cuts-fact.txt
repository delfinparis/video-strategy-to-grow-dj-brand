Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed rate jumped to 7.28% for the week ending October 1, up from 7.03% the week before, the largest one-week increase since October 2022. At the same time, Realtor.com's September report found 20.8% of active listings carried a price reduction, the highest September reading since 2018. Higher rates and more price cuts in the same month means real negotiating room for a buyer who can handle today's payment.
