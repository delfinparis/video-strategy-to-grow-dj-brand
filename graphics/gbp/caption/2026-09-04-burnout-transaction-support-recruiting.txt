A RISMedia panel on agent burnout this week pointed to a specific fix: brokerages that provide real transaction coordinator support and centralized systems can take a closing from 35-40 days down to about 10, freeing agents to spend their time with clients instead of paperwork. eXp's Kendall Bonner put the framework simply: systemitize, delegate, automate, eliminate. If your current brokerage hands you the busywork instead of the support, that's worth a conversation. Learn more at joinkale.com.
