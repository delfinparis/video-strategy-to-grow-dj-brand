Redfin's August 2026 data shows sellers gave buyers concessions in 44.7 percent of U.S. home
sales, the highest share for August since at least 2020. Chicago sat at 21.9 percent, one of the
lowest rates of any major metro Redfin tracked, behind only San Jose, New York and San Francisco.

open with: the national headlines about a buyers' market are true almost everywhere except here.
