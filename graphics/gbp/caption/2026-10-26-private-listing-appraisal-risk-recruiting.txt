HousingWire reported this week that appraisers, including Cotality's chief valuation officer Shawn Telford, are warning that private and off-MLS listings hide the pricing history and condition data an appraiser needs, something one appraiser called "severe data scarcity." A seller who skips full market exposure to avoid a price-adjustment history can end up with a harder appraisal and a slower closing instead.
