Illinois REALTORS and the Institute for Housing Studies at DePaul University's August 2026 report found City of Chicago condo and townhome prices rose 11.4 percent between July 2025 and July 2026, inventory fell about 24.6 percent over that same stretch, and homes sold about a week faster than the year before. The condo market is tightening on its own terms, separate from the single-family story most agents default to repeating. Learn more at joinkale.com.
