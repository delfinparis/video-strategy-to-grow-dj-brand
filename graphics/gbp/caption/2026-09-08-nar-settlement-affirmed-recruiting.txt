The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit affirmed the National Association of REALTORS antitrust settlement on August 19, 2026, upholding more than $1 billion in combined settlements, including NAR's $418 million payment and HomeServices America's $250 million payment. The ruling closes off the last real uncertainty around the settlement: buyer-broker compensation is negotiated off the MLS, and a signed buyer representation agreement comes before a showing, permanently.
