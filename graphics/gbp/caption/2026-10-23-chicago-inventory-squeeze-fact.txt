The Chicago Association of REALTORS' October 2025 snapshot found the city had 4,281 homes for sale, down 21.5% from 5,454 a year earlier, with months of supply down to 2.3. The median sales price rose 7.2% to $370,000 over the same stretch. A market this tight rewards the agent who already has next week's listing lined up. Learn more at joinkale.com.
