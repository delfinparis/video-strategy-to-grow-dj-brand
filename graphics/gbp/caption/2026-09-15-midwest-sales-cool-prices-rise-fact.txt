The National Association of REALTORS' August 2026 Existing-Home Sales report, released September 10, 2026, shows Midwest sales down 3.1 percent from July and down 2.1 percent from a year ago, while the region's median price climbed to $340,400, up 3.3 percent year over year. Fewer sales and higher prices at the same time usually means the buyers still active are serious ones.
