A new Reventure analysis, reported by Crain's Chicago Business, found Chicago has the smallest gap between renting and owning of any of the 20 largest U.S. metro areas. The median Chicago-area mortgage payment runs about $714 a month more than the median rent, a 32 percent premium. In Los Angeles that gap is $3,842 a month. In New York it's $2,016. Nationally, owning costs about 48 percent more than renting on average. If you're walking a renter through the numbers this month, Chicago is simply a more rational market to have that conversation in. Learn more at joinkale.com.
