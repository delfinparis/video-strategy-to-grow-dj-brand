Compass launched its Global Division this week, an internal network connecting its U.S. agents to independently licensed professionals abroad for cross-border referrals. Tier 1 membership is reserved for Compass agents who are also independently licensed to transact in a foreign market, according to Real Estate News. It is a real service for clients buying property overseas, and it is also a structural reason to stay inside one brokerage's pipeline instead of building your own international network. Learn more at joinkale.com.
