Illinois REALTORS reported the Chicago Metro Area's median home price at $401,000 in July 2026, a 6.8% gain over the $375,500 median a year earlier. Homes for sale in the nine-county area fell 11.9% year over year to 13,970. Prices climbing while inventory shrinks is a seller argument you can make with a number instead of an opinion.
