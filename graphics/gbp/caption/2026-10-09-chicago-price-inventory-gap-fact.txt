Illinois REALTORS and the Institute for Housing Studies at DePaul University's August 2026 report found City of Chicago single-family home prices ran about 12 percent higher in July than a year earlier, while the inventory of homes for sale fell almost 29 percent over that same stretch. Fewer homes on the market and higher prices both point the same direction. A seller sitting on the fence right now has less competition to worry about than they think. Learn more at joinkale.com.
