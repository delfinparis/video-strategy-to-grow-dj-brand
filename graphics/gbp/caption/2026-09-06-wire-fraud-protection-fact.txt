Wire fraud tied to real estate transactions hit a record $275 million in 2025 across more than 12,000 FBI-reported victims, up from $173 million the year before, according to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center. CertifID's 2026 State of Wire Fraud report found more than one in five consumers now report receiving a suspicious communication during closing, and AI is making those messages harder to spot. Before you wire a down payment or closing funds, ask your agent for a written callback protocol, not just a verbal warning. Kale Realty agents build one into every closing.
