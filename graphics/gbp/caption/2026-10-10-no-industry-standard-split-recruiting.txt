An agent asked what the industry-standard commission split was after hitting a production tier that was supposed to raise theirs. RISMedia's answer, in a September 3 piece on the state of agent contracts: there isn't one. Splits, fees, forms, caps and referrals vary company to company and office to office, and keep shifting as mergers hand a few large companies the power to set the pace for everyone else.
