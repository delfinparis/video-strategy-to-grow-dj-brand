Chicago posted the strongest annual home-price gain of any of the 20 major metros tracked by the S&P Case-Shiller index for the fourth consecutive month, up 6.9 percent year over year, according to an OptionPremier.com analysis published September 2, 2026. Active listings are running 6 percent below last year and new listings are down 7.5 percent, while the median list price sits near $395,000, up 5.4 percent year over year. That is not a one-month blip. It is a market where sellers still have real leverage. Learn more at joinkale.com.
