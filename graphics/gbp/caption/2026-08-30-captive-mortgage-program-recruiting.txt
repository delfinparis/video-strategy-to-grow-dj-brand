Days after closing its $880 million acquisition of RE/MAX, Real Group announced it will stop selling new Motto Mortgage franchises and is folding mortgage services into its own One Real Mortgage platform, per RISMedia (August 28, 2026). Existing Motto franchisees can continue under their current agreements or mutually terminate.
