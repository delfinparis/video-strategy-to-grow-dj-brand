Redfin's Cook County, IL housing market report, published August 4, 2026, found homes in the top 5 percent price tier sold in 41 days in July, seven days faster than a year earlier, while homes in the bottom 5 percent price tier took 69 days, twelve days longer than last year. The county-wide median was 49 days, down two days year over year. Sellers reading one market-wide number are missing which end of it they're actually on. Learn more at joinkale.com.
