Cook County's median home price hit $396,338 in July 2026, up 8.6 percent year over year, roughly triple the national pace of about 3 percent, according to Redfin's July 2026 Cook County market data. Nearly half of homes, 48.6 percent, sold above asking price, and active listings were down 3 percent year over year. Sellers in this market are still holding real leverage right now.
