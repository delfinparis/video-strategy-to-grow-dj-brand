NAR's 2026 Member Profile found that 27 percent of REALTORS point to affordability as the single biggest thing keeping buyers out of the market, more than double the next factor, lack of inventory, at 12 percent. NAR Deputy Chief Economist Jessica Lautz put it plainly: today's market is split between repeat buyers with equity who move easily and first-time buyers struggling to save a down payment.
