REMAX's National Housing Report, as reported by Chicago Agent Magazine on August 20, 2026, put Chicago closed home sales at 10,089 transactions in July, down 0.4 percent year over year and down 8.2 percent from June. That is a real cooldown from June's numbers, not a headline blip. Sellers and their agents pricing off a spring pace are working against a market that just lost momentum month over month.
