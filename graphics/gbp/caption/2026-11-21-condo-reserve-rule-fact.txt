Fannie Mae's updated condo standard raises the minimum reserve requirement from 10% to 15% of a building's annual budgeted assessment income, effective January 4, 2027, and Fannie Mae's own estimate puts the added cost at $13 to $14 a month per owner. Five national trade groups just asked FHFA to delay it a year, but as written it takes effect in about six weeks, and a building that misses the requirement risks losing eligibility for conventional financing. If you have condo clients anywhere in Chicago, this is worth knowing before they ask you about it. Learn more at joinkale.com.
