The Institute for Housing Studies at DePaul University's August 2026 forecast found City of Chicago single-family inventory down almost 29% year over year as of July 2026, while single-family prices rose about 12% over the same period. Closed sales fell too, almost 13% versus last July, meaning fewer homes changed hands for more money with less to choose from.
