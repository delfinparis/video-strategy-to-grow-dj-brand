Redfin's August 2026 Cook County report puts the median sale price at $383,716, up 5.1 percent year over year, more than double the national rate. Active listings fell 3.6 percent to 19,578, and over 43 percent of homes sold above asking, against 24 percent nationally. The typical home sold in 51 days.
