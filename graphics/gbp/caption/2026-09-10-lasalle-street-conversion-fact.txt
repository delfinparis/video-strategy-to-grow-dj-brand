The Rector Building at 79 W. Monroe St., the first completed project in Chicago's LaSalle Street Reimagined initiative, opened September 9, 2026, with 117 apartments (41 affordable, 76 market-rate) backed by $28 million in city TIF funding. Five more conversions in the same initiative are planned through 2028, adding more than 1,700 apartments total downtown.
