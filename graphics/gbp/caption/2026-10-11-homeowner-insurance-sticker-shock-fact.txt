SoFi surveyed 520 homeowners in April 2026 and found household insurance costs rose 6.9 percent over the prior year, with nearly two in five seeing a single renewal jump more than 20 percent. Forty eight percent said they're considering raising their deductible or dropping to thinner coverage to manage it, a move that can leave a lender force-placing a pricier policy if coverage lapses.
