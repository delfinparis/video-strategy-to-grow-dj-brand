Illinois REALTORS' June 2026 report found 9,927 single-family and condo sales closed in the nine-county Chicago Metro Area, up 3.9 percent from June 2025. For-sale inventory fell 14.4 percent over the same period. More homes are selling out of a shrinking pool, which is a different pressure than a rising median price alone. Sellers weighing when to list, and buyers weighing when to move, are both working against a tighter window than last year. Learn more at joinkale.com.
