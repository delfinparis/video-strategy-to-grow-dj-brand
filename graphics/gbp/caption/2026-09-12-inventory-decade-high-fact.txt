NAR's August 2026 existing-home sales report shows total inventory crossed 1.6 million units for the first time since November 2019, pushing months of supply to 4.9, the highest level in more than a decade. Sales still slowed 2.0% from July as mortgage rates stayed elevated, and NAR chief economist Lawrence Yun noted that rates and sales activity are moving in opposite directions right now. If you are buying or selling in this market, that extra supply is real room to negotiate. Learn more at joinkale.com.
