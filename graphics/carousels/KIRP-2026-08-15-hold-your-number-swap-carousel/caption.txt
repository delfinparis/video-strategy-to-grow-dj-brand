Realtor.com's June 2026 timing analysis found something worth saying out loud at a listing appointment. Homes that go under contract around week four sell 1.8 percent closer to asking than the average home sold in the same period. Homes still sitting at week eighteen close 1.3 percent under it, a spread of more than three percentage points.

That gap usually isn't the market. It's the number an agent agreed to before there was anything to negotiate, because another agent had already said yes and nobody wanted to be the one who didn't.

The swap for your next appointment:

1. Don't repeat the seller's number back as agreement. Say this first: "I'll tell you what it's worth, and I won't tell you what you want to hear."
2. Don't blame the market for a number you agreed to.
3. Don't wait for thin showings to prove you wrong.
4. Don't confuse losing the appointment with losing the listing.

You can win a listing with somebody else's number. You just can't sell it.
