I keep hearing agents say the same thing when they think about leaving a brokerage. My
clients won't follow me.

I get why it feels true. It doesn't hold up.

66 percent of sellers found their agent through a referral or someone they'd already
worked with, not a brokerage brand (NAR 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers). Your
clients picked you. The sign was never the reason.

The fear is real. The math behind it isn't. A brokerage that can't compete on culture or
support sells agents a story about their own book instead, and the story keeps agents in
place a lot longer than the facts would.

If you ever do move, the playbook is simple. Call your top clients before the rumor does.
Tell them where and why in one line. Keep doing the thing that earned the referral in the
first place. None of that depends on your letterhead.
