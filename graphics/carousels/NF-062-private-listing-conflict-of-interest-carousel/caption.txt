Almost every reason you've been given to pull a listing off the MLS was written by someone who makes money when you do.

Most agents pitching private listings aren't the villain here. You were handed the script. So let's actually read it.

When a listing never hits the MLS, the listing brokerage has the best shot at the buyer too. Same firm, both sides of the commission. That's the engine behind the big private-exclusive programs. Inventory nobody else can see also recruits agents and pulls buyers to the office. Every one of those wins goes to the house.

The seller hears three words: privacy, control, exclusivity. Bright MLS and Drexel studied over a million sales. Homes on the MLS sold for about 17.5% more, roughly $54,000 on a typical sale. Zillow put the total seller loss past a billion dollars.

Two reasons actually hold up: real safety risk, and a seller who sees the number and still chooses privacy on the record. Everything else is a handful of luxury deals, not a default.

Slide 8 is the question to ask before your next private-listing pitch.
