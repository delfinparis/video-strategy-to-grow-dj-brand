Sellers outnumbered buyers by 51.3 percent nationally in July, nearly matching December's record of 51.8 percent (Redfin, published August 13, 2026). That is the headline every seller and buyer in Chicago has probably already read.

Chicago's own gap was 4.9 percent over the same period, 26,360 sellers against 25,125 buyers. Redfin classifies that as a balanced market, not a buyer's market.

Four things to bring to your next listing appointment instead of the national headline:

1. Pull your own metro's buyer-to-seller ratio.
2. Set buyer expectations to local leverage, not the national number.
3. Reframe balanced as still moving, not stalled, for sellers.
4. Check your own submarket. A citywide gap can hide neighborhoods running hotter or colder.

Pull the local ratio before the next appointment. Not the national one.
