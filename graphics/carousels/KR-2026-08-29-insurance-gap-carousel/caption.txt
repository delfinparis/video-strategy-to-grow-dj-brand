Most buyers comparing a new build to an older home focus on price alone.

Realtor.com's 2026 analysis found the average annual home insurance premium on a newly built home ran 35 percent lower than on a 20-year-old home, about $1,002 a year and roughly $7,300 over the first decade.

Newer wiring, newer roofs, and current building codes mean fewer claims, and insurers price that in before a buyer ever asks. Put a same-day insurance quote on the comparison sheet before the offer, not after.
