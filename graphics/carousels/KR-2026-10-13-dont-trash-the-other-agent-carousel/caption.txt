Every agent has sent a frustrated text about the other side of a deal. NAR's Code of Ethics
Article 15 says REALTORS can't knowingly or recklessly make false or misleading statements about
other real estate professionals, and that duty covers repeating someone else's version too.

The brokerages that actually prepare you for this don't just hand you the rule book and move on.
They talk through the exact language before you're mid-negotiation and frustrated, not after a
complaint gets filed.

Describe the behavior, not the person. Name what you don't know as what you don't know. Put the
venting in a text to your broker, not your client.
