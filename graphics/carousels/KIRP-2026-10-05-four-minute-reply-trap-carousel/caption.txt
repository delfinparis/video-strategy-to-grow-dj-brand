Replying to a client text within a few minutes feels like good service. It's actually a promise
you didn't mean to make.

Answer that fast once, at 9pm, on a Sunday, during your kid's game, and you've trained the
client to expect that exact speed on every question after it. The fix isn't slowing down on
purpose. It's naming the policy before they discover it by accident.

Tell every new client, on day one: these are my hours, this is how fast I reply, this is when I
go dark. Clients don't push back on a stated policy. They push back on being surprised by one.
