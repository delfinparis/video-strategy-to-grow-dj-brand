Sarah Maslowski has closed over $150 million in real estate from YouTube. No paid ads, no cold calling.

On a recent Keeping It Real episode, she told us about the week she missed a post. Her editor had a family emergency, and she had no video bank, no backup editor, no buffer.

"I had nothing in the bank, no backup video, no buffer to speak of," she said.

Her fix: a video bank of two to three fully edited videos, a backup editor identified in advance, and a strict split between planning weeks and filming weeks. None of it is complicated. All of it requires actually doing it.
