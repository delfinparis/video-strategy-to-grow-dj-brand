Illinois' Residential Real Property Disclosure Act (765 ILCS 77) makes a seller who knowingly
leaves a known defect off the form liable for the buyer's actual damages, plus court costs.

A basement that floods every spring, painted over and marked "unaware," doesn't disappear. It
waits for the first storm, then it's the seller's bill and the agent's name on the file. A
defect disclosed and priced in, repaired or credited at closing, still closes. The same defect
hidden becomes a rescission fight or a judgment.

Ask the direct question yourself before your next listing goes live. Put the answer on the form.
Price the flaw in instead of hiding it.
