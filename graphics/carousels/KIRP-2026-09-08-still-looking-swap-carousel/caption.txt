"Are you still looking?" is the most common cold-lead follow-up question in the business, and it
is a yes/no question with a built-in exit. Most leads take the exit, and the call ends before you
learn anything.

The fix is a question that assumes they're still in the process. "I've been keeping an eye on the
market for you. Has your timeline or price range shifted at all?" The exact wording is in the
carousel.
