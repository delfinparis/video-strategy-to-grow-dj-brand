Homeowners 62 and older are holding $14.66 trillion in home equity right now, an all-time high,
according to HousingWire.

That number doesn't do anything on its own. It sits there until someone connects it to a real
decision: downsizing into something that fits their life now, helping a kid with a down payment,
or aging in place with a plan instead of a worry. Most of the homeowners holding that equity
haven't run the math on what their house is actually worth to them today, and most agents
haven't asked.

If you have clients in your sphere who are 62 or older, this is the week to call three of them
and start that conversation.
