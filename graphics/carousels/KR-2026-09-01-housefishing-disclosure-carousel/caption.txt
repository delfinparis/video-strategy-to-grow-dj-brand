If a buyer walks into your listing and it doesn't match the photos, that's not a lighting issue anymore. It has a name: housefishing.

California's AB 723 now requires a disclosure and a link to the real, unedited photo on any digitally altered listing image. New York City has proposed the same rule for rentals. Zillow told Fortune it supports the idea.

Brokerages that build the habit now, label the edit, keep the real photo on file, aren't scrambling when a local MLS makes it a rule instead of a suggestion.
