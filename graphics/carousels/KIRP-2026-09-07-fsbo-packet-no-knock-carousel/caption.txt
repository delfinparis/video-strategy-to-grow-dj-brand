Megan Walters, co-founder of the Walters Team at eXp in Columbia, Missouri and one of NAR's 2026
30 Under 30, does not cold call FSBO or expired listings. Every Monday and Wednesday she pulls
the list. Every Tuesday and Thursday she drops a printed packet on each doorstep: a title
company, a lender, an inspector, her cell number. No knock, no introduction.

"I converted so many listings by doing that," she told us, "because they would call and say, do
you know how many agents just told me I was being stupid for doing FSBO?"
