Ben Lalez runs a Chicago team doing several hundred million dollars a year. On this month's Keeping It Real, he made a point most agents skip past: when someone says "I already have an agent," that is not the end of the conversation.

Why. 85% of agents never contact their client again after the sale. So "already has an agent" and "has nobody actually checking in" are usually the same person. Ben told a story about running into a stranger who couldn't remember the name of the agent who sold them their home.

His own move this quarter: pull the last 10 closed deals and trace each one back to its real source, then repeat only the things that actually worked for the next 90 days. And when someone already has an agent, he says this instead of walking away: "I'm glad you have somebody. I'd love to be your backup in case anything ever changes."

The agent who stays in touch wins the client who's already taken.
