I look at my own Instagram insights more than I look at my follower count these days.

Last month, 72.2% of the views on my Reels came from people who don't follow me
(Metricool export, April 2026). The algorithm was never checking who followed the account.
It was checking whether the people it showed the video to stopped and watched.

Follower count is a real estate marketing metric that made more sense five years ago than it
does now. The growth services selling engagement pods and follow-for-follow challenges are
still selling it because it is an easy number to sell, not because it is the number
deciding your reach.

Three numbers actually matter: completion rate, sends, and saves. Completion decides who
sees your next post. A send means one specific agent thought of you and passed you along.
A save means someone plans to come back and use what you posted. None of the three cares
how many followers you had when you posted it.

Open your own insights tonight and look at completion first.
