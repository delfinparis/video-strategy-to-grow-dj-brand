Four showings, no offer, and the seller wants to talk price. That call goes better when the checkpoint was set at the listing meeting, not when panic sets it.

HomeLight surveyed 859 top agents for its 2026 housing market outlook, fielded December 2-9, 2025. Nationwide, those agents reported it takes an average of 14 showings to sell a house in the current market, and that held even in the Northeast, the only region agents called a seller's market.

Agree on the checkpoint before the sign goes up: judge the price at showing fourteen, not showing four. This isn't a case for holding an aggressive list price. In the same survey, 57 percent of those agents said pricing slightly below market is the best seller strategy for 2026.

Source: HomeLight, Top Agent Insights: Housing Market Outlook and Predictions for 2026.
