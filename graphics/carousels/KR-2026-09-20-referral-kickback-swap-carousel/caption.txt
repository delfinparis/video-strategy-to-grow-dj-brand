Most agents build a referral list of lenders, inspectors, and title companies without ever writing down which ones pay them for the send. That gap is a federal violation waiting to surface, not a technicality.

RESPA Section 8 bans an undisclosed fee or kickback for referring a client to a settlement service. Get caught, and the exposure is a fine up to $10,000, up to a year in jail, or both, plus triple the referral fee owed back to the client. A brokerage that never trains agents on this leaves them carrying that exposure alone.
