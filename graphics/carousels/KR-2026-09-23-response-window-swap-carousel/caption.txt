Going quiet after the contract is stupid, and it costs agents referrals they never see coming.

You answer every text in minutes while a client is shopping for an agent. The day the contract is signed, that window quietly drifts to a day or two, and nobody agreed to the change. The client can't tell what normal looks like, so they stop asking real questions and start apologizing for texting you at all.

The fix is one sentence, said out loud at the signing table: the standing day for updates, the same-day window for anything urgent. Put it in writing right after. Then hold it.

A brokerage that hands you the tools to run a business like this, instead of just a desk and a split, is worth a look.
