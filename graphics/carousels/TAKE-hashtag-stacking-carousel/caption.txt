Every real estate content coach still says the same thing about hashtags: stack them, all

interactions than posts with none (Metricool 2026 Social Media Study, 24 million-plus posts
analyzed). Correlation, not proof of cause, but the direction isn't close, and it's held up
every time this repo has re-checked it since July.

The tag stack was never buying you reach. It may have been signaling a weak post the whole
time. Discovery moved to search, not tags, a while ago. Three to five specific, realtor-first
