Bobby Kerr has optimized more than 1,000 real estate agent Google Business Profiles. On Keeping It Real, he explained why most five-star reviews do nothing for an agent's visibility.

"It matters to Google what's in the review. It matters to the LLMs what's in the review," he said. A review that just says "great experience, highly recommend" doesn't prove to Google or an AI model that a real client had a real transaction.

His fix: coach the client to name the business exactly as it appears on the profile, name the neighborhood or street where the deal happened, and include one specific, provable result.
