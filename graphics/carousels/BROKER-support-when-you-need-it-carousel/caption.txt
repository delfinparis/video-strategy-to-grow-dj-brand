Every brokerage says it offers full support. Almost none write down what that means.

62 percent of agents with two years or less of experience earned under $10,000 in 2023
(NAR 2024 Member Profile). That's the exact window a new agent needs a mentor who answers,
a broker who checks their contract, someone who catches the mistake before it costs the
deal. It's also the window a lot of brokerages have the least reason to spend real time on
an agent who isn't producing yet.

Here's the test. A mentor who actually answers, not one assigned on a roster you have to
chase. A second set of eyes on your contract before you sign, not after. Real coaching,
not a login to old webinars. A name for who owns a deal when it falls apart, not an
instruction to "escalate."

Ask your broker one question. Who do I call at 9pm on a Sunday? Write down the answer.
