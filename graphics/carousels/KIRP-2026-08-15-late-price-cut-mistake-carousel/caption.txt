Denver's Q2 2026 data puts a number on a mistake most listing agents have made at least once. Listings that cut price between day eight and day fourteen averaged a $2,321 reduction. Listings that waited until day fifty to fifty-six averaged $28,278, more than twelve times as much.

Nobody decides on day fifty to give up twenty-six thousand dollars. It happens one week at a time. Showings are thin in week two, nobody wants to be the agent who brings up price already, so the listing gets one more week. Then one more.

Active inventory has held above 1.1 million for seven straight weeks, so the window is tighter than it was a year ago.

Four things to do instead of waiting:

1. Put a price-review date in the listing agreement on day one.
2. Track showings, not weeks.
3. Say the quiet part at the appointment, not at day fifty.
4. Watch inventory, not just the listing.

You're not deciding whether to cut. You're deciding what it costs.
