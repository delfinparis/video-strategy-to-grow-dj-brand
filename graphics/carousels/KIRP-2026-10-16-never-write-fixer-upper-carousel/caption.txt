Never write "fixer-upper" in a listing.

Zillow's 2026 listing-words study looked at more than two million homes sold in 2025 across
600-plus phrases. Homes described as fixer-uppers sold for 14% less than expected. "Turnkey"
was tied to 2.9% more, and "remodeled" to 2.2% more.

Fixer-upper homes often do need work, so part of that gap is the house. But the label also
reaches every buyer before they have toured a single room, and it tells them exactly how to
open the negotiation.

Write what the house needs instead. Original windows. Dated kitchen. A roof that needs
replacing. Specific facts let buyers price the work themselves.

Check your active listings today and take the word out.
