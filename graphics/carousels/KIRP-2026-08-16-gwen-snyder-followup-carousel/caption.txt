Gwen Snyder has sold half a billion dollars in Colorado luxury real estate. She has never made a cold call.

On this month's Keeping It Real, she said the reason plainly: most agents, by her own count 85 percent, never follow up with a client after the closing. She follows up with every one.

Her system is not complicated. Fifty to one hundred just-sold postcards to the whole neighborhood after every closing. Sixty new people a year, one a week, no pitch attached. Every past client added to a CRM and followed up with, especially right after they move in.

"Doing most and more is what you got to do," she said. Not a personality trait. A process, one any agent can copy.
