The agents who book the most appointments are not the ones talking the most.

Kuvaal Patel, Director of Agent Development & Team Growth at Anderson Real Estate Group, tracks
one number: how many conversations it takes an agent to book an appointment. Agents with a high
ratio "talk far too much, ask far too little and pitch their services before they understand how
they can help the consumer." Agents with a low ratio "lead their conversations with questions,
slow the pace and listen far more than they speak."

On your next call, ask questions first. Find out what they need. Pitch last.

Kale Realty agents keep more of what they close, which buys the time to actually slow down on
a call instead of rushing to the pitch because the split is eating the clock.
