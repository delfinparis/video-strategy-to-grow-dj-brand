Carrie McCormick is a top 0.001% real estate producer in Chicago, out of 43,000 licensed agents. She gets the same question at every conference and every listing appointment this year: is AI going to replace real estate agents.

Her answer, from this month's Keeping It Real: that's the wrong question. AI is not replacing agents. It is replacing average marketing, the kind of listing description a client can already spot as AI-written.

What AI still can't do is tell you why a comp sold low. A mold problem in the basement. A telephone pole behind the lot. That local knowledge is still the whole job.
