The week before closing is the most anxious week of the whole transaction, and it's also the
week most agents go quiet because nothing new has technically happened yet.

A buyer who doesn't hear from you that week doesn't assume calm. They assume the deal is
falling apart, and they start checking email at midnight for wire instructions that should
never arrive there in the first place.

A daily one-line update, even with nothing new to report, and a standing rule that wire
instructions only ever come by phone, is the whole fix. It costs five minutes a day and it's
the difference between a client who refers you and one who just disappears after closing.
