A class action filed by real estate agents alleges Zillow conditioned customer referrals on
routing buyers to its in-house lender, Zillow Home Loans. An economic analysis cited in the
case found those borrowers pay 10 to 15 basis points more on a conventional 30-year mortgage
than they would elsewhere. The litigation is active, with no ruling yet.

Whatever the outcome, the lesson holds now. A referral tied to where you place the loan is not
free, your client pays the spread. If your brokerage has never walked you through what's baked
into the leads it hands you, that is worth asking about.
