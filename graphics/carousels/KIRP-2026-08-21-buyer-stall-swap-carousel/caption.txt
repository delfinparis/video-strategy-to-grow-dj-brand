"I need to think about it" is the sentence that ends more conversations than any actual no.

Most agents respond with "take your time, here's my card," and hand the buyer a clean exit.
That stall almost always comes down to one of three things: the price, the timing, or something
they haven't said out loud yet.

Ask which one it is. Directly. Then stop talking and let the silence sit. Whoever talks next
loses the frame, and it should not be you.

The buyer who stalls hasn't said no. They've handed you a question you haven't asked yet.
