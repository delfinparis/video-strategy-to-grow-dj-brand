Seller concessions just hit a record for the month. Redfin's May 2026 data shows 46.2 percent of home sales included a concession to the buyer, up from 43.1 percent a year earlier and the highest share for any May since Redfin started tracking in 2019.

The reason is the ratio underneath it: 47 percent more sellers than buyers nationally right now. That imbalance is what's driving the concession rate, not seller generosity. Real estate listing appointments this month need this number in them, because a seller who thinks concessions are optional is about to find out the market disagrees.
