NAR's 2026 Member Profile asked REALTORS what most limits their clients from buying. Housing affordability topped the list at 27 percent, more than double lack of inventory at 12 percent and difficulty finding the right property at 11 percent.

Rates never top that list. If the people doing the deals say affordability is the real objection, a buyer conversation built around waiting for rates to drop is answering a question nobody asked.

Open with the payment instead of the price. Bring a lender to the first call. Recalibrate the search before the tour, not after three rejected offers.
