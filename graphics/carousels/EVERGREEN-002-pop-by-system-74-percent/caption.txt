A Kansas City agent grew her business 74% a year for five years. She spent almost nothing on lead generation.

Her whole engine is the pop-by. A small, seasonal gift, hand-delivered to a past client, with no transaction ask. The magic is not the gift. It is the rhythm: the same people, four times a year.

Start this Saturday:

1. List your 10 favorite past clients.
2. Block four hours.
3. Hand-deliver each one with a 30-second hello, no ask.
4. Repeat in three months.

Block the four hours on your calendar before you close the app.
