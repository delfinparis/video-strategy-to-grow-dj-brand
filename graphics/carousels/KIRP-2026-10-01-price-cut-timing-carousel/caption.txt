Price cuts get more expensive the longer a Denver listing sits, and Q2 data puts a dollar figure
on the gap.

REcolorado's analysis of closed Denver-metro listings found cuts taken between day eight and
fourteen averaged $2,321. Cuts taken between day fifty and fifty-six averaged $28,278, more than
twelve times as much.

Nobody decides on day fifty to give up twenty-six thousand dollars. It happens one week at a
time. Showings go quiet in week two, nobody wants to be the agent who raises price already, so
the listing gets one more week.

The fix costs nothing and goes in before you need it. Put a price-review date in the listing
agreement on day one. If there's no offer by day fourteen, the number moves, agreed on day one
while the correction is still small.
