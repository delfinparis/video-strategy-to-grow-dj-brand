Rocket Close, Rocket Companies' title division, was hit with a $175 million compensatory damages verdict in a March 2026 retrial over trade secret theft, and the case still isn't resolved. HouseCanary, the company that won the verdict, has since filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy trying to protect the award from its own lender.

None of that touches your buyer directly. But it's a real, ongoing legal fight inside the company a lot of bundled closings now route through, and it's worth remembering the next time a client defaults to "one less thing to coordinate."

If you stopped referring an independent title or lending partner because a client chose a bundle, that relationship is worth one phone call. Ask what they're offering now, and bring both options to your next buyer consult instead of defaulting to the bundle.
