I have interviewed more than 700 top producing agents on the Keeping It Real podcast. When I look back at the listing-appointment advice, four lines show up on repeat.

They are not pitches. Each one hands the decision back to the seller and makes the choice feel obvious.

1. Price-shopped: "cheapest agent, or the one who nets you the most?"
2. "I'll try it myself": "what is your plan if week two comes and goes with no offer?"
3. "Zillow already shows my house": "the portal shows it, I position it."
4. Gone quiet at the end: "name the one thing still holding you back."

Pick the one that fits your next seller and say it out loud before you walk in.
