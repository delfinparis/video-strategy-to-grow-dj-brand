The average buyer's agent commission fell to 2.36% in the third quarter of 2024, the exact quarter the new NAR rules took effect. By the third quarter of 2025 it was back up to 2.42%, per Redfin's own reporting.

That's the opposite of what most sellers assume happened. The settlement didn't shrink buyer-agent commissions. A seller who refuses to offer one isn't saving money, they're shrinking their buyer pool to whoever can cover that fee on their own.

The agent who brings this number into the listing appointment is having a very different conversation about concessions than the one most sellers expect.
