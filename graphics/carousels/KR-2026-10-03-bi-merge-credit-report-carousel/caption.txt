FHFA Director Bill Pulte says the agency is moving Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac from a three-bureau
credit pull to a two-bureau model for loans the GSEs back. The formal announcement is expected
October 12, 2026, at the Mortgage Bankers Association conference in Chicago, with lenders phasing
it in over the following one to three months.

Dropping one bureau from the pull changes the credit picture a lender sees. A buyer who looks
weak on one bureau but strong on the other two could qualify differently than they did under the
old three-bureau model.

If you had a buyer denied or priced up on credit in the past year, this is worth a second look
once lenders implement it. Ask your loan officer how they're planning to handle the transition.
