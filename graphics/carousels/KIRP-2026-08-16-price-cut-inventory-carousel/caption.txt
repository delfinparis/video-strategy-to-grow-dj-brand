Active housing inventory rose to 871,063 listings in mid-August, and 41.67 percent of those listings already carry a price cut, with mortgage rates still near this year's highs (HousingWire, Housing Market Tracker, August 2026).

Most sellers hear that as background noise. It isn't. A price cut doesn't just cost the dollars on the sign. It reads as a red flag to every buyer who already saw the listing at the old number.

The fix is on the front end. Show the seller the price-cut number before you agree to a list price. Set the exact week you'll revisit pricing if showings stall, before you sign the listing, not after 30 days of silence.

Price it right once. Nearly half the market is currently learning why that matters the hard way.
