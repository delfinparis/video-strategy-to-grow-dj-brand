Open houses do not find buyers, and the data has said so for years.

Per NAR's Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, 4% of buyers find their home through a yard sign or open house. 52% find it online. That gap has held across editions.

So run the open house for what it actually delivers: the neighbors in the room become your next listings.

Three moves:

1. Reset the seller pitch to "feedback and neighbors," not "we will find your buyer here."
2. Move your prep time into the online listing, where most buyers actually look.
3. Track every visitor in your CRM. Names and conversations are the real output.

Rewrite your open-house pitch to "feedback and neighbors" before the next one.
