The CEO of Realtor.com just said agents who push private listings are failing their own clients. I think he's right.

His name is Damian Eales. This week he called private listings a right side of history test. A home is most of a family's wealth. Hiding it from the market for a quiet deal isn't serving the seller. It's the agent putting their own commission ahead of the client's price.

Yes, Eales runs a listing site, so he benefits from open data. Name the bias and the math still holds. The pitch gets called "seller choice." But choice only counts when the seller knows the cost: fewer buyers, fewer offers, a lower number.
