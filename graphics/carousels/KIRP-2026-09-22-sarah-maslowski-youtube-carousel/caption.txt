Sarah Maslowski posted YouTube videos for two years before her channel produced a single deal.

This year, the same channel closed $65 million with 50 clients, and she put five deals under contract while on vacation in Europe.

She told us on Keeping It Real what she had in place before any of that: a three-month commitment made before she filmed video one, and a rule that she wouldn't post without an editor, set before she had a single subscriber.

"Your first videos are going to suck. You're going to get no views. And that's still okay," she said. She kept posting anyway.
