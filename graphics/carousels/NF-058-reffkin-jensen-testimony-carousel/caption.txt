Zillow offered Compass a $1.3 to $1.6 billion annual revenue uplift to stop keeping listings off Zillow. Robert Reffkin testified he turned it down.

Then MRED CEO Rebecca Jensen took the stand and testified that a Zillow executive told her to expect her phone dumped, her texts made public, millions spent on litigation, and a public spectacle. She refused.

Two days of testimony wrapped July 2 in front of Judge John Tharp Jr. in Chicago federal court. The biggest brokerage in the country and the MLS are aligned against the biggest portal, and the ruling could land within weeks.

If you are in Chicagoland, pull the last listing agreement you signed and check whether it names the portals the home actually shows up on. If it does not, that is this week's fix. Everywhere else, watch this one. It is the model going national.
