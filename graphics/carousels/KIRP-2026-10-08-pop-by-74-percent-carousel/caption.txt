gift hand-delivered to a past client with no ask attached. Coffee on the way to a showing. A
pumpkin on a porch in October. It still works for the same reason it always did. Showing up at
someone's door is the one marketing move an email blast can't fake.

List ten past clients, pick one cheap seasonal item, block four hours this Saturday, and
hand-deliver each one with a thirty-second hello. Do it again in three months.
