The 2024 International Residential Code sets an exact test for a legal bedroom: a window with a
net clear opening of at least 5.7 square feet, at least 24 inches of height and 20 inches of
width, plus 70 square feet of floor area and a real heat source.

A finished-basement nook with no window, or a converted attic with a door and nothing else, fails
that test the moment an appraiser or inspector measures it. The listing word doesn't change the
code. It just decides who catches the gap first.
