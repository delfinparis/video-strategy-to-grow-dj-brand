NAR's Code of Ethics Article 15 says REALTORS shall not knowingly or recklessly make false or
misleading statements about other real estate professionals, their businesses, or their business
practices. Standard of Practice 15-2 extends that to repeating someone else's false statement.
SOP 15-3 adds a duty to correct or remove one on any electronic media you control.

Here's where that lands on an ordinary Tuesday. "That agent's impossible" or "the seller's lying
about the repairs," said to your own client with nothing behind it, is the exact conduct the
article exists to stop.

Four swaps that keep you on the right side of it. Describe the behavior, not the person. Say
what you don't know as what you don't know. Keep a guess about the seller's state of mind out of
your client's head. Put the frustrated version in a text to your broker, not your client.
