Nick Schlekeway runs Amherst Madison Real Estate Advisors in Boise, top 1% in production
nationally, and he does not time-block.

His reason: a time block survives right up until the phone rings, usually around 10:30am, and
then the rest of the day runs on whatever is loudest, not on what matters. So he built his
schedule around energy instead of the clock. Mind, body, and spirit first, because that's the
one activity that builds energy rather than spending it. Then prospecting, immediately after,
while that energy is at its highest, because prospecting is the task that costs the most of it.
Conversion and client service come after, because they can run on what's left.

His line that stuck with me: schedule prospecting for the afternoon and "you're sending yourself
up to fail."
