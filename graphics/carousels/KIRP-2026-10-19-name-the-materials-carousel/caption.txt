"Open floor plan" reads like a selling point in a listing description. Zillow's research says it sells for less.

Homes described that way sold for 0.2% less than expected. Almost half the homeowners Zillow surveyed picked "open floor plan" as the feature most likely to raise their sale price. The data says the opposite.

Homes that name real materials instead, like white oak flooring, walnut cabinets, and soapstone counters, sold for as much as 3.5% more.

Walk the house before you write the description. Name what it's made of.
