Sarah Maslowski has closed over $150 million in real estate from YouTube alone.

She told us on Keeping It Real that she never films content the week she posts it. Her cycle: one week for planning, one week for filming, always running two weeks ahead of her actual post date.

"Content planning and content filming are two completely different mental tasks, and they should never happen on the same day," she said.

Her handoff to her editor is just as deliberate: raw footage, full script, and B-roll shortcuts in one folder, every time.
