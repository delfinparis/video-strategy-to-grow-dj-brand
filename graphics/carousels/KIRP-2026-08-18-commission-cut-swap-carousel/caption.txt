Your buyer just asked if you'll cut your commission. Maybe nicely. Maybe not.

Don't defend the number. Don't list everything you do. Don't fold because you're afraid of
losing them. All three are the trap.

Ask one question instead, then stop talking: "What part of what I do do you want me to do less
of?"

The commission conversation was never about price. It's about value perception. Most buyers
can't name a single thing, because they never thought past "opens doors."

You don't defend the number. You ask them to define the work.
