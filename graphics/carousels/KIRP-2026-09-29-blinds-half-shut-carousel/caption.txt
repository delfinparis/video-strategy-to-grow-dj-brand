Your listing photos have the blinds half shut.

Almost nobody pulls them all the way up. They twist the slats open, which still blocks most of the light. So the photo shows half a window in a house where the light is what's supposed to sell it.

None of the fixes cost money. Pull every blind all the way up. Move furniture a few inches off the walls. Take the couch out from in front of the window. Give every bedroom a headboard and two lamps, even borrowed ones.

It takes an afternoon before the photographer shows up, not a check to a stager.
