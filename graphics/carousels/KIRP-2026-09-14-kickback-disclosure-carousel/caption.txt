Federal law bans getting paid to refer your client to a lender, inspector, or title company
without telling them.

RESPA Section 8 makes it illegal to give or accept a fee, kickback, or anything of value for a
referral of a settlement service, and the penalties are real. Up to $10,000 or a year in prison,
plus liability to the client for three times the fee.

The rule doesn't ban recommending someone good. It bans doing it quietly.
