NAR's 2026 Member Profile asked its own members where their 2025 business actually came from, and virtual tours came back near the bottom. 81% of Realtors said the format produced zero business for them all year.

It photographs well on a listing page. It is not the thing generating the next deal for most agents paying for it.

Three things worth doing instead: find out what the tour actually costs per listing, put that money into direct outreach to past clients, and keep the tour only for the one buyer who genuinely can't make it in person.

A pretty tour doesn't replace a phone call.
