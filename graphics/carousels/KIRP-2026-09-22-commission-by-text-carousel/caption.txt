A seller texts "what's your commission?" and it's tempting to just answer with a number.

Don't. The moment you text back a percentage, the conversation becomes a price comparison, and price comparisons favor whoever quotes lowest, not whoever actually earns the fee.

HousingWire's Darryl Davis puts it plainly: "In almost every area of life, price and quality travel together." That case only lands out loud, on a call, where you can explain what the fee pays for before the seller lines you up against someone else's text.

Move the conversation to a call, frame the value before the price, and give a range tied to the property instead of a flat rate to everyone who asks.
