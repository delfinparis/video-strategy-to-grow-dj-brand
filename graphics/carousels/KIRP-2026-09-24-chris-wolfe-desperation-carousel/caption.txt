Chris Wolfe ran a house-flipping business for 12 years. In 2022 the Austin market crashed and
he spent two years losing it, then trying to figure out what came next.

He moved to a new market, El Dorado Hills, California, with no sphere of influence and no past
clients. First year: 33 transactions, $510,000 in net commission. Second year to date: 40
transactions, $1 million.

Ninety five percent of that first year came from two free sources, split almost exactly 50/50:
cold calling expired listings and for-sale-by-owners, and hosting open houses. "It was that
ingredient of desperation," he told us on Keeping It Real. "I need now business."
