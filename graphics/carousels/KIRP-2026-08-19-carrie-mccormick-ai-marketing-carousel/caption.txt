Carrie McCormick sells hundreds of millions of dollars a year, one of the top 0.001% of agents in Chicago. On this month's Keeping It Real, she named the question she hears at every listing appointment and conference panel: is AI going to replace real estate agents. Her answer: it's the wrong question.

"AI isn't replacing real estate agents. It's replacing average marketing," she said.

Her reasoning: AI can already pull the comps and the closest five sales. What it can't know is that one of those homes sold at a discount because of a mobile home next door, or mold in the basement nobody disclosed publicly. "When information becomes free, judgment becomes priceless."

Her three predictions worth building a content calendar around: lifestyle will outperform square footage as a value driver, hyper-local expertise becomes one of the most valuable assets an agent has, and the best agents turn into small media companies and educators.
