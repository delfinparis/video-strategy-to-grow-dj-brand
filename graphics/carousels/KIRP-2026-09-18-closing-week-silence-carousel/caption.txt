The week a buyer wires their life savings is the week a lot of agents go quiet. Nothing dramatic
is happening, so it feels like nothing to report. The buyer does not experience it that way.

The typical home that went under contract this year took 64 days to get there, the longest
stretch in six years, per Redfin. Buyers reach closing week already worn down. A daily one line
update in that window, even when nothing moved, is what keeps a deal calm instead of anxious.
