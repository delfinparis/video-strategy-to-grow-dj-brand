Just one in three home sellers who use an agent ever ask about the commission.

Ninety-three percent of the ones who ask get a lower rate, according to a Clever Real Estate survey of home sellers published in August 2026. The other two thirds just pay full rate and never find out they had room to negotiate.

The agents who don't lose that conversation aren't the ones with the best answer. They're the ones who bring it up first, before the seller has to work up the nerve to ask.
