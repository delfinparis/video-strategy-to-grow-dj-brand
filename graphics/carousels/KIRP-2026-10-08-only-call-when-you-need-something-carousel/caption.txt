Calling a past client only when you want a referral trains them to screen your calls.

Every time the ask is the whole reason you called, they notice. They see your name, they know
what's coming, and eventually they stop picking up. You didn't lose that relationship. You
taught them what your name means.

Flip the ratio instead. Three calls that give them something, a nearby sale, a tax deadline, a
contractor worth using, for every one call that asks for something. Open with the thing you know
about them, not "how's the market." Then when you do ask, ask once, clearly, and stop.
