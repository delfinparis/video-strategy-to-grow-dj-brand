$2,165. That is what a seller loses on average when one agent sits on both sides of the deal.

Zillow released a study on May 14, 2026 analyzing more than 15 million transactions from 2023 to 2025. Sellers in dual-agency deals lost an average of $2,165 per home compared with sellers who had separate representation. The pattern appeared in every year of the study.

Three ways to handle it:

1. Refer the buyer out. You keep the listing side, your seller keeps the full price.
2. If you stay dual, disclose it in writing and cite the study, above your state minimum.
3. If you stay on both sides, credit money back to the seller. Around $2,000 is a fair floor.

Pick one and put it in writing before your next double-ended deal.
