A buyer asks if rates are going to drop. The honest answer isn't yes or no. It's a plan.

Guessing in either direction gives a buyer a reason to stall. What works instead: "Today's rate is 6.54%. Let's run your actual payment at that number, so you're deciding on something real, not guessing about the future."

Offer to re-run the numbers if the rate moves. That makes the buyer feel tracked, not pressured.

They didn't ask for optimism. They asked for a plan they could act on.
