Federal law already answered the question about referral fees. Most agents just hope nobody asks it.

RESPA Section 8 bans an agent from taking a fee or kickback for referring a client to a lender, inspector, or title company without disclosing it first. Get caught, and the penalty is a fine up to $10,000, up to a year in jail, or both, plus triple the referral fee owed back to the client.

The fix costs nothing. Put the relationship in writing, offer real options, and say the sentence out loud before anyone has to ask it.
