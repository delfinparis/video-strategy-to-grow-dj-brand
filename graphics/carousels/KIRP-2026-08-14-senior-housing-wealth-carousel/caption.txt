Homeowners age 62 and older now hold a record $14.66 trillion in home equity (National Reverse Mortgage Lenders Association, RiskSpan Reverse Mortgage Market Index, Q3 2025 data, reported January 23, 2026).

Most of that wealth is sitting in houses that have not been listed, because most conversations with senior clients start with the mortgage rate instead of the equity they already have.

Four ways to open that conversation this month:

1. Ask about equity first, not the rate.
2. Put an annual downsizing check-in on the calendar for every client over 62.
3. Know one lender who actually underwrites senior wealth well.
4. Refer, do not advise, on the reverse mortgage question. That is a licensed lending decision.

Pick three clients over 62 this week and ask them what they would do with the equity, not the rate.
