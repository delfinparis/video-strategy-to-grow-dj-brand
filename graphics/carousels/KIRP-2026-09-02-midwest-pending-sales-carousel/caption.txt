NAR's July 2026 Pending Home Sales Index fell 2.3% month over month nationally and 2.2% year over year, a pullback chief economist Lawrence Yun tied to "the highest mortgage rates of the year" hitting mid-summer.

The regional breakdown tells a different story. The West fell 4.7% for the month and 7.1% for the year. The Midwest fell just 0.7% for the month and was the only region up year over year, at 1.7%.

Before your next listing appointment, know your region's number, not the national one.
