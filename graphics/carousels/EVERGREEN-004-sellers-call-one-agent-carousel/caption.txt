Eighty percent of recent sellers contacted only one agent before choosing who would list their home (NAR 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, Exhibit 7-3). Twelve percent called two. Five percent called three.

So the listing appointment is usually not a competition. It is a confirmation. The same report shows where that one call comes from: 37 percent of sellers were referred by a friend, neighbor, or relative, 29 percent used the agent they had already bought or sold with, and 1 percent found their agent through a social page they had no connection to.

Four things that put you in that one call:

1. Write the list of people who would say your name, then count it. That number is your real pipeline.
2. Put every past client's closing date in your calendar and make one call a year on it.
3. Post for the people who already know you. The feed is a reminder to your circle, not a net for strangers.
4. Rehearse your pricing explanation out loud. Nobody is comparing you to another agent, so the reasoning has to hold up alone.

Pick five past clients today and put their closing dates in the calendar.
