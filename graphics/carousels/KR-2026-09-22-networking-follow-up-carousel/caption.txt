Most agents walk into a networking event trying to be the memorable one. Sharp pitch, business cards ready, big numbers if anyone asks.

The agents who actually get referred back months later do the opposite. They ask a question that has nothing to do with the transaction, find the thing the other person actually cares about, and leave with one specific thing to follow up on.

A prospect forgets a pitch by the parking lot. They don't forget being asked a real question, and they don't forget a follow-up that proves you remembered the answer.
