There's a word now for AI-altered real estate photos that don't match the actual property: housefishing.

California's AB 723, effective this year, requires any digitally altered listing photo to carry a disclosure and a link to the unedited original. New York City's mayor has proposed the same rule for rentals. Zillow told Fortune it supports the disclosure and wants buyers able to see the original image next to the altered one.
