A listing's first ten days online can decide its final sale price before a single open house.

Chase Whitney of The Whitney Team in Orange County says the first 7 to 10 days on market
largely set the price. A weak online launch, thin photos, an unfinished description, reduces
tours, offers, and leverage right when it matters most.

He puts the swing at 2 to 3 percent. On a $1.1 million home, that's $22,000 to $33,000.

Don't put a listing live until the photos and the full description are finished. Day one has to
be the best version of the home, not a placeholder.
