Redfin's newest numbers: 57.9 percent more home sellers than buyers nationally in August, up from 52.1 percent in July. That is the widest gap in Redfin's records, which go back to 2013.

Sellers in the market grew 3.9 percent in a single month, the biggest jump on record. Buyers grew a tenth of a percent.

Redfin's senior economist Asad Khan put it plainly: the surplus of sellers over buyers "makes it a good time to be a house hunter, in some respects." His advice to buyers is to negotiate on price and ask for concessions.
