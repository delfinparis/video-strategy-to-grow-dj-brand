Compass sued Washington's Northwest MLS over fines its agents faced for using "coming soon" pre-marketing listings. The settlement creates a new listing status, launching September 4, that lets a seller market a home for up to 21 days without a public days-on-market clock or price-change history.

Compass CEO Robert Reffkin called the old rule "the textbook definition of an antitrust violation."

If your own MLS fines brokers for the same thing, this settlement is worth reading in full, not just the headline.
