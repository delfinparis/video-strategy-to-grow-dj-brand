Going quiet after the contract is stupid, and most agents have done it without noticing.

During the search, you answer every text in minutes. You want the business. The day the contract is signed, response time quietly drifts to a day or two, and nobody agreed to that.

The client can't tell whether two hours or two days is normal, because nobody told them. So they stop asking real questions, and every text starts with an apology for bothering you.

Fix it at the signing table, out loud, before anyone leaves the room. Tell them the actual window: a standing day for updates, and a same-day window for anything urgent. Put it in writing right after. Then hold the number you set.
