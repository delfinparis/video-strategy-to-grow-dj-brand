You keep calling a client who only reads texts.

You call. You leave a voicemail. She never calls back, so you decide she went cold. She didn't.
She read every text and wondered why you disappeared. Neither of you did anything wrong. Nobody
asked which channel the other one actually uses.

Ask three questions at the first meeting instead. Call, text, or email? What hours are okay? Do
you listen to voicemail, or should I skip it? Write the answers in their contact record, not on a
sticky note, then talk to them the way they actually listen.

Thirty seconds of questions. A whole deal's worth of less friction.
