The FTC settled its case against Zillow and Redfin on August 24. The facts, from the FTC's own release: in February 2025 Zillow paid Redfin $100 million, and Redfin agreed to shut down its rental listings business and hand its customers to Zillow for up to nine years.

Five states joined the suit. The settlement forces Redfin back into that market within six months, specifically because regulators decided one company controlling that much of it was the problem.
