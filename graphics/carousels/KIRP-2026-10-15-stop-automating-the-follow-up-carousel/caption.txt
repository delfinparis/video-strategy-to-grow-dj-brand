Carrie McCormick has sold $3.5 billion in Chicago real estate and leads the Carrie McCormick
Luxury Group at properties Christie's International Real Estate. On this month's Keeping It
Real, she said something most agents' CRM settings quietly argue against.

"Staying in touch with clients through personalized communication is more effective than
automation," she said. She's stayed away from automated touchpoints like drip emails and
digitally signed cards on purpose. Instead: phone calls, newsletters, and staying visible on
social media.

Her reasoning: "We never know when our clients are going to make a decision, but when they do,
we want to be right there for them." You don't control the timing. You control whether you're
reachable when it happens.
