42.5 percent of active listings took a price cut last week. That's up from 41.5 percent the same week a year ago, according to HousingWire's weekly Housing Market Tracker, published September 27, 2026.

That is not a market in freefall. It is a market that is repricing faster than a single comp pull at the listing appointment can keep up with.

Before your next listing presentation: pull sold comps from the trailing 30 days, not 90. Re-check them every two weeks the listing sits active. And say the comp's age out loud before you say the number. "The closest sale is 41 days old" earns more trust than a number with no date attached.
