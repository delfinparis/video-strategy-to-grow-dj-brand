You're trained to close the gap between what a buyer wants to pay and what it takes to win. Push
hard enough and you can usually get there. Nobody tells you the bigger number also raises your
own commission on the same call.

taxes included at both prices. Anchor to their ceiling, not the number they'll agree to after
three lost offers and no sleep. "This one's over your comfort line, there will be another" costs
you a deal sometimes. It also builds the kind of trust that sends you the next one.
