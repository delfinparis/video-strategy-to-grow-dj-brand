Most brokerages never teach you the sentence that stops a bad listing appointment before it starts. You learn to nod along with a seller's number because the agent before you already did, and losing the appointment feels worse than losing the sale six weeks later.

Realtor.com's June 2026 data shows the cost of that habit. Homes under contract around week four sell 1.8 percent closer to asking than average. Homes still sitting at week eighteen close 1.3 percent under it, a spread of more than three percentage points.

The fix is one sentence, said before you give a number: "I'll tell you what it's worth, and I won't tell you what you want to hear." Agents who train on scripts like this one instead of guessing at the appointment hold their price more often, and keep more of the listing.
