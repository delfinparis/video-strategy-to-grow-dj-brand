Sam Burke is a Top 1% broker in Snohomish County, Washington, and a 2026 NAR 30 Under 30 nominee. He's also 28, which means he built that production while most agents were still trying to get taken seriously.

Here's the discipline behind it. Early on, Sam noticed that full-service marketing (real photography, real video, real prep) tended to show up only on the highest-priced listings in his market. He decided his own listings wouldn't work that way.

Same photographer. Same videographer. When a deal doesn't cover cleaning or window washing, he's paid for it himself. Price point never changes the effort.

It became his differentiator in a market where that level of service usually gets rationed by commission size, and it closed a credibility gap that had nothing to do with marketing: being the youngest broker in the room.
