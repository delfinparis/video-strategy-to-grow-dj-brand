A misspelled name. A blank line where an initial goes. An old version of the form. Nobody calls
to tell you. The other agent just decides the whole deal will be sloppy, and when her seller has
two offers, she points to the cleaner one.

Read the whole contract out loud before you send it next time. Your eyes skip the mistakes your
ears catch. Then check four things: the names match the lender's letter, the address has the unit
number, the price matches in numbers and words, and the date is right.

Nobody tells you that's why you lost the deal.
