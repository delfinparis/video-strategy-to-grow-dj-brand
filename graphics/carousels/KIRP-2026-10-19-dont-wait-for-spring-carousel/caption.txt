"Let's just wait until spring" is advice a lot of buyer's agents give without checking it.

Realtor.com's 2026 research found listing prices typically fall 3.5% from their seasonal high by early fall. Redfin's September analysis found early October is typically the best time to find a deal in the Chicago market. Sellers who listed over the summer are often the ones most open to negotiating right now, not in April.

Get serious buyers touring this month. Tour three homes this week. If none fit, wait, with the facts instead of a guess.
