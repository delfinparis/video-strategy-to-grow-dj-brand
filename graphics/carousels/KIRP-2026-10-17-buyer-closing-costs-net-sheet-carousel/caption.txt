Most seller net sheets are missing a line, and it is costing agents a hard conversation later.

Zillow's 2025 Consumer Housing Trends Report for Agents found that about two-thirds of sellers covered some or all of the buyer's closing costs. Most listing presentations still quote one number: sale price minus commission. The seller plans around that number, then finds out at the table that a chunk of it went to the buyer's side instead.

The fix is two lines on the net sheet, not a new conversation. Run the estimate both ways at the listing meeting: what the seller walks away with if the buyer asks for nothing, and what they walk away with if you cover part of their closing costs. Tell them to plan on the second number.

Show them the smaller check first. It is the one they will actually get.
