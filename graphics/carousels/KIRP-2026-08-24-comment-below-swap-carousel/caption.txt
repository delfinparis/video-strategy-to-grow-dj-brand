The "comment below" line at the end of your caption is the reason fewer people see the post it's on.

Meta's Transparency Center still classifies that as engagement bait, a policy live since December 2017. Posts that explicitly ask for comments, tags, or likes get their reach reduced, and accounts that keep doing it see bigger drops over time.

The ask persists because a comment count is easy to put on a slide. It's harder to explain what any single comment actually bought an agent.

Delete the line from your next caption. Replace it with one sentence you'd actually send another agent, unprompted. That version travels because a send can't be begged for.
