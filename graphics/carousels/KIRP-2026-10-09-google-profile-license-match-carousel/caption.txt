A Los Angeles agent fixed one detail on his Google Business Profile, and his visibility in local
search changed because of it.

Alex Maldonado, a REALTOR at Circle Real Estate, told D.J. Paris on Keeping It Real that most
agents lose search ranking over something fixable: their Google Business Profile name doesn't
exactly match their state real estate license. Google treats the mismatch as a weaker listing.
Maldonado also writes his specialty directly into the profile title, not a bio paragraph, so the
words a buyer actually searches are already on the page.

Pull up your license tonight and compare it to your profile name, character for character. Then
check your name, address, and phone match everywhere else you show up online.
