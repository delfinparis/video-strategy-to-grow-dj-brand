# Zillow Unlock 2026: Speaker Interview Field Guide

Oct 6, 2026 · D.J. Paris

> Duplicate of the live Claude Doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/QWp6ANA6veYhMbpCrhqXWo (copied 2026-10-11). Edits made in the doc after that date are not reflected here.

## How to use this guide

Get 5 minutes on camera with each speaker below, then cut one 30-second clip per question for D.J.'s socials and KIRP. Every question is written to be read out loud as is.

- **Event:** Zillow Unlock, Fontainebleau Las Vegas. Registration and welcome reception Mon 10/12. Main days are **Tue 10/13 and Wed 10/14**. Closing party Wed 7pm at LIV Beach.
- **All times are Pacific.** Sessions are in the Main Stage, the Hub (15-minute talks), Lazuli 1 and 3 (45-minute sessions), or the Royal rooms (workshops).
- **Best moment to ask:** right as a speaker walks off stage, or at the Hub after their 15-minute talk.
- **One question per clip.** Don't stack two questions into one answer.
- **Ask them to start with the answer, not "great question."** The first spoken line is the hook, because captions are built from the audio.
- **Let them say their own numbers.** Several stats are self-reported, so the claim should come out of the guest's mouth, not D.J.'s.
- **Creator access:** the Hub runs live podcasts. Email unlockconference@zillowgroup.com, or ask Ericka Kurtz (Zillow VP of Agent Marketing, who opens both days) about a media slot.

## Tier 1: best clips for agents

These eight have the sharpest tips, data or predictions for working agents. Get all of them if you can.

### Kara Ng, Zillow Senior Economist

**Catch her:** Wed 11:00am, Lazuli 1 (affordability panel). Low profile and media-trained, so she's very gettable. She has fresh September data, including Chicago numbers.

1. "A buyer says 'I'll wait until rates drop.' What do you say back?"
2. "High-intent shoppers are up 21%, but sales are only up about 4%. Where are those buyers hiding?"
3. "New construction now costs about the same per square foot as resale in Chicago. Should buyers be looking at new builds?"

### Mischa Fisher, Zillow Chief Economist

**Catch him:** Wed 9:05am, Main Stage. He teaches at Northwestern, so open with the Chicago connection.

1. "Give me one prediction for spring 2027 that agents should plan around right now."
2. "Zillow's forecast went from up 1.2% to slightly negative. What do I tell a seller who wants to wait for spring?"

### Barry Jenkins, Team Leader, Legacy Home Team (LPT)

**Catch him:** Tue 11:30am workshop (Royal 13-14), or Wed 12:00pm at the Hub. His team does about 900 homes a year. Author of *Too Nice for Sales*.

1. "You connected AI to your database and it found 8 buyers your agents met and never called. How does that happen?"
2. "Give me the one prompt every agent should run on their database tonight."
3. "What will AI replace about agents, and what can't it touch?"

### Brett Siegal, Founder and CEO, Ruuster

**Catch him:** Tue 2:00pm, Hub. He's based in Chicago, so he's the most likely of anyone here to reshare.

### Tarasa Hurley, Team Lead, River Point Realty (Pittsburgh)

**Catch her:** Tue 3:00pm, Lazuli 3. #4 team in Pittsburgh (119 sides in 2025).

### Chase Whitney, The Whitney Team (Orange County)

**Catch him:** Tue 1:30pm at the Hub, or Wed 12:15pm in Lazuli 1. $431M in career volume. His 10-day listing launch isn't published anywhere yet, so this clip would be a first.

## Watch-outs

**Keep these topics off camera:**

- **Zillow v. MRED/Compass.** On Sept 15 the judge denied Zillow's injunction and sent the MRED claims to arbitration ([RE News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/09/15/mred-scores-important-victory-in-legal-battle-with-zillow)). Zillow people won't answer, and asking burns goodwill. Fine to chat about off camera.
- **Christie Cannon** (#1 team in Texas) is suing Keller Williams. Ask her about listings, not the lawsuit.
- **Dan Duffy, United Real Estate.** His pitch is that United's flat-fee model has paid agents $1B more. That's a competitor's version of the Kale pitch, so skip him for Kale content.

**Numbers to handle carefully:**

- **"87% of agents quit within 5 years"** (from the Shilo session) is a widely disputed stat. Don't repeat it on our channels.
- **Britany Boatwright's numbers conflict.** Her session says "nearly $1B in volume," but HousingWire reported $103M for 2025 ([source](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/exp-agent-builds-top-realtrends-team-despite-cancer-battle/)). Let her state her own number.
- **Self-reported stats:** Gerdes 98.99%, Hurley 80%, Colton Whitney +52%, and Siegal's 91% and 13%. Have the guest say them.
