# NAR NXT 2026: Top 50 Walk-and-Talk Hooks

Nov 16, 2026 · D.J. Paris

About 150 hook candidates came out of 20 session summaries from NAR NXT (New Orleans, Nov 6-8, 2026) plus NAR's 2026 buyer and seller research. The Short-Form Council cut them to these 50. Each one is a hook candidate, not a finished script. When D.J. picks one, it still goes through the full four passes.

## Read this before filming any of them

- **Session summaries are machine-written from audio.** They mishear names and blend numbers. A number marked `check first` is not cleared for camera.
- **Receipt labels:** `confirmed` = found in a named published source. `check first` = plausible, source named, not yet verified at the original. `speaker's story` = one person's account, say it as theirs ("an agent at NAR said...").
- **D.J. doesn't practice.** Every agent story is told in third person.
- **No hook names a company, product or person as wrong** (heat 5 is banned). Hook 12 says "the online estimate," not the brand.
- **Recording clients needs consent.** Any script that tells agents to record a meeting says "ask first."

## Council Review

**Who led:** Kane and Lazine on the stat hooks, Hormozi on the tactical ones, Berger and Heath as witnesses, Miller on clarity.

**What the board rewarded**
- **Kane:** a number or a flat "stop doing X" in the first five words. Hooks 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7 scored highest because the stat is the whole hook.
- **Berger:** high-arousal emotion. Anxiety (fraud, fair housing, safety) and indignation (commission check, price promises) beat warm advice. Each top pick survives being retold in one sentence.
- **Hormozi:** an exact thing to say or do. That's why the "exact words" block is eleven deep.
- **Simon:** recognition. Hooks 21, 27 and 29 are things every agent has done this month.
- **Heath:** each hook opens a gap the script then closes. Hooks 30 and 47 are the purest curiosity gaps.

**What got cut, and why**
- **No receipt found:** "74% of clients judge you in 30 seconds" and the other figures credited to a 750-person study. A search found no published version. They return if the study turns up.
- **Vendor sales claims:** a search consultant's "$6 million in listing interviews" and "beat Zillow in 60 days."
- **Heat 5:** "stop copying" a named TV agent, and any hook that names a portal's estimate as wrong.
- **Too soft (Berger: low arousal):** "know your why," positive self-talk, storytelling theory, personality-type frameworks.
- **Duplicates:** the two fraud sessions and the two AI-search sessions overlapped, so the best version of each idea stayed.
- **Off-lane:** rent control and tenant-union politics, commercial retail, tool lists.

**The one real disagreement:** Lazine wanted the list stacked with stat hooks, because numbers travel. Hormozi wanted the tactical "say this" hooks, because they earn saves and follows. Decision: 14 number hooks and 36 tactical, because only 14 numbers had a receipt or a nameable source. A stat hook with no receipt is worse than no stat.

**The dissent (next A/B test):** Chris Do says 49 of 50 are tactics and only hook 14 costs anything emotionally. Test hook 14 as the biweekly identity script against a tactical hook from the same week, and judge it on follows, not views.

## Bench (first in if a top-50 hook fails its receipt)

1. "74% of clients judge you in the first 30 seconds." Needs the study found.
2. "Chicago logged 165 source-of-income violations." Needs the source and the period.
3. "Pending home sales are the lowest since 2001." Reported by Homes.com from Lawrence Yun's remarks; news lane, time-sensitive.
4. "Who gets your client list when you retire?"
5. "Ask for the Google review 48 hours after closing, not at the table."
6. "A perfect five-star record looks fake." Needs the consumer research.
7. "A rate hike is the seller's problem." Only works in a rising-rate week.
8. "One listing meeting isn't enough. The best agents run three."
9. "Before you share a headline, ask three questions."
10. "Your list price should never end in 0, 5 or 9." One agent's habit, weak evidence.
