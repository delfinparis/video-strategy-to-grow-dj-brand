If you can't explain your commission in sixty seconds without flinching -- you're going to lose it.

It won't. And every time you stumble, they hear one thing: even my agent doesn't believe they're worth it.
Here's how you fix this. Sit down with Claude and type: "Help me build a clear, confident two-minute value story for why my commission is worth every dollar. My average sale price is [X]. Then practice it out loud until you can say it without thinking. Until it feels like a story you own -- not a pitch you borrowed.
