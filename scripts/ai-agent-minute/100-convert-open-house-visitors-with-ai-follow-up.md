## Full Script (Spoken)

You're sitting open houses every weekend and converting almost none of them -- and it has nothing to do with the house.
Here's what's actually happening. Most agents collect sign-in sheets, send a generic "thanks for stopping by" email the next morning, and consider it done. The lead goes cold because there was never a real continuation of the conversation -- just a transaction.
And here's the part that stings -- open houses give you something most lead sources never will. You had a real conversation with that person. Most agents waste it.
Here's the fix. After your open house, spend five minutes typing quick notes about each visitor into Claude -- what they said, what caught their attention, how serious they seemed. Then type: "Write a personalized next-day follow-up for each visitor that references the specific conversation we had, and include a tailored listing recommendation based on what they told me they were looking for."
Not a template. A message that makes them think: this agent actually remembered me.
What's your current open house follow-up process?
