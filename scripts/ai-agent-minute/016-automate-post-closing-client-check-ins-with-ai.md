## Full Script (Spoken)

Eighty-eight percent of buyers say they'd use their agent again. Only thirteen percent actually do. That gap has a name -- and it's not disloyalty.
Here's what happens after most closings. You send a thank-you note, maybe a closing gift, and then you disappear. The client loved working with you -- but six months later, when their neighbor asks for a realtor recommendation, they can't quite remember your last name. Not because you did bad work. Because you vanished.
Most agents lose their best clients not to a competitor -- but to their own silence.
Here's the fix. Use Claude to build a twelve-month post-closing check-in sequence for every client. Type: "Write twelve monthly check-in messages for a past real estate client: month one is a personalized move-in note, month three is a home value update, month six is a seasonal maintenance checklist, month twelve is a one-year anniversary message with a market update. Write all twelve in a warm, personal tone that sounds like me."
Load them into your CRM. Set them to send automatically. Your clients feel remembered without you lifting a finger.
Your past clients are your most valuable asset. AI makes sure you never lose one to neglect again.
