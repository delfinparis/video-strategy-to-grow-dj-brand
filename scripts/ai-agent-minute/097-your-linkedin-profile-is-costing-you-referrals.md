## Full Script (Spoken)

While every agent fights for attention on Instagram, the platform most likely to send you corporate relocation clients, investor referrals, and high-income buyers is sitting completely ignored.
LinkedIn is the most underutilized platform in real estate -- and the gap between what most agents have there and what top producers have is enormous. Most agent profiles read like a bad resume: job title, brokerage name, years licensed. No story. No specific audience. No reason for a corporate professional, a relocation HR manager, or a high-net-worth buyer to reach out. If your LinkedIn profile were your only first impression, would it get a call?
Here's what that costs you -- the professionals in your area who get transferred, who inherit money, who get promoted and buy up -- they research agents on LinkedIn before they ever search Google.
Here's the fix. Ask Claude to rewrite your LinkedIn profile as a thought-leadership hub, not a resume. Your headline should describe what you help people do -- not just your title. Your about section should open with a story, not credentials. Your featured section should include your best market content, client testimonials, or media appearances. Give Claude the raw material -- your career story, your specialty, your best results -- and it writes every section. Then commit to one piece of content per week: a market insight, a professional tip, a client win. LinkedIn compounds slowly. But it compounds.."]
On LinkedIn, you're not competing with every agent in your market -- you're talking to professionals who refer other professionals. One good connection there is worth a hundred Instagram followers.
When's the last time you updated your LinkedIn profile? Drop the year -- I won't judge.
