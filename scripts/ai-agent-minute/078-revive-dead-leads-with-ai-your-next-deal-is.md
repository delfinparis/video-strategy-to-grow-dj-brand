You have hundreds of dead leads in your CRM right now -- and most of them aren't dead. You just ran out of things to say.
Here's what's actually happening. The average agent follows up three times, gets silence, and marks the lead cold. But research consistently shows most buyers need seven or more touchpoints before they act. You're quitting at three -- and handing those leads to the agent who doesn't.
And here's the part that stings -- you're not even running out of time. You're running out of things to say.
Here's the fix. Give Claude a lead's basic info -- area of interest, price range, when they first reached out -- and type: "Write a six-touch, six-month re-engagement sequence. Each message provides real value: a neighborhood update, a market insight, a useful tip. No message should ask if they're still looking."
Every touch should make them think: this agent actually pays attention.
Your next deal is probably already in your database. You just stopped showing up. Go find five dead leads tonight and run this on them.
