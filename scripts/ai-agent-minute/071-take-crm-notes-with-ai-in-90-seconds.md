## Full Script (Spoken)

Your CRM is only as good as the notes you actually put in it -- and most agents put in almost nothing.
Here's how it actually plays out. After every call or showing, you tell yourself you'll update the CRM later. Later never comes. Six months go by and your database is full of half-completed records with no context. When you finally reach back out to someone, you have no idea what you talked about, what they were looking for, or why they went cold.
You're starting from zero every time -- with a lead you already paid for.
Here's the fix -- and it takes ninety seconds. Right after any call or showing, open your voice-to-text app and talk for sixty seconds: what the person said, what they care about, where they are in the process. Then paste that voice memo into Claude and type: "Reformat this into clean CRM notes with a summary, next action, and follow-up date."
Paste the result into your CRM. Done.
Your database is your retirement account. Treat every note like a deposit.
How up-to-date are your CRM notes right now? Scale of one to ten --
