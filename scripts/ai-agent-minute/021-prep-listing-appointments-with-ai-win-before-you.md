## Full Script (Spoken)

The listing appointment is won before you ever walk in the door -- and the agent who knows that almost always gets the listing.
Here's the reality most agents don't think about. That seller has already Googled you, looked at your recent sales, and formed an opinion before you ring the doorbell. You're walking in already behind -- showing up with a generic CMA and the same presentation you use for every house.
Meanwhile the top listing agents in every market I've talked to in twelve years all have the same habit: they do homework the night before that most agents never do.
Here's what that looks like. The night before any listing appointment, give Claude the property address, recent neighborhood comps, and the seller's name. Then type: "Generate the five most likely objections I'll face at this specific listing appointment, a response to each one, and three value-differentiators specific to this property and neighborhood."
Walk in knowing what they'll push back on. With your answer already ready.
This prep takes fifteen minutes. It is the highest-ROI fifteen minutes in listing sales.
