## Full Script (Spoken)

The average real estate agent is paying for five tools they barely use -- and missing the one workflow that would actually change their business.
Here's what happened to most of us. Tech in real estate exploded. CRM, transaction management, social scheduling, email marketing, lead gen platforms, video tools -- you signed up for a few of them, used each one for two weeks, and forgot about them. Now you're spending hundreds of dollars a month on software that isn't moving the needle.
And the worst part? The overwhelm of having too many tools often stops agents from going deep on any of them.
Here's the fix. List every tool you're currently subscribed to and how often you actually use each one. Give that list to Claude and type: "For a solo real estate agent doing [X] deals per year, which of these tools overlap, which should I cut immediately, and what's the one missing capability that would have the highest ROI?"
Then actually cancel what it tells you to cut. And go all in on what stays.
One tool mastered is worth ten tools barely touched. Most agents have it exactly backwards.
