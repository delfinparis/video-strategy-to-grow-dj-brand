# The Chicago broker who just broke a record no North Shore agent ever had

> **WOW: A live, dated, named-source record, plus a second career angle.** Chicago Agent Magazine named Megan Mawicke Bradley (Jameson Sotheby's International Realty) its 2026 Agents' Choice "Industry MVP, Suburbs" on 2026-11-02, two weeks before this posts, and the same article states she is the first North Shore agent ever to hit $1 billion in career sales. She spent 22 years as a CBS 2 Chicago sports reporter first, holding her real estate license and brokering deals on the side the whole time, before going full-time in 2020. The teachable lesson: the "side job" years were not wasted time, they were reps. Pure amplification, no poaching tone, heat stays at 3 (tribute, not friction).

> **Strongest Line:** "The side job became the whole career, and then the record."

**Fact-check note on her broadcast tenure:** the June 2020 Chicago Agent Magazine article (the piece reporting her move, closest to the event) says 22 years at CBS 2 Chicago ending 2020.
