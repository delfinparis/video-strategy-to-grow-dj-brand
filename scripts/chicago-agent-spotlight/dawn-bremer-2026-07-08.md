# The agent who ran a real estate team before she ever took a listing

> **WOW: Person + lesson.** The scroll-stopper is the reveal that a top-selling broker/owner spent 12 years behind the scenes as a transaction coordinator first. The lesson (lead with service, guide people through the change) is what earns the reshare and lands with DJ's agent audience. Pure amplification + warm relationship; no poaching tone.

> **Strongest Line:** "A true top producer doesn't just solve problems. They guide people through the biggest change of their life."

## Full Script (Spoken) — target 50-55s

**HOOK (0:00-0:09)**
Most people think the best agents are the best salespeople. The agent I want you to meet spent twelve years as a transaction coordinator before she ever took a listing of her own.

**STORY (0:09-0:38)**
Her name is Dawn Bremer. She runs a woman-led team out of Keller Williams Success in Barrington, and Chicago Magazine has named them a top-selling team six years running. That is not why I am making this video. Dawn has chaired her office's Red Day for six years. She is a school board president. She runs a nonprofit for kids in need in McHenry County. She sells houses. She leads with service.

**PAYOFF (0:38-0:55)**
Here is the line of hers that stuck with me. A true top producer doesn't just solve problems, they guide people through the biggest change of their life. If you sell real estate, that is the whole job. Go find the agent in your market who leads with service, and study them. Dawn, you're one of the good ones.

*Spoken word count: ~132. Under the 135 cap and under 60 seconds. One story beat, service-first framing, no engagement ask.*
