## Deep Dive Brief (the research behind the script)

**Who she is.** Elizabeth "Lizzie G" Elie. Chicago-based residential real estate broker at Kale Realty, positive hip hop artist, and founder/CEO of LIZZIE G ENTERTAINMENT, LLC. Born and raised in Chicago to Haitian parents. Lifelong Morgan Park resident.

**The music.** Found hip hop at 11, mixing her own beats and rhyming over them. Released her first album at 16 while at Curie High School. Five albums to date, including MAKE IT HAPPEN (Feb 2015), LEVEL UP (Feb 2017), and EMBRACE THE GRIND (Sept 2019). Signature tracks: "No Bully Zone," "Everyday My Birthday," "Crank Dat Finess." Every song is clean. She turned down the easier radio path rather than add profanity: "I just think that if the radio is not going to support it, let me keep grinding. All of my songs have no profanity. I'm a leader to these youth."

**The education work.** Founded Lizzie G Entertainment in 2016. Arts, STEM, and music education programs, social-emotional learning workshops for K-12, anti-bullying programming, motivational speaking. Partners include Chicago Public Schools, Walgreens (Expressions Challenge), the University of Chicago's Logan Center for the Arts, Youth Guidance, and DCASE. Also runs a nonprofit, ELIE EMPOWERMENT INC. Presented a workshop at the 2026 National Mentoring Summit in Washington, D.C.

**The credentials.** Bachelor's from Northern Illinois University (2012). Master's in Integrated Marketing Communications from Roosevelt University (2015). 2022 Social Justice awardee, Roosevelt University. Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.

**The real estate side.** Kale Realty broker, DRE# 478.027107. Serves Chicago and surrounding Illinois: Humboldt Park, Hyde Park, South Shore, Auburn Gresham, Oak Lawn, Chicago Ridge, Calumet City, Hazel Crest. Association service: 2026 Chair of the CAR Young Professionals Network Advisory Board (Vice Chair previously), Member Care Task Force. She has also MCed Kale Realty's Q2 Summit.

**The 2026 news.** Named a 2026 Rising Star by the Chicago Association of REALTORS(R) (per D.J.; see the verification note in Data Source). As YPN Chair she set 2026 priorities: a monthly education breakfast series at Manny's Deli (money management, database development, mental health), relationship-building mixers, expanded volunteer and community service work, a better new-member experience, and a stronger advisory board. Her quote: "YPN's power has always come from its people, professionals willing to learn openly, share generously and support one another's success." In June 2026 she served as master of ceremonies for the Obama Presidential Center's opening weekend on John Lewis Plaza.
