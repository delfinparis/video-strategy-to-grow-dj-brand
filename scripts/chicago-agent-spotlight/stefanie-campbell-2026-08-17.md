# She priced a house past her town's record, then wouldn't fill the silence with a guess

> **WOW: Person + lesson.** The scroll-stopper is the dollar figure: a brand-new Frankfort estate just listed a million-plus over the town's previous sale record. The lesson (price on story, not just comps; protect the client instead of guessing to fill a silence) is what earns the reshare and lands with D.J.'s agent audience. Pure amplification, established top producer, no poaching tone.

> **Strongest Line:** "She didn't guess. She just said she couldn't say."

## Full Script (Spoken) -- target 50-55s

**HOOK (0:00-0:08)**
Four point two million dollars. That's the price on a brand new house in Frankfort, Illinois, and it just blew past the town's old sale record by more than a million bucks.

**STORY (0:08-0:38)**
The agent who listed it is Stefanie Campbell. She co-leads The Gold Group at Properties Christie's International with her sister-in-law, Kaylin Goldstein, and Stefanie's been a top one percent producer in this market for years running. Here's the part I love. The house is brand new. Reclaimed brick from a torn-down church. Copper gas lanterns shipped in from Louisiana. So when a reporter asked why the sellers are already letting it go, Stefanie didn't invent an answer to fill the silence. She just said she couldn't say.

**PAYOFF (0:38-0:55)**
That's the whole skill in one move. You can price a house past the comps if the story earns it, and you can protect your client without making something up. Go follow Stefanie Campbell and The Gold Group. Nice work.

*Spoken word count: ~130. Under the 135 cap and under 60 seconds. One story beat, restraint-under-pressure framing, no engagement ask.*
