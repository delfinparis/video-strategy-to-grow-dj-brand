# Who your cap is for

> **WOW: the cap is the only line in a split that is sold as protection and priced as a target. BP-008 proved the gap with arithmetic. This one does not argue the gap at all, it asks who the number was written for, and the answer is an agent the viewer has been asked to become for six years.**

> **Strongest Line:** "That cap is for the agent they want you to become."

Thirty seconds, so here is the look, and there is no number in it from me because none of mine would be yours.

What a cap is, plainly. It is the point in a year where you stop paying your brokerage a split and keep everything after it. That is why it is the headline of every pitch: it is the one line that sounds like protection.

The check, and it takes two minutes. Write down your gross commission for last year. Write down your cap. Those two numbers next to each other are the whole video. Ask what a cap is at three other firms at your production level, and ask what happens after it, because those are two different questions and they get blended constantly in a recruiting conversation. One firm's low cap with fees after it can cost more than a higher cap with nothing after it.
