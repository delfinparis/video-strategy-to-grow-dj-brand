# Nobody breaks out the bill

> **WOW: the brand premium is the only large recurring expense an agent pays where no itemized version has ever existed. Not hidden, not disputed. Never produced. A bundle priced as one number cannot have an empty item found in it, which is the whole reason it is sold as one number.**

> **Strongest Line:** "You know what the other two did."

## Teleprompter

Nobody shows you the bill line by line.

It's sold as one price. Brand, support, coaching, tools, leads.

Take your last ninety days. How many clients did the brand bring? The coaching, a recorded Zoom? The tools, do you open them? That's three of five. You know what the other two did.

Find out what other firms include. Ask for it line by line. Then look at your own again.

### Pinned first comment (all platforms)

Thirty seconds, so here is the long version, and there is no number in it from me because none of mine would be yours.

What the bundle is. When a brokerage quotes a split, a cap or a desk fee, the number is quoted once and the benefits are described as a package: the brand, support, coaching, tools, leads. Five things, one price. That is not a trick, it is just how the business prices itself. The consequence is the part worth noticing. A package priced as one number has no line items, and a thing with no line items cannot be found empty.

The check, and it takes one evening. Write the five down the left side of a page. Next to each one, write what it did for you in the last ninety days, in your own handwriting, with specifics. Not what it could do. What it did. Three of the five will be easy to answer and two of them will take longer, and the two that take longer are the interesting ones.

Then the look. Ask at three other firms at your production level what their package includes, and ask for it broken out item by item rather than as a pitch. Ask what the coaching actually is, who runs it, and what time it happens. Ask whether the tools are included or billed. Ask what support means on a Saturday. You are not asking for a number, you are asking for a list, and a list is much harder to dress up.

Why this is worth an evening. Every agent I talk to can tell me their split inside two seconds and almost none of them can tell me what the split buys, itemized, without guessing. That gap is not a failure of the agent. Nobody ever produced the list.

I am not telling you what to do with it. I am saying the list exists somewhere, it is yours to ask for, and it has never once been handed to you.
