# Available to who

> **WOW: training is the one item in the bundle that can be fully provided and fully useless at the same time, and the schedule is where that happens. A ten in the morning class is not withheld from a working agent. It is offered to her at the hour she cannot take it, and offered counts.**

> **Strongest Line:** "Available to who?"

## Teleprompter

Nobody tells you the training's at ten.

Tuesday, ten in the morning. You're with clients. Or on the phone.

They list that class as available. It's in what you pay. Count this year. How many could you have actually sat in? Not the replays. The ones you could be in. Available to who?

Find out when other firms run theirs. Ask what time. Then look at your Tuesday again.

Thirty seconds, so here is the longer version, and I am not going to answer the question at the end of it because I do not think there is a good answer.

What the video is actually about. Training is one of the things a split or a fee is supposed to buy, and it is the only one that can be fully delivered and still do nothing for you. A class exists. It is on the calendar. It is included. And it runs at the hour a working agent is with a client, on the phone, or at an inspection. Nobody withheld anything. It was offered to you at a time you cannot take it, and offered counts on a feature list.

The count, and it takes ten minutes. Open your calendar for this year and the training schedule next to it. Mark the sessions you could physically have been in, live, with your camera on, able to ask a question. Not the ones you could watch later. Watching a replay is content, and sitting in a room where you can ask a question is coaching, and they are not the same product even when they carry the same name.

Then the look. Ask at three other firms what time they run theirs and who runs it. Not whether they have training. What time. Evenings, weekends, early mornings, on demand with live office hours attached, or ten on a Tuesday. That one question tells you who the schedule was built around, and it is a faster read on a brokerage than any benefit list you will ever be handed.

Why the time matters more than the content. Every firm in this business can produce a training calendar. Very few can produce one that a producing agent could actually attend, and the ones that can had to decide to, which costs somebody something. A schedule is a decision with a budget behind it.

I am not telling you what your answer means. I am saying you have probably never once counted it.
