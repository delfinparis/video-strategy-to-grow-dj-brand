# The Take — scripts

Contrarian walk-and-talks, 3 per week (Mon/Wed/Fri), ungated on every platform.
The shape is *realtors shouldn't do X, do Y instead.*

- **Standard:** [`../../docs/series/take-standard.md`](../../docs/series/take-standard.md)
- **Candidate bank + rotation log:** [`../../data/sacred-cows.md`](../../data/sacred-cows.md)
- **Pillar context:** [`../../docs/content-pillars.md`](../../docs/content-pillars.md) (Pillar 5)

## Naming

`TAKE-###-slug.md`, numbered from `TAKE-001`. The existing
`../carousels/TAKE-hashtag-stacking-carousel.md` predates this series and is a carousel,
not a video, so it does not consume a number.

## The five beats

```
1. THE COW      - the wrong default, first spoken line, no warm-up
2. WHO PROFITS  - who makes money from this belief surviving (the turn)
3. THE RECEIPT  - one real number, named source, year
4. THE SWAP     - do Y instead. Physical, tomorrow, at their own business
5. LOOP-BACK    - the last line reframes the hook (Rule 9.1)
```

## The three things that get a draft sent back

1. **No swap.** An indictment with nothing to do instead is a rant.
2. **No receipt.** A take without a number is a mood, and the comments will find that out.
3. **A named party.** Naming a brokerage, coach, product, or agent is heat 5 and banned
   outright in this lane, not rationed.

| Day | Heat | Hook family |
