# Stop letting AI write your listing description

> **WOW: Contrarian take (2) carried by an earned inside-the-industry position (8). D.J. builds the AI tools his own agents use and is telling them where to stop, which is the one voice on this topic an agent cannot dismiss as anti-AI.**

> **Strongest Line:** "The tool didn't cost you the showing. The paragraph that sounded like everyone else's did."

## Spoken Script (22-30 seconds, 70 words)

**THE COW**
> I build AI tools for our agents. Stop letting one write your listing description.

**WHO PROFITS**
> The vendor selling that one-click button doesn't lose the showing. You do.

**THE RECEIPT**
> Sixty-eight percent of buyers want to be told when AI wrote it. That's Cotality, this year.

**THE SWAP**
> Run the draft, then rewrite the first two sentences yourself before you post.

**LOOP-BACK**
> The tool didn't cost you the showing. The paragraph that sounded like everyone else's did.

## Production Note

- **Hook family 3 (Defector), not 2.** The slot table puts Sacred Cow or System Indictment on Wednesday, but `data/sacred-cows.md` flags this entry specifically as Defector material: D.J. teaches AI and builds the tools, so credential-versus-claim is the shape that makes the hook undeniable rather than another anti-AI opinion. Documented deviation, per the bank's own note on this entry.
- **Pattern interrupt: Location Cold-Open.** Planted in front of a real listing or a sign before the first word, so the frame does not look like an AI-opinion video.
- **No re-hook.** At 70 words there is no invested lull to rescue. The turn at "You do" is doing that job.
- **Heat 4 aimed at the vendor incentive**, never at the agent using the tool. The agent is the one who eats the cost, which is the whole reason the swap is generous rather than scolding.
- **Register check:** this is a limit on a tool D.J. sells the benefits of everywhere else. Do not deliver it as a warning. Deliver it as the thing he would say to one agent over coffee.

## Data Source

| Claim | Source | Who was measured | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 68% of buyers want clear notification when AI is involved in outputs | Cotality, *AI in Housing 2026*, survey fielded January 29 to February 9, 2026; reported via HousingWire, 2026 | Recent buyers (purchased within 5 years) and prospective buyers (2 to 5 years out), US, Canada, UK, Australia | **Verified 2026-08-18** against Cotality's own writeup |

**Re-verification note, and a correction to the brief.** The Aug 17 brief's second half of the receipt, *"more than a third say mandatory,"* does not survive the primary source. Cotality does not publish a single "over a third" figure; it reports the mandatory-labeling question by generation, at 61 percent of Baby Boomers and 25 percent of Gen Z saying labeling should be legally mandated. There is no supportable "one in three buyers" claim, so the brief's proposed hook line was rebuilt on the 68 percent figure, which is clean, first-party, and stronger anyway.

**Also verified but deliberately unused:** US prospective buyers' trust in AI for home search fell from 30 percent in 2025 to 16 percent in 2026, a 14-point drop. Real, and it is a second number. Beat 3 takes one.

**Who-profits support:** category-level and uncontroversial. One-click description generators are a standard feature across listing-marketing tools. No product is named, per the lane's heat-5 floor.

## Council Review

**Convened:** Kane, Hormozi, Chris Do, Byron Lazine, MrBeast. **Witnesses:** Heath, Berger.

### Spoken scroll-stop variants

- **A. Default, Family 3 Defector.** "I build AI tools for our agents. Stop letting one write your listing description."
- **B. Family 2, Sacred Cow.** "The AI listing description is the laziest paragraph in your marketing, and it's the one buyers actually read."
- **C. Family 6, diagnosed loss.** "You know why they scrolled past your listing? A robot wrote the only part they read."

### The room

**Kane.** The Defector is the hook point and the ingredient is Personality, not Text. Anyone can say AI writing is generic. Almost nobody saying it also ships the tools. That's the thing that can't be copied off you, so lead with it. My note is that variant A buries the cost, and B has the cost in it. Test them.

**Hormozi.** Here's the thing. The reward is thin. "Rewrite the first two sentences" tells them the where, not the what. Give away the actual move or you're training people to scroll the next one.

**Chris Do.** Disagreed, and this is the rare one where withholding is the honest choice. He's telling agents to put their own voice back in. Handing them a formula for the voice would contradict the video inside the video.

**Byron.** The take is correct and it's safe, which worries me slightly. Every agent already suspects this. What makes it land is that D.J. is the AI guy. Keep the credential welded to the imperative and it survives the comments.

**Heath.** The Defector breaks a guessing machine: the viewer's model says the AI builder promotes AI, and the first six words violate it. Gap opens on "why would he say that," closes on the 68 percent. Aha after Huh, and it fits in 70 words because the credential and the verdict share one breath.

**Berger.** This travels on Social Currency. Passing it on makes an agent look like they know where the line is, and the brand survives the retell because the surviving detail is *the guy who builds the tools says stop here*, which is inseparable from D.J.

**MrBeast.** 70 words, no lull, nothing to re-hook. the turn at "You do" is the only place attention could sag and it doesn't. close is fine, it's abrupt.

### Why it works

**Hook (Heath):** credential-versus-claim violates the viewer's model of who says this, opening the gap in six words. **Share driver (Berger):** Social Currency, and the brand is load-bearing in the retell. **Retention (MrBeast):** no re-hook needed at this length; the who-profits turn carries the middle.

### The dissent to A/B

**Hormozi holds his objection on the thin reward.** Run the current swap against a version that names what goes in sentence one (the specific thing about the house that a generator cannot know), judged on saves. If the specific version wins on saves without denting the voice argument, the payload moves and this becomes the template for the lane.
