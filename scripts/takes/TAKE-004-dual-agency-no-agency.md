# Dual agency is the most expensive favor in real estate

> **WOW: A sacred practice with a price tag on it. Every agent has been taught that disclosed dual agency is clean because the form was signed. Zillow put the seller's cost at $2,165 a home, and there is a statutory third option almost nobody uses: keep the buyer as a customer, not a client, and never take the second side at all.**

> **Strongest Line:** "Coach their offer and you're dual anyway."

## Production Note

- **Pattern interrupt: The Stop.** Walk fast for one beat, plant on "expensive." The cheat sheet pairs motion-to-freeze with Sacred Cow, and TAKE-003 used Gesture-On-Beat, so the move rotates.
- **Family 2, not Family 9.** The swap shape is in beat 4, but the hook attacks the practice rather than opening with "stop doing X." TAKE-003 ran Family 9, and this is the Wednesday slot, which the standard assigns to Family 2 or 4.
- **The villain is the structure, not the agent.** Every agent watching has done this, most of them believing the signed form settled it. Beat 2 points at the brokerage economics and the form, never at the viewer. Heat 4, no identifiable party.
- **"Customer" is the whole payload, and it is a narrow doorway.** This is the one beat that will get argued in the comments, and D.J. has to be able to hold it. In Illinois the customer relationship covers ministerial acts only: showing the specific property the buyer asked to see, giving factual information off the data sheet. The moment you opine on value, discuss other listings, or help shape the offer, the buyer is a client by operation of law and you are a dual agent whether or not anyone signed anything. That is exactly why beat 4 ends on the warning rather than the permission.
- **Do not say "no agency is a business model."** Illinois REALTORS' own compliance manual says the opposite in as many words. The correct claim, and the one the script makes, is that the buyer who calls off the sign starts as a customer and stays one only if the agent keeps the relationship ministerial.
- **Jurisdiction disclaimer if D.J. gets asked in the comments:** non-agency rules are state law and they vary a lot. Florida's statutory default is transaction brokerage, Colorado has a transaction-broker status, and Illinois has the narrow customer category described above. The safe reply is "the form is called something different in your state, ask your managing broker what yours is called," not a fifty-state claim.
- **Rant test:** strip beat 4 and what remains is "double-ending costs your seller money and your brokerage likes it." Still postable, noticeably thinner. The swap is carrying real weight, which is what the lane wants.
- **Recruiting test:** a good agent watching this sees a brokerage president telling them to protect the client and handing them the mechanism. It reads as fiduciary spine, not poaching.

## Social Media

### LinkedIn

Dual agency is the most expensive favor in real estate.

Zillow's 2026 analysis put a number on it. Across more than 15 million transactions from 2023 to 2025, sellers whose buyer was represented by the same agent netted about $2,165 less than sellers whose buyer had separate representation. The penalty showed up in all three years.

The disclosure form is what keeps the practice alive. It makes the second commission legal. It does not make the $2,165 go away, and your seller is the one paying it.

Most agents think the choice is double-end it or lose the deal. There is a third door and almost nobody walks through it.

A buyer who calls off your sign is a customer, not a client. In Illinois you can show that specific property, give factual information off the data sheet, hand them the Notice of No Agency Relationship, and send them to their own agent or attorney for anything that looks like advice. Your seller keeps your undivided loyalty. You keep one commission and a clean file.

The discipline is the hard part. The second you offer an opinion of value, bring up other listings, or help shape their offer, that buyer is your client by operation of law and you are a dual agent whether anyone signed anything or not.

Non-agency rules are state law and they vary. Ask your managing broker what the form is called where you practice.

The real favor is staying on one side of the table.

## Council Review

### Spoken scroll-stop variants

- **A. Default, Family 2 Sacred Cow.** "Dual agency is the most expensive favor in real estate."
- **B. Family 4, System Indictment.** "The dual agency form protects your brokerage, not your seller."
- **C. Family 6, Named Stakes.** "Two thousand one hundred sixty-five dollars. That is what your seller pays for your second commission."

### The room

**Kane.** A is a verdict on a practice, which is the right shape, but "favor" is doing quiet work that a scrolling viewer may not unpack in 1.5 seconds. B is the sharper device because it names two parties and puts them on opposite sides in nine words. That is a real test.

**Youshaei.** The first frame is the problem, not the line. Dual agency looks like a compliance video in the feed and compliance videos die. The Stop has to be violent enough to read on mute, and the plant needs to land on "expensive," not on "dual."

**Byron Lazine.** The story is four months old and that is fine, because nobody in this industry actually changed behavior when it broke. The news value here is not the study. It is that there is a statutory third option and the trade press never covered it. Lead the comments with that, not with the stat.

**Hormozi.** The reward density is the highest in the lane so far. The viewer walks away with a named form they can ask their broker for tomorrow, and the downside they avoid is a license complaint. That is a real transaction, not a content transaction.

**Miller.** One problem, one plan, and the hero is the agent who wants to do this right. My only flag is beat 4 carrying both the permission and the prohibition. That is two ideas in one breath and the prohibition is the one that will get misheard. It survives because the prohibition is last and it is seven words.

**Eric Simon.** The recognition beat is real. Every listing agent has had the sign call and felt the pull of the second check. What makes it sendable is that it is not a scold. It is a way to keep the deal without the exposure.

**Heath.** Concrete in the strict sense: "the no-agency notice" is an object with a name, not a concept. The Curse of Knowledge risk sits in "customer, not a client," which is a term of art that agents have heard and mostly cannot define. The script survives because beat 4 defines it by behavior rather than by definition, which is the correct move.

**Berger.** High-arousal and the arousal is anger, which is the most shared of the set. Social Currency is unusually strong here because the viewer learns something their own managing broker probably never told them. The one-sentence retell test: "you don't have to go dual, you can keep them as a customer" survives intact. That is the valuable-virality condition met.

### Why it works

**Hook (Heath):** a sacred practice gets a verdict and a price in one line, which opens the loop with a number the viewer wants. **Share driver (Berger):** anger plus Social Currency, and the retell survives compression. **Retention (MrBeast):** the receipt lands by second eight, and the swap is a doorway the viewer did not know existed, which is the strongest possible reason to stay for the back half.

### The dissent to A/B

**Kane holds that B outperforms A.** Test "Dual agency is the most expensive favor in real estate" against "The dual agency form protects your brokerage, not your seller" on equal cold spend, judged on 3-second retention and sends. B names the beneficiary in the hook instead of holding it for beat 2, which is either a faster indictment or a spent beat. If B wins, the who-profits beat moves into the hook as a standing option for this lane.

**Miller's separate note, not a test:** if the comments argue about the no-agency mechanics rather than about dual agency, that is not a failure. Pin the Illinois form name and the manual page in the first comment and let the thread run.
