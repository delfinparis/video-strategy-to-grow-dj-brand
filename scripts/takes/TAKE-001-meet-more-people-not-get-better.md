# Your database is a three-deal-a-year business

> **WOW: Criterion 4 (surprising statistic) into criterion 2 (contrarian take). The 2.8% turnover number is five times smaller than the 10-15% every coach quotes, and once an agent hears the real one, the whole "get better" project stops making arithmetic sense.**

> **Strongest Line:** "Nobody's making a living teaching you to shake more hands."

## Spoken Script (~55 seconds)

**THE COW**

Your database is a three-deal-a-year business. No course you buy is going to change that.

**WHO PROFITS**

Nobody tells you that, because your skill gap is somebody's recurring revenue. Courses, coaching, another designation. You can sell that every year. Nobody's making a living teaching you to shake more hands.

**THE RECEIPT**

Last year, 28 out of every thousand American homes changed hands. That's Redfin. Lowest turnover in thirty years. Run that against a two-hundred-person database and you get about six transactions. Close half, that's three deals.

**THE SWAP**

So go fix the number. Count your sphere tonight. Whatever it is, that's how many people you meet this year. Two hundred names is four a week. Four. A coffee, an open house, your kid's game, one conversation you'd normally skip.

**LOOP-BACK**

You're not three deals away from being a better agent. You're doing three deals because you know two hundred people. Go meet four more this week.

## Data Source

**The receipt (spoken).**
Redfin, "Only 28 Out of Every 1,000 U.S. Homes Changed Hands This Year, The Lowest Turnover in Decades," published **October 31, 2025**. https://www.redfin.com/news/home-turnover-report-2025/

- **Who was measured:** U.S. homes that changed hands during calendar 2025, as a share of all U.S. homes.
- **The figure:** 27.7 per 1,000, which is 2.77%, rounded in the report headline to 28 per 1,000 and 2.8%.
- **Context in the report:** 27.8 per 1,000 in 2024. 44 per 1,000 in 2021. 40 per 1,000 in 2019. Described as the lowest turnover rate since at least the early-to-mid 1990s.
- **Status:** verified against the Redfin report 2026-08-14. **Re-verify before air.** Redfin publishes this annually and a 2026 edition may exist by post date. If the number has moved, the arithmetic in the script moves with it.

**The arithmetic, and its honest limit.**
"About six transactions" is the national turnover rate applied to a 200-person database (0.0277 x 200 = 5.5). That is an estimate against a national rate, **not a measured sphere conversion rate.** A personal sphere can run above or below national depending on who is in it. The script says "run that against" precisely so the arithmetic is shown rather than asserted. This is the one line a sharp commenter will push on, which is why the swap tells them to count their own list instead of trusting the average.

D.J.'s original framing used 15%, which would put a 200-person sphere at 30 transactions a year. That is roughly five times the real national rate and is not sourceable. It was cut.

**Supporting figure (not spoken, for the caption and the pinned comment).**
NAR, *2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers*: **66% of sellers found their agent through a referral or used an agent they had worked with before.** This is why adding people works even at 2.8% turnover. A new name is not only a possible transaction, it is a possible referral. Source: NAR, 2025. https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/research-reports/highlights-from-the-profile-of-home-buyers-and-sellers

**The who-profits claim.**
Category-level observation, not a statistic. The script names courses, coaching, and designations as a class of recurring revenue and does not quantify the industry, name a company, or name a program. No number is claimed for beat 2, so none needs sourcing.

**Do-not-cite check:** clean. No behavioral-science finding is cited.

- **Share/save driver (Berger/Hormozi):** the receipt is screenshot bait and the reframe is social currency. An agent forwarding "the real number is 2.8, not 15" looks in the know. It survives the retell because the arithmetic is the point, not the messenger. Practical value is the save driver: count, divide, four a week.
- **Retention move (MrBeast):** the re-hook lands at 0:15 where a pure indictment would sag. "Twenty-eight out of every thousand" is a hard number arriving exactly when the viewer expects more opinion. The close is abrupt on "go meet four more this week," no wind-down.
