# An Hour to Write One Email

> **WOW: Contrarian take + tactical fix -- everyone says AI saves time, but used wrong it is slower than doing the work, because you solve the same blank-box puzzle every time. The fix: never start from blank. tapthis.co has 620 prompts already written, including the Sunday-night email.**

**Filming:** Walk and talk, relaxed, evening or golden hour if possible. No screen recording.
**Destination:** tapthis.co

## Shareable Moment
> "That's not a time-saver. That's a new hobby."

## Script (~3:30, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:20)
It's Sunday night. You open ChatGPT to write one follow-up email. One. Forty-five minutes later you've rewritten the prompt six times, argued with the robot, and you've got something you're "okay with." For one email. That's not a time-saver. That's a new hobby.

*Production: Walking, slightly exasperated, like you've been there. Quick cut. Text overlay: '45 minutes. One email.'*

### PROBLEM (0:20-0:55)
Nobody says this part out loud. Used wrong, AI is slower than just doing the work yourself. Because every time you start from a blank box, you're solving the same puzzle again. How do I word this. What do I tell it. Why does this sound off. You're not running a business. You're prompt engineering at 10pm. And it's exhausting enough that most agents quit and go back to copy-pasting their own old emails. The tool that was supposed to give you your nights back is eating them.

*Production: Slow down, look at camera. Text overlay: 'Used wrong, AI is slower.'*

### SOLUTION (0:55-2:00)
So here's the whole idea behind tapthis.co. You should never start from a blank box again. 620 prompts, already written, already tested. Sunday-night email? It's in there. Whole lead-gen plan for the quarter? Tap "Plan My Strategy." Audit your database, build a thirty-day content calendar, map out a farm? In there. You don't write the prompt. You find the one you need, you fill in your brackets, your market, your client, your numbers, and you paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. The forty-five minutes of fighting? Somebody already did it. It's done. It's free.

*Production: Walking again, energy up. Text overlays: 'Never start from blank.' / 'Find it. Fill it. Paste it.'*

### RESULTS (2:00-3:00)
So that Sunday email goes from forty-five minutes to about thirty seconds. And it's better, because the prompt was built by pulling apart what actually works, not whatever you could think of at 10pm. Now run that across your whole week. The emails, the descriptions, the follow-ups, the plan you keep meaning to write. That's not thirty seconds saved. That's your Sunday night back. AI was always supposed to do this. Most agents just never got handed the instructions.

*Production: Stop walking, look at camera (visual beat first). Text overlay: '45 minutes -> 30 seconds.' Visual climax.*

### CLOSE (3:00-3:15)
The robot was never the slow part. You, feeding it from scratch every single time, that's the slow part. The prompts are already written and waiting at tapthis.co. Go get your Sunday night back.

*Production: Turn to camera. Clean exit, no hard sell, just the wink and the URL.*
