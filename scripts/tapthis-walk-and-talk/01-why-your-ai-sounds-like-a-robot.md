# Why Your AI Sounds Like a Robot

> **WOW: Mindset reframe + tactical specificity -- agents blame the AI for robotic listings when the real problem is a nine-word prompt. The reframe shifts the blame off the tool and onto the input, then hands over the exact free fix (tapthis.co, the right category, fill the brackets, paste).**

## Script (~3:30, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:20)
You typed "write me a listing description" into ChatGPT, and it gave you something with the words "nestled," "boasts," and "must-see" in it. And you stared at it and thought, I can't send this. It sounds like a brochure from 1998. So you closed the tab and wrote it yourself. Again.

*Production: Already mid-stride, camera catches up. Quick cut after the "nestled, boasts, must-see" line for comedic timing. Text overlay: 'nestled. boasts. must-see.'*

### PROBLEM (0:20-1:00)
Here's the thing nobody tells agents about AI. It's not that the AI is bad. It's that the prompt is bad. "Write me a listing description" is a lazy instruction. So you get a lazy answer. Garbage in, garbage out. The AI doesn't know your market, your buyer, your voice, or what makes that specific kitchen worth driving across town for. You didn't tell it. You gave it nine words and hoped. And most agents do this, decide AI "doesn't really work for real estate," and walk away from the most useful tool they'll ever have. Over one bad listing description.

*Production: Slow down, look at camera. Text overlay: 'It's not the AI. It's the prompt.' This is the thesis, let it land.*

### SOLUTION (1:00-2:10)
So here's what I did about it. I have never sold a house in my life. But I've interviewed more than 700 agents who have, on my podcast. And I took what actually works, and I built a free site called tapthis.co. It's 620 prompts. Already written. Engineered for real estate. You don't write the prompt. You copy it. There are four doors in. Write Something For Me. Help Me Follow Up. Coach Me Through It. Plan My Strategy. You pick the thing you're stuck on, you grab the prompt, you paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. That's the whole move.

*Production: Walking again, energy up. Text overlays: 'tapthis.co' / 'Copy. Paste. Done.'*

### RESULTS (2:10-3:00)
So that same listing. Instead of nine words, the prompt asks you for the neighborhood, the buyer you're targeting, the one feature you'd brag about at a dinner party. You fill in the blanks. You paste it. And now the AI writes a description that sounds like a person who's actually been in the house. Not "nestled." The actual coffee shop on the corner. The light in the afternoon. The reason somebody would fall in love with it. Same ChatGPT you already gave up on. The only thing that changed was the words you fed it.

*Production: Stop walking, look at camera (visual beat before this section). Text overlay: 'Same AI. Better answer.'*

### CLOSE (3:00-3:15)
AI isn't going to take your job. But an agent who knows how to talk to it might. So go learn the words. They're free, they're at tapthis.co, and they're already written. You just have to copy that.

*Production: Genuine smile, turn to camera. Slow the cuts so "copy that" reads as the wink it is.*
