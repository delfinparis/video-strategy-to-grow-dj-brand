# The Leads You're Scared to Text

> **WOW: Reframe + permission slip -- the lead in your phone isn't dead, your "just checking in" text is. It names the private guilt every agent feels about the database they avoid, then hands over the free fix: tapthis.co Follow Up category, reactivation prompts.**

**Filming:** Walk and talk, slower pace, a touch confessional. No screen recording.
**Destination:** tapthis.co

## Shareable Moment
> "The lead didn't go cold. The message did."

## Script (~3:30, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:20)
You've got a name in your phone right now. A lead from eight months ago. They were going to buy, then they went quiet. And every few weeks you see their name, think "I should reach out," and then you don't. Because you don't know what to say that isn't "just checking in." So you say nothing. And the lead gets colder.

*Production: Walking, like you're confessing something. Quick cut. Text overlay: 'just checking in'*

### PROBLEM (0:20-0:55)
Dead leads are almost never dead. They're just waiting for a reason to answer that isn't a guilt text. "Just checking in" tells them nothing. It puts the work on them. So they leave you on read, and you take that as proof they weren't serious. They were serious. Your follow-up just gave them nothing to grab onto. The lead didn't go cold. The message did.

*Production: Slow down, look at camera. Text overlay: 'They're not dead. They're waiting.' The turn, make it the boldest moment.*

### SOLUTION (0:55-2:00)
So here's the move. Try this instead. tapthis.co. Tap "Help Me Follow Up." There's a whole section in there for cold and dormant leads. Reactivation texts. Re-openers. The break-up message that, weirdly, gets the most replies. You grab one, you paste it into your AI, and you fill in what you remember. They were looking in Oak Park. They had a dog. Their lease was up in spring. The prompt turns those scraps into a text that sounds like you actually remember them, because you do.

*Production: Walking again, energy up. Text overlays: 'tapthis.co -> Follow Up' / 'A reason to reply.'*

### RESULTS (2:00-3:00)
And what you send isn't "just checking in." It's "saw a place two blocks from that park you liked, thought of you and the dog." That's a text a human answers. Now do that for ten names tonight. Not four hours of agonizing over wording. Ten texts in the time it used to take you to talk yourself out of one. Some won't answer. That's fine. You only need a couple of the leads you'd already written off to wake up.

*Production: Stop walking, look at camera (visual beat first). Text overlay: 'Not a guilt text. A real one.'*

### CLOSE (3:00-3:15)
Your leads aren't ghosting you. Your follow-up is ghosting them. The fix is sitting at tapthis.co, and that name in your phone has waited long enough.

*Production: Turn to camera. This is the gut-punch, slow the cuts and let it breathe.*
