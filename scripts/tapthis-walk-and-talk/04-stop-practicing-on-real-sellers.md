# Stop Practicing on Real Sellers

> **WOW: Mindset reframe + tactical specificity -- agents rehearse their highest-stakes conversations live on real sellers, the most expensive rehearsal space there is. The free fix: tapthis.co Coach category, where the AI plays the skeptical seller and you run the objection ten times before the appointment.**

**Filming:** Walk and talk, brisker pace, pep-talk energy. No screen recording.
**Destination:** tapthis.co

## Shareable Moment
> "You wouldn't wing the listing presentation. So quit winging the objections."

## Script (~3:30, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:20)
You're sitting at a seller's kitchen table. Everything's going great. Then they say it. "Why is your commission so high?" And you freeze. You say something about "the value I provide." It comes out mushy. You watch them not buy it. You knew that question was coming. You always know. And it still got you.

*Production: Walking, a little wound up, like you just left a meeting. Quick cut. Text overlay: '...the value I provide...' Hold on D.J.'s face on the mushy line.*

### PROBLEM (0:20-0:55)
We do something a little insane in this business. We practice our hardest conversations live. On real sellers. With real commission checks on the line. That's the most expensive rehearsal space on earth. You fumble the commission objection on a Tuesday, and it costs you a fifteen-thousand-dollar listing. Then you "get better" by losing the next one too. Nobody would prep for a championship by only playing championship games. But that's how we train for the conversations that pay us.

*Production: Slow down, look at camera. Text overlay: 'You practice on real sellers.' Thesis, biggest text moment.*

### SOLUTION (0:55-2:00)
So here's the fix, and it costs you nothing. tapthis.co. Tap "Coach Me Through It." There are roleplay prompts in there. You paste one into ChatGPT or Claude, and the AI becomes the seller. The skeptical one. The one who heard about discount brokers. The one whose neighbor "sold it themselves." And it pushes back on you. Out loud. In your car, in your kitchen, before the appointment. You answer, it counters, you answer better. You can run the commission objection ten times before lunch. For free. Nobody's listing on the line.

*Production: Walking again, energy lifts hard, this is the empowering turn. Text overlays: 'tapthis.co -> Coach' / 'The AI plays the seller.'*

### RESULTS (2:00-3:00)
So now you walk into that kitchen and they say "why is your commission so high," and it doesn't land as a surprise. You've heard it ten times this week. From a machine that wouldn't let you off easy. You don't say "the value I provide." You say the real thing, the clean thing, the thing you found on rep number seven. Calm. Because it's not your first time. It's your eleventh. That's the difference between an agent who hopes the hard question doesn't come up, and one who's almost daring it to.

*Production: Stop walking, look at camera (visual beat first). Text overlay: 'Heard it 10 times before they said it.'*

### CLOSE (3:00-3:15)
You wouldn't wing the listing presentation. So quit winging the objections. Run the reps at tapthis.co, where the only thing on the line is your pride, and save the real seller for the real close.

*Production: Turn to camera, half smile. Confident and dry, no hard sell.*
