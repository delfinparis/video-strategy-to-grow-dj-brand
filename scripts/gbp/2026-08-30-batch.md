**Sub:**
Kale Realty doesn't tie your business to a captive program a corporate parent can retire.

**Caption:**
Days after closing its $880 million acquisition of RE/MAX, Real Group announced it will stop selling new Motto Mortgage franchises and is folding mortgage services into its own One Real Mortgage platform, per RISMedia (August 28, 2026). ## CARD chicago-sales-up-inventory-down

**Headline:**
Chicago home sales rose 3.9% in June while inventory fell 14.4%.

**Sub:**
The market is tightening from both sides at once, not just on price.

**Caption:**
Illinois REALTORS' June 2026 report found 9,927 single-family and condo sales closed in the nine-county Chicago Metro Area, up 3.9 percent from June 2025. For-sale inventory fell 14.4 percent over the same period. More homes are selling out of a shrinking pool, which is a different pressure than a rising median price alone. Sellers weighing when to list, and buyers weighing when to move, are both working against a tighter window than last year. Learn more at joinkale.com.
