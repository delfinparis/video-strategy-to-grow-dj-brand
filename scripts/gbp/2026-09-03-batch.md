## CARD affordability-top-buyer-blocker

**Headline:**
Affordability is blocking more buyers than anything else, by a mile.

**Sub:**
27 percent of buyers name it the top constraint. Inventory is a distant second at 12 percent.

**Caption:**
NAR's 2026 Member Profile found that 27 percent of REALTORS point to affordability as the single biggest thing keeping buyers out of the market, more than double the next factor, lack of inventory, at 12 percent. NAR Deputy Chief Economist Jessica Lautz put it plainly: today's market is split between repeat buyers with equity who move easily and first-time buyers struggling to save a down payment. If you're pricing a listing or coaching a buyer through financing options right now, that's the conversation that actually moves the deal. Learn more at joinkale.com.

## CARD exp-mortgage-jv-wind-down-recruiting

**Headline:**
eXp's in-house mortgage pitch just lost its own case study.

**Sub:**
Success Lending is winding down after volume fell from $658 million to $270 million.

**Caption:**
eXp and Kind Lending are winding down their Success Lending joint venture, the in-house mortgage platform eXp has used to recruit agents since 2021, after production fell from $658 million in 2024 to $526.5 million in 2025 and just $270 million year to date in 2026. eXp says it's now exploring a new venture with Newrez. If a brokerage is pitching you a captive lender as a reason to join, ask for the joint venture's funded-loan volume trend before you sign, not after. Learn more at joinkale.com.
