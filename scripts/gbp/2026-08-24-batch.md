## CARD commission-vs-split

**Headline:**
Buyer commissions went up after the settlement, not down.

**Subhead:**
Average buyer's agent commission: **2.42%** in Q3 2025, up from 2.36% in Q3 2024 (Redfin).

**Caption:**
A year after the new NAR rules took effect, the average buyer's agent commission rose from 2.36% to 2.42%, according to Redfin's own data. The settlement that was supposed to shrink commissions didn't. What actually eats into an agent's paycheck is the split their own brokerage keeps on every closing, a number that has nothing to do with the settlement at all.
