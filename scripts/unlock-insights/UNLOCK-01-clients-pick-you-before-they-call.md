## Production Notes

- **Speaker lower-third:** Amanda Pendleton headshot + Zillow logo lower-third on "At Zillow Unlock, Amanda Pendleton shared Zillow's 2026 consumer survey" (about 0:06-0:09). The spoken credit names the 2026 survey on purpose: the 54/67 belong to that report and nothing else. Captions build from audio; no other manual text.
- **One Prop:** hold the phone up on "phone" in the hook, then lower it and walk. The phone comes back into frame on "search your name" in PAYOFF, which ties the open to the close.
- **Numbers said the session's way.** "Over half" for 54% (the session's own words are "over half of buyers (54%)"), and "sixty-seven percent" for sellers, not "two in three," so nothing is rounded.
- **Not a Zillow ad.** The fix is "search your own name," not "update your Zillow profile." The session's next step names the Zillow profile; the reel keeps it platform-neutral on purpose.
- **"Fix any old photo, bio or dead link"** is D.J.'s fix, not a session statistic. It replaced round 1's "Fix what's old. That's the call you never got," which did not say what to fix and closed on a clever line. Friction points at the stale profile, never at the agent.
- **D.J. does not practice.** Every line is "you" and "they." No "my clients."
- Hook family 4 (System Indictment): the decision moved online and nobody told the agent who still waits for the phone. Heat 4, no named party.

## Council Review

Round 2 (fresh eyes): tied the spoken credit to Zillow's 2026 survey and swapped the vague, clever close ("Fix what's old. That's the call you never got.") for a fix the viewer can actually do tonight.

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B):**
1. "You're losing clients before your phone even rings."  [hook_family: 4 System Indictment | emotion: anxiety]
2. "Most sellers hire the first agent they talk to."  [hook_family: 6 Named Stakes | emotion: surprise]
3. "Your old profile already cost you a client."  [hook_family: 1 Mirror | emotion: anxiety]

**Pattern interrupt:** One Prop (the phone), confirmed. It is the thing that never rings, and it comes back on "search your name."

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): it opens "how do I lose them before they call?" and THE POINT closes it with the first-contact numbers by second ten.
- Share/save driver (Berger/Hormozi): anxiety plus a ten-minute fix with three named things to check; team leads forward it to agents with stale profiles.
- Retention move (MrBeast): no dead close anymore; the last words are the to-do list, then cut.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Byron Lazine wants variant 2 because the number is the news and agents have not heard it. Miller says variant 1 keeps the agent as the hero with a problem, not a statistic. Ship variant 1; cut variant 2 as the A/B open and compare 3-second hold. Do not run either until the 54/67 are confirmed against the 2026 report or Pendleton's slides.
