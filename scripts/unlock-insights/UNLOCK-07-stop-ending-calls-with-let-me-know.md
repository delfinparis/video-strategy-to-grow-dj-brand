# Stop ending calls with "let me know"

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: The Stop (walk fast, plant and lean in on "Stop").

## Teleprompter

Stop ending calls with "let me know."

That soft ending lets good leads go cold.

At Zillow Unlock, Colton Whitney shared his team's numbers. Some leads got a booked next step within three conversations. Those closed nine percent of the time. The rest closed just one point six percent of the time.

Before you hang up, ask, "Does Tuesday at four work?" Then nobody has to let you know.
