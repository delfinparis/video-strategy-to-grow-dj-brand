# Your old leads are still clicking. Nobody calls.

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: One Prop (hold your phone up to the lens, a CRM contact list on screen, on "still clicking").

## Teleprompter

Your old leads are still clicking. Nobody calls.

Brett Siegal of Ruuster studied over ten million leads. Ninety-one percent never heard from a human.

He showed it at Zillow Unlock. A cold lead takes about twenty-three and a half touches to book. The leads opening emails and viewing homes? They convert three point three times as often.

Today, pull old leads who clicked this week. Call them first.

## Production Notes

- **Speaker lower-third:** Brett Siegal headshot + Ruuster logo lower-third on "Brett Siegal of Ruuster studied over ten million leads" (about 0:01.5-0:05); "He showed it at Zillow Unlock" follows at about 0:06.5. Captions build from audio; no other manual text.
- **One Prop:** phone up on the hook, scrolling a contact list (blur any real names). Lower it on "Ninety-one percent."
- **Scale caveat, said carefully:** the ten million leads are across CRMs connected to Ruuster, mostly teams that buy leads. The script says "leads," not "your leads," in TENSION and THE POINT for that reason. The hook ("Your old leads are still clicking. Nobody calls.") is the viewer's own database, not a claim about the data set.
- **Round 2 re-angle (overlap with UNLOCK-13):** the old hook "You paid for leads no person ever contacted" and the close "call them before buying more" nearly repeated UNLOCK-13 (Barry Jenkins, priciest leads never called). This reel now leads on Siegal's own point, the engaged leads, so the two make different asks: 13 = find the paid leads with no call logged; 30 = call the ones clicking first.
- **"Never heard from a human"** is the summary's "never receive a human touchpoint." Do not change it to "never got a call"; a human text or email also counts as a touch.
- **"Three point three times as often"** = the summary's "leads showing recent engagement convert at 3.3x the rate of cold outreach." Engagement in the session means property views, email opens and clicks; the script names opens and views.
- **Say the numbers slowly:** "ninety-one percent," "twenty-three and a half," "three point three." Three numbers is the limit; do not add the "$" database value or any other figure.
- **Not a Ruuster ad.** The session's next step was a Ruuster database audit; the script leaves it out. The fix works in any CRM with a "clicked this week" filter.
