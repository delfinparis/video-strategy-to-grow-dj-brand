## Production Notes

- **Speaker lower-third:** Bobby Moats headshot + Speculo AI logo on "At Zillow Unlock, Bobby Moats" (about 0:04-0:07, the top of THE POINT). No other manual text.
- **The Stop:** plant on "everything." UNLOCK-11 used One Prop.
- **"Useless" is the edge, and it's fair.** Moats's point is that overloading AI (or a human assistant) with broad duties "leads to poor results." Friction is at the habit of vague, do-everything prompting, not at AI, not at any product, not at the viewer.
- **No tool named.** Moats listed four assistants (Dots, Gemini Spark, Grok, Muse) and his own company sells setup help. The script names none of them; the fix is the job description, which works in any tool.
- **"Where it stops for your OK"** is Moats's "approval boundary," said plainly.
- **The turn (round 2):** TENSION now carries the cost, "It does every job badly. You blame the AI." The blame line is the setup the fix overturns: the problem is the job, not the tool. Friction stays on the do-everything habit.
- **Rule of three:** "gets / hands back / stops" is the one list in the script.
- **Pinned first comment:** "Bobby Moats at Zillow Unlock 2026: make three lists (tasks you struggle with, tasks you avoid, tasks that take you away from clients or family), circle one, and give it to an AI assistant within 48 hours."
- **Cold read:** Mistake: asking AI to handle everything. Fix: give it one written job, and check its work fifteen minutes a day.
