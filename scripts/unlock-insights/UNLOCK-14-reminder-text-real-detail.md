## Production Notes

- **Recipient-neutral on purpose (round 2).** Round 1 assumed the texts went to clients. The session text leans the other way: two lines say the texts went from Barry's personal number to his agents. The likeliest reading is that the AI texted each agent before an appointment with one client detail to bring up. The spoken claim now says only that "each text had one real detail" and that "twice as many booked meetings happened," which is true under either reading. The viewer's action ("write down one thing each client told you, put it in the reminder") works for a solo agent texting a client and for a team lead texting an agent. Do not add "to the client" or "to your agents" back into the spoken lines until the slides settle it.
- **If the slides confirm agents:** an optional team-lead re-cut is in the Council Review dissent. The current cut can still post.
- **Speaker lower-third:** Barry Jenkins headshot + Legacy Home Team / LPT logo and Marissa Canario headshot + Ylopo logo, side by side, on "At Zillow Unlock, Barry Jenkins and Marissa Canario" (about 0:06-0:10, the top of THE POINT). No other manual text.
- **Gesture-On-Beat:** palm to the lens on "costing." UNLOCK-13 used Push-In.
- **"Twice as many booked meetings happened"** is the plain form of "doubled appointment-met rates." It is attributed on camera ("They said") because the summary calls it reported, not measured by an outside source. "Booked" keeps it a rate on meetings already set, not more meetings overall.
- **No AI tool needed for the viewer.** Their texts were AI-written; the viewer can do it by hand tonight. No product named.
- **The bathroom** is the session's own example, not invented.
- **Pinned first comment:** "From Barry Jenkins and Marissa Canario at Zillow Unlock 2026. They trained the AI on Barry's own book so the texts sound like him."
- **Cold read:** Mistake: a plain reminder text, so booked meetings fall through. Fix: put one real thing the client said into each reminder.
