- **Speaker on screen:** Claire Bauman and Mina Khan headshots + Meta logo, lower-third on "At Zillow Unlock, Claire Bauman and Mina Khan from Meta showed this." (about 0:07-0:11).
- **Pattern interrupt:** The Push-In on "four tenths." The number is the hook; the move makes it feel short.

- **"Don't open with your name or a hello" is D.J.'s application, not a Meta quote.** The session says use a strong hook in the first few seconds; the hello example is D.J.'s plain version of that. The slate's "most agent videos open with 'Hey guys'" was dropped: no source counts that.

- **Friction target:** the habit of warming up before the point. Not agents, not Meta. No product pitch: the session also demoed Meta's Edits app; left out on purpose.
- **Practice what it preaches:** D.J. must hit the first word on frame one. Trim any lead-in in CapCut.
