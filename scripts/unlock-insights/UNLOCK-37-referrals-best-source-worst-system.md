- Hook mechanism (Heath): "best" and "worst" in one line is a contradiction the brain wants resolved.
- Share/save driver (Berger/Hormozi): team leaders forward it to the team; five calls with one question is a save.
- Retention move (MrBeast): ninety-four percent, then the turn to "a third," lands at 0:15, the drift point.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Welsh flags that UNLOCK-04 also ends on calling past clients; Hormozi says the daily five-call habit is a different, smaller ask. Decision: keep, and post at least two weeks from UNLOCK-04. **Experiment:** variant 3 against variant 1 on holds; if 3 is used, its back half must be "a third rate their follow-up five out of ten," never "most ignore it."
