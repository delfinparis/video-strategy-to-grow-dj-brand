# Stop running open houses that last all day

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: Gesture-On-Beat (palm to the lens, a "stop" hand, on "Stop").

Stop running open houses that last all day.

Buyers show up one at a time. The house feels empty.

Chase Whitney of First Team Real Estate spoke at Zillow Unlock. His rule: run it one to four, not all day. Now buyers walk in and see other buyers. They worry they'll lose the house.

Do this today. Set your next open house for one to four. Not all day.
