# Cutting your fee won't win you the listing

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: The Stop (walk fast for one beat, plant and lean in on "fee").

## Teleprompter

Cutting your fee won't win you the listing.

You give up real money for something sellers rank lower.

At Zillow Unlock, Amanda Pendleton shared Zillow's 2026 consumer survey. Only forty-nine percent of sellers said a lower rate was a priority. They cared far more about trust and fast answers.

When a seller asks for a discount, say this. "I don't cut my fee. I call back within the hour."
