# Your busy season causes your slow season

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30s max. Visual open: The Stop (walk fast for one beat, plant and lean into the lens on "causes").

## Teleprompter

Your busy season causes your slow season.

You have deals to close, so you stop calling.

At Zillow Unlock, Ryan Young of the Young Team showed it. Appointments peak January through March. After that, they drop. His team used AI to keep following up. Their appointments are up sixty-two percent this year.

Today, block one hour of calls every weekday. Keep it in your busiest month.

- **The Sacred Cow** is the belief that busy months are the time to stop prospecting and focus on closing. The friction is at that habit, never at the agent.
- **Story turn:** TENSION is the habit (busy, so you stop calling); THE POINT shows what it does (appointments drop after March) and the team that broke it.
- **Round 2 edits:** "then drop all year" became "After that, they drop," which is what the session says (they decline after Q1). "Here's what you do now" is cut to make room for "Their appointments," and the close starts on "Today." "One hour" is D.J.'s instruction, not a session figure.
- **"Zillow Unlock"** contains a banned verb; it is the event's proper name and stays.
