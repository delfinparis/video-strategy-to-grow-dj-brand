## Production Notes

- **Speaker lower-third:** Phil Stringer headshot + headshot only (no AI Academy logo found yet) on "At Zillow Unlock, Phil Stringer shared numbers" (about 0:05-0:08). Pull it before "Every one of them had a website." No other manual text; captions build from audio.

The study the number matches (Local Falcon, below) measured only agents and teams ranked by production who publish a website. Saying "agents" would upgrade the claim. - **"Zillow Unlock"** is the event's proper name; the banned-word list (unlock) does not apply to it.
- **Not a Zillow ad, not an AI-product ad.** The fix is articles on the agent's own website. No tool named.
- **D.J. is not the agent here.** Every action is "you." No first person.
- **Pinned first comment:** "From Phil Stringer's session at Zillow Unlock 2026, 'When AI recommends an agent, will it be you?' His 5-step system starts with 'best choice' articles: 200-300 words, third person, answering the exact question a client types." That is where the credit and the detail live, since captions carry no source names.
