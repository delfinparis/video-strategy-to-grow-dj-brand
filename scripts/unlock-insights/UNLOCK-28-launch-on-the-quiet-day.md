## Production Notes

- **Speaker lower-third:** Chase Whitney headshot + First Team Real Estate logo (Whitney Team) lower-third on "Chase Whitney of First Team Real Estate spoke at Zillow Unlock" (about 0:06-0:10). Captions build from audio; no other manual text.

- **Payoff (round 2)** is Whitney's next step ("run a day-of-week listing analysis") as two plain steps: pull last month's new listings, count them by day of the week. "Near you" stands in for MLS/zip codes.
- **Receipt dropped on purpose:** the slate paired this with Whitney's "3.5-5.5x views and saves" number. In the session that number measures his whole triple-launch plan (Zillow Preview, Coming Soon, then Active with Showcase) against competing listings, not the launch day alone. Saying it here would credit the day with a result the session gives to the full plan, and would turn the reel into a pitch for Zillow listing products. The reel runs stat-free on the day-of-week tip, which the session states plainly.
- **No product names.** Preview / Coming Soon / Showcase are left out. The day-of-week tip works on any MLS.
- **If asked in comments:** Coming Soon and preview rules differ by MLS. Whitney's own next step was "verify your local MLS rules." Point people there.
- **"Yours blends in" / "fewer homes compete with yours"** is the session's "stand out in buyers' inboxes," said plainly. It is not a measured claim.
