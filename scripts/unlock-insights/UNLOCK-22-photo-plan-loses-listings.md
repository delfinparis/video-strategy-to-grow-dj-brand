# Listing agents, you're losing listings over your photos

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: Walk-Toward (camera held out, walking straight at it on "Listing agents").

## Teleprompter

Listing agents, you're losing listings over your photos.

Show up without a photo plan and that seller looks elsewhere.

At Zillow Unlock, Zillow's Mike Caputo shared this. Offer high-res photos. Seventy-eight percent of sellers are more likely to hire you. Add a virtual tour and floor plan. Seventy-five percent say the same.

Do this today. Write that plan on one page. Bring it to every listing appointment.

## Production Notes

- **Speaker on screen:** Mike Caputo headshot + Zillow logo lower-third on "At Zillow Unlock, Zillow's Mike Caputo shared this" (about 0:06.5-0:09). No other manual text; captions build from audio.
- **Walk-Toward** fits the cohort callout: D.J. walks at the lens as he names the listing agents he's talking to.
- **Not a Zillow ad.** The session was a product walkthrough of Zillow's listing tools (Zillow Pro, Preview, Showcase, a performance dashboard). The script uses only the seller-survey numbers and never names a product or tells agents where to buy media. Keep it that way in the comments too: if asked "who should I use for photos," the answer is "whoever you trust locally."
- **"High-res photos," not "pro photos."** The survey wording is high-resolution photography. Say "high-res" cleanly.
- **"Virtual tours and floor plans," not "3D tours."** The slate said 3D tours; the session summary and Zillow's release say virtual tours and (interactive) floor plans. The spoken line follows the release.
- **"Offer high-res photos. Seventy-eight percent of sellers are more likely to hire you."** is the survey's "more likely to hire agents who offer high-resolution photography," split into two short sentences (round 2) so the old "hire for high-res photos" clunk is gone. Same for the 75% line. Do not say "want" or "demand"; it is "more likely to hire."
- **"Write that plan on one page"** = who shoots the photos, the virtual tour, the floor plan. Editorial action, no product named.
- **Two numbers, two lines.** The rule is max two numbers per line; each survey figure gets its own sentence. Percentages stay as percentages because 78 and 75 don't convert to clean "X in Y" ratios without rounding.
- **Truth filter on TENSION:** "that seller looks elsewhere" is the plain reading of "more likely to hire agents who offer." It doesn't claim every seller walks.

- Share/save driver (Berger/Hormozi): two public, sourced numbers a listing agent can quote in their own presentation. That is the save.
- Retention move (MrBeast): "Offer X, here's the number" runs twice in a row, so the middle builds instead of sagging.
