# They paid the most for leads nobody called

**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: Push-In, phone pulled toward the face on "paid the most."

## Teleprompter

They paid the most for leads nobody called.

That's what Barry Jenkins found on his own team.

He showed it at Zillow Unlock with Marissa Canario. They had Claude, an AI tool, read their lead database. One in four of the most expensive leads never got a call.

Today, pull your paid leads. Find every one with no call logged. Call those before you buy more.

## Production Notes

- **Speaker lower-third:** Barry Jenkins headshot + Legacy Home Team / LPT logo on "That's what Barry Jenkins found" (about 0:02-0:05), then Marissa Canario headshot + Ylopo logo on "with Marissa Canario" (about 0:06-0:09). No other manual text.
- **Push-In:** pull in on "paid the most." UNLOCK-12 used The Stop.
- **The number is about one team, and the script says so.** "One in four" is what their database showed. Never say "one in four of your leads" or imply it's an industry rate.
- **"Claude, an AI tool"** is named because it's in the session title and it's what they used. It isn't a recommendation, and the viewer action needs no AI: any CRM can show paid leads with no logged call.
- **Friction points at the gap** (paid leads going uncalled), not at Jenkins's agents or the viewer. Jenkins framed the find as making coaching "fact-based, less emotional," and the script keeps it there.
- **Pinned first comment:** "Barry Jenkins (Legacy Home Team, LPT) and Marissa Canario (Ylopo) at Zillow Unlock 2026. Their database also flagged buyers agents met face to face and never followed up with."
- **Cold read:** Mistake: buying leads and not checking whether anyone called the expensive ones. Fix: pull your paid leads, find the ones with no call logged, call them before buying more.
