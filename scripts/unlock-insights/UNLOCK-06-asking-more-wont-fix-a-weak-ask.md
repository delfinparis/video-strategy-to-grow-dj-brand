# Asking more often won't fix a weak ask

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: Gesture-On-Beat (one finger up on "more," then drop the hand and walk).

## Teleprompter

Asking more often won't fix a weak ask.

You just hear no more often.

At Zillow Unlock, Colton Whitney showed the fix. Make a small ask tied to what the lead needs. About one in ten used to say yes. With the better ask, three in ten or more did.

Next call, skip "want to meet?" Say, "Want to see the two homes in your budget Saturday?"
