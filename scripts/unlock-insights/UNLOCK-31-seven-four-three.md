# More showings won't get your buyer to an offer

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: Walk-Toward (camera held out, walk straight at it on the hook).

## Teleprompter

More showings won't fix a stuck buyer.

They like too many houses. So they pick none.

At Zillow Unlock, Phil Gerdes of Shift Immersive Coaching gave the fix. Your buyer texts you their top seven homes. They cut to four, then three. By three, the offer comes on its own.

Text your stuck buyer tonight: "Send me your top seven." No more showings until they cut to three.
