## Production Notes

- **Speaker lower-third:** Mischa Fisher headshot + Zillow logo on "At Zillow Unlock, chief economist Mischa Fisher had the number" (about 0:05-0:09). Name/logo lower-third only; captions build from audio.
- **Round 2 hook:** round 1's "renters who'd buy if rates fell" turned "very or extremely likely" into a sure thing. The new hook names the habit and leaves the exact number to THE POINT. "Say mortgage rates fall" became "Suppose mortgage rates fall" so it does not collide with the "Say this" in the close.
- **Say the number exactly as given:** "about four in ten," "very or extremely likely." Do not shorten to "would buy" or "four in ten will buy." It is a stated likelihood, conditional on rates falling.
- **No rate forecast.** Fisher's outlook was roughly flat with rates easing toward the mid-6% range. The script makes no prediction; the viewer's text says "if rates drop," not "when."
- **Not a Zillow ad.** He is Zillow's economist, but nothing here points to a Zillow product. The action is a plain text from the agent's own phone.
- **"Buyers aren't gone. They're waiting."** is his "delayed, not gone," said plainly. It is the one contrast in the script.
- **"Say this. If rates drop, I'll tell you that day."** is the viewer's text to their renter. D.J. does not practice; "Say this" frames it as the agent's line.
- **"Zillow Unlock"** contains a banned verb; it is the event's proper name and stays.
