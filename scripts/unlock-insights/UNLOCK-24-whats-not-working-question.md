# Stop asking buyers what's wrong with their house

**Filming:** Walk and talk, 30 seconds. Visual open: Gesture-On-Beat (palm-to-camera "stop" on the first word).

## Teleprompter

Stop asking buyers what's wrong with their house.

It sounds like you're judging their home. So they shut down.

At Zillow Unlock, Micah Laurendeau gave the better question. Ask, "What about your home isn't working for you anymore?" Now it's about their life, not your opinion of their house.

Write that question down. Ask it on your next buyer call. Then stop talking. Let them answer.
