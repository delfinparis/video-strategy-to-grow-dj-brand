# Your AI Email Gives You Away

> **WOW: Contrarian take from a credentialed source -- the guy who teaches AI to agents is telling them to stop using AI to write their emails, and naming the exact tells clients see.**

## Script (Spoken)

I teach AI to real estate agents. And I can tell in the first sentence when you used ChatGPT to write an email.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

You paste your idea in. It gives you back something that sounds polished. You send it. Your client reads one line and knows a human didn't write it. The em dashes give you away. "I hope this email finds you well" gives you away. They stop reading. The lead goes cold.

Most agents think clean AI writing makes them sound professional. Your clients read it and decide you didn't care enough to write them yourself. Same rule for the AI voicemail greeting on your phone. Nobody hiring an agent wants to talk to a robot before they talk to you.

Here's what you do now. Before you send your next email, read it out loud. If a line sounds written instead of spoken, rewrite it the way you'd actually say it. Then send it.
