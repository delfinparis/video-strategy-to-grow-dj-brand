# One Standard or Two?

> **On-Screen Text:** "The $200K listing is killing your $2M career."

## Script (Spoken)

The two hundred thousand dollar listing is killing your two million dollar career.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

Most agents say they treat every client the same. But your calendar, your response time, your staging budget tell a different story. Kristee Leonard built a fifty million dollar business in Austin and she says this: "If you choose to take on that client, you need to represent them just like a multimillion dollar deal." That 250K seller has a sister looking at 3 million. That first-time buyer has a boss who just got promoted. They're watching how you show up.


Do this today: Think about the last time you let your standard slide for a client. Decide right now: one standard for everyone, every time.


See you next time.
