# HUD Just Gave You Permission. Most Agents Are Going To Blow It.

> **WOW: Permission slip + tactical specificity -- HUD's "Dear Colleague" letter (HUD No. 26-028, April 24, 2026) said sharing crime data and school quality data with buyers is NOT a Fair Housing Act violation. The catch most agents will miss: data is fine, opinion is not. Concrete tonight-action: build a one-page buyer doc, two links, no commentary, hand it to every buyer at the start of every search.**

## Script (Spoken)

Your buyer already asked you about crime and schools. You ducked it. HUD just told you to stop.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

April 24th. HUD dropped a Dear Colleague letter — HUD No. 26-028. Sharing crime data and school quality data with buyers is NOT a Fair Housing Act violation. NAR confirmed it the same week.

Here's the part most agents are about to miss. HUD said the DATA is fine. Your OPINION of the data is not. Crime stats — fine. "Bad neighborhood" — still a violation. School ratings — fine. "I wouldn't send my kid there" — still a violation. Data, yes. Judgment, no.

The agent who hands a buyer objective data looks like an expert. The agent who says "I can't talk about that" looks like they're hiding something. HUD just handed you the high ground. Don't waste it.

Here's what you do now. Tonight. Build a one-page buyer doc. Two links: your city's crime dashboard, your state's school report card. No commentary. No highlighting. Hand it to every buyer at the start of every search starting tomorrow. You just stopped ducking the question. Without crossing the line.

## Data Source

- **"April 24, HUD Dear Colleague letter, HUD No. 26-028":** Confirmed via [NAR Washington Report](https://www.nar.realtor/washington-report/hud-issues-dear-colleague-letter-on-school-and-crime-data) (2026-04-29).
- **"Sharing crime data and school quality data with buyers is not a Fair Housing Act violation":** Direct paraphrase per NAR Washington Report 2026-04-29: "[the letter] emphasiz[es] that it is not a violation of the Fair Housing Act to share information with prospective homebuyers about neighborhood crime rates and school quality data." Confirmed.
- **"Data is fine, opinion is not" (the catch):** NAR Washington Report 2026-04-29 explicitly cautions: "Subjective commentary, personal opinions, or hearsay about schools or crime have been cited as evidence of discriminatory intent." This is the load-bearing nuance most agents will miss reading only the headline. Confirmed.
- **Concrete tonight-action (one-page buyer doc):** D.J. Paris editorial recommendation. Public objective data sources exist in every U.S. metro -- city/police-department crime dashboards and state school report cards. For Chicago specifically: [data.cityofchicago.org](https://data.cityofchicago.org) (crime) and [illinoisreportcard.com](https://illinoisreportcard.com) (schools). Spoken script keeps it generic so agents in any market can apply it.
