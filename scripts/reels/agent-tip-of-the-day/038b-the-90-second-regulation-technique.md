# The 90-Second Regulation Technique

> **On-Screen Text:** 1. NOTICE. 2. NAME. 3. BREATHE. 4. ASK.

## Script (Spoken)

Paul Salter teaches a 90-second technique for leaders who get defensive under pressure.

Notice the tightness. Name it silently -- "I feel threatened." Take three breaths. Then ask one question: "What if they're right?"

That pause between feeling and reacting -- that's where you keep your top performers.

Before your next team meeting, practice this sequence once. So when someone challenges your strategy, you have a choice -- not just a reflex.
