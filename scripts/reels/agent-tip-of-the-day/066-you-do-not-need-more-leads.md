# You Do Not Need More Leads

> **WOW: Contrarian take from a credentialed source -- a top 1% Chicago agent of twenty years says the answer isn't more leads or two-hour call blocks. It's one specific reach-out, every day, to people you already know.**

## Script (Spoken)

You do NOT need more leads.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

Amy Duong Kim. Top 1% Chicago agent for twenty straight years. I asked her on Keeping It Real what she actually does to grow her business. Her exact words: "If I say I'm going to call for two hours, I'm never going to do that. That sounds horrible."

So here's what she does instead — and has done since day one. One person. Just one. A specific text or a specific call. Not "thinking of you." Something only she would say to that specific human.

Two hundred working days a year. Two hundred specific touches. Even at a 2% conversion, that's four deals you didn't have last year — from people who already know you.

The whole industry is screaming for more leads. The top 1% agent says reach out to one person you already have.

Here's what you do now. Pick the one person who just popped into your head. Send them one specific text. Then do it again tomorrow. That's the whole thing.

- **"Top 1% Chicago agent for 20 years":** Amy Duong Kim, team lead at Duong Kim Global at Compass. Multiple-year top 1% production per Chicago Association of REALTORS, Chicago Agent Magazine, and RealTrends rankings referenced in her introduction. Confirmed.
- **"Two hours sounds horrible" + one-person-a-day rule:** Direct quotes, Keeping It Real Podcast, live webinar episode with Amy Duong Kim, June 2025. Verbatim transcript at [data/transcripts/2025-06-10_item58_Amy-Duong-Kim.txt](../../../keeping-it-real-content-system/data/transcripts/2025-06-10_item58_Amy-Duong-Kim.txt). Confirmed.
- **Episode link:** Keeping It Real Podcast — "Are You Making Calls to Your Sphere Every Single Day?" (Amy Duong Kim, June 2025).
- **Two-hundred-business-days math:** Standard U.S. business-day count (~252 weekdays minus holidays/PTO ≈ 200 working days). Used in-episode by D.J.: "if you're only working 200 business days a year, it's still 200 people." Confirmed.
