## Script (25-30 seconds)

### HOOK (0:00-0:05)

### BODY (0:05-0:22)
At Kale, we run skill-building events every month, and we invite agents from every brokerage in Chicago. Not just ours. The idea is simple: you don't become a better agent by only learning from the people at your own company. You become a better agent by spending time with anyone who's sharpened a tool you haven't picked up yet.

Most agents never take advantage of events like this. They assume training means their own broker's training. That's the trap.

The agents who show up at everyone else's trainings are the ones nobody can beat. Find the next one in your market. Show up.
