# Stop Pricing Listings Off The National News

> **WOW: Contrarian take backed by a stat almost every agent is misusing -- Redfin's "34.2% of sellers cut prices" headline is national, but the underlying data shows San Antonio at 58%, Austin at 55%, Tampa at 46%. Sun Belt is doing all the lifting. Agents importing the national pricing conversation into a different local market are about to miscalibrate every listing appointment they walk into.**

## Script (Spoken)

If your pricing conversation tomorrow starts with a national headline, stop.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

Redfin says thirty-four percent of sellers cut their price in February. Highest rate since 2012. You've seen the headline. Probably twice. Here's what the headline didn't tell you. Thirty-four percent isn't a national rule. San Antonio is fifty-eight percent. Austin fifty-five. Tampa forty-six. Sun Belt is doing all the lifting.

Same week, Illinois REALTORS reported the opposite. Median prices up. Inventory down. Sales down because there's nothing to sell. My agents at Kale are walking into listing appointments already strategizing for multiple offers.

Two markets. Opposite directions. Same week. The agent who walks into a listing appointment tomorrow with "we have to price conservative, the market's softening" just borrowed somebody else's market. That's the miscalibration that loses listings.

Here's what you do now. Before your next listing appointment, pull one stat from your STATE realtors association. Median price year-over-year. Inventory. Days on market. Not Redfin. Not CNN. Your state. Walk in with that number. Now you're priced for the market you actually live in.

## Data Source

- **"34.2% of February sellers nationally cut their asking price -- highest February rate since 2012":** Redfin analysis reported by RISMedia, "Home Sellers Cutting Prices at Record Rate as Buyer's Market Intensifies, Per Redfin Study" (2026-04-17). Confirmed.
- **"San Antonio 58%, Austin 55%, Tampa 46% cut rates":** Same Redfin analysis. Exact figures: San Antonio 57.9%, Austin 55.2%, Tampa 45.9%. Confirmed.
- **"Illinois REALTORS February: median prices up YoY, inventory down, sales down ~6%":** [Illinois REALTORS February 2026 Housing Report](https://www.illinoisrealtors.org/marketstats/) (published 2026-03-18). 7,356 homes sold Feb 2026 vs. 7,820 Feb 2025 (5.9% YoY decline). Confirmed.
- **"Kale agents strategizing for multiple offers":** D.J. Paris direct observation as VP Business Development, Kale Realty (Chicago). Pattern reported by Kale agents Q1-Q2 2026.
- **Companion long-form:** [Inside the Industry NF-007 -- "Chicago Didn't Get the Buyer's Market Memo"](../../inside-the-industry/NF-007-home-sellers-cutting-prices.md) (post date 2026-04-20). This walk-and-talk is the agent-action pull-through, generalized for any market (not just Chicago).
