# Stop Letting ChatGPT Write Your Emails

> **WOW: Contrarian take from a credentialed source -- the person who teaches AI to real estate agents is telling them to stop using it to write emails, and naming the exact tells clients spot in the first line.**

## Script (Spoken)

Stop using ChatGPT to write your emails. I teach AI to real estate agents, and your clients can spot yours in the first line.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

Em dashes. "I hope this email finds you well." "In today's market." "I wanted to reach out." Every one of those is a signal a human didn't write the email. Your client reads it, knows a robot wrote it, and the lead goes cold before they reply.

You think it sounds professional. Your client thinks you're lazy.

Here's what you do now. Before you send your next email, read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it on the phone, cut it. Then send.
