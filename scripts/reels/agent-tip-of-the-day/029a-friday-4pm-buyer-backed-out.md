# The Friday 4pm Buyer Backed Out

> **On-Screen Text:** HE DROVE THERE THAT NIGHT

## Script (Spoken)

A buyer backed out at 4pm on a Friday. The seller was furious.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

Jay Cannone -- 40 years in luxury, top 1% every single year -- could've hidden until Monday. Instead, he drove to their house that night. Sat at the kitchen table. Told them exactly what happened and exactly what he'd do next. No spin. No excuses.

Then he told me: "Show up. Do the work. Be straight. Do that long enough, and success becomes inevitable."

Do this right now: Think about the difficult conversation you've been avoiding. Send the text. Make the call. Waiting only makes it worse.
