# Keep AI Off Your Voicemail

> **WOW: Contrarian take from a credentialed source -- the person who teaches AI to real estate agents is naming the one place agents should never put it, tied to a specific consequence: the caller assumes a robot greeting means a robot in the transaction.**

## Script (Spoken)

The AI voice on your voicemail is costing you the call. I teach AI to real estate agents, and this is the one place to never use it.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

Your voicemail greeting is the first thing a new client ever hears from you. If it sounds like a robot, they assume you will too. They hang up and call the next agent on the list.

Top producers record their own greeting. Sounds like them. Tells every caller a real human picks up real calls.

Here's what you do now. Open your phone. Delete your AI greeting. Record a new one in your voice. Say your name. Say you'll call back the same day. Hang up. Do it before your next showing.
