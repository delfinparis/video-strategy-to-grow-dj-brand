# Raise the Bar -- Here's How

> **On-Screen Text:** THE PROFESSION EARNS RESPECT WHEN IT DEMANDS COMPETENCE

## Script (Spoken)

If you've been watching part-timers ghost clients mid-transaction and tank your reputation by association -- this isn't controversial. It's overdue.

The profession doesn't earn respect until it demands competence at the door.

Go to your state REALTOR association's website. Find the advocacy page. If raising licensing standards isn't listed, email your government affairs director and ask why not.
