# Listing Presentations Don't Matter

> **WOW: Contrarian take from a credentialed source -- the guy who teaches agents how to win listings, telling you to stop practicing the listing presentation. Tied to a specific NAR stat: 81% of sellers contacted only one agent before signing.**

## Script (Spoken)

Listing presentations don't matter. I know that sounds insane. Hear me out.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

NAR 2024 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers. 81% of recent sellers contacted only one agent before they signed. Eight in ten. The decision was already made before any agent walked through the door.

So if you're not the one call that seller makes, you didn't lose the listing. You were never in the running.

Which means the courses, the scripts, the role-plays for the kitchen-table pitch are solving the wrong problem.

Here's what actually wins listings. Pick one person right now you genuinely think will sell in the next twelve months. Open your texts. Send them something specific. Their kid's school. A comp on their block. Something only you would say to them. Not "thinking of you."

Do that today. Then four more tomorrow. That's how you become the only call.

Be the only call. The presentation takes care of itself.

- **81% of sellers contacted only one agent before signing:** NAR 2024 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, the largest annual survey of who actually sold a home and how they picked their agent. Consumer-reported. Confirmed.
