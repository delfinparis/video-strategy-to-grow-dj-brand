# Your Commission Didn't Drop. Your Confidence Did.

> **WOW: Contrarian take backed by hard data -- nearly two years after the NAR settlement, buyer-agent commissions actually went UP (2.5% to 2.82%) and two-thirds of agents report zero meaningful change. The agents who lost income did it to themselves in the conversation.**

## Script (Spoken)

Your commission did not go down. You just stopped charging it.

*[Agent Tip of the Day bumper]*

Two years ago everyone screamed that the NAR settlement was going to crash buyer-side commissions. Here is the actual data. Buyer-side commissions dipped to 2.5% for about a minute — then bounced back to 2.82%. UP. And the latest Cotality survey: two-thirds of agents report ZERO meaningful change since the settlement.

So the commission didn't drop. The conversation around it did. Half the industry walked into listing appointments and buyer consults treating compensation like an apology. They ate the discount before the seller even asked.

The agents earning more right now are the ones who stopped flinching when the number came up. They name the fee. They explain exactly what it pays for. They do not wait to get cornered.

Here's what you do now. Open whatever buyer presentation or listing presentation you have on your phone right now. Find the slide where you talk about commission. Read it out loud. If you sound apologetic, rewrite it tonight. The market already moved on. So should your script.

## Data Source

- **"Commissions dipped to 2.5%, rebounded to 2.82%":** Clever Real Estate, "Is a 6% Real Estate Commission Still Standard in 2026?" (2026-03-11). 533 partner-agent survey, February 2026. Confirmed via secondary [Inman coverage](https://www.inman.com/2025/12/08/buyers-agent-commissions-see-rebound-in-wake-of-settlement/) (2025-12-08).
- **"Two-thirds of agents report no meaningful change":** Cotality / ResiClub joint survey, conducted February-March 2026, 213 agent respondents. Reported in [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2026/03/30/agent-commissions-show-stickiness-nearly-2-years-after-nar-settlement/) (2026-03-30). Confirmed.
- **"Tuccori settlement no additional practice changes":** [NAR Newsroom Tuccori statement](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/national-association-of-realtorsr-reaches-agreement-to-resolve-nationwide-homebuyer-claims-in) (2026-04-10). Confirmed.
