# Your Brain Is Sabotaging Your Calls

> **On-Screen Text:** PROGRAMMED TO SURVIVE, NOT THRIVE

## Script (Spoken)

Your brain is sabotaging every follow-up call you don't make.

Performance coach Paul Salter says your subconscious runs on one job: keep you alive. Not happy. Not wealthy. Alive.

So every time you try something risky -- calling a cold lead, saying no to a 9pm showing, raising your commission -- your brain reads it as danger and pulls you back to what's safe.

You're not lazy. You're wired for survival.
