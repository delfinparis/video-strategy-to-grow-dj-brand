# Rates Went Up -- Your Client Wants to Wait -- Now What?

> **On-Screen Text:** What do you say in the next 60 seconds?

## Script (Spoken)

Your client just texted: "Rates went up again. We're thinking of waiting." What do you say in the next sixty seconds?

Most agents either panic alongside the client, give a vague "rates always come down" answer, or launch into something that sounds Googled. None of those build confidence.

A client who loses confidence doesn't pause -- they disappear for months. Most agents try to predict the market. Top producers help clients decide despite it. That's a completely different skill.
