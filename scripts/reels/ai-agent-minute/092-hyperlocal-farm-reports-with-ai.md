# Write Hyperlocal Farm Reports With AI

> **On-Screen Text:** Make it feel like the agent lives down the street

## Script (Spoken)

Your farm mailers look exactly like every other agent's. That's exactly why they go straight in the trash.

This is D.J. with Keeping It Real Podcast and Kale Realty, and this is your AI Agent Minute.

Every homeowner in your farm gets three mailers a month -- same postcard, same generic stats, same stock photo. Nobody keeps them.

Here's the fix. Use Perplexity to pull hyperlocal data -- recent sales, days on market, price-per-square-foot trends. Then tell Claude: "Write a one-page neighborhood market report in a conversational tone. Make it feel like the agent writing this actually lives down the street."

That's the mailer they put on the refrigerator.
