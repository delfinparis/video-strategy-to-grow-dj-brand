# You Said "More Deals" in January -- Do You Remember That?

> **On-Screen Text:** Intentions vs. a plan

## Script (Spoken)

Most agents start the year saying "I want more deals" and "I want more money" -- then spend January reacting to whatever hits their inbox. By February, the goal is abstract. By March, it's forgotten.

This is D.J. with Keeping It Real Podcast and Kale Realty, and this is your AI Agent Minute.

Not because they didn't work hard. Because they were working without a map.

A one-hour investment in planning is worth more than a hundred hours of unfocused effort. The math is not close.


Try this: Open Claude and type "Build me a one-page annual business plan I'll actually use -- not a 30-page document I'll forget by February."


See you next time.
