# The 9 PM Texts and "Why Didn't You Tell Me?" -- All Preventable

> **On-Screen Text:** From expectations that were never set

## Script (Spoken)

Ninety percent of client problems come from expectations that were never set.

*[AI Agent Minute bumper]*

The 9 PM texts. The "why didn't you tell me about this?" conversation. The client who expects a response within the hour on a Sunday. Every one of those moments was preventable -- if someone had explained clearly at the start what the process looks like.

Most agents hope clients figure it out. Top producers explain it upfront -- before the first problem.

Here's what I'd do. Open Claude and type: "Help me write a client expectations document I can send at the start of every transaction to prevent after-hours emergencies."
