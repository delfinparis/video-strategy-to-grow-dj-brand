# The Friday Business Review With Claude

> **On-Screen Text:** Compare actual vs. target -- identify biggest gap -- one adjustment

## Script (Spoken)

You can't grow a business you're not measuring. And most agents aren't measuring anything.

Every top producer I've interviewed in twelve years does a weekly business review. Fifteen minutes. That's the habit that separates growers from plateauers.

Every Friday, give Claude your week's numbers and type: "Compare my actual activity against my weekly targets, identify my single biggest gap, and give me the one adjustment with the highest impact next week."

Fifteen minutes of focused reflection beats fifteen hours of unfocused effort.
