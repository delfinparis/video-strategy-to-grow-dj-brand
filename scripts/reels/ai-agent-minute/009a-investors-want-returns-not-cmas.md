# Investors Don't Hire Agents -- They Hire Analysts

> **On-Screen Text:** They didn't want a CMA. They wanted a return analysis.

## Script (Spoken)

Investors don't hire agents. They hire analysts who happen to have a real estate license.

An investor asks you to evaluate a rental property. You pull a CMA showing what comps sold for. They never call back. Why? They didn't want to know what it's worth. They wanted to know what it returns. Cap rate. Cash-on-cash return. Net operating income.

If you can't give them that, they'll find an agent who can -- and that agent will have them for life. Investors buy repeatedly. Lose one and you've lost a decade of deals.
