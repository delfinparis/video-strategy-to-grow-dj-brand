# Stop Asking for Referrals From Partners You Never Gave One To

> **On-Screen Text:** Asking vs. building -- completely different things

## Script (Spoken)

Most agents only think about referral partners when they need a referral. So every interaction feels transactional. The lender, the attorney, the financial advisor -- they feel the ask coming and quietly keep their referrals to themselves.

This is D.J. with Keeping It Real Podcast and Kale Realty, and this is your AI Agent Minute.

A one-way relationship isn't a relationship. It's a network of people who don't call you back.

Referral relationships are built on giving, not asking. AI helps you systematize the giving.


Try this: Open Claude and type "Help me create a 90-day referral partner outreach plan that leads with value before I ever ask for anything."


See you next time.
