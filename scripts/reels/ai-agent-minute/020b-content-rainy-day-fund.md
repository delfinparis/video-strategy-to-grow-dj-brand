# Build a Content Rainy Day Fund With Claude

> **On-Screen Text:** 30 posts. One hour. Never go silent.

## Script (Spoken)

Here's how to never go silent on social media again.

This is D.J. with Keeping It Real Podcast and Kale Realty, and this is your AI Agent Minute.

Spend one hour with Claude. Type: "Generate thirty pieces of evergreen real estate content that don't need timely data. Include myth-busting posts, client FAQ answers, 'did you know' market facts, process explainers, and seasonal home tips. Write them all ready to post."

Save them in a Google Doc. On the days you have nothing to say -- pull from the fund. You never go dark again.

Inspiration is optional. Systems are not. AI builds the system that shows up even when you don't feel like it.


See you next time.
