# The 20-Minute Monday Morning Market Briefing

> **On-Screen Text:** 20 minutes every Monday = always briefed

## Script (Spoken)

Build a twenty-minute Monday morning market intelligence routine.

Ask Perplexity for the top five real estate news stories from the past week -- national and local. Then give those to Claude and type: "Summarize each in two sentences and suggest how I'd explain each one to a client in plain language."

You start every week fully briefed. You become the agent who always knows what's happening -- because you built a system to make sure you do.

Twenty minutes. Every Monday. That's the whole investment.
