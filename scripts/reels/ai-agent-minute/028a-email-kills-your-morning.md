# You Checked Email This Morning -- You Already Lost

> **On-Screen Text:** Email is the enemy of income -- disguised as productivity

## Script (Spoken)

If you checked email first thing this morning, you already lost your most valuable hour.

This is D.J. with Keeping It Real Podcast and Kale Realty, and this is your AI Agent Minute.

You opened your inbox, started reacting, ninety minutes disappeared, and by ten AM you hadn't prospected. Your day now belongs to everyone else's agenda.

Email is the enemy of income-producing activity. It's just disguised as productivity. The top ten percent of agents haven't checked email first thing in years.
