# Never Respond to a Difficult Client in Real Time

> **On-Screen Text:** Never respond in real time from emotion -- with no strategy

## Script (Spoken)

Your most difficult client isn't testing your patience. They're testing your systems -- and most agents don't have one.

This is D.J. with Keeping It Real Podcast and Kale Realty, and this is your AI Agent Minute.

Difficult clients are almost always anxious, uninformed, or feeling out of control. Most agents respond by getting defensive, avoiding the conversation, or matching their energy.

The worst thing you can do is respond in real time, from emotion, with no strategy. All of those put the deal at risk.


Here's what I'd do. Open Claude and type: "A client just sent me a frustrating message. Help me draft a calm, professional response." Then paste it in.
