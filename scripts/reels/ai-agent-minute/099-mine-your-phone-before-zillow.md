# Mine Your Phone Before Zillow

> **On-Screen Text:** They bought strangers while their sphere went quiet

## Script (Spoken)

New agents are spending thousands on Zillow leads while sitting on five hundred untapped contacts in their phone.

*[AI Agent Minute bumper]*

Half your sphere doesn't even know you're in real estate. And the agents who go broke in year one almost always made the same mistake -- they bought strangers while their sphere went quiet.

Open Claude and list your contact categories -- neighbors, former coworkers, college friends. Type: "Write a personal, non-salesy outreach message for each group explaining why I got into real estate. Not a mass announcement -- individual messages that feel like they came from a friend."

Your sphere just needs to be reminded you exist.
