## Script (Spoken)

If you sell real estate at a smaller, independent brokerage -- the last 72 hours just gave you something agents at indies haven't had in twenty years.

Inventory parity with the giants.

When MRED opened its private-listings network nationally, it didn't just open it for Compass. It opened it for every member brokerage. That includes the indies. Any agent at any MRED brokerage can now see -- and show -- Compass's off-market book.

Here's what that means on Monday. The pitch you've always made -- "you get me, not a five-hundred-agent franchise" -- just got an upgrade. Same off-market access as Compass, plus the personal service the giants can't deliver. That's a recruiting argument.

The risk is the opposite. If your broker isn't filing your office's off-market inventory into MRED's national pool, you're handing buyers to the indies who are. Ask them this week. If the answer is "I'm not sure," that's the answer.

My read: in ninety days you'll see a wave of agent moves out of KW and eXp into well-positioned indies who figured this out first. The big three need to respond. Indies just need to move.

What's your broker doing this week? Drop it in the comments. See you next time.

## Source

- MRED announcement: private-listings network opens nationally to member brokerages (2026-04-24)
- Compass contribution of off-market inventory to MRED national pool

## Key Facts for Reference

- MRED has ~46,000 subscribers across ~3,000 member offices (most are independents)
- Network access is structurally available to every member, not just Compass
- "Inventory parity" depends on each member office actually filing their off-market inventory into the national pool
- Independent brokerages have historically lost recruiting battles on inventory access, not service
- Closing the arc: Day 1 (buyer-facing) → Day 2 (KW/eXp/BHHS) → Day 3 (indie agents)
