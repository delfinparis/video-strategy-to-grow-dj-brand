## Script (Spoken)

Thirty days ago, MRED opened Chicago's off-market network nationally, Compass dumped its entire inventory in, and I named three brokerages that had to respond.

Here's the verdict.

Keller Williams moved. HomeServices of America -- Berkshire Hathaway's parent -- moved. On May twelfth, both launched Cotality BLX: a platform where brokerages control their own listing data before it goes anywhere. They didn't join MRED's network. They built a competing one.

eXp said nothing.

Same day as the BLX launch, Zillow filed a federal antitrust suit against MRED and Compass. Eight days later, MRED pulled forty-three thousand Chicago listings off Zillow. A judge ordered them restored two days after that.

My original call: one signs with MRED in thirty days. What actually happened: two built a fortress to fight it.

The window closed. The war didn't.

## Key Facts for Reference

- **Cotality BLX** (Broker Listing Exchange): launched May 12, 2026 by Cotality (formerly CoreLogic), with HomeServices of America and Keller Williams as founding partners
- HomeServices of America is the parent company of Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices -- the BHHS named in NF-007
- BLX is a brokerage-first listing management platform: brokers enter listings into a brokerage-controlled system before distributing downstream to MLSs and portals
- HomeServices CEO Chris Kelly quote: "Our priority is making sure our agents and brokerages control their listing content from the moment it's created -- not after it's been distributed and returned to them."
- BLX rollout timeline: 3-6 months from launch (not live day-one for all agents)
- **eXp Realty**: no major strategic announcement in response to MRED. Had pre-existing Homes.com/Realtor.com pre-market deal from March 2026 (before the MRED story). CEO Leo Pareja on record: "I do not feel that exclusivity is the right choice."
- **Zillow antitrust suit** (May 12, 2026): alleges MRED and Compass conspired under Sherman Antitrust Act; sought injunction + treble damages
- **MRED listing feed cut** (~May 20): MRED suspended Zillow's access, ~43,000 Chicago-area listings vanished from Zillow
- **Federal judge order** (~May 22): court ordered MRED to restore Zillow's Chicago listing feed
- Gary Keller (May 6) warned privately-marketed listings could relegate MLSs to "market of last resort"
- Compass CEO Robert Reffkin (May 6): "We are bringing MRED national"
- Realtracs (Tennessee MLS) also went national with Compass in a parallel move

## Prediction Verdict

**Scenario B -- Half-validated.**

Original prediction (NF-007): "One of the three signs with MRED inside thirty days. The other two spend a year explaining why they didn't."

Actual outcome: Two of the three (KW and BHHS parent HomeServices) responded in 18 days -- not by signing WITH MRED, but by launching a competing brokerage-controlled platform (Cotality BLX). The timeline was right. The direction was the opposite. eXp made no significant strategic move.
