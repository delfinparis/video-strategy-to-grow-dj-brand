## Key Facts for Reference

- Compass: ~30,000 agents (post-Anywhere acquisition: ~330,000)
- Keller Williams: ~160,000 US agents, Command platform, no national private-listings product
- eXp Realty: ~85,000 agents, cloud-based, no off-market inventory shelf
- Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices (HomeServices of America): ~50,000 agents, luxury-heavy
- MRED: Midwest Real Estate Data, Chicago's MLS, ~46,000 subscribers
- "Coming Soon" and "Private Exclusive" listings historically not visible outside the originating brokerage
