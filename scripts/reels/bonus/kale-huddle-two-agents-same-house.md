## Data Source

- **Claim:** "The appraiser makes adjustments... Condition. Upgrades. Lot size."
  - Source: Standard appraisal methodology (the sales comparison approach adjusts comparable sales for differences in condition, upgrades, lot size, and similar factors). Factual framing of how appraisers price, not a statistic.
  - Status: confirmed

- **Claim:** "The one closest to the appraiser is the one whose deal survives... the person whose number is the only one that counts"
  - Source: Standard residential mortgage lending practice. The lender bases the loan on the lower of the appraised value or contract price, so the appraiser's number governs whether a deal survives to closing. Framing, not a stat.
  - Status: confirmed
  - EP note: The hook intentionally defines the "win" as the deal surviving (closing), not winning the listing. In practice, an agent who overprices can win the listing but lose the deal at appraisal, so "whose deal survives" is the accurate, defensible claim.

- **Event logistics:** Kale Huddle hosted by A&N Mortgage on North Elston; Thursday, 3:30-6:00 PM; free with limited seats; registration at kalehuddle.com; food, drinks, and raffle prizes on site.
  - Source: D.J. Paris / Kale Realty (event organizer).
  - Status: confirmed by organizer
