## Data Source

- **Claim:** "The appraiser's [number] is the number the bank lends on"
  - Source: Standard residential mortgage lending practice. A lender bases the loan amount on the lower of the appraised value or the contract price, which is why a low appraisal forces a renegotiation. Factual framing, not a statistic.
  - Status: confirmed

- **Event logistics:** Kale Huddle hosted by A&N Mortgage on North Elston; Thursday, 3:30-6:00 PM; free with limited seats; registration at kalehuddle.com; food, drinks, and raffle prizes on site.
