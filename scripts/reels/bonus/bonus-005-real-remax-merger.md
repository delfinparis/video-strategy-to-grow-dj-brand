## Script (Spoken)

Breaking realtor news. Real Brokerage and RE/MAX announced an eight hundred eighty million dollar deal this morning. Every headline reads the same: "Real acquires RE/MAX." Every headline is wrong.

Read the actual deal.

The RE/MAX balloon stays on every sign. The franchises stay. Dave Liniger -- the founder, who controls thirty-eight percent of the votes -- backed the deal and stayed in. Motto Mortgage keeps its name. The brand on your card tomorrow is the same brand that was on it yesterday.

So what actually changed?

RE/MAX just got thirty-three thousand new colleagues -- the Real agents -- selling alongside them. And RE/MAX just got a free tech department -- reZEN, the AI tools, the back office Real spent five years building. Same balloon. Same sign. Same brand. New horsepower under the hood.

That's not an acquisition. That's a hiring story.

My read: the only people who lost today are the recruiters at every cloud brokerage who built their whole pitch on "RE/MAX agents are underserved on tech." That pitch just went in a drawer. It's not coming out again.

## Source

- Real Brokerage / RE/MAX Holdings merger announcement (2026-04-27)
- Combined entity: "Real REMAX Group"
- Coverage: Inman, Bloomberg, HousingWire, Real Estate News, Storeys, FranchiseWire, Real & RE/MAX investor releases

## Key Facts for Reference

- Enterprise value: ~$880M (≈7x fully synergized 2025 EBITDA)
- Pro forma combined: ~180K agents in 120+ countries; ~100K+ in US/Canada; ~8,500 franchisees; ~$2.3B annual revenue
- Real brings ~33K agents; RE/MAX brings ~145K — RE/MAX is ~80% of combined headcount
- Cash/stock election: $13.80 cash or 5.152 Real REMAX Group shares per RMAX share — **cash capped at $60–80M total** (under 10% of deal value); the rest is stock
- Dave Liniger (RE/MAX co-founder) controls ~38% of RMAX voting power; agreed to vote in favor and elected stock — i.e., he is rolling, not exiting
- Tamir Poleg (Real CEO) becomes Chairman & CEO of Real REMAX Group; HQ Miami, ops continue in Denver
- Brands: RE/MAX, Real, and Motto Mortgage all continue under existing brands; franchisees keep autonomy
- Agent-facing tech: reZEN transaction platform + AI tools + Real Wallet rolled out to RE/MAX network
- $30M annual run-rate cost synergies projected, mostly realized by 2027
- Close expected H2 2026, subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals

- The "hunters became the hunted" angle (Real + RE/MAX both on retention defense for 18 months → poaching window for every other broker) is the natural Day 2 follow-up if this script hits.
