## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B):**
1. "Agents one to three years in. There's a kind of stuck nobody warned you about, and it doesn't show up in your numbers." [hook_family: 8 Cohort Callout | emotion: recognition]
2. "You had a great week, then a week where you couldn't do one thing for your business. That gap isn't discipline." [hook_family: 1 Mirror | emotion: relief]
3. "The thing that ends most real estate careers isn't the crash. It's getting used to the crash." [hook_family: 2 Sacred Cow | emotion: dread -- strongest line in the script, currently spent at 0:45]

**Pattern interrupt:** Gesture-On-Beat (move 7) -- one sharp point off-frame on the cohort callout, then straight back to the lens. Cohort hooks need a physical "yes, you" and this is the cheapest one on a selfie stick. Rotates off Push-In, One Prop, and Walk-Toward across the batch.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): the cohort callout self-selects the viewer in the first two words, then the "doesn't show up in your numbers" clause opens the gap. Naming an unnamed feeling is the strongest form of the curiosity gap because the viewer already has the data and no label for it.
- Share driver (Berger): "not the crash, the getting used to it" is the sendable line, and emotional-arousal content that names a private state outperforms tactical content on private sends. High Social Currency for the sender.
- Retention move (MrBeast): the two-beat silence after "why bother" is the pattern interrupt in the audio, and "Here's the turn" at 0:28 re-hooks at the drift point.
- The human (Chris Do): this is the batch's identity script per Rule 10.7. The close is a question about the viewer's own last Thursday, not a pitch, which is what earns the follow rather than the click.
- Guardrail (Hormozi): no engagement ask, no tactic promised and undelivered. The action is honest reflection plus one door.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Welsh flags that this script has no portable takeaway, which makes it the least repeatable format in the batch and the hardest to run weekly. His experiment: cut a version that keeps the emotional open but adds one concrete diagnostic at 0:45, something like counting how many days this month you opened the CRM before noon, so the viewer leaves with a measurement rather than a mood. Hypothesis: saves rise, completion holds, and the follow-rate advantage of the pure identity script partly erodes. Worth learning which trade this audience prefers.
