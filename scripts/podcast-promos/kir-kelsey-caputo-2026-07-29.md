Kelsey Caputo -- top 2% at Berkshire Hathaway, one of NAR's 30 Under 30 class of 2026 -- came on Keeping It Real to talk about where AI fails at real estate valuations, why local knowledge becomes your edge as AI gets more popular, and how to use AI to actually prep for every client appointment.

Here's what we solved. The Florida Keys has boating access laws, seawall grades, and short-term rental zoning that no AI training data includes. When a buyer walks in with a ChatGPT number, you don't fight it. You explain what it couldn't see. That's your advantage.

Here's the one thing. Before your next appointment -- buyer or seller -- open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, any of them. Type this: "What objections would a buyer have about this property?" Pick the ones you're weakest on. Prep your response. Kelsey does this before every single appointment.
