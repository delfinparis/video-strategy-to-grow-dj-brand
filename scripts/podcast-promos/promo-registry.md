# Podcast Promo Registry — Hype Machine Dedup Ledger

Every podcast-promo walk-and-talk gets a row here. The point: across 700+ KIR
episodes, the same takeaway ("stay in touch with past clients") will surface over
and over. Before finalizing a promo, check your planned **Hook** and **Tip**
against the columns below. If the tip already aired, pick a different tactic from
the same episode. See [`../../docs/series/podcast-promo-hype-machine.md`](../../docs/series/podcast-promo-hype-machine.md) → Dedup Protocol.

For Coffee Talk, this stacks on top of the stat registry in
`coffeetalk-episode-registry/registry.md` — stat dedup applies first.
