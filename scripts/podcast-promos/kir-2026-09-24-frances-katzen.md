## Council Review

**Hook mechanism:** Family 7 Forbidden. "She told her client not to buy" is the most taboo act in buyer representation -- it means killing your own commission on purpose. Opens a loop: what happened? (twelve referrals -- viewers have to know why.) Heath's curiosity gap: the forbidden act and the payoff are stated in the same two-beat exchange, making the mechanism (how does not buying produce referrals?) the question that holds the screen.

**Share driver:** Berger's practical value: the tip (monthly market report with your actual opinion) is actionable and specific enough to do this week. The hook story (told a client not to buy, got 12 referrals) is inherently shareable -- every agent knows the fear of killing a deal, and the outcome inverts it. An agent who watches this could text the core line ("Not just stats. Her actual position on what to do.") to their team as a model for their own report.

**Retention move:** The loop-back structure holds. Hook opens with the forbidden act. THE POINT delivers the payoff (twelve referrals) and then the repeatable tactic behind it. PAYOFF closes with a direct action. The viewer who almost swiped away at the TENSION beat (credentialing line) stays because they need the answer to how "not buying" became twelve referrals.

**2-3 scroll-stop variants to A/B test:**

1. *(Family 7 Forbidden, current)* "She told her client not to buy." -- 8 words, forbidden-act loss frame, opens the loop cleanly.
2. *(Family 4 Data Reveal)* "Twelve referrals. One honest conversation." -- 5 words, leads with the outcome; risk is the number lands flat before the context arrives. Use if the current hook underperforms on TikTok where pattern interrupts hit harder.
3. *(Family 1 Mirror)* "You're afraid to tell your client the truth. She isn't." -- 10 words; confronts the viewer's own avoidance. Slightly long for 1.5s but punchy as a caption-native test.

**Single dissent:** THE POINT links the "don't buy" honest advice to the monthly market report tactic as two expressions of the same operating principle. That connection is logical but not explicitly made in the analysis JSON -- the 12 referrals came from a specific client conversation (@ 15:45) and the market report is a separate tactic (@ 08:00). An agent who listens expecting Frances to say "I got those referrals because of my market report" may feel the promo slightly overpromised the causal chain. A/B test: try a version where THE POINT opens with the market report tactic and drops the referral count entirely, using the referral story only in the hook. Measure whether completion rate changes when the tip is the first reveal rather than the third beat.
