## Council Review

### Scroll-Stop Variants (spoken, mapped to hook family)

**1. Family 2 (Contrarian Claim) -- current version:**
"Your clients aren't trapped by a 3% rate. That rate is their down payment strategy for the next home."
*Why it works:* Inverts the dominant agent belief in the first seven words. "Down payment strategy" is a surprising reframe that demands explanation and keeps the viewer for Act 2.

**2. Family 7 (Forbidden) -- alternative:**
"There's a conversation agents aren't having with their best past clients. It's the one that turns a stuck homeowner into a move-up buyer."
*Why it works:* Implies the viewer is missing something they should already know. Curiosity gap + mild shame loop.

**3. Family 4 (Data Reveal) -- alternative:**
"Austin Clarence says a bridge loan lets your buyer purchase before they sell, with zero contingencies. Most agents haven't mentioned it yet."
*Why it works:* Leads with the tactical fact, names the authority, closes with the problem (agents silent on a tool that exists). Retention built on "wait, is my lender doing this?"

### Why It Works (one line)
Hook inverts the stuck-client belief with a concrete reframe; the tip is one specific question agents can deliver in the next phone call.

### Single Dissent to A/B Test
The hook's "down payment strategy" is memorable but abstract. Test it against the Facebook version ("Your past clients with low rates aren't stuck") to see whether the concrete agent-action framing or the reframe language drives more episode link clicks.
