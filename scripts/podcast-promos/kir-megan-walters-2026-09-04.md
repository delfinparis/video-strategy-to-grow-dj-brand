## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, mapped to hook families):**

1. *(Data Reveal, Family 4 -- current)* "This agent closed 66 deals at 28." -- Why it works: concrete number + qualifier opens a hard loop the tip immediately pays off. The 28 signals outsized achievement (youth + volume = proof); the 66 is specific enough to arrest attention.
2. *(System Indictment, Family 3)* "Most agents knock the FSBO door. That's why most of them get hung up on." -- Puts the viewer on the right side of the room before the tip lands. Higher friction, higher signal if the audience is already skeptical of FSBO prospecting.
3. *(Forbidden, Family 7)* "Stop buying leads. This agent made the leaderboard without paying for one." -- Addresses the lead-gen spend objection directly. Strongest for agents currently spending on lead platforms.

**Why it works (current v3):** The Data Reveal hook creates a curiosity gap (how?) that the tip closes without delay. The loop-back ("Zero bought leads") in the PAYOFF seals the gap and reinforces the hook's implicit promise. The "But she never knocks" sentence is the turn -- subverts the expected FSBO behavior and earns the next three lines.

**Dissent to A/B test:** Variant 2 (System Indictment). If the Data Reveal hook plateaus in the first three days, test "Most agents knock the FSBO door" -- the friction framing puts the viewer on the right side of the room earlier and may outperform on the FSBOs-aware segment.
