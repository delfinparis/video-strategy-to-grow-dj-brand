Your lender can close a financed deal in 10 days. Most agents don't know that, and the ones who do are winning every multiple-offer situation right now.

That's from Zach Geisendorfer -- he leads a $600M team at FAM Real Estate Group in Southern California -- and he's on this week's Keeping It Real. We got into three things. The mega open house system that consistently fills his pipeline with qualified clients. How his team scaled too fast, hit a wall, then rebuilt with a two-week boot camp called Fam Camp that gets new agents into production fast. And the offer strategy that wins in competitive situations without writing the highest price.

Here's what we actually solved. Open houses fail because agents show up and wait. Zach calls 50 neighbors before every event, knows the property cold, and promotes it aggressively -- and the pipeline fills. On offers, most agents write standard 30-day closes and wonder why they keep losing.

Here's the one thing you can do on your next offer. Before you submit, call your lender and confirm you can close in 10 days. Then -- after you submit -- have your lender personally call the listing agent to confirm your buyer's financing. Sellers want certainty more than the highest number. Give them both.
