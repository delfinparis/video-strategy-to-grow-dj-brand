### X-Y-Z

On this week's Keeping It Real, Carrie and I got into why AI will actually create more luxury buyers than it replaces agents, how to win high-end pitches by selling lifestyle over square footage, and what it means to run your business like a small media company.

### WHAT WE SOLVED

Agents keep asking whether they should be scared of AI. Carrie's answer: not if your marketing is something AI can't do. If you're posting generic content, AI does it better and cheaper. If you're the hyper-local expert who knows what the algorithm doesn't -- you become more valuable, not less.

### THE TIP

So here's what you do this week. Find one thing you know about your market that no AI can know. A school boundary change, a new development that isn't public yet, a listing coming that hasn't hit the MLS. Write two sentences about what it means for buyers and sellers in your area. Post it. That's the content only you can make. Carrie calls that being a small media company. AI pulls averages. You know the street.

### CLOSE

Go listen to this week's Keeping It Real with Carrie McCormick. Link is in my bio. Then pick your one hyper-local story and post it.
