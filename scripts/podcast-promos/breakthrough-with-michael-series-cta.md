## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B):**
1. "Nobody at your brokerage is going to ask you why you stopped picking up the phone." [hook_family: 4 System Indictment | emotion: recognition + quiet anger]
2. "The agents applying for our free coaching session aren't the ones failing. That's the part I didn't expect." [hook_family: 6 Named Stakes | emotion: curiosity]
3. "Your brokerage has a plan for your contracts and no plan at all for the week you stop answering your phone." [hook_family: 4 System Indictment | emotion: recognition]

**Pattern interrupt:** Walk-Toward (move 4) -- D.J. walking straight at the lens rather than the side-profile stroll. The forward motion matches an indictment delivered to the viewer's face. Ep. 2 CTA used The Stop, so this rotates.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): the indictment opens a gap the viewer already feels but has never heard named. Curiosity comes from "so who does ask?" and the segment is the answer. Concrete beats abstract: "haven't opened your CRM in three weeks" is a specific image, not a mood.
- Share driver (Berger): Social Currency plus practical value. An agent sends this to another agent as "this is us" (Simon's test) because the CRM line is a private behavior said out loud.
- Retention move (MrBeast): the promise lands at second three, the credential stack is a mini re-hook at 0:12, and the real payoff, that the winners are the ones applying, arrives at 0:30 right before drift.
- Clarity (Miller): one problem (nobody's asking), one plan (one agent a month, free), one CTA (apply). The viewer is the hero; D.J. is the guide with a door.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Hormozi argues the surprise beat is the actual hook and it's buried thirty seconds deep. His experiment: cut a version that opens cold on "The agents applying for our free coaching session aren't the ones failing" and moves the brokerage indictment to the middle. Hypothesis: three-second retention drops slightly because the line needs more setup, but completion and application clicks both rise because the counterintuitive claim carries the whole video.
