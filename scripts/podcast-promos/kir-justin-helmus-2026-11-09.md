**Why it works:**
- Hook mechanism: Sacred Cow + loss framing. "Waiting for clients" is a category behavior almost every agent defaults to, so naming it as a cost implicates the viewer directly in the first three words. "It's costing you deals" closes the loop in four more.
- Share driver: "Pick one street you want to own in five years" is a sendable assignment. An agent screenshots it and sends it to their team because it's concrete and startable in under an hour. The specificity is the share mechanism.
- Retention move: "Start your street list tonight" loops back to the hook's cost frame -- the viewer hears the promise resolved (here is what you do instead of waiting). The loop closes in the last line.
