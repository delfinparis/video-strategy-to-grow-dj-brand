That's Sam Burke. He's a 2026 NAR 30 Under 30 honoree and a top 1% broker at John L. Scott in Snohomish, Washington. On this week's Keeping It Real, Sam and I got into how he built a business from scratch to top 1% before turning 30, how a tiny YouTube channel outperforms a big one when you answer the right buyer questions, and the cold-calling system he uses to find investment properties before they ever hit the market.

### WHAT WE SOLVED

A lot of agents think they need a massive following to get leads from video. Sam proves they're wrong. He's not chasing views -- he's answering the exact questions buyers in his market type into search. 200 subscribers, real clients. The size of your channel isn't the point.

### THE TIP

Here's the one thing you can do this week. Sam calls it a buy box. Write down exactly what you'd want to buy: a specific neighborhood, a price range, a property type. Find owners of properties that match that description. Call them -- not to list their house, just to ask if they've ever thought about selling. That's how he builds his investment portfolio while he's building his business.

### CLOSE

Go listen to this week's Keeping It Real with Sam Burke. Link is in my bio. Then write your buy box and make three calls.
