Tim and I spent this week's Coffee Talk on that gap. Why the room with the biggest crowd has the least competition. The three excuses that keep you out. And a three-move method to own your local corner in about a year.

Here's the one that stings. NAR's 2024 survey puts agents on TikTok at fewer than two in ten. Almost everyone else is crowded onto Facebook, shouting at the same people. Before you write it off, open TikTok and search your town plus the words real estate. Count how many agents come up. Then listen to this week's Coffee Talk and film your first answer. An empty stage in front of a packed room. The whole price is looking silly for thirty seconds.
