## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (three spoken first-line options mapped to hook families):**

1. "Every October, your competition stops calling." (Family 2 -- Contrarian Claim -- loss framing via the competitor's behavior, challenges Q4 wind-down belief) [USED]
2. "You're already losing spring deals and it's only October." (Family 1 -- Mirror -- direct indictment of viewer; higher heat) [A/B test]
3. "The agents winning spring started in October." (Family 2 -- Contrarian Claim -- positive inverse framing; lower heat) [lower-urgency test]

**Why it works:**
- Hook mechanism: Contrarian Claim + time-framing. October as a performance decision point is counterintuitive and creates immediate curiosity.
- Share driver: The three-step tip (pull closings, find three easiest deals, call this week) is specific, physical, and sendable. An agent DMs this to a struggling colleague.
- Retention move: "Separation Season" is a named concept. Named concepts are quotable, memorable, and make the episode feel distinct.

**Single dissent to A/B test next:** Try the Mirror variant ("You're already losing spring deals and it's only October") -- a direct indictment of the viewer tends to outperform pointing at the competition, because the loss is personal and immediate rather than observed. Test as a second take on the same filming day.
