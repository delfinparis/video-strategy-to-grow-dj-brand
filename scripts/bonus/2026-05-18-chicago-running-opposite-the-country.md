# Half the Country's Prices Are Cooling. Chicago Isn't. -- 3 Conversations to Have This Week

> **WOW: Tactical specificity + earned observation from D.J. Paris: half the U.S. is softening. Chicago's running about 5x the national pace -- in the other direction. Your past clients don't know that, and they're reading national headlines.**

**Guest:** D.J. Paris | **Avatar:** All | **Pillar:** Market Intelligence
**Post Date:** Monday, May 18, 2026 (Bonus -- timely market script)

> **Strongest Line:** "Your past clients aren't reading Case-Shiller. They're reading headlines that say 'prices stall' and assuming that's their block too. It isn't."

## Script

### HOOK (0:00-0:03)
Half the country's home prices just went negative year over year. Chicago's running about five times the national rate. The other way.

### SETUP (0:03-0:12)
I'm D.J. Paris -- Keeping It Real Podcast, Kale Realty.

Case-Shiller says national price growth has basically stopped. More than half the metros they track are in year-over-year decline. Chicago isn't. We're running opposite -- and not by a hair.

### INSIGHT (0:12-0:38)
So here's what you should be doing this week. Three conversations.

One -- past sellers from twenty-two and twenty-three. The ones who told you they wished they'd waited. Send them a screenshot of their zip code's last twelve months. One line: "Wanted you to know what your house is worth today." That's not a pitch. That's a favor.

Two -- stuck buyers. The ones still waiting for the crash that's coming everywhere except here. Send them the headline. "Half the country's softening. Chicago's not. Your window's closing." Then ask if they want to look this weekend.

Three -- your sphere. Anyone who's mentioned moving in the last twelve months. The line is: "If you're selling in the next year, the data says go now, while we're outperforming."

### REFRAME (0:38-0:50)
Most agents won't make these calls. They figure everyone already knows. Nobody knows. I've been doing this fifteen years -- your past clients aren't reading Case-Shiller. They're reading headlines that say "prices stall" and assuming that's their block too. It isn't. Be the one who tells them what's actually happening on their street.

### CTA (0:50-0:60)
Five past sellers. Five stuck buyers. Five sphere contacts. Fifteen messages, before you close your laptop tonight. Not posts. Messages. I've watched agents do this in May and write three extra deals out of it by August. Every time. Go.
