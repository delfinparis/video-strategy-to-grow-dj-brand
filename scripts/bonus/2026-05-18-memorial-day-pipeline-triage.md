# Memorial Day's One Week Away -- 3 Calls Every Agent Should Make Before Friday

> **WOW: Tactical specificity + earned observation from D.J. Paris: every lead in your pipeline either closes this week, books for fall, or gets cut. The agents who finish spring strong aren't working harder -- they're carrying fewer ghosts.**

**Guest:** D.J. Paris | **Avatar:** All | **Pillar:** Sales Process

> **Strongest Line:** "The agents who finish spring strong aren't working harder. They're carrying fewer ghosts."

## Script

### HOOK (0:00-0:03)
Memorial Day's one week away. Every lead in your pipeline either closes this week, books for fall, or gets cut. No fourth bucket.

### SETUP (0:03-0:12)
I'm D.J. Paris -- Keeping It Real Podcast, Kale Realty.

Nobody warns new agents about this: real estate has a real cooldown that starts Memorial Day weekend. Showings drop. Inboxes go quiet. The leads you're "still working" in May? Half won't be back to a serious conversation until fall.

### INSIGHT (0:12-0:38)
So what should you actually be doing? Three calls before Friday.

One -- every active buyer who's been "looking since March." Ask them straight: "Are we writing an offer in the next two weeks, or picking back up after Labor Day?" Don't soften it. They book the showing, or they release you. Both are wins.

Two -- every seller you've been "talking to about listing this spring." Same question. "On market by June first, or a fall listing?" If they hedge (and they will), they're fall.

Three -- your "thinking about it" pile. The ones ghosting since February (don't pretend you don't have one). One text: "If anything's changed, let me know. If not, I'll circle back in the fall." Get the no. Free up the space.

### REFRAME (0:38-0:50)
This isn't aggressive. It's clear. Why don't most agents do this? They think "no" is the worst outcome. It isn't. The worst outcome is a maybe that drags all summer -- eating the calendar, the follow-up, the mental energy. The agents who finish spring strong aren't working harder. They're carrying fewer ghosts.

### CTA (0:50-0:60)
Pull up your CRM tonight. Five active buyers. Five spring sellers. Five ghosters. Three buckets: close, fall, or cut. Send the texts before Friday. By Memorial Day weekend you'll either have a sharper pipeline -- or you'll actually enjoy the long weekend. Go.
