# We Walked Into a Trivia Night and Left With Three New Friends

> **Note:** written by D.J., filed as-is. Off-lane personal story with a realtor turn at the end.
> No gate, no CTA. Judged on sends and dwell, not leads. Runs ~70 seconds at 173 spoken words,
> which is over the 22-30s walk-and-talk cap on purpose (same exception as the neuroplasticity
> bonus). See [`../../docs/content-pillars.md`](../../docs/content-pillars.md).

## Shareable Moment

## Spoken Script (as written)

Last night my wife had an idea. Let's walk to Mrs. Murphy's, a bar, for trivia night. We had never done this before. When we walked in, my wife wisely asked the host if they could place us on an existing team, and they did.

We met three men who have been close friends since college and we had the best time with them. They're nerdy like us and couldn't have been friendlier. Just awesome dudes.

At the end of the evening, we won by the way, they said, "you're on the team, come back next week." And bam, just like that, we made three new friends.

At one point during the night they asked what we did for work. Now if I were a practicing realtor, I would have asked for their socials, friended them, and maybe sent a nice IM. Hey, it was great meeting you, see you next week.

That's how easy it can be to meet people. There's trivia nights where you live. Show up and see what happens.

## Production Note

**The open is warm-up.** "Last night my wife had an idea" is a soft first line for a platform that
decides in three seconds. If you want the sharper version without changing anything else, front-load
the result and let the story back into it: *"We walked into a bar we'd never been to and left with
three new friends."* Everything after that stays exactly as written.

**Pattern interrupt:** Location Cold-Open. If you can shoot the walk-up to the bar, or even a
sidewalk stop with the sign in frame, that's the visual open. Second choice is The Stop on
"and bam."

**Register.** This works because it isn't a tactic, it's a Tuesday. Do not add a business framing to
the front. The realtor turn arrives late on purpose and should sound like an afterthought, because
that's what makes it land.

**No CTA, no gate.** The close is "show up and see what happens" and nothing after it.
