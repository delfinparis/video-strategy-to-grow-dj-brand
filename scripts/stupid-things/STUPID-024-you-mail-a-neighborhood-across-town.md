# You Mail A Neighborhood Across Town

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Your easiest neighborhood is the one you live in."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 90 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 16 words
Hey Realtors, mailing postcards across town while your own building doesn't know you is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 24 words
You already know what sold in your building and what it went for. Nobody else is mailing them, and somebody moves out every year.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 10 words
Here's the solution - start with the building you live in.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 31 words
Four times a year, send your neighbors what sold, what it went for, and what that means for their unit. Go to the board meetings. Say hi in the mail room.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 9 words
Your easiest neighborhood is the one you live in.

## Production Notes
- Location Cold-Open on the CLOSE line.
- "Nobody else is mailing them" is D.J.'s own experience in his building, told on the Gia Devenyi episode, stated as the general case.
- Honest limit, kept out of the script: direct mail is how only 2 percent of sellers found their agent (NAR 2025, Exhibit 7-2). The point is it costs nothing in your own building.
- D.J. does not practice real estate (Rule 6). SAY THIS is the viewer's words or steps, framed as "say this" or as orders, never as D.J.'s own deals.
- Receipt posture carries over from the earlier cut; see Data Source. No new statistic was added in the re-cut.
- Built to D.J.'s own format (STUPID-030, 2026-09-29): "Hey Realtors, [the thing] is really stupid.", the why as cause and effect, "Here's the solution", the exact words or steps in first person, one closing line.
- Friction points at the behavior, never at the agent. Rule 9.4.

## Data Source
- **Claim:** "Nobody else sends them anything." - Status: D.J.'s own observation, four years in his building with no agent mail, told on the Gia Devenyi episode, Keeping It Real 2024-11-22.
- **Fabrication audit:** No statistic is spoken. The 2 percent direct-mail figure is disclosed in Production Notes, not used to sell the idea.
