# You Mail A Neighborhood Across Town

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Every year, somebody in there moves out."

## Script (30 seconds max, 60 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5) - 6 words
You mail a neighborhood across town.

### VERDICT (0:01.5-0:03.5) - 5 words
That's really stupid. Here's why.

### TENSION (0:03.5-0:08) - 9 words
Your own neighbors have no idea what you do.

### THE POINT (0:08-0:22) - 26 words
You already know your building. You know what sold and what it closed at. Nobody else sends them anything. Every year, somebody in there moves out.

### PAYOFF (0:22-0:30) - 14 words
Four times a year, mail your building. Show what sold and for how much.

**Word count:** 60 spoken words (60-70, 72 max; counted, not estimated)

**What got cut:** D.J.'s own story: four years in a new Wicker Park building and not one piece of mail from any agent. It's first person, so it lives in the LinkedIn caption where D.J. can tell it himself.

## Production Notes
- Location Cold-Open outside D.J.'s own building if he's comfortable showing it; any building works.
- "Nobody else sends them anything" is D.J.'s own experience stated as the scene. Do not add a number to it.
- Honest limit, kept out of the script but true: direct mail is how only 2 percent of sellers found their agent (NAR 2025, Exhibit 7-2). The point is that it costs nothing in your own building, not that mail is powerful.
- Built to the STUPID-004 format model: two flat hook sentences, one-line scene, one fact per sentence ending on the cost, the fix as orders.
- Friction points at the behavior, never at the agent. Rule 9.4.

## Data Source
- **Claim:** "Nobody else sends them anything." - Status: D.J.'s own observation, four years in his building with no agent mail, told on the Gia Devenyi episode, Keeping It Real 2024-11-22.
- **Fabrication audit:** No statistic is spoken. The 2 percent direct-mail figure is disclosed in Production Notes, not used to sell the idea.
