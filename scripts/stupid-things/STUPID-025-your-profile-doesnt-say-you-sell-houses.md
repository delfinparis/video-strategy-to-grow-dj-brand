# Your Profile Doesn't Say You Sell Houses

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "They can't tell what you do."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 87 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 15 words
Hey Realtors, having a profile that doesn't say you sell real estate is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 23 words
When a friend of a friend looks you up, they see a sunset and your kids, and they can't tell what you do.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 10 words
Here's the solution - fix your bio today, in plain words.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 33 words
Put Realtor and your neighborhood right in your name or bio. Something like Chicago Realtor, Logan Square. Make sure the phone number works. Then check it on your phone, like a stranger would.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 6 words
Make it obvious in one second.
