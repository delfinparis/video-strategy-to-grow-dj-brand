## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
Your profile doesn't say you sell houses.

A sunset cover photo. Your kids in the profile picture. Beautiful. Nothing about real estate.

Courtney Hatfield asked it on my show: "When I go to your page, your Facebook page, your Instagram page, how do I know that you sell real estate?"

Here's why it matters. Somebody hears your name, maybe from a friend, and looks you up. They find a family picture and can't tell what you do. So they call the agent who made it obvious.

Today: put "Realtor" and your neighborhood in your name or bio, in plain words. Make sure the phone number works. Then look at it on your phone, logged out, like a stranger would.
