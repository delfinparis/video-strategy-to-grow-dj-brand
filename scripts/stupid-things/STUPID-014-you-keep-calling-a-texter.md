# You Keep Calling A Texter

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "She thinks you disappeared, and you think she went cold."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 88 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 14 words
Hey Realtors, leaving voicemails for a client who only reads texts is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 21 words
If you never ask how she wants to hear from you, she thinks you disappeared, and you think she went cold.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 13 words
Here's the solution - ask at the very first meeting, and write it down.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 31 words
Ask three things on day one. Do you want a call, a text, or an email? What hours are okay? And do you listen to voicemail, or should I skip it?

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 9 words
Then talk to them the way they actually listen.

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You keep calling a client who only reads texts.

You call. You leave a voicemail. She never calls back. So you decide she went cold.

She didn't. She reads every text. You picked the one thing she ignores, and now she thinks you're the one who disappeared.

Ask at the first meeting. Call, text or email? What hours are okay? Do you listen to voicemail, or should I not bother? Who else should I copy? Write the answers in their contact record, not on a sticky note. Then do it their way.

Thirty seconds of questions. A whole deal of less friction.
