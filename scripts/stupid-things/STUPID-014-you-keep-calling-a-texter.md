# You Keep Calling A Texter

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "She didn't go cold. You picked the one thing she ignores."

## Script (30 seconds max, 62 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5) - 5 words
You keep calling a texter.

### VERDICT (0:01.5-0:03.5) - 5 words
That's really stupid. Here's why.

### TENSION (0:03.5-0:08) - 9 words
You call. You leave voicemails. She never calls back.

### THE POINT (0:08-0:22) - 27 words
She reads every text. She didn't go cold. You picked the one thing she ignores. Now she thinks you're the one who disappeared. Nobody ever asked her.

### PAYOFF (0:22-0:30) - 16 words
Ask at the first meeting. Call, text or email? Then write it down and do it.

**Word count:** 62 spoken words (60-70, 72 max; counted, not estimated)

**What got cut:** The other questions to ask at the first meeting: what hours are okay, and who else should be copied. They are in the LinkedIn caption.

## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You keep calling a client who only reads texts.

You call. You leave a voicemail. She never calls back. So you decide she went cold.

She didn't. She reads every text. You picked the one thing she ignores, and now she thinks you're the one who disappeared.

Ask at the first meeting. Call, text or email? What hours are okay? Do you listen to voicemail, or should I not bother? Who else should I copy? Write the answers in their contact record, not on a sticky note. Then do it their way.

Thirty seconds of questions. A whole deal of less friction.
