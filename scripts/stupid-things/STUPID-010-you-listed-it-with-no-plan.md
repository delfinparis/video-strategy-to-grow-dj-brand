## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You listed it with no plan.

No open house booked. No video shot. It went up on a Tuesday afternoon and everyone waited.

The first three days are when every buyer searching in that area gets an email about your house. That crowd never gets that email again. If nothing is ready when they look, you spent your best shot on nothing, and by week three you're asking the seller to cut the price.

Run the same plan every time. List it Thursday night so it hits Friday morning emails. Open house Saturday. Post the video Sunday. Monday morning, look at the numbers: saves, showings booked, who came to the open house. Tell the seller before you list which numbers you'll show them Monday.
