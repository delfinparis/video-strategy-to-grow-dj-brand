# Going Quiet After The Contract

> **WOW: The first cut of this script opened on an effect with no cause and closed on a line that sounds true and teaches nothing. This one names the behavior, says it is stupid, and spends its middle on the one thing the client is actually experiencing: they cannot tell whether two hours or two days is normal, because nobody ever told them. RECEIPT: the bank carried this entry as `confirmed (NAR, 2026)`; the cited NAR page is from April 4, 2024, ranks agent-selection qualities as experience 21% / honesty 19% / reputation 15%, and has no responsiveness ranking and no Gen X or Boomer split. The bank was corrected to `needed` on 2026-09-10 and this script speaks no statistic.**

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** Thursday, September 10, 2026

## Shareable Moment
> "They start every text with sorry to bug you."

## Script (~30 seconds, 78 words)

### HOOK (0:00–0:01.5) — 7 words
Going quiet after the contract is stupid.

### TENSION (0:01.5–0:06.5) — 12 words
You answered their texts in four minutes. Now you take two days.

### THE POINT (0:06.5–0:21.5) — 37 words
Your client can't tell if two hours is normal or two days, because nobody told them. So they stop asking, and they start every text with sorry to bug you. That's a client who won't refer you.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5–0:30) — 22 words
At the signing table, say it out loud. Every Friday, and four hours on anything urgent. Then they never have to guess.

**Word count:** 78 spoken words (68-84; counted, not estimated)

**What got cut:** The bank's swap has two halves and one survives at 78 words: the sentence said out loud at the table stayed, the "put a recurring Friday task in your phone" half came out. Also cut, deliberately, is the first cut's closing line, "Silence can't be a breach until you name the standard." It was the most quotable line in the script and it was doing no work. An agent cannot act on it, and at speaking speed most people cannot parse it.

## Production Notes
- **This is the plain-language re-cut.** The first version opened on "Your client apologizes before every text now," which states an effect and hides the cause, and it closed on an aphorism. Both are banned under Rule 5's comprehension rule. If an edit drifts back toward either, that is the failure the rule exists to stop.
- The verdict is the hook and it is said once. The payoff belongs to the fix.
- NEEDS RECEIPT posture. No statistic is spoken. "Four minutes," "two days," "two hours," "every Friday," "four hours" are the scene and the promise the agent is being told to make, not cited research.
- `target: sideways`. The friction points at the practice of never publishing a standard. The client is not treated as needy and the agent is not called lazy.
- The other half of the swap, for comments only if a viewer asks: put the recurring Friday task in your phone before you leave the table. Keep it out of the script.
- Not a brokerage-economics script. If it drifts toward "your broker should give you a transaction coordinator," pull it back to the behavior.
- Evergreen. Nothing here decays.
