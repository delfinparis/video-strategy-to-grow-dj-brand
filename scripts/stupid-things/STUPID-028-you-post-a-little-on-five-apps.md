# You Post A Little On Five Apps

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Video works. Doing five things halfway doesn't."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 88 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 13 words
Hey Realtors, posting a little bit on five different apps is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 23 words
If nothing gets real effort, you never learn what works, so after six months you quit and decide that video just doesn't work.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 15 words
Here's the solution - pick one app and stick with it for the next six months.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 30 words
Pick the one app where your clients actually are, not the one you like. Post there three times a week. Count conversations, not views. Only then add a second app.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 7 words
Video works. Doing five things halfway doesn't.

## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You post a little on five apps. Same graphic everywhere, a few likes, no clients.

Levi Lascsak picked one platform on purpose, and his reason is the clearest I've heard: "you're going to put a 20% effort into five different platforms and you'll never truly master one."

The real cost isn't the hours. It's that you never learn what works, because nothing got enough effort to tell. Six months later you quit and decide video doesn't work. Video works. Doing five things halfway doesn't.

Pick one app for six months. Pick it based on where your clients are, not where you like to be. Post three times a week, at a pace you can keep on your worst week. Judge it by conversations, not views.
