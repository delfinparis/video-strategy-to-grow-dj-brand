## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You post a little on five apps. Same graphic everywhere, a few likes, no clients.

Levi Lascsak picked one platform on purpose, and his reason is the clearest I've heard: "you're going to put a 20% effort into five different platforms and you'll never truly master one."

The real cost isn't the hours. It's that you never learn what works, because nothing got enough effort to tell. Six months later you quit and decide video doesn't work. Video works. Doing five things halfway doesn't.

Pick one app for six months. Pick it based on where your clients are, not where you like to be. Post three times a week, at a pace you can keep on your worst week. Judge it by conversations, not views.
