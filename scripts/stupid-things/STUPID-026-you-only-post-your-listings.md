# You Only Post Your Listings

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "When they do move, they don't think of you."

## Script (30 seconds max, 60 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5) - 5 words
You only post your listings.

### VERDICT (0:01.5-0:03.5) - 5 words
That's really stupid. Here's why.

### TENSION (0:03.5-0:08) - 8 words
Just listed. Just sold. An award. Then repeat.

### THE POINT (0:08-0:22) - 28 words
Almost nobody following you is moving this month. So they scroll right past. They don't know anything about you. When they do move, they don't think of you.

### PAYOFF (0:22-0:30) - 14 words
Post about your life and your neighborhood most days. Post listings once a week.

## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You only post your listings. Just listed, just sold, an award, repeat.

Almost nobody following you is moving this month. So they scroll right past, and they never learn anything about you. When they finally do move, you're not the one they think of.

Rachel Adams Lee has the rule: 80 percent of what you post is who you are, what you're doing, why someone would want to hang out with you. Twenty percent is real estate. In her words, "people might find you because you're in real estate, but they stick around because of who you are and how you make them feel."

Post about your life and your neighborhood most days. Post listings once a week. And keep politics off the business page.
