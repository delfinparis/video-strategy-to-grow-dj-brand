# You Sat Behind The Dining Table

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "They leave without you learning a single name."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 87 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 15 words
Hey Realtors, sitting behind the dining table at your own open house is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 21 words
Everyone who walks in chose to spend their Saturday looking at houses, and they leave without you learning a single name.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 13 words
Here's the solution - stand by the door and greet everyone in five seconds.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 32 words
Say this. Hi, I'm the listing agent. What brought you in today? Then know three things about the house that aren't in the listing, and offer them what sold on this block.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 6 words
Best free lead you'll ever get.
