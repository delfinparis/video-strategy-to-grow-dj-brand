# You Skipped The Presentation For Your Cousin

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Because you're family, you get exactly what my other clients get."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 86 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 15 words
Hey Realtors, skipping the listing presentation because the seller is your cousin is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 23 words
If you skip it, you never talk about price, and when the first offer comes in low, you're fighting about money with family.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 12 words
Here's the solution - do the full presentation anyway, and tell them why.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 28 words
Tell them this. Because you're family, you get exactly what my other clients get. Same numbers, same paperwork, same plan. I don't want to guess with your money.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 8 words
Family deserves your best work, not your shortcuts.
