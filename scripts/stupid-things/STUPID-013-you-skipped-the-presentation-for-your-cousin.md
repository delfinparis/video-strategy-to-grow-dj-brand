# You Skipped The Presentation For Your Cousin

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Do the full presentation anyway."

## Script (30 seconds max, 65 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5) - 7 words
You skipped the presentation for your cousin.

### VERDICT (0:01.5-0:03.5) - 5 words
That's really stupid. Here's why.

### TENSION (0:03.5-0:08) - 9 words
You already know the house. You already know him.

### THE POINT (0:08-0:22) - 29 words
So you never talked about price or who does what. Then the first offer comes in low. Now you're fighting about money with family. You'll see him at Thanksgiving.

### PAYOFF (0:22-0:30) - 15 words
Do the full presentation anyway. Same numbers, same paperwork. Tell him why you're doing it.
