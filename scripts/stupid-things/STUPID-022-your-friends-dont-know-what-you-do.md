# Your Friends Don't Know What You Do

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "People remember a story. They forget a job title."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 85 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 16 words
Hey Realtors, just saying you're a Realtor when people ask what you do is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 19 words
They hear the job title, forget it by Monday, and next year they hire the agent they actually remember.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 11 words
Here's the solution - tell one real story from your week instead.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 30 words
Try something like this. I just helped a couple win a house after they lost out three times. They were so relieved. No pitch, just something true from your week.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 9 words
People remember a story. They forget a job title.

## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B).** The opener "Hey Realtors," and "is really stupid." are fixed; only the thing in the middle varies:
1. "Hey Realtors, just saying you're a Realtor is really stupid." - Berger emotion: curiosity.
2. "Hey Realtors, answering what you do with your job title is really stupid." - Berger emotion: curiosity.
3. "Hey Realtors, keeping your job a secret from your friends is really stupid." - Berger emotion: anxiety.

**Pattern interrupt:** Walk-Toward.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): it sounds like bad advice, so people stay to find out why.
- Share/save driver (Berger): "one real story, no pitch" is easy to try this weekend.
- Retention move (MrBeast): "next year they hire the agent they actually remember" is the stakes.

**The dissent:** Kane says the contrarian hook is strong enough to lead with alone. Test it with no WHY sentence before the solution.
