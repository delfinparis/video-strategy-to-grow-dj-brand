### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
Your friends don't know what you do.

At a party someone asks, you say "I'm a Realtor," and the conversation moves on. They heard it. By Monday they forgot it. Next year one of them sells a house, and they hire the agent they remember.

Sarah Stone has the rule I'd copy. She never leaves a social event without people knowing she's a Realtor, and she never pitches. She's not asking where you live or if you're moving. She just wants you to remember.

So next time, don't say the job title. Say what you did this week. One true story about a real deal. No pitch. Then once a year, tell everyone you know, plainly, that you'd be glad to help.
