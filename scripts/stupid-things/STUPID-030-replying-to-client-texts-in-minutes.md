# Replying To Client Texts In Minutes

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Set the right expectation from the start."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 95 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 14 words
Hey Realtors, replying to a client text within a few minutes is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 23 words
If you reply to a text in four minutes, you've trained the client to expect that same reply speed on every future question.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 14 words
Here's the solution - at the start of the relationship tell them your communication policy.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 37 words
From 8am to 8pm Monday through Friday I respond to every client text within two hours. I stop texting at 8pm so I can spend time with my family. Here's what I do on Saturdays and Sundays.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 7 words
Set the right expectation from the start.

## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B).** The opener "Hey Realtors," and "is really stupid." are fixed; only the thing in the middle varies:
1. "Hey Realtors, texting clients back in four minutes is really stupid." - Berger emotion: curiosity.
2. "Hey Realtors, answering client texts at 10pm is really stupid." - Berger emotion: anxiety.
3. "Hey Realtors, being available 24/7 is really stupid." - Berger emotion: anger.

**Pattern interrupt:** Walk-Toward.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): it calls something agents are proud of stupid, and they stay to find out why.
- Share/save driver (Berger): the policy is word for word. Agents copy it into their welcome email.
- Retention move (MrBeast): the first-person policy at about twenty seconds is the payoff the hook promised.

**The dissent:** Kane says variant 3 is the bigger scroll-stop because every agent has an opinion about being on call. It's broader and loses the specific four-minute picture. Test it.
