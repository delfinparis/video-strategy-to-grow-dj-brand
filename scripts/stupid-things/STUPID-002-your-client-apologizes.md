# Your Client Apologizes For Asking

> **WOW: The mistake is not slow replies, it is that the client was never told what normal looks like, so two hours and two days feel identical and they start pre-apologizing for needing their own agent. The turn is that silence cannot be a breach until a standard exists, which makes the fix one sentence said at the signing table instead of a personality change. RECEIPT CORRECTED: the bank carried this entry as `receipt: confirmed (NAR, 2026)` with the claim "responsiveness ranks among the qualities buyers value most; Gen X and Boomers rank it above process knowledge." The cited NAR page was published April 4, 2024 off the 2024 Generational Trends Report, lists agent-selection qualities as experience 21%, honesty and trustworthiness 19%, reputation 15%, friend or family 12%, and carries no responsiveness ranking and no Gen X or Boomer breakdown for it. The only responsiveness numbers findable elsewhere are a lead-vendor blog and a July 2026 UK estate-agent survey, neither citable here. So this script speaks no statistic and runs on the scene and the swap.**

## Script (~30 seconds, 81 words)

### HOOK (0:00–0:01.5) — 7 words
Your client apologizes before every text now.

### TENSION (0:01.5–0:06.5) — 13 words
Four minute replies while they shopped. Now their inspector question sits two days.

### THE POINT (0:06.5–0:21.5) — 40 words
The delay isn't what breaks it. Nobody told them what normal looks like, so two hours and two days feel the same, and they'd rather feel needy than ask twice. Silence can't be a breach until you name the standard.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5–0:30) — 21 words
Say the window out loud at the signing table. Fridays, plus four hours on anything urgent. Then nobody apologizes for asking.

**Why it should work:**

- Retention move (MrBeast): "they'd rather feel needy than ask twice" is the re-hook at roughly the fourteen-second mark, which is exactly where a 30-second cut sheds viewers, and it reframes the client rather than repeating the complaint.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Hormozi objects that the hook names a symptom, not a price. It costs the referral"), testing whether a named commercial cost out-saves the recognition hook with agents who do not yet see themselves in the scene.
