## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B):**
1. "You put it online and waited." - Family 1 Mirror, Berger emotion: anxiety.
2. "Every listing agent says the same sentence." - Family 4 System Indictment, Berger emotion: curiosity.
3. "Your buyer lives six blocks away and doesn't know." - Family 7 Forbidden, Berger emotion: curiosity.

**Pattern interrupt:** The Stop.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): it describes the default, so almost every listing agent recognizes it.
- Share/save driver (Berger): sellers share this to agents, which is the sideways share the lane wants.
- Retention move (MrBeast): "The buyer is out there" at about sixteen seconds turns the story from a complaint into a hunt.

**The dissent:** Kane says "You put it online and waited" is too mild to stop a scroll. Test variant 3, which gives the viewer a specific buyer to picture.
