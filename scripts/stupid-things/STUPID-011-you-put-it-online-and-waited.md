# You Put It Online And Waited

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "None of them went out and found the buyer."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 88 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 13 words
Hey Realtors, telling a seller you'll just put it online is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 20 words
Every agent who pitched that seller said the same thing, and none of them went out and found the buyer.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 15 words
Here's the solution - go find the buyer, and tell the seller how you'll do it.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 34 words
Before the appointment, write down who buys this exact house. Like the couple six blocks north who need a third bedroom. Find where people like them live now, and go tell ten of them.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 6 words
That's how you win the listing.

## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B).** The opener "Hey Realtors," and "is really stupid." are fixed; only the thing in the middle varies:
1. "Hey Realtors, telling a seller you'll just put it online is really stupid." - Berger emotion: curiosity.
2. "Hey Realtors, saying every agent will see it is really stupid." - Berger emotion: anger.
3. "Hey Realtors, waiting for the buyer to find your listing is really stupid." - Berger emotion: anxiety.

**Pattern interrupt:** The Stop.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): it names the exact sentence most listing agents say.
- Share/save driver (Berger): sellers share it to agents, the sideways share this lane wants.
- Retention move (MrBeast): "none of them went out and found the buyer" turns a habit into a gap.

**The dissent:** Eric Simon says "That's how you win the listing" oversells. Test a close that says "Tell the seller that plan."
