# You Say Your Contact List Is Overwhelming

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Fifty isn't too many."

## Script (30 seconds max, 63 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5) - 7 words
You say your contact list is overwhelming.

### VERDICT (0:01.5-0:03.5) - 5 words
That's really stupid. Here's why.

### TENSION (0:03.5-0:08) - 8 words
Then you open it. There are fifty names.

### THE POINT (0:08-0:22) - 29 words
The list isn't the problem. Fifty isn't too many. You just have no plan for them. So every name feels like a chore. And you end up calling nobody.

### PAYOFF (0:22-0:30) - 14 words
Pick the ten who'd take your call today. Call each of them every month.

## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You say your contact list is overwhelming. Then you open it, and there are fifty names.

Fifty isn't too many. It feels like too many because there's no plan, so every name feels like homework and you end up calling nobody.

Chase Craig said it best on my show: most agents start with a big list and treat everybody the same, and you don't have time to do that. Start small and treat those people like kings.

Sort your list into three groups. The ten who'd take your call today get a call every month. The people who know you get four touches a year. Everyone else gets something useful when you have it. One list, one system. A notebook you use beats software you don't.
