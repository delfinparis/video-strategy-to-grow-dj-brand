# Listing A Home Without A Floor Plan

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Post Date:** TBD
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: One Prop (hold up a printed floor plan on "floor plan").

## Shareable Moment
> "Let buyers see the layout before they visit."

## Teleprompter

Hey Realtors, listing a home without a floor plan is really stupid.

If you skip the floor plan, buyers guess the layout from photos. NAR's 2025 survey found 57 percent of online buyers call floor plans very useful.

Here's the solution. Order the floor plan with the photos on every single listing.

Book it in the same visit as the photographer. Ask for room sizes on the plan. Make it the second photo in the listing. Print it for every showing.

Let buyers see the layout before they visit.

## Data Source
- **Claim:** "NAR's 2025 survey found 57 percent of online buyers call floor plans very useful."
  - Source: National Association of Realtors, 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, Exhibit 3-8 "Value of Website Features." PDF: https://www.rirealtors.org/clientuploads/documents/NAR/Homebuyers_and_sellers_trend_2025.pdf
  - Exact wording: "Buyers who used the internet during their home search found photos (81 percent), detailed information about properties for sale (77 percent), floor plans (57 percent), real estate contact information (44 percent), and virtual tours (38 percent) to be very useful."
  - Who was measured: recent primary-residence buyers who used the internet in their search (survey of buyers who purchased July 2024 to June 2025).
  - Status: confirmed (re-opened 2026-10-14). "Buyers who used the internet during their home search" said as "online buyers."
