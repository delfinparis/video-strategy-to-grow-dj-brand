# You Start Every Video With Your Name

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Your name is the least interesting thing you have."

## Script (30 seconds max, 62 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5) - 7 words
You start every video with your name.

### VERDICT (0:01.5-0:03.5) - 5 words
That's really stupid. Here's why.

### TENSION (0:03.5-0:08) - 8 words
Hey guys, my name is... and they're gone.

### THE POINT (0:08-0:22) - 28 words
People decide fast whether to keep watching. Your name is the least interesting thing you have. They don't know you yet. They don't care who you are yet.

### PAYOFF (0:22-0:30) - 14 words
Start with the most interesting thing you'll say. Say your name later, or never.

## Social Media

### LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You start every video with your name.

Rachel Adams Lee acted this out on my show and it's exactly right: "they'll be like, hey guys, my name's Rachel and today, gone. Lost him."

People decide fast whether to keep watching. Your name is the least interesting thing you have, because they don't know you yet. They'll care who you are after you've said something worth hearing.

Start with the most interesting sentence you have. Say it out loud, not just as text on the screen, because captions come from what you say. Cut the first three seconds of every recording. That's almost always the "hey guys." Say your name later, or never.
