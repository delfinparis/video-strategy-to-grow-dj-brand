# You Start Every Video With Your Name

**Pillar:** Practice | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** TBD

## Shareable Moment
> "Your name is the least interesting thing you have."

## Script (about 40 seconds, 92 words)

### HOOK (0:00-0:06) - 12 words
Hey Realtors, starting your videos with your own name is really stupid.

### WHY (0:06-0:15) - 23 words
People decide fast whether to keep watching, and your name is the least interesting thing you have, because they don't know you yet.

### THE SOLUTION (0:15-0:21) - 13 words
Here's the solution - open with the most interesting thing you're going to say.

### SAY THIS (0:21-0:36) - 37 words
Don't say, hey guys, my name is. Say, this building's taxes just jumped and nobody knows why. Say it out loud, not just as text on the screen. Then tell them who you are, or skip it.

### CLOSE (0:36-0:40) - 7 words
Get their attention first. Then your name.
