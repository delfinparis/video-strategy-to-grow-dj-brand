# Walk & Talk — "Seventy Grand For a Name"

**Format:** Talking-head / walk-and-talk, ~65s
**Pillar:** Kale-signaling (recruiting)
**Created:** 2026-06-22
**Avatar:** Forgotten Middle (3) + Prestige mid-producer
**Objection answered:** "I'd lose the brand's credibility with clients."
**Heat:** 4 · **stat_anchored:** true
**Scroll-stopper:** Spoken in line one (captions.ai pulls from audio)
**WOW:** Surprising statistic + contrarian. Opens on the literal brand tax, then proves the client chose the agent, not the sign.

## Spoken Script

> Some agents pay over seventy thousand dollars a year. For a name on a business card.
>
> That's the pitch every big brokerage runs. Pay the fees, give up the commission, because the brand is what wins you business.
>
> So look at your last closing. That client didn't pick you for the name on the building. They picked you because someone they trust said call you, and you answered. The brand didn't bring them in. You did. Then the brokerage took a cut of the check your name earned.
>
> At Kale, you're the brand. Your client doesn't care where you hang your license. So we stopped charging you for a name that mostly just helps us recruit the next agent.
>
> The brand was never working for you. You were working for the brand.
>
> Here's what you do now. Pull up your last five closings. Next to each, write why that client picked you. If the brokerage's name isn't on the list, you've been paying for smoke.
>
> You were always the brand. They just charged you a fortune to forget it.

## Data Source

- "Some agents pay over seventy thousand dollars a year." Source: joinkale.com/compare, 2026 (Coldwell Banker and Century 21 modeled at $70,700/yr; Jameson Sotheby's $73,700). Who measured: Kale Realty's own published cost comparison, 20 sides at $400K average. Status: confirmed (published on Kale's site); note it is Kale's model, not third-party.
- Clients choose the agent, not the brokerage brand. Source: NAR 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers (~2 of 3 sellers via referral/repeat); Baylor Keller Center 2008 via Inman 2017 ("national brand" not important). Status: confirmed (survey).
- Kale doesn't charge agents for consumer-facing brand. Source: D.J. Paris direct, June 2026. Status: confirmed (founder statement).
