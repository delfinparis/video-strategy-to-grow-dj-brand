# Stop Opening Your Listing Pitch With Your Bio.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2025 seller survey: help with pricing / understanding the local market was the #1 most valuable agent service for 40% of sellers and top-2 for 67%. Negotiation was a distant second (18% / 41%). The listing presentation that opens with 'about me' is spending the first ten minutes on the thing sellers ranked lowest.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Hook Family:** Sacred Cow | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: The Stop (Walk fast, plant and lean in on 'Stop.')

## Shareable Moment
> "Put the price on page one."

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
Stop opening your listing pitch with your bio.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)
Sellers don't care about you yet. They want their number.

### THE POINT (0:06.5-0:21.5)
Zillow asked sellers what their agent did that mattered most. Help with price came first. Forty percent picked it number one. Two out of three put it in their top two.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5-0:30)
So put the price on page one. Show the comps. Your bio can wait.

**Duration:** 30 seconds | **Word Count:** 63 spoken words (HOOK 8 / TENSION 10 / THE POINT 31 / PAYOFF 14)

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): Concrete: 'page one' and 'comps' are things you can picture. Curse of Knowledge check: 'comps' is industry shorthand, but agents are the audience and the captions say 'comps that back it up.'
- Share/save driver (Berger/Hormozi): Practical value plus mild amusement at the shared cringe of the 'about me' slide. Brokerage trainers will share it.
- Retention move (MrBeast): Short punchy sentences in THE POINT hold the pace. The 'Your bio can wait' callback is a clean, abrupt end.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Chris Do argues trust has to come before the number, which is why bios exist. Miller sides with the script: the seller is the hero, and the bio makes the agent the hero. **Experiment:** re-cut the close as 'Earn trust with the number, not the résumé' and A/B saves.
