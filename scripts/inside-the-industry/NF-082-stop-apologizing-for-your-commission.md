# Stop Apologizing for Your Commission.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2025 survey: 74% of buyers and 77% of sellers called their agent's commission fair. 14% of buyers and 16% of sellers said too high. The fee conversation agents dread is one most clients have already settled.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Hook Family:** Sacred Cow | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** All agents, newer agents most
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: The Stop (Walk fast, plant on 'Stop.')

## Shareable Moment

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
Stop apologizing for your commission.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)
Most clients already think it's fair. Zillow asked them.

### THE POINT (0:06.5-0:21.5)
Seventy-four percent of buyers said their agent's fee was fair. Seventy-seven percent of sellers said the same. Fewer than one in six called it too high. The apology is what makes them doubt it.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5-0:30)
So say your fee once, plainly. Then say what you do for it. No sorry.
