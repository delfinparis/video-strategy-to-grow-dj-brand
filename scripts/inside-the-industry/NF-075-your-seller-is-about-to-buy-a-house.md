# Your Seller Is About to Buy a House.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2025 seller survey: 57% of sellers also bought a home, and 73% of those used the same agent for both deals. Every listing appointment has a second deal in it, and the agent who asks gets it.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Hook Family:** Forbidden | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents, all agents taking listings
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: The Whip (Camera on a for-sale sign for one beat, whip to face on 'Your seller.')

## Shareable Moment
> "Where are you moving next?"

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
Your seller is about to buy a house.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)
Most agents never ask where they're moving. That's a second deal.

### THE POINT (0:06.5-0:21.5)
Zillow's newest seller survey says fifty-seven percent also bought a home. Seventy-three percent of those used the same agent for both. So that second deal is usually yours. But only if you ask.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5-0:30)
At the listing appointment, ask one question. Where are you moving next? Then go find them that house.

**Duration:** 30 seconds | **Word Count:** 70 spoken words (HOOK 8 / TENSION 11 / THE POINT 33 / PAYOFF 18)

## Council Review

**Scroll-stop variants (spoken, pick one to A/B):**
1. "Your seller is about to buy a house."  [hook_family: Forbidden | emotion: curiosity]
2. "Every listing you take has a second deal in it."  [hook_family: Named Stakes | emotion: excitement]
3. "Stop asking sellers when they want to list. Ask this."  [hook_family: Swap / List | emotion: curiosity]

**Pattern interrupt:** The Whip confirmed: sign to face reads as 'this house, and the next one.' On-site at a listing, use Location Cold-Open instead.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): The loop opens because the hook sounds wrong. The viewer thinks 'my seller is selling' and needs the next line. The payoff closes it with one question.
- Share/save driver (Berger/Hormozi): Practical value. 'Where are you moving next?' is the kind of line agents screenshot and send to their team.
- Retention move (MrBeast): Two numbers in a row in THE POINT is the danger zone. 'So that second deal is usually yours' is the re-hook that saves it.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Eric Simon thinks 'your seller is about to buy a house' is too obvious to an experienced agent and wants the inside joke ('you sold their house and their cousin sold them the next one'). That would be a fabricated scene, so it is not allowed. **Experiment:** run variant 2 against this hook with agents who have been licensed 5+ years.
