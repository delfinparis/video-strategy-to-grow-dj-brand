# Stop Asking Longtime Owners If They'll Sell.

> **WOW: NAR's 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, Exhibit 6-15: "the most commonly cited reason for selling their home was the desire to move closer to friends and family (26 percent)," more than double the next reasons (home too small, 10%; home too large, 10%).**

**Pillar:** Lead Generation | **Hook Family:** Mirror | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: Walk-Toward (walking straight at the lens on "You keep asking").

## Shareable Moment

## Teleprompter

You keep asking longtime owners if they'll sell.

It sounds like a sales pitch. So they say no.

NAR's 2025 survey asked sellers why they sold. The top reason was moving closer to friends and family. Twenty-six percent said that. That's more than double the next reason.

So don't ask if they're selling. Ask, "Where do your kids and grandkids live now?"

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "NAR's 2025 survey asked sellers why they sold. The top reason was moving closer to friends and family. Twenty-six percent said that."
  - Source: National Association of REALTORS, 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers (released Nov 2025), Chapter 6, Exhibit 6-15, "Primary Reason for Selling Previous Home, by Miles Moved." PDF copy: https://www.rirealtors.org/clientuploads/documents/NAR/Homebuyers_and_sellers_trend_2025.pdf
  - Exact wording: "For all sellers, the most commonly cited reason for selling their home was the desire to move closer to friends and family (26 percent), followed by the home was too small (10 percent), the home was too large (10 percent), and a change in family situation (eight percent)." Table row: "Want to move closer to friends or family: 26%" (All sellers).
  - Who was measured: recent sellers, drawn from NAR's survey of recent buyers who also sold a home ("Information about sellers comes from those buyers who have also sold a home"). Transactions July 2024 to June 2025; survey mailed July 2025.
  - Status: confirmed (re-opened 2026-10-14). Spoken as the exact percentage per NF source rigor.
- **Claim:** "That's more than double the next reason."
  - Source: math on the same exhibit: 26% versus 10% for each of the next two reasons ("home is too small," "home is too large"). 26 / 10 = 2.6.
  - Status: confirmed (math on real data).
- **Claim:** "It sounds like a sales pitch. So they say no." / payoff action.
  - Source: editorial framing and recommendation. No statistic implied.
  - Status: commentary.
- **Verified, not spoken (on purpose):** "The typical age of home sellers was 64 this year... the highest recorded in this data set" (Exhibit 6-1); "The median number of years a seller owned their home was 11 years an all-time high" (Chapter 6 highlights). Both support "longtime owners" in the hook. Distance matters too: among sellers who moved 51-100 miles, 45% cited friends and family (Exhibit 6-15); not used, second idea.
