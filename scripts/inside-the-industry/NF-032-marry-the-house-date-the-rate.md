# Marry the House, Date the Rate. Half of That Advice Is a Fairy Tale.

> **WOW: Contrarian myth-sort on the most-repeated line in real estate. "Marry the house, date the rate" is half true and half fantasy, and most agents repeat it without knowing which half. The real half: rates dipped enough by early 2026 that ICE counted 4.8 million homeowners already able to refinance into a lower rate, so a buyer who overpays on rate today is not locked into it forever. The fairy-tale half: "just assume the seller's low rate" works only on the narrow slice of government-backed loans, and only if the buyer can cover the seller's equity in cash, usually six figures. Seller buydowns are real but not a magic wand. The edge in 2026 isn't repeating the slogan. It's being the agent who knows exactly where the real lever ends and the Facebook-comment strategy begins.**

## Script (~56 seconds)

### HOOK (0:00-0:07)
There's a phrase running through this whole business. "Marry the house, date the rate." Half of it is real. Half of it is a fairy tale. Let me sort it out.

### CONTEXT (0:07-0:22)
The real half first. Rates dipped enough earlier this year that ICE counted 4.8 million homeowners who could already refinance into something lower. So the buyer who overpays on rate today is not stuck with it. If rates keep sliding, they refinance. They dated the rate. That part is true, and you can tell a nervous buyer that with a straight face.

### WHY IT MATTERS (0:22-0:40)
Now the fairy tales. "Just assume the seller's 3 percent loan." That only works on government-backed loans, and only if your buyer can cover the seller's equity in cash, which is usually six figures. Real, but rare. Same story with seller buydowns. A real tool, not a magic wand. If your listing strategy leans on either one, you don't have a strategy. You have a hope.

### WHAT YOU DO NOW (0:40-0:53)
Two moves this week.

One. On every listing you take, ask the seller if their loan is FHA or VA. If it is, you may have an assumable loan, and that is a real, narrow selling point most agents never check.

Two. Learn the refinance math cold, so you can tell a buyer the truth. Today's rate is a starting line, not a life sentence.

### CLOSE (0:53-0:56)
