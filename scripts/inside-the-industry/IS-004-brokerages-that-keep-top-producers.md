## Data Source

**Top producer behaviors (all D.J. Paris direct observation from 700+ Keeping It Real Podcast interviews):**
- CEO-mindset / on-the-business vs in-the-business: pattern observed across top-producer guests including Garrett Maroon (script 003), Bari Mill (script 032), and multiple others
- Relationship-as-business-model: consistent across 700+ episode archive
- Mentorship / coaching as a precondition for breakout: observed across rookie-to-top-producer trajectories
- Delegation with retained client ownership: pattern observed across $25M+ producers
- Family buy-in for work hours: pattern surfaced in multiple "how do you sustain this" podcast conversations
- Daily structure / pre-planning: consistent across Kristee Leonard, Carrie McCormick, and other named guests
- Full responsiveness / answering own phone: See IS-002 for the canonical version of this observation

**Brokerage practices referenced (all Kale Realty operating model, D.J. Paris VP Business Development, direct observation):**
- Real mentorship program: ~25 active mentors, close to 100 mentees, voluntary pairing model
- Cross-company skill-building events: open to agents from every Chicago brokerage, monthly cadence
- Leadership accessibility: Kale leadership answers own phone, direct lines to agents
- Family-owned culture: Kale is privately/family-owned, long-game decision making vs. public-company quarter-to-quarter
- "Colleagues, not competitors" posture with other brokerages: reflected in event programming and referral culture
