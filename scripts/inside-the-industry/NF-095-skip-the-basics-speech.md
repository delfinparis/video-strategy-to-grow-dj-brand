# Your New Buyer Doesn't Need the Basics Speech.

> **WOW: Zillow's Q2 2026 shareholder letter (Aug 5, 2026): the average buyer who closes with a Zillow Preferred agent partner "visits Zillow nearly 100 times -- totaling 15 hours of time using Zillow -- before they even reach out to connect with an agent." CEO Jeremy Wacksman, April 2026: "The more noise, the more data that's out there, the more they want someone to help them."**
