# Showing Homes First Is Costing You Buyers.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2026 Agent Trends Survey (Feb 19, 2026): "Nearly half of agents say most new clients overestimate how much home they can afford, and very few buyers arrive with a high degree of financial literacy."**

**Pillar:** Client Management | **Hook Family:** Swap / List | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Buyer agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: The Whip (camera on a for-sale sign for one beat, whip to face on "Showing homes first").

## Shareable Moment
> "A house tour won't fix that. A real monthly number will."

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
Showing homes first is costing you buyers.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)
They love a house they can't afford. Then you start over.

### THE POINT (0:06.5-0:21.5)
Zillow surveyed real estate agents this year. Nearly half said most new clients think they can afford more. A house tour won't fix that. A real monthly number will.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5-0:30)
Before the first showing, work out their monthly payment together. Then show homes at that number.
