# More Than Half of Sellers Lose a Buyer.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2025 seller survey: 54% of sellers had at least one offer fall through. Financing (39%) and a low appraisal (28%) were the top reasons. The agent who says this at signing turns a crisis into the plan.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Hook Family:** Forbidden | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
