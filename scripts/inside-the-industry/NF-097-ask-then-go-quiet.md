# You Asked for the Listing, Then Kept Talking.

> **WOW: Molly Fletcher, a former sports agent who says she landed more than $500 million in deals, makes "Embrace the Pause" one of the five steps in her negotiating book. Her line: "the moment of quiet between the ask and the answer is a chance to think and reflect." Most agents spend that moment talking.**

**Pillar:** Practice | **Hook Family:** Mirror | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: Walk-Toward (Walk straight at the lens on the hook, background sliding past. Stop walking and go silent for one full beat after "Ask, then go quiet.")

## Shareable Moment
> "Ask, then go quiet."

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
You asked for the listing, then kept talking.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)
Every extra word gives the seller less time to think.

### THE POINT (0:06.5-0:21.5)
Molly Fletcher was a sports agent. She says she landed more than five hundred million dollars in deals. One of her five steps is embrace the pause. Ask, then go quiet.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5-0:30)
Next meeting, ask for the listing. Then stop talking. Count to five. Let the seller go first.
