# NAR Is Going to Sunset Some of the Designations Behind Realtor Names

> **WOW: Earned inside-the-industry observation -- in a closed-door NAR Q1 readout on 2026-04-30, fellow content creator Katie pushed back hard on the value of real estate designations. Her line: "All you should need behind your name is the word realtor." NAR's senior team agreed -- and confirmed they plan to sunset designations that aren't bringing real value.**

### INSIGHT (0:30-0:50)

That's not a forum post. That's not a critic. That came directly from NAR -- in a room where the comment was meant for working agents. If you've been paying annual dues for a designation that hasn't moved a single client decision, the conversation about whether it survives is already happening at the top.

### CLOSE (0:50-0:60)
Katie was right. Some of those letters behind realtor names are going away. The question is which ones, and which ones still mean something to a consumer when they do.
