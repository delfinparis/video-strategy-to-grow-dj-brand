Never write "fixer-upper" in a listing.

Zillow's 2026 listing-words study looked at more than two million homes sold in 2025. Homes described as fixer-uppers sold for 14% less than expected. "Turnkey" was tied to 2.9% more, and "remodeled" to 2.2% more.

Fixer-upper homes often do need work, so part of that gap is the house. But the label also tells every buyer to arrive with a lowball before they have seen a room.

Say what the house needs instead. Old kitchen. Original windows. Let buyers price the work themselves.

Check your active listings today.
