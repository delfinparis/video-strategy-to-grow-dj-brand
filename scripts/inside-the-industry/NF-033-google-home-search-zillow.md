# Google Just Jumped Into Home Search Without Asking Zillow

> **WOW: Earned inside-the-industry observation + cost-of-inaction -- Google just expanded a real estate listings test into eight major markets without going through Zillow or NAR, routing directly through HouseCanary, eXp Realty, and three MLSes. When investors first saw this in December, Zillow lost $1.6 billion in market cap in a single day. The script names the structural shift and gives every agent the one question to ask their broker this week.**

## Script (~65 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:09)
Google just jumped into home search. They didn't ask Zillow. They didn't ask NAR.

### CONTEXT (0:09-0:28)
This week Google relaunched a test that drops home listings into mobile search. Eight major markets. The data comes through HouseCanary, eXp Realty, and three MLSes. When Google ran a smaller version of this last December, Zillow lost one-point-six billion in market cap in a single day.

### WHY IT MATTERS (0:28-0:46)
This matters even if your listing isn't in the pilot. If Google becomes where buyers default to search, the path from buyer to listing runs through whichever brokerage or MLS feeds Google trusts. Right now, that's a short list. If your brokerage isn't on it, your listing isn't there.

### CLOSE (0:46-1:00)
Here's what you do now. Ask your broker one question. Which MLSs and partners feed our listings into Google's pilot? If the answer is "none of them," that's not a problem today. It's going to be one fast.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Google just jumped into home search... a test that drops home listings into mobile search... Eight major markets"
  - Source: Inman, "Google Home Search Pilot Resurfaces With MLS Data, eXp Listings," 2026-05-18 ([link](https://www.inman.com/2026/05/18/google-home-search-listings-housecanary-exp-crmls/)); The Real Deal, "What Google's Home Listings Test Means for Fractured MLS Scene," 2026-05-20 ([link](https://therealdeal.com/national/2026/05/20/what-googles-home-listings-test-means-for-fractured-mls-scene/)); Real Estate News, "Google home listings are back in 8 major markets," 2026-05-18 ([link](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/05/18/google-home-listings-are-back-in-8-major-markets)).
  - Markets confirmed: Chicago, Miami, Cleveland, Austin, San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, San Diego, and New York. Mobile-only.
  - Status: confirmed

- **Claim:** "The data comes through HouseCanary, eXp Realty, and three MLSes"
  - Source: The Real Deal 2026-05-20 and Inman 2026-05-18. The three MLSes are California Regional Multiple Listing Service (CRMLS), San Diego MLS, and My State MLS (a nationwide MLS). Many of the My State MLS listings come from eXp Realty -- eXp is sending all of its active MLS listings, including those from NextHome (which eXp acquired May 7), into the Google pilot. HouseCanary is Google's data and brokerage partner.
  - Status: confirmed

- **Claim:** "They didn't ask Zillow. They didn't ask NAR."
  - Source: Editorial characterization grounded in the verified routing. Google's pilot bypasses Zillow's portal entirely and routes around NAR's traditional MLS coordination role by going directly to one national MLS (My State MLS) plus regional MLSes plus a brokerage data partner (HouseCanary) plus a brokerage (eXp). Not a quote.
  - Status: confirmed (fair characterization of the verified architecture)

- **Claim:** "The first time Google ran this test last December, Zillow lost one-point-six billion in market cap in a single day"
  - Source: GeekWire, "Google's real estate listings 'experiment' sends Zillow shares down more than 8%," December 2025 ([link](https://www.geekwire.com/2025/googles-real-estate-listings-experiment-sends-zillow-shares-down-more-than-8/)); cross-referenced in 2026-05 industry coverage. The December 2025 reaction erased roughly $1.6 billion from Zillow's market cap; Zillow shares fell more than 8% that day. CoStar (Homes.com) fell 6.4% and Rocket (which acquired Redfin earlier in the year) fell 2.8% on the same news.
  - Who was measured: Zillow Group market capitalization (public company stock).
  - Status: confirmed
  - **Important accuracy note:** The $1.6 billion / 8%+ stock figure is from the December 2025 initial test, NOT from the May 2026 relaunch. The script anchors this explicitly with "The first time Google ran this test last December." Do not let the figure get attached to the May relaunch in any copy edit -- there is no confirmed May 2026 stock-impact figure of equivalent magnitude in the verified sources at the time of writing.

- **Claim:** "If your brokerage isn't on it, your listing isn't there"
  - Source: Logical implication of the pilot's architecture (Google reads from specific MLS and brokerage feeds; listings outside those feeds do not appear). Framing, not a stat.
  - Status: confirmed (framing)
