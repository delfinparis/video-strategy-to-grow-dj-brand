# One in Five Gen Zers Still Believes Homeownership Is the American Dream. The Other Four Don't.

> **WOW: Surprising statistic + counter-narrative -- the LGBTQ+ Real Estate Alliance just published a survey where only 22.6% of straight Gen Z respondents and 16.8% of LGBTQ+ Gen Z respondents said homeownership will "definitely" remain part of the American Dream. Both numbers are under one in four. Gen Z is the next first-time-buyer cohort and most of them are checking out before they've even started. The script reframes the checked-out Gen Z buyer as a 5-year pipeline opportunity and hands the agent a CRM-tag-and-quarterly-touch playbook.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Hook Family:** Named Stakes | **Avatars:** All, buyer-side agents most
**Filming:** Walk and talk. No demo.

## Shareable Moment

## Script (~72 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:11)
Only one in five Gen Zers thinks homeownership is still part of the American Dream. That means four out of five don't. Every agent should be paying attention to that number.

### CONTEXT (0:11-0:32)
The LGBTQ Real Estate Alliance just surveyed almost four hundred members and consumers. About three quarters identified as LGBTQ. Twenty-two point six percent of the straight Gen Z respondents said homeownership will definitely remain part of the American Dream. Sixteen point eight percent of the LGBTQ Gen Z said the same. The gap isn't the point. Both numbers are under one in four.

### WHY IT MATTERS (0:32-0:50)
Gen Z is the next first-time-buyer cohort. They're aging into peak buying years right now. And most of them are checking out before they've even started. The twenty-eight-year-old who tells you they're never going to own a home isn't trying to be difficult. They're already convinced. Every conversation you don't have with them is a relationship someone else's database picks up in 2030.

### CLOSE (0:52-1:12)
Here's what you do now. Stop writing off the twenty-eight-year-old who says they're not buying. Add them to your CRM with a five-year tag. Send them one helpful thing every quarter. A neighborhood breakdown. A new construction permit. A first-time-buyer program update. Today's not their conversation. 2030 is. Be the agent they remember when it is.

  - Source: LGBTQ+ Real Estate Alliance, 2026 report on Gen Z views of major life milestones, published 2026-06-25. Coverage: Real Estate News, "Gen Z views on homeownership 'should concern everyone' in housing," 2026-06-25 ([link](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/06/25/gen-z-views-on-homeownership-should-concern-everyone-in-housing)); Inman, "Discrimination, Lack Of Support Keeps LGBTQ+ Gen-Zers Behind: Report," 2026-06-25 ([link](https://www.inman.com/2026/06/25/discrimination-and-a-lack-of-support-is-keeping-lgbtq-gen-zers-behind-report/)); HousingWire, "Report lays out wealth, housing gaps facing LGBTQ+ Gen Z" ([link](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/report-lays-out-wealth-housing-gaps-facing-lgbtq-gen-z/)).
  - Status: confirmed (high-level framing); see methodological notes below.

- **Claim:** "Twenty-two point six percent of the straight Gen Z respondents said homeownership will definitely remain part of the American Dream. Sixteen point eight percent of the LGBTQ Gen Z said the same."
  - Source: Alliance 2026 report. Direct figures from the survey.
  - Status: confirmed
  - Important methodological notes held for transparency (not narrated on camera):
    - Total respondent base: 389 (Alliance members + consumers).
    - ~73% of respondents identified as LGBTQ+; the sample is not nationally representative of Gen Z.
    - The 22.6% / 16.8% figures specifically measure "definitely" believe. Some additional share likely "somewhat" believes -- the script's "four out of five don't" framing collapses "doesn't believe" and "not sure" into a single bucket. Defensible directional framing per Rule 5 (round / collapse only when the directional point is unchanged), but accurate to flag here.
    - The "one in five" / "four out of five" headline framing in the spoken script is a rounding of the 22.6% heterosexual share for cadence (per Rule 5 ratio convention). The combined / weighted Gen Z share across the full survey is closer to ~18%, even lower than the 22.6% used in the hook.

- **Claim:** "The LGBTQ Real Estate Alliance just surveyed almost four hundred members and consumers. About three quarters identified as LGBTQ."
  - Source: Alliance 2026 report. 389 respondents; approximately 73% LGBTQ+.
  - Status: confirmed (the spoken script explicitly names the Alliance as the source so the methodological skew is transparent to viewers).

- **Claim:** "Gen Z is the next first-time-buyer cohort. They're aging into peak buying years right now."
  - Source: NAR / Census / Fannie Mae demographic data on first-time-buyer age distribution. The U.S. median first-time-buyer age has run in the late 30s in recent years, but the leading edge of Gen Z (born ~1997-2003, currently ~23-29) is entering its peak first-time-buyer years now and through the next decade.
  - Status: confirmed (general demographic framing)

- **Claim:** "Every conversation you don't have with them is a relationship someone else's database picks up in 2030."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's editorial framing. The 2030 horizon reflects the realistic 5-year lag between an initial agent touch with a 28-year-old who isn't ready to buy and the actual transaction.
  - Status: D.J.'s tactical framing, not a stat

- **Claim (tactical):** "Add them to your CRM with a five-year tag. Send them one helpful thing every quarter."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's recommended long-pipeline nurture cadence. Quarterly touches over 5 years = 20 touches before transaction, consistent with sphere/SOI nurture best practice across NAR, Buffini, Tom Ferry coaching content.
  - Status: tactical guidance
