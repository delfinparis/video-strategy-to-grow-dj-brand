# Every Year. Three Big States. The Zillow Pattern Isn't a Fluke.

> **WOW: Surprising statistic plus contrarian counter-argument-killer. Zillow's May 14, 2026 study didn't just produce one big number ($1.49B). It produced a pattern that replicated across three years (2023, 2024, 2025) and across the three biggest housing markets it broke out by state: California $533M aggregate seller losses, Florida $217M, New York $146M. The most common objection a listing agent will hear when they cite the $2,165 per-home figure is "but maybe it's a fluke" or "but maybe it's just my market." This script kills that objection. Three years of consistency across three diverse markets is the kind of replication that survives any "your sample is biased" pushback. The take: the data isn't going to change its mind, and the "fluke" defense for double-ending is gone.**
