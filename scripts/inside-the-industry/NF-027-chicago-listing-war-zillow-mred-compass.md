# 43,000 Chicago Listings Vanished Off Zillow. A Judge Put Them Back.

> **WOW: Surprising number plus local-stakes hook. Roughly 43,000 Chicagoland listings disappeared off Zillow when MRED cut Zillow's feed; on May 22, 2026 a federal judge (John Tharp Jr.) ordered them restored via a TRO in Zillow's Sherman Antitrust Act suit against MRED and Compass. The take: the national private-listing war is being fought in Chicago first. Where a listing lives is now a federal question, and agents need an answer ready for sellers plus a watchpoint on the case. This is the rare NF where the news happened in D.J.'s own market.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** Monday, May 25, 2026

## Script (~58 seconds)

### HOOK (0:00-0:10)
About forty-three thousand Chicago listings disappeared off Zillow last week. A federal judge put them back on Friday. Your sellers noticed.

### CONTEXT (0:10-0:27)
Here's what happened. Zillow sued our own MLS, MRED, and the brokerage Compass, in federal court. The claim? They teamed up to choke off Zillow's access to Chicago listings unless Zillow agreed to show Compass's private listings nationwide. Zillow called it a group boycott under the Sherman Antitrust Act. MRED pulled the feed. Friday, Judge John Tharp ordered the listings back on.

### WHY IT MATTERS (0:27-0:39)
This is the private-listing fight landing in your backyard. Where a listing lives, on the MLS, off it, on a portal, is now a federal case. And Chicago is the test market.

### WHAT YOU DO NOW (0:39-0:53)
Two things. One, when a seller asks why their home blinked off Zillow, you've got the answer. Tell them before they panic. Two, watch this case. It's a restraining order, not a verdict. If it goes to trial, the rules for where listings live could reset. I'll cover it when it moves.

### CLOSE (0:53-0:58)
Forty-three thousand listings. One judge. This isn't over.
