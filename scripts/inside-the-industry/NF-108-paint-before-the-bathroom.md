# Stop Telling Sellers to Redo the Bathroom.

> **WOW: NAR's 2025 Remodeling Impact Report estimates a bathroom renovation recovers 50% of its cost at sale. Realtors' top recommended project before selling: "Paint The Entire Home" (50% of Realtors).**

**Pillar:** Client Management | **Hook Family:** Swap / List | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents

## Teleprompter

Stop telling sellers to redo the bathroom.

Your seller pays for it, and buyers won't pay it all back.

NAR put out a remodeling report in 2025. It estimates a bathroom remodel gets back half its cost. Realtors' top pick before selling was painting the whole house.

Tell your next seller to paint the whole house first. Leave the bathroom for the buyer.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "NAR put out a remodeling report in 2025. It estimates a bathroom remodel gets back half its cost."
  - Source: National Association of REALTORS and NARI, "2025 Remodeling Impact Report," April 2025: https://remodelingdoneright.nari.org/media/jjjaoome/2025remodelingimpactreport.pdf . Chart "Top Cost Recovery For Remodeling Projects": "Bathroom Renovation 50%" (same chart: "Minor Kitchen Upgrade 60%", "Add New Bathroom 56%").
  - Who was measured: cost recovery is calculated by NAR. "REALTORS provided an estimate of the likely dollar value each project would add to the house at resale. NAR calculated a Recovered Project Cost percentage by comparing that dollar value to the estimated cost of each project provided by NARI members." NAR member survey: 4,005 responses; NARI cost survey: 177 member companies, summer 2024.
  - Status: confirmed (re-opened 2026-10-14). Row checked: "Bathroom Renovation," not "Add New Bathroom." "Renovation" said as "remodel"; "50%" said as "half." It's an estimate, and the script says "estimates."
