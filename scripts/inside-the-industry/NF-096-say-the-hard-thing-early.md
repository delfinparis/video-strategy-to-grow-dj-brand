# Holding Back Bad News Hurts Your Seller.

> **WOW: Barry Jenkins, author of Too Nice for Sales, on his book site: "the most respectful thing you can do is say the hard thing, kindly, and early." And: "Vague niceness feels kind in the moment but delays the truth." The agent who waits weeks to bring up price is being nice to themselves, not the seller.**

**Pillar:** Practice | **Hook Family:** Sacred Cow | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: The Stop (Walk fast for one beat, plant and lean in on "hurts.")

## Shareable Moment

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
Holding back bad news hurts your seller.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)

### THE POINT (0:06.5-0:21.5)
Barry Jenkins wrote Too Nice for Sales. He says clarity is kindness. If the price is too high, say so at the first meeting. If the house needs work, say that too.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5-0:30)
Before your next listing meeting, write down the hard thing. Say it before you leave. Kindly.
