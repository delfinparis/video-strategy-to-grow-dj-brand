# Closing Costs Are Shocking Almost Half of Buyers.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2025 buyer survey: 45% of buyers said final closing costs were more than they expected (49% of first-time buyers). Only 21% paid less than expected. The surprise is preventable with two texts.**
