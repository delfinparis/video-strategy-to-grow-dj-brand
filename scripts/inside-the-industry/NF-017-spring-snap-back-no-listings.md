# The Spring Market Snapped Back. Here's What to Do If You Have No Listings.

> **WOW: Surprising statistic plus contrarian take plus tactical specificity. Pending home sales jumped 7.7% as of May 3, the highest level in over three years (Real Estate News, May 7, 2026). Google searches for "homes for sale" are up over 20% year over year. The 30-year mortgage rate dropped to 6.37%, below where it was at this time last year. The spring market did snap back. The contrarian frame: most agents spent winter waiting and don't have the pipeline to capture the demand. Three concrete plays for the agent without listings: sphere outreach to the "someday" sellers, expired-listing outreach to sellers who tried and gave up, and comp-driven door-knocking. The agents who fill their pipeline this week win the next 90 days. The ones who don't watch the spring market move past them.**
