# If You Don't Have Listings Right Now, You're Losing. Here's Why.

> **WOW: Direct-address hook plus surprising statistic plus tactical specificity. Pending home sales jumped 7.7% as of May 3, the highest level in over three years (Real Estate News, May 7, 2026). Google searches for "homes for sale" are up over 20% year over year. The 30-year mortgage rate dropped to 6.37%, below where it was at this time last year. The spring market did snap back. The contrarian frame, delivered direct: if you don't have listings right now, you're losing -- because buyers are back and you have nothing to sell them. Three concrete plays for the agent without listings: sphere outreach to the "someday" sellers, expired-listing outreach to sellers who tried and gave up, and past-client outreach with a hyperlocal hook. The door-knock-recent-comps play was cut from this version because most agents won't run it; the past-client play replaces it with a mechanism most agents have access to but underuse.**

## Production Notes

- **Walk-and-talk friendly.** This script reads naturally for walk-and-talk delivery. The three action plays at WHAT YOU DO NOW are checklist-style and lend themselves to slight pauses between numbers as DJ walks. If DJ wants to extend to a longer 3-4 minute walk-and-talk, the natural expansion points are: (1) a one-line story per action (e.g., "I had a guest on the podcast last month who closed three deals from one Saturday morning of sphere calls"), and (2) a brief beat on what NOT to do (e.g., "stop sending market-update emails this month, those are background noise -- the past-client play above is a personal note, not a blast").
- **Time sensitivity.** The pending-sales number (7.7%, May 3) is fresh as of May 7 reporting. If filming is delayed more than 7-10 days, re-verify the number with the next weekly pending sales release. The structural take (spring snap-back, no-inventory plays) holds regardless.
- **Why door-knocking was cut.** An earlier draft had "door-knock the recent comps in your market" as Action 3. Removed because most agents won't run that play, and an action layer is only useful if the actions actually get executed. Replaced with past-client outreach -- same goal (find listing opportunities outside the existing sphere overlap), accessible mechanism (call or text), and an honest editorial reframe ("the listing pool most agents stop calling").
