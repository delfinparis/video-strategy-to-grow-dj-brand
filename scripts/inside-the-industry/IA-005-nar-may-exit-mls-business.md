# NAR May Be on the Way Out of the MLS Business

> **WOW: Earned inside-the-industry observation -- D.J. attended a closed-door NAR Q1 strategic-plan readout on 2026-04-30 alongside Rob Hahn (Notorious R.O.B.) and a handful of other content creators. ## Script (45-60 seconds)

### HOOK (0:00-0:08)
I just sat in a closed-door NAR meeting with Rob Hahn -- Notorious R.O.B. -- and a handful of other content creators, and NAR basically admitted they may be on the way out of the MLS business.

### CONTEXT (0:08-0:25)
About six of us in the country were in the room for a Q1 strategic-plan readout with NAR's senior team. One of the topics was the relationship between NAR and the MLSs. NAR said it directly. Some MLSs are telling them to get out of the business. Some are saying please don't. NAR is doing what they call de-risking their portfolio.

### INSIGHT (0:25-0:50)
Here's the part that mattered. Rob pushed harder than anyone in the room. He told NAR straight up that the smart play is to just get out of the MLS business entirely. Then he asked the follow-up question. If NAR writes a rule and the local MLS just refuses to follow it, how do you make them comply. The honest answer was, I don't have a direct answer for you on that one.

That's a structural shift. The largest MLSs are consolidating. NAR's legal exposure has mostly come through the MLS side. If NAR steps back from enforcement, how listings get shared and how data flows becomes a regional decision, not a national one.

### CLOSE (0:50-0:60)
The MLS landscape that built modern real estate is quietly being rewritten. Most agents won't notice until it's already changed.
