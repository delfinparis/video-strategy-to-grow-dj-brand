# Stop Spreading Staging Money Across Every Room.

> **WOW: NAR, 2025 Profile of Home Staging (May 6, 2025): "For home buyers, staging the living room was considered the most important (37%), followed closely by the primary bedroom (34%) and the kitchen (23%). In contrast, staging the guest bedroom (7%) was the least important."**

**Pillar:** Practice | **Hook Family:** Swap / List | **Heat:** 3.5 | **Avatars:** Listing agents

## Shareable Moment

## Teleprompter

Stop spreading staging money across every room.

Your seller ends up paying for rooms buyers care about least.

NAR asked agents which staged room matters most to buyers. The living room came first, at 37 percent. The primary bedroom was next. The kitchen came third. The guest bedroom got just seven percent.

Put the money in the living room, primary bedroom and kitchen. Leave the guest room for last.

  - Source: National Association of Realtors, "NAR Report Reveals Home Staging Boosts Sale Prices and Reduces Time on Market," press release on the 2025 Profile of Home Staging, 2025-05-06: https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/nar-report-reveals-home-staging-boosts-sale-prices-and-reduces-time-on-market
  - Exact wording: "For home buyers, staging the living room was considered the most important (37%), followed closely by the primary bedroom (34%) and the kitchen (23%). In contrast, staging the guest bedroom (7%) was the least important."
  - Who was measured: Realtors. Methodology in the release: "In February 2025, NAR invited a random sample of 49,806 active Realtors® to complete an online survey. NAR received 1,266 usable responses." So the ranking is agents' view of what matters to buyers, not buyers' own answers.
