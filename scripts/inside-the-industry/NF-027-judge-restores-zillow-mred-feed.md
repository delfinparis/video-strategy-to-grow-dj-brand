# A Judge Just Ordered Chicago's MLS to Turn Zillow Back On.

> **WOW: Surprising-number disproportion plus local insider stakes. On Wednesday May 20, MRED -- Chicago's MLS -- suspended Zillow's listing feed over nine listings Zillow refused to display under its Listing Access Standards (Zillow says none of the nine were even in MRED's Chicagoland service area). That cut took roughly 43,000 Chicago listings dark on the largest portal in the country. By Friday a federal judge ordered the feed restored. The disproportion is the hook: 43,000 listings held hostage over nine houses. The take only D.J. can make: he runs a 250-agent independent Chicago brokerage on this exact MLS and is on record on this case in The Real Deal. The frame is the series thesis -- the MLS is neutral co-op infrastructure, and the second it gets weaponized in a portal-vs-giant fight, the seller's listing becomes the bargaining chip. Two concrete agent moves plus a forward look at the antitrust trial that's only just starting.**
