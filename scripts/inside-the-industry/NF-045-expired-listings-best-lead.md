# Expired Listings: The Best Lead Nobody Calls

> **WOW: Surprising statistic + tactical specificity -- expired listings convert at 44% list rate and 20.7% sold rate per REDX, the highest of any lead type, and most agents skip them because the call is uncomfortable. The script gives the exact opener that gets you to the kitchen table.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Avatars:** All, listing-focused agents especially
**Filming:** Walk and talk. No demo.

## Shareable Moment
> "The discomfort is the moat."

## Script (~70 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:10)
The highest-converting lead source in real estate has a forty-four percent list rate. Most agents won't call. The reason they won't is the reason it works.

### CONTEXT (0:10-0:28)
Expired listings. REDX has them at forty-four percent list rate, twenty-point-seven percent sold rate. Highest of any lead type. Average cycle is about thirty days from call to listing. Nobody wants to phone a stranger whose house just got rejected by the market.

### WHY IT MATTERS (0:28-0:43)
The discomfort is the moat. Cold leads don't know they need an agent. FSBOs think they don't. Expireds know they do, and they're embarrassed. That's why the conversion is high and the competition is thin.

### CLOSE (0:43-1:10)
Here's what you do now. Pull every listing that expired in your market in the last sixty days. Open with this. "I know your home just came off the market and the last few months were probably frustrating. I'm not calling to sign you up today. I'm calling because I want to understand what happened so the next agent you talk to doesn't make the same mistakes." That gets you to the kitchen table.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Expired listings... forty-four percent list rate, twenty-point-seven percent sold rate. Highest of any lead type."
  - Source: REDX 2026 lead-conversion data, "Why Expired Listing Leads Convert 43% Better" ([link](https://www.redx.com/blog/why-expired-listing-leads-convert-better/)) and "Best Real Estate Leads 2026: ROI Rankings & Conversion Data" ([link](https://www.redx.com/blog/best-real-estate-leads-2026-ranking-guide/)). National 44% list rate and 20.7% sold rate on expired listings; expireds rank highest among lead types in REDX's 2026 benchmarks. FSBO comparison: 27.8% list rate.
  - Who was measured: REDX user-platform activity (the de facto industry benchmark for expired-listing and FSBO prospecting data).
  - Status: confirmed
  - Note: REDX is a prospecting platform, not a neutral academic source, but is the industry-standard benchmark for these specific lead-type metrics and is widely cited across real estate trade press. The 44%/20.7% figures originated with REDX and were republished by AgentZap (the source in the brief).

- **Claim:** "Average cycle is about thirty days from call to listing"
  - Source: REDX 2026 ranking guide (above). Average expired-listing conversion cycle reported as about 30 days from initial call to signed listing.
  - Status: confirmed

- **Claim:** "Cold leads don't know they need an agent. FSBOs think they don't. Expireds know they do, and they're embarrassed."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's framing of the seller-psychology spectrum across lead types. Reasoning, not a stat.
  - Status: confirmed (framing)

- **Claim (the exact-words opener):** "I know your home just came off the market and the last few months were probably frustrating. I'm not calling to sign you up today. I'm calling because I want to understand what happened so the next agent you talk to doesn't make the same mistakes."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's recommended opener. Designed to (a) acknowledge the seller's frustration, (b) remove the immediate-pitch pressure, (c) position D.J. or the calling agent as the alternative to the previous agent's strategy. Tactical script, not a stat.
  - Status: confirmed (D.J.'s tactical recommendation)
