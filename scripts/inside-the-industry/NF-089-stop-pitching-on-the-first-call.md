# Stop Pitching Yourself on the First Call.

> **WOW: Kuvaal Patel, HousingWire, 2026-05-27: the number to track is how many conversations it takes an agent to book one appointment. Agents with a high count "talk far too much, ask far too little and pitch their services before they understand how they can help the consumer." Agents with a low count "lead their conversations with questions, slow the pace and listen far more than they speak."**
