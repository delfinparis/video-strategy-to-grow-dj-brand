# You Shot the Photos and Stopped There.

> **WOW: NAR's 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers: "58 percent of buyers considered online video sites very useful." Only 5 percent of sellers said their agent marketed the home on video hosting websites.**

**Pillar:** Practice | **Hook Family:** Mirror | **Heat:** 3.5 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: The Whip (camera on a for-sale house for one beat, whip to face on "You shot the photos").

## Shareable Moment
> "That's one seller in twenty."

## Teleprompter

You shot the photos and stopped there.

Buyers search video sites too, and your listing isn't there.

NAR surveyed buyers and sellers in 2025. Fifty-eight percent of buyers called video sites very useful. Only five percent of sellers said their agent posted a video there. That's one seller in twenty.

Don't stop at the photos. Shoot a one-minute walkthrough video and post it on YouTube.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Fifty-eight percent of buyers called video sites very useful."
  - Source: National Association of Realtors, 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, Exhibit 3-4 "Usefulness of Different Information Sources." PDF: https://www.rirealtors.org/clientuploads/documents/NAR/Homebuyers_and_sellers_trend_2025.pdf
  - Exact wording: "Real estate agents were regarded as very useful by 77 percent of buyers, while 60 percent found mobile or tablet devices very useful. Additionally, 58 percent of buyers considered online video sites very useful."
  - Who was measured: recent primary-residence home buyers (bought July 2024 to June 2025).
  - Status: confirmed (re-opened 2026-10-14). "Online video sites" said as "video sites."
- **Claim:** "Buyers search video sites too."
  - Source: same report, Exhibit 3-2 "Information Sources Used in Home Search": "Online video site" used by 52% of all buyers.
  - Status: confirmed; spoken without the number.
- **Claim:** "Only five percent of sellers said their agent posted a video there. That's one seller in twenty."
  - Source: same report, Exhibit 7-8 "Methods Real Estate Agent Used To Market Home (Percent of Respondents Among Sellers Who Used an Agent)": "Video hosting websites 5%."
  - Who was measured: recent sellers who used an agent (seller-reported).
  - Status: confirmed. Five percent = one in twenty (math). "Posted a video there" = sellers reporting the agent used video hosting websites to market the home.
- **Claim:** "Shoot a one-minute walkthrough video and post it on YouTube."
  - Source: editorial recommendation. The research pack suggested 60-90 seconds; no source sets a length, so the script uses one minute as advice only.
  - Status: commentary.
- **Not used (on purpose):** Exhibit 3-8 rates "Videos" on listing websites very useful for 29% of online buyers. It cuts against a "buyers love listing videos" reading, so the script stays on the narrower, accurate claim about video sites.
