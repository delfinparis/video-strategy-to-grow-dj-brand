# Your Seller Saw a Compass Billboard. Now They're Calling You.

> **WOW: Earned inside-the-industry observation + tactical specificity -- a billboard fight between Zillow and Compass has put the private-listing question directly in front of Chicago consumers, and most agents will fumble the answer. D.J. gives the straight, no-agenda broker take plus the exact question to ask the seller back.**

## Shareable Moment

## Script (~60 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:08)
Your seller just saw a billboard that says Zillow doesn't have all the listings. Now they're calling you about going private. Here's what to tell them.

### CONTEXT (0:08-0:20)
Behind that billboard is a federal lawsuit. Zillow, Compass, and the Chicago MLS are fighting over private listings. For a few days, thousands of Chicago homes vanished from Zillow. A judge just ordered most of them back. Your client doesn't know any of that. Their feed just looked broken.

### THE TAKE (0:20-0:43)
So here's the straight answer, no agenda. A private listing is a home marketed without hitting the public sites first. It helps a seller in exactly one case. When privacy is worth more than money. A sensitive divorce. A famous owner. For everyone else, it costs you. Fewer eyeballs, fewer offers. Fewer offers, lower price. Public exposure is what creates competition, and competition is what gets you over asking.

### CLOSE (0:43-0:58)
Here's what you do now. When that call comes in, ask one question back. "Do you want privacy, or do you want the highest price?" If they say price, you list it everywhere. Don't let a billboard set your seller's strategy.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Zillow, Compass, and the Chicago MLS are fighting over private listings... a federal lawsuit"
  - Source: The Real Deal, "Zillow takes Compass, Chicago-area MLS to federal court over private listings," 2026-05-12 ([link](https://therealdeal.com/chicago/2026/05/12/zillow-sues-chicago-mls-and-compass-in-over-private-listings/)); Inman, 2026-05-12 ([link](https://www.inman.com/2026/05/12/zillow-sues-compass-and-chicagos-mls-over-private-listing-battle/)). MLS is MRED (Midwest Real Estate Data). Zillow alleges MRED and Compass conspired in violation of antitrust law.
  - Status: confirmed (allegations, not findings of fact)

- **Claim:** "For a few days, thousands of Chicago homes vanished from Zillow"
  - Source: CNN Business, "Thousands of Chicago-area home listings just went dark on Zillow," 2026-05-20 ([link](https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/20/business/zillow-compass-private-home-listings-lawsuit)); RISMedia, 2026-05-20 ([link](https://www.rismedia.com/2026/05/20/mred-zillow-listing-feed-deadline-passes/)). Chicago Zillow listings fell from roughly 5,000 to just over 2,000 after MRED cut the feed.
  - Status: confirmed

- **Claim:** "A billboard that says Zillow doesn't have all the listings"
  - Source: Inman, "Zillow, Compass Take Listings Fight To Social Media, Chicago Billboards," 2026-05-22 ([link](https://www.inman.com/2026/05/22/zillow-compass-mred-listings-chicago-billboard/)). Compass CEO Robert Reffkin revealed billboard renderings carrying the message "Zillow doesn't have all the listings."
  - Status: confirmed (exact billboard wording per Inman; note: Inman article is paywalled, wording corroborated via search index. D.J. should eyeball the live billboard or the Reffkin post before filming to confirm exact copy.)

- **Claim:** "A judge just ordered most of them back"
  - Source: Chicago Agent Magazine, "Judge orders MRED to restore Zillow's access to listing data," 2026-05-22 ([link](https://chicagoagentmagazine.com/2026/05/22/judge-orders-mred-to-restore-zillows-access-to-listing-data-amid-antitrust-lawsuit-against-mred-compass/)). Judge John Tharp granted in part Zillow's preliminary injunction, ordering MRED to restore Chicagoland listings to Zillow.
  - Status: confirmed

- **Claim:** "A private listing is a home marketed without hitting the public sites first... exposure creates competition"
  - Source: General real estate market mechanics. MRED's Private Listing Network shares properties to MLS subscribers without posting to public portals (per CNN/Axios coverage above). The "exposure drives competition drives price" principle is standard listing-strategy guidance, not a cited statistic.
  - Status: confirmed (framing, not a stat)
