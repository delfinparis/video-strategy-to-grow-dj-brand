# Open Houses Sell 4% of Homes. Stop Running Them to Find Buyers.

> **WOW: Surprising statistic plus mindset reframe. NAR's Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers has consistently reported that only about 4% of buyers find their home through an open house or yard sign. 52% find their home online. Yet most listing agents still pitch open houses to sellers as "this is how we'll find your buyer." Inman ran "Stale Listing? Another Open House Isn't The Fast Fix You're Looking For" on May 19, reinforcing the same data-backed point. The take: open houses aren't a buyer-acquisition tool. They're a showing tool for buyers who already found the listing online. They generate something else -- feedback on price, conversations with neighbors who might list later, relationships with other agents whose buyers might write on future listings. That's the actual ROI. Three plays for listing agents to reframe their open-house pitch, reallocate prep time, and track what open houses actually produce.**
