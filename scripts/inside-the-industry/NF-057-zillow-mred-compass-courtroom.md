# Your Listings Are In Court Right Now

> **WOW: Earned inside-the-industry observation + cost-of-inaction -- three CEOs (Zillow's Wacksman, Compass's Reffkin, MRED's Jensen) are in a Chicago federal courtroom July 1-2 for the preliminary injunction hearing in Zillow's antitrust case against MRED and Compass. MRED filed a last-minute motion to compel arbitration two days before the hearing. The outcome shapes whether Zillow keeps access to MRED's listing feed while the broader case proceeds.
