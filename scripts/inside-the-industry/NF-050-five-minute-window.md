# Your Business Lives in the First Five Minutes

> **WOW: Surprising statistic + cost-of-inaction -- 78% of home buyers hire the first agent who responds to their inquiry, and the industry-average response time is five point seven hours. That gap is the agent's entire business, sitting in plain sight. The script reframes "lead conversion" as a clock problem and hands the agent the three-step fix.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Avatars:** All, buyer-side agents most, team leads and ISA-running brokerages too
**Filming:** Walk and talk. No demo.

## Shareable Moment
> "The lead doesn't pick the best agent. They pick the fastest."

## Script (~65 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:10)
Seventy-eight percent of buyers hire the first agent who responds. The average agent takes five point seven hours. That gap is your entire business.

### CONTEXT (0:10-0:28)
Harvard Business Review's lead-response study. A lead you contact in five minutes is twenty-one times more likely to qualify. Wait half an hour and the number collapses. NAR's buyer report says nearly four out of five buyers go with whoever calls back first. The lead doesn't pick the best agent. They pick the fastest.

### WHY IT MATTERS (0:28-0:46)
Five point seven hours is almost a work day. Meanwhile the lead you'd have closed just hired somebody else. You didn't lose on price or experience. You lost because they got an answer and you didn't show up.

### CLOSE (0:46-1:06)
Here's what you do now. Turn lead notifications on for every source today. Write one sixty-second response. "Hi, it's D.J., I saw your inquiry, can I call you in two minutes." Save it as a text shortcut. Then block the first five minutes of every hour for the next two weeks. That's how you go from five point seven hours to five minutes. The only conversation that wins.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Seventy-eight percent of buyers hire the first agent who responds."
  - Source: National Association of REALTORS, Home Buyers and Sellers Generational Trends Report (annual). Long-standing NAR finding consistently republished across the industry: roughly 78% of buyers work with the first real estate agent who responds to their inquiry. Corroborated across 2026 lead-response benchmarks (e.g. AgentZap, GreetNow, Apten 2026 round-ups).
  - Status: confirmed
  - Who was measured: U.S. home buyers who initiated an online or referral inquiry and went on to transact with a licensed agent.

- **Claim:** "The industry-average response time is five point seven hours."
  - Source: GreetNow 2026 Lead Response Time Statistics ([link](https://greetnow.com/blog/lead-response-time-statistics)) and corroborating 2026 round-ups (Apten Speed-to-Lead Benchmarks 2026 [link](https://www.apten.ai/blog/speed-to-lead-benchmarks-2026)). Real estate industry average response time: 5.7 hours.
  - Status: confirmed (2026 print)

- **Claim:** "A lead you contact in five minutes is twenty-one times more likely to qualify than one you contact half an hour later."
  - Source: Harvard Business Review, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (Oldroyd, Elkington, McAfee, 2011), based on the MIT/InsideSales.com Lead Response Management Study. Finding: leads contacted within five minutes of inquiry were 21x more likely to qualify than leads contacted within 30 minutes. Replicated across subsequent lead-response research and reaffirmed in 2026 benchmark reports.
  - Status: confirmed (canonical HBR/MIT finding, still cited in 2026)

- **Claim:** "Nearly four out of five buyers go with whoever calls back first."
  - Source: Rounded restatement of the NAR 78% figure for cadence.
  - Status: confirmed (derived from cited stat)

- **Claim (tactical):** "Turn push notifications on... write a sixty-second response... block the first five minutes of every hour for the next two weeks."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's recommended agent protocol. The push-notification + templated-response + lead-window combination is the standard speed-to-lead playbook in inside-sales coaching (Mike Ferry, Tom Ferry, Ylopo, BoomTown, FUB coaching content).
  - Status: tactical guidance
