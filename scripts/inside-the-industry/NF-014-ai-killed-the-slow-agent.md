# AI Didn't Kill the Real Estate Agent. It Killed the Slow Agent.

> **WOW: Contrarian take plus mindset reframe plus memorable one-liner. The dominant tech-doomer narrative has predicted AI's disintermediation of real estate agents for years. NAR's 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers shows 88% of buyers still use an agent. RISMedia's April 2026 reporting confirmed consumers expect AI in the homebuying process and still want a person. The agents losing business aren't losing it to AI. They're losing it to faster agents who use AI. The real disruption is response-time compression and workflow speed. The contrarian framing flips the industry's AI anxiety: the threat isn't AI replacing you, it's the agent across town using it faster than you.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** Tuesday, May 6, 2026
