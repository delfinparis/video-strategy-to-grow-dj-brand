# Realtor.com Just Built an AI That Does Half Your Job. Good.

> **WOW: News-pegged contrarian reframe. On June 2, 2026, Realtor.com launched RealAssist AI, a buyer-facing tool built on Google Maps AI that compares homes and neighborhoods side by side, runs personalized affordability math, and maps a listing against your daily commute. The doomer reading writes itself: AI is coming for agents. The reframe flips it. RealAssist didn't automate the agent's job. It automated the information-middleman part of the job, the comps-pulling and neighborhood-touring that felt like the value but never was. The buyer who runs RealAssist before calling you isn't a threat. They're a more qualified client. What's left, judgment, negotiation, and hyper-local knowledge, is the part AI can't touch and the part the good agents were always selling. Chicago layer: AI can't tell you which block floods every spring or which one's about to get reassessed. That's the human.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Avatars:** All
**Post Date:** Monday, June 2, 2026
