# Phone Photos Are Costing Agents Listings.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2025 seller survey: 78% of sellers are more likely to hire an agent who includes high resolution photography, and 75% say the same of virtual tours or interactive floor plans. The media package is part of the hiring decision, not just the marketing after it.**
