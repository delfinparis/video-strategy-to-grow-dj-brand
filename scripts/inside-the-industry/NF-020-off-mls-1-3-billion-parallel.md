# Dual Agency Cost Sellers $1.5B. Off-MLS Cost Them Another $1.3B. Same Problem.

> **WOW: Surprising statistic plus pattern reveal. Zillow's May 14, 2026 study reported two parallel findings from the same dataset. Sellers in dual-agency transactions lost $1.49 billion over three years. Sellers whose homes were marketed off-MLS (private listings, pocket listings, "coming soon" exclusives) lost another $1.3 billion. The take: both are transparency failures from the same root. When the agent narrows the buyer pool -- by representing both sides or by limiting marketing to a private network -- the seller pays. This is the macro counterpoint to the MRED Private Listing Network expansion (NF-008 era) and the Compass Private Exclusives push: the data on those structures is now in, and the seller is the one absorbing the cost.**
