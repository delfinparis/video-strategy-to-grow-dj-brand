# Stop Telling Buyers They Need 20% Down.

> **WOW: Zillow's 2025 buyer survey: 48% of mortgage buyers put down less than 20%, and 9% put down less than 3%. The median was 20%, which is exactly why the myth survives. Half the market bought without it.**

**Pillar:** Market Intelligence | **Hook Family:** Swap / List | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Buyer agents, agents working with renters and first-time buyers

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
Stop telling buyers they need twenty percent down.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)
