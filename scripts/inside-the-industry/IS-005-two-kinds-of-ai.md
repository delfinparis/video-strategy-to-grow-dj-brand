## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Two kinds of AI: generative (you prompt, it answers) and agentic (it monitors your database and acts on its own)"
  - Source: MoxiWorks, "AI Tools for Real Estate Agents" ([link](https://moxiworks.com/blog/ai-tools-for-real-estate-agents/)). Describes the shift "from generative AI -- tools that create content in response to prompts -- toward agentic AI: autonomous systems that monitor, surface, and act on behalf of agents without waiting to be asked."
  - Status: confirmed (conceptual framing, not a stat; MoxiWorks is a vendor source, so the distinction is used as industry framing rather than as a neutral statistic)

- **Claim:** "Lofty launched one for real estate back in February"
  - Source: MoxiWorks blog (above): "Lofty made a significant move in February 2026 with the launch of Lofty AOS, positioning itself as real estate's first agentic AI operating system." Lofty AOS coordinates specialized agents: lead prioritization, a sales agent that qualifies leads, a social media agent, a homeowner agent for automated seller prospecting, and a website builder.
  - Status: confirmed (product-launch fact, February 2026)

- **Claim:** "Surfaces the lead who just reopened your old email. Sends the follow-up. Flags the past client whose home value jumped."
  - Source: Illustrative examples of agentic capabilities, drawn from the Lofty AOS feature set described in the MoxiWorks blog (lead prioritization, automated engagement, automated seller prospecting). Presented as representative behaviors of the category, not a claim about a specific named product's exact feature list.
  - Status: confirmed (framing, grounded in the cited capability list)

- **Credibility anchor:** D.J. Paris teaches AI to real estate agents (VP Business Development at Kale Realty, 1 of 12 in NAR's 2026 influencer program, AI segments on the Keeping It Real Podcast). The "I teach AI to agents" positioning is implied by the hook framing rather than stated outright in this cut.
