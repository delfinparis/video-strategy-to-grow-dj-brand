# Stop Telling Sellers What You Do. Add This.

> **WOW: René Rodriguez, in Amplify Your Influence, says a point without its ending is "like a joke without a punchline. It's unfinished and doesn't add value." The ending he means spells out what the point is worth to the listener. Most listing pitches are a list of services with no ending at all.**

**Pillar:** Practice | **Hook Family:** Swap / List | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: Gesture-On-Beat (Palm-to-camera "stop" on "Stop," hand down on the next word. One finger up on "Add this.")

## Shareable Moment
> "After every point, add what it means for the seller."

## Script (30 seconds, walk and talk)

### HOOK (0:00-0:01.5)
Stop telling sellers what you do. Add this.

### TENSION (0:01.5-0:06.5)
If you don't say why it matters, the seller decides alone.

### THE POINT (0:06.5-0:21.5)
René Rodriguez wrote Amplify Your Influence. He says to end every point with why it matters. Don't just say, I hold open houses. Add, so more buyers see your house.

### PAYOFF (0:21.5-0:30)
Read your listing pitch tonight. After every point, add what it means for the seller.

**Duration:** 30 seconds | **Word Count:** 64 spoken words (HOOK 8 / TENSION 11 / THE POINT 30 / PAYOFF 15)

**Pattern interrupt:** Gesture-On-Beat confirmed. Per the cheatsheet, move 7 is what makes a Swap land. NF-096 uses The Stop and NF-097 uses Walk-Toward, so the three seller-conversation scripts never share a visual open.

**Why it should work:**
- Hook mechanism (Heath): "Add this" opens a loop the payoff closes by name, so the viewer knows exactly what they're waiting for. The open-house example is concrete, which beats the Curse of Knowledge: nobody has to picture what "a point's ending" sounds like.
- Share/save driver (Berger/Hormozi): Saves. It's a fix an agent can do to their own pitch tonight, and the one sample sentence is the thing they screenshot.
- Retention move (MrBeast): The before-and-after sample line at 0:15 is the moment. Say "I hold open houses" flat, then "so more buyers see your house" with weight.

**The dissent (your next A/B test):** Miller wants variant 3 because it puts the seller at the center from the first word. Heath warns it's a slogan the viewer has heard before, and it says the seller "doesn't care," which edges toward a claim Rodriguez never made. **Experiment:** test variant 2 (Forbidden, "missing one sentence") against the shipped Swap hook for 3-second hold.
