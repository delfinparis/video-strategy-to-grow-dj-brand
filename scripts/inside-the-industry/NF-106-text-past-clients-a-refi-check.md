# Stop Texting Past Clients "Just Checking In."

> **WOW: Redfin, Mar 12, 2026: "One in five (19.8%) U.S. homeowners with a mortgage could save money by refinancing to a lower rate." Yet "Just 9.1% of homeowners who could save money by refinancing to today's average rate (6.08%) have actually done so, as of the first quarter of this year," the lowest take-up rate since the beginning of 2020.**

## Teleprompter

Stop texting past clients "just checking in."

It asks for their time and gives them nothing back.

Give them a reason. Redfin ran the numbers in March. One in five homeowners with a mortgage could save by refinancing. Fewer than one in ten of them refinanced. Rates went up after that.

Don't promise savings. Text this. Want a lender to check, for free, if refinancing saves you money?
