# Four Showings, No Offer, and Your Seller Panics.

> **WOW: HomeLight's survey of 859 top agents (Dec 2025): "Nationwide, agents reported that it takes an average of 14 showings to sell a house in the current market."**

**Pillar:** Client Management | **Hook Family:** Mirror | **Heat:** 4 | **Avatars:** Listing agents
**Filming:** Walk and talk. Visual open: Walk-Toward (camera held out, walking straight at it on the hook).

## Shareable Moment
> "We'll judge the price at showing fourteen, not showing four."

## Teleprompter

Four showings, no offer, and your seller panics.

Don't let that panic decide the price of the house.

HomeLight surveyed top agents for its 2026 outlook. On average, they said it takes fourteen showings to sell a house. So four showings is too early to judge the price.

At the listing meeting, say this. We'll judge the price at showing fourteen, not showing four.

## Production Notes
- Walk-Toward: walk straight at the lens on "Four showings, no offer." Pairs with the Mirror family.
- Say "fourteen" slowly in PAYOFF. It is the number the seller remembers.
- Not a "never cut the price" script. The checkpoint is agreed up front, which is the same discipline as NF-067's "put the date in the agreement." If a viewer asks what happens at showing fourteen with no offer, that is a price conversation on a number both sides agreed to.
