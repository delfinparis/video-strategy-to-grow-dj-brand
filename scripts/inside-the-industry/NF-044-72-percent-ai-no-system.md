# 72% of Agents Use AI Daily. Most Built a Worse Version of What They Had.

> **WOW: Surprising statistic + mindset reframe -- 72% of agents now use AI every day, but most bolted on so many tools across so many functions that they made their own workflow worse. The fix is a system, not another tool: one tool per lane across leads, content, scheduling, and transactions.**

### HOOK (0:00-0:09)
Seventy-two percent of agents say AI is part of their daily workflow. Most of them built a worse version of what they already had.

### CONTEXT / MIRROR (0:09-0:25)
Look at your own setup. One AI tool for writing. Another for leads. A chatbot. Something for transactions. Three browser extensions you forgot about. None of them talk to each other. You adopted AI to save time. Now you're the IT department for your own business.

### THE FIX (0:25-0:44)
The rule that fixes it is simple. One tool per lane. You've got four. Leads. Content. Scheduling. Transactions. The agent who wins in 2026 isn't the one with the most AI tools. It's the one who actually learned the four they kept.

### CLOSE (0:44-0:58)
Here's what you do now. List every AI tool you're paying for. Sort them into those four lanes. Any lane with more than one, keep the best and cancel the rest. Any empty lane is your next move.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Seventy-two percent of agents say AI is part of their daily workflow"
  - Source: National Association of REALTORS 2025 Technology Survey. 72% of agents reported using at least one AI tool daily. Reported via Ascendix, "AI for Real Estate Agents" ([link](https://ascendix.com/blog/ai-real-estate-agents/)) and corroborated across industry coverage (Real Estate News, "AI use now the norm among real estate agents," 2026-01-29 [link](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/01/29/ai-use-now-the-norm-among-real-estate-agents)).
  - Who was measured: REALTORS / practicing agents (agent-reported, NAR membership survey).
  - Status: confirmed
  - Accuracy note: The 72% figure is the NAR 2025 Technology Survey number ("use at least one AI tool daily"), which maps cleanly to "AI is part of their daily workflow." A newer 82% figure exists (Realtors Property Resource survey, February 2026) but comes from a much smaller sample (225 professionals). The 72% NAR Technology Survey figure is the better-sourced, flagship number and is the one used here. If D.J. prefers the fresher 82%, swap the hook and re-cite to the RPR Feb 2026 survey, noting the smaller sample.

- **Claim:** "One tool per lane... Leads. Content. Scheduling. Transactions."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's workflow framework. The four-lane model is a tactical organizing system, not a cited statistic.
  - Status: confirmed (framework, not a stat)

- **Claim:** "The agent who wins in 2026 isn't the one with the most AI tools. It's the one with the fewest, each doing one job all the way."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's reframe / opinion. Not a stat.
  - Status: confirmed (opinion, not a stat)

- **Production note -- no named tools on purpose:** The script deliberately avoids naming specific "winning" AI tools. Three reasons: (1) the source roundup could not be independently verified at draft time; (2) named tools date a short-form video within weeks as products change; (3) D.J.'s brand is built on judgment and systems, not product endorsements. The tip is the lane system, not a shopping list. If D.J. wants a follow-up that names his actual per-lane picks, that should be a separate script where each tool is one he genuinely uses and can stand behind.
