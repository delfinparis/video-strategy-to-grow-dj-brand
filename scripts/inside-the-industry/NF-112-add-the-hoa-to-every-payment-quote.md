# You Quoted the Payment Without the HOA.

> **WOW: Realtor.com, Jan 27, 2026: "nearly 44% of homes for sale now subject to a monthly HOA fee," up from 34.3% in 2019. For condos and townhomes it is 84.8%. The median HOA fee reached $135 in 2025, up from $108 in 2019.**

- **Claim:** "Realtor.com checked homes for sale in 2025. Nearly forty-four percent have a monthly HOA fee."

  - Exact wording: "Homeowners associations (HOAs) continued their steady growth across the U.S. housing market in 2025, with nearly 44% of homes for sale now subject to a monthly HOA fee." / "In 2025, 43.6% of U.S. home listings included a non-zero HOA fee, up from 41.9% last year and well above pre-pandemic levels."
  - Who was measured: for-sale listings on Realtor.com (weekly snapshots, 2019 to 2025). A listing counts if it shows a monthly HOA fee above zero.
  - Status: confirmed (re-opened 2026-10-14). "Nearly forty-four percent" is Realtor.com's own rounding of 43.6%.
- **Claim:** "For condos and townhomes, it's nearly eighty-five percent."
  - Source: same release. Exact wording: "HOAs remain far more common among condos and townhomes, with 84.8% of those listings subject to monthly dues."
  - Status: confirmed (re-opened 2026-10-14). 84.8% said as "nearly eighty-five percent," the same rounding style the release uses for 43.6%. Actual number documented here per Rule 1.
- **Claim:** "The typical fee is a hundred thirty-five dollars a month."
  - Source: same release. Exact wording: "The median HOA fee reached $135 in 2025, up from $125 last year and $108 in 2019." Methodology: "Median monthly HOA dues calculations for a given geography or listing segment include only nonzero HOA dues."
  - Status: confirmed (re-opened 2026-10-14). "Median" said as "typical"; it is the median among homes that have a fee.
- **Claim:** "The real monthly cost is higher than the number you gave." / payoff action.
  - Source: editorial. A quote without a fee the buyer must pay is lower than the real cost. No statistic implied.
  - Status: commentary.
