# AI Keeps Offering to Do Your Work.

> **WOW: Tim Ferriss, The Tim Ferriss Show #859 (Mar 26, 2026), asked what you should never use AI for: "I would say any skill you want to preserve in your head, you should probably not use AI for." He describes the model offering to rewrite his draft "because I want to keep you using the model," and says "that's where I have deliberately hesitated." Then: "if you lose it, it's a hell of a lot harder to reclaim it." Criteria hit: a famous name pushing back on the tool everyone is told to use for everything, with a picture every viewer knows (Google Maps and your sense of direction).
