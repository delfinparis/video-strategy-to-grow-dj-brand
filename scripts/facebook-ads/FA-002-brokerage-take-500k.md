## Production Notes

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical for Reels + Stories placement, 1:1 square as secondary cut for Feed.
- **On-screen text overlay (hard requirement):** `$3,125` hits on a hard cut at 0:09 in large bold type. Hold 2 seconds.
- **Supporting overlays:** `$500,000 sale price` at 0:01, `2.5% = $12,500` at 0:05, `75/25 split` at 0:07. Stack the math one line at a time so the viewer is doing the arithmetic in their head before the reveal lands.
- **B-roll:** none. Walk-and-talk straight to camera. Same Chicago exterior setup as FA-001 so the four ads cut together as a sequence if Meta serves them to the same user.
- **Wardrobe:** match FA-001 exactly. The four-ad sequence reads as ONE campaign visually -- changing the shirt between ads breaks the pattern.
- **End frame:** 0:14-0:15 hold on D.J.'s face after "we should talk." No outro card. Music drops to silence under the close.
- **Click destination:** same single-page recruiting URL as FA-001.

## Data Source

- **Claim ($500,000 × 2.5% = $12,500):** standard real estate single-side commission math. Arithmetic, not a cited source.
- **Claim ($12,500 × 25% = $3,125):** standard 75/25 split math. Hypothetical.
- **Editorial note on rounding:** $3,125 is exact. Do not round.
