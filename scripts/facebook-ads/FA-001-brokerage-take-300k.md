## Production Notes

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical for Reels + Stories placement, 1:1 square as secondary cut for Feed.
- **On-screen text overlay (hard requirement):** the dollar figure `$1,875` should hit on a hard cut at 0:09 in large bold type, lower-third or center. This is the visual scroll-stop. Let it sit 2 seconds.
- **Supporting overlays:** `$300,000 sale price` at 0:01, `2.5% = $7,500` at 0:05, `75/25 split` at 0:07. Stacked math, one line at a time, so the viewer follows the calculation in their head before the reveal.
- **B-roll:** none needed. Walk-and-talk straight to camera, exterior Chicago background per filming guide.
- **Wardrobe:** Kale-neutral. No Kale logo visible -- this is a Meta ad, not a Kale-branded organic post. The brand reveal happens in the landing page, not the creative.
- **End frame:** 0:14-0:15 hold on D.J.'s face after "we should talk." No outro card, no logo flash. The ad ends on the human, not the brand. Lower the music to silence under the final sentence.
- **Click destination:** djparis.com/recruit (or whatever single-page recruiting URL Kale is running for this campaign). Not joinkale.com top-level. Single-page = single-decision.

## Data Source

- **Claim ($300,000 × 2.5% = $7,500):** standard real estate commission math, single-side gross commission income (GCI). Not a cited source -- arithmetic.
- **Claim ($7,500 × 25% = $1,875):** standard split math. 75/25 is a commonly used competitor split structure in Chicago and most major metros. Not cited because it's a hypothetical, not a market claim.
- **Editorial note on rounding:** the figure $1,875 is exact. Do not round to $1,900 or "about $1,875" in any cut of this ad. Precision IS the credibility signal.
