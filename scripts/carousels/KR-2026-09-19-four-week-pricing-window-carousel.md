## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The gap is more than three points.

**Body:**
Realtor.com's own research, released June 2026 by senior economist Joel Berner, found homes that
close around the four-week mark sell 1.8 percent above the norm relative to their asking price.
Homes still sitting on the market at eighteen weeks close 1.3 percent below it. That is a gap of
more than three percentage points between a home that sells fast and one that doesn't, on the
same asking price. The agent who quotes the highest number to win the listing isn't doing the
seller a favor. They're setting up the slow sale that costs the seller the difference.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Protect the seller from this before you sign:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Price to the comps, not to the competition for the listing.** A number no other agent will match isn't a strategy. It's a discount, paid later.
2. **Show the seller the actual math.** A home that sells in four weeks nets more than one still sitting at eighteen. Put both numbers in front of them before they pick an agent.
3. **Put the price-cut schedule in writing on day one.** If the seller wants to test a high number, agree now on when and how much it drops.
4. **Track your own listings' days on market.** The gap shows up in your own numbers, not just Realtor.com's.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Fast and priced right beats high and hoping.

**Subhead:**
One sourced number, one seller conversation, before you sign the next listing agreement.
