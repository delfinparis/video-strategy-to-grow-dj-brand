## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
This isn't a traffic problem. It's a seriousness problem.

**Body:**
Zillow measures "engaged shoppers" as people who actually saved or shared a listing, not people
who scrolled past it. That number just dropped to 3.5 per listing nationally for the third
quarter of this year, down almost five percent from a year ago, the lowest Zillow has ever
tracked. The portal isn't sending you fewer names. It's sending you a smaller pool of people who
are genuinely deciding, which means the follow-up script built for a crowded, competitive buyer
pool is talking to a buyer who isn't there anymore.
