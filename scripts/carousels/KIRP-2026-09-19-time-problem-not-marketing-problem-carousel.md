**Headline:**
Run this before you add one more system:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Name one commitment that isn't real estate or family.** A board seat, a committee, a training block. Something real, not a rounding error.
2. **Look three to five years out, not just this quarter.** Opyd's method with Farrell: if this year repeats exactly, where does that actually leave you?
3. **Trade that one commitment for the block your follow-up keeps missing.** Same day, same time, every week.
4. **Protect that block like a client's closing.** A stalled business isn't short on tactics. It's short on time.
