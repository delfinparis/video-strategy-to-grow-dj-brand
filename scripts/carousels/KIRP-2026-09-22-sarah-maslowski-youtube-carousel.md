## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
She removed the decision before she needed to make it.

**Body:**
Maslowski committed to three months of weekly videos before she filmed the first one, no matter how few views came back. And she made a rule before she had a single subscriber: if she didn't have an editor, she wasn't putting out a video. Both decisions happened before the empty view counts showed up, which is the only time a decision like that gets made cleanly. Two years in, the channel that had earned nothing put five deals under contract in one month while she was on vacation in Europe.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Before you post your first video:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Set a hard runway before you film video one.** "I was going to at least try this for three months before I gave up" is a decision made once, not renegotiated every week the views stay flat.
2. **Hire an editor before you need one.** Maslowski's rule from day one: no editor, no video. Removing her own out kept a bad week from becoming a missed week.
3. **Bank two or three videos before launch.** She had videos ready to go before her first upload, so one slow week never showed up as a gap on the channel.
4. **Put a scheduling link in place once leads start calling.** She runs it through Go High Level so a lead who calls in doesn't turn into a week of back-and-forth on a calendar.
