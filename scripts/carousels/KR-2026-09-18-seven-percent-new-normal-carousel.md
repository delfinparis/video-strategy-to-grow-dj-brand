## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The leverage window is right now, not later.

**Body:**
NAR chief economist Lawrence Yun said on September 16 that rates could ease only if oil prices
fall and a credible federal deficit plan appears, and called both "highly uncertain." Meanwhile
Redfin reported pending sales fell to 299,126 that week, the lowest level in nearly three years
and down 5.4 percent from a year earlier. Telling a buyer to wait for 6 percent again asks them to
sit out a window where they're actually negotiating from strength, in exchange for a rate cut
nobody can put a date on.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to do differently this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Give buyers the honest math, not the hopeful one.** "7 percent is what NAR's chief economist
   is calling the new normal, not a temporary spike." Set that expectation before the first
   showing, not after they've fallen for a house they can't recalculate for.
2. **Bring the reduced-competition number into the pitch.** Pending sales just hit their lowest
   point in nearly three years. Fewer buyers are chasing the same inventory right now.
3. **Reframe the seller conversation around days on market, not price.** With buyers slower to
   commit, a seller's pricing strategy needs a longer runway built in, not a faster one.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**

hear it from someone else.

**Subhead:**
This is the market. Work the leverage that's actually in it.
