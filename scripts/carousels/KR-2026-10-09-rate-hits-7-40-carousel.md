## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What you do with every "waiting for rates" buyer this week:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pull their real numbers today.** Run the payment at 7.40 percent on the house they liked
   three months ago, side by side with what it would have cost them then.
2. **Say the trend out loud.** Tell them rates have risen seven straight weeks since the Fed's
   hike, not dropped once. Waiting has been a bet, and it hasn't paid off yet.
3. **Price the wait, not just the house.** Show them what another six months of this trend costs
   if it continues, against what locking in now protects.
4. **Ask them to decide on the numbers, not the guess.** "Are you deciding based on what's in
   front of you, or on what you're hoping happens next?"
