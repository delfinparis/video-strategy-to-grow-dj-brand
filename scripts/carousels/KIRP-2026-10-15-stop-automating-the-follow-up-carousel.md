## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
We never know when a client decides. We control whether we're there when they do.

**Body:**
Most agents' past-client touch is automated: a drip email, a holiday card that's digitally
signed. It feels like contact. It isn't memorable, because nobody made it. Carrie McCormick says
she's stayed away from automated touchpoints on purpose and instead calls, sends newsletters,
and stays visible on social media. Her own words on why: "we never know when our clients are
going to make a decision, but when they do, we want to be right there for them."

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**What she does instead of the drip:**
1. **Stop the auto-email.** A digitally signed card or an automated "thinking of you" note reads
   like everyone else's, because it is.
2. **Call instead of letting an app send something.** She picks up the phone with past clients
   rather than relying on an automated touch sequence.
3. **Post consistently, so they keep seeing you.** Clients who scroll but never comment are
   still watching. Showing up in the feed repeatedly keeps you top of mind without asking them
   for anything.
4. **Be the easiest person to reach when they decide.** You don't control the timing. You
   control whether you're reachable the moment it happens.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Pick three past clients today. Call them. Don't let an app do it for you.

**Subhead:**
Being first never won the client. Being reachable when they decided did.
