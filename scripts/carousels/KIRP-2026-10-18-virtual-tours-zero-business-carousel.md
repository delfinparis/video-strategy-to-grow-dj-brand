**Headline:**
Where to put that budget instead:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Ask what the virtual tour actually costs per listing.** Most agents have never put that number next to the deals it produced.
2. **Put that dollar amount into direct outreach to your past clients instead.** A call or a handwritten note still outperforms a 3D walkthrough nobody asked to see.
3. **Keep the tour only for the one buyer who genuinely can't attend in person.** Use it as a convenience for that showing, not a marketing line item on every listing.
