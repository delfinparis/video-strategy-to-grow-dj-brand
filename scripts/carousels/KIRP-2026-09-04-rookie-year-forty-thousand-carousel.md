## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The bio says $55 million. The rookie year said $40,000.

**Body:**
D.J. introduced Chris Wands by his record $55 million sale and a first year of "35 properties."
Chris stopped him. "I want to correct you on one thing," he said. "It was 35 transactions, not 35
sales... about five sales and 30-plus rentals... I think I made like $40,000 that year." The
polished version of a career skips the part that actually built it. His was mostly rentals worth a
few hundred dollars in commission each, at a time he says he "talked to everyone" and "talked to
the wall if I could" just to make himself useful.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What Chris Wands tells agents stuck on small commissions:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Take the $1,000 rental.** "What is a thousand dollars versus not? It's still a thousand dollars," he said, when asked what he'd tell someone disheartened by a small check.
2. **Don't skip it because you're "too busy."** He pointed at agents who claim they have no time, then won't work a $1,000 rental someone hands them.
3. **Expect it to compound, not pay off immediately.** His own words: "that $1,000 rental could lead you to the $20,000 rental, which can lead you to the $20,000 buyer."
