## SLIDE 3 -- WHAT IT IS
**Headline:**
It is called a pop-by.

**Body:**
A low-cost gift you drop off in person to someone in your sphere. No pitch. No "know anyone selling." Just a hello.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- WHY IT WORKS
**Headline:**
It is a relationship, not a portal.

**Body:**
Paid leads rent you a stranger's attention once. A pop-by keeps you top of mind with people who already trust you.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- THE CADENCE
**Headline:**
The rhythm that makes it compound.

**Body:**
Same people, four times a year. Consistency is the whole trick. One drop-off is a nice gesture. Four is a system.

---

## SLIDE 6 -- WHAT TO BRING
**Headline:**
Keep the gift small and seasonal.

**Body:**
Coffee on the way to a showing. A pumpkin on the porch in October. A school-supply pack in August. Thoughtful beats expensive.
