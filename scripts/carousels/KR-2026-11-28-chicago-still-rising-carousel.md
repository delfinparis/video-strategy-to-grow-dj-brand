## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Here's what Redfin actually found.

**Body:**
Redfin's October index shows U.S. home prices up **0.14%** month over month, the smallest gain in
six months, with buyers holding more negotiating power than at any point in Redfin's records back
to 2013. Only 3 of the 50 biggest metros are still seller's markets. Chicago broke from that
pattern. Prices here rose 1.1% in October and 9.5% over the year, the third-largest monthly gain
and third-largest annual gain of any metro Redfin tracks. Redfin's head of economics research,
Chen Zhao, points to sellers nationally staying anchored to prices from the past few years, which
is holding a floor under the market even as higher rates keep demand soft.
