**The one idea:** "most agents use AI" is true and also not the useful number. NAR's own 2026
REALTORS Technology Report splits usage into daily (23%), weekly (25%), occasional (31%),
curious-but-untried (9%), and none (12%) -- so 48% have a real habit, and 40% are still at the
tried-it-once stage the survey counts as "using AI." The fix isn't becoming an AI power user,
it's picking the one task agents who already use it say works (listing descriptions, 75%) and
running it every week instead of once. Hook family `myth-bust` (undoing "I already use AI" as

## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The real split isn't yes or no. It's daily versus once.

**Body:**
NAR's 2026 REALTORS Technology Report breaks agent AI use into five groups: 23 percent use it
daily, 25 percent use it weekly, 31 percent are still experimenting occasionally, 9 percent are
curious but haven't tried it, and 12 percent aren't using it and don't plan to. Forty eight

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
How to move from "tried it" to "daily," this week:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pick the task agents who already use it say works.** Listing descriptions, the single most
   common use among agents who use AI, at 75 percent.
2. **Run it on every listing this week, not one.** One use is a demo. Every listing is a habit.
3. **Keep the same tool for seven days straight.** Switching tools every time you try resets you
   back to "experimenting."
4. **Add a second task only after the first is automatic.** Social captions and follow-up emails
   are next most common, at 56 and 52 percent. Stack them one at a time.
