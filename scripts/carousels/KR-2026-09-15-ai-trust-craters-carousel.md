## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
AI adoption went up. Trust in it went down.

**Body:**
Cotality's 2026 AI in Housing report surveyed U.S. homebuyers between late January and early
February. Trust in AI to help find a home fell fourteen percentage points, from thirty percent
in 2025 to sixteen percent in 2026, even as AI tools kept showing up across more of the buying
process. Fifty-five percent of buyers said they'd rather work with a person to secure a
mortgage, up from forty-six percent the year before, and forty-four percent said they'd pay
extra just to have a human check an AI's work. The tools got better. The willingness to hand the
biggest financial decision of someone's life to a chatbot did not.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What this means for how you market yourself:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Put your name on the number, not the app.** A buyer trusts you to check the AI estimate,
   not the estimate to check itself.
2. **Say the quiet part in your marketing.** "I read every disclosure myself" and "I call the
   lender, I don't send a bot" are differentiators now, not throwbacks.
3. **Lead with a transaction you walked someone through**, not a tool you use. The receipts a
   chatbot can't produce are the ones a buyer remembers.
4. **Offer the verification before they ask for it.** Buyers already said they'd pay for a human
   to double check AI. Be the one who offers it first.
