## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Their 2-3% rate was never the **real** wall.

**Body:**
Agents assume a client sitting on a 2021-2022 rate is done moving and cross them off the list. Austin's point: life doesn't wait for rates. A new baby, in-laws moving in, a new job, the house still has to change. What most agents miss is the client doesn't have to sell that rate away to do it. After 12 months in the home, a lease on the old property can help them qualify for the next mortgage, rate and all, still intact.

## SLIDE 4 -- WHAT TO DO DIFFERENTLY MONDAY (screenshot payload)
**Numbered list:**
1. **Call your sub-4% clients first.** Austin says these are the owners who've mentally written themselves off. They're actually the easiest yes in your database, they just don't know it yet.
2. **Pitch the rental, not the sale.** After living in a home 12 months, a lease can help a client qualify for the next mortgage without giving up the old rate.
3. **Point them to Zillow and ChatGPT.** Free tenant screening and background checks on Zillow, a free lease template start in ChatGPT. The landlord jump is smaller than it sounds.
