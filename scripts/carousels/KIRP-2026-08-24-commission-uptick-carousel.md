## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Refusing to pay a buyer's agent doesn't save your seller money.

**Body:**
Redfin's own data shows the average U.S. buyer's agent commission fell to 2.36% in the third
quarter of 2024, the quarter the new NAR rules took effect, then climbed back to 2.42% by the
third quarter of 2025. A seller who won't offer a buyer-agent concession isn't cutting a cost.
They're shrinking their buyer pool to whoever can cover that fee on their own, and the math
pushes the number back up, not down.

What to say at the listing appointment:

**Numbered list:**

3. **Price the concession in from the start.** Make it a line item the seller expects at listing, not a surprise renegotiation at the offer.
