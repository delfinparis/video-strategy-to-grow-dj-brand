## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Here's what that actually changes about your pipeline.

**Body:**
NAR's chief economist, Lawrence Yun, says buyers could see sizable pent-up demand if mortgage
rates decline, which is another way of saying a lot of first-timers are sitting out and waiting.
Three straight months of a shrinking first-time share means a bigger slice of every closing
right now is coming from people who already own. The agents still closing deals are talking to
that group, not just hunting for someone buying their first home.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
What to change about where you spend your time this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Call your past sellers before your past buyers.** Someone sitting on equity is more likely
   to transact right now than someone waiting on their first shot.
2. **Stop assuming every new lead is a first-timer.** Ask about their current home status before
   you build the plan. The mix just shifted under you.
3. **Treat the first-time buyers who do show up as serious.** Anyone still house hunting despite
   these numbers isn't tire-kicking. They're motivated enough to push through a shrinking window.
