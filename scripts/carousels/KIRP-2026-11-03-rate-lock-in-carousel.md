## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Most agents read "not now" as "not motivated."

**Body:**
The data says otherwise. A seller sitting on a rate from a few years ago isn't comparing their
house to the comps, they're comparing their current payment to what a new loan on their next
house would cost them every month, and right now that gap is real money. Some are solving it
without you: the share of homes listed for rent on Zillow that were previously listed for sale
hit its second-highest level in nearly six years this year. If you don't bring up the math, they
go find their own answer, and it might not involve selling at all.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to do differently this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Ask for their current rate before you ask for their timeline.** The number stopping them is
   their rate, not the market.
2. **Run the monthly payment gap with them, in dollars, on paper.** Their current payment next to
   the payment on their likely next home at today's rate. Not percentages, actual numbers.
3. **Bring up renting instead of selling, yourself, first.** Some sellers will choose it over
   listing. Raise it before they do and you stay their agent either way.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Here's what you do now.

**Subhead:**
Pull your hesitant-seller list today. Call the top three and ask one question: what rate are you on right now? Build the math with them before you build the pitch.
