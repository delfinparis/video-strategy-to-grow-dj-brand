## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Gwen's follow-up system, four pieces:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Send 50 to 100 "just sold" postcards to the whole neighborhood after every closing.** Not just the buyer or seller. The block that watched it happen.
2. **Solve the problem before the client asks.** Gwen calls it being a concierge REALTOR. "Doing most and more is what you got to do."
3. **Meet 60 new people a year, one a week.** Coffee, breakfast, lunch. Not a pitch, just a relationship added to the list.
4. **Add every past client to your CRM and follow up, especially after they've moved in.** That's the step Gwen says most agents skip.

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY

Pick one past client today. **Follow up.**
