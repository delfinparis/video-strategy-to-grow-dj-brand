## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What changes when the payment is the objection:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Bring the lender into the first conversation, not the third.** A payment scenario answers the real objection faster than another listing.
2. **Show the monthly number before the sale price.** Affordability is a payment problem, and the price alone doesn't show that.
3. **Ask about the down payment gap up front.** It surfaces the actual blocker instead of guessing at "the wrong house" for weeks.
