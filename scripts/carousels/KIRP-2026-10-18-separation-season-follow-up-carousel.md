## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Most agents aren't losing to a better agent. They're losing to **silence.**

**Body:**
Ben's point on Keeping It Real: nearly everyone a Realtor talks to already has an agent, so most agents hear that and move on. But 85% of agents never contact their client again after the sale, which means "already has an agent" and "has nobody actually checking in" are usually the same person. Ben told a story about a stranger at an event who couldn't even remember the name of the agent who sold them their home. The job isn't beating that agent on a pitch. It's being the one still there when the other one went quiet.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What Ben does instead:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pull your last 10 closed deals and trace each one back to the real source.** Not "referral." The specific person and the specific moment it started.
2. **Write down the 15 things you did this year that actually led to a deal, and repeat only those for the next 90 days.** Most of what fills a calendar isn't on that list.
3. **Say this to anyone who already has an agent:** "I'm glad you have somebody. I'd love to be your backup in case anything ever changes." Then stay in touch until it does.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
The agent who stays in touch wins the client who's already "taken."

**Subhead:**
Follow-up is the whole game once the sale is done.
