Carrie says AI can hand a buyer five recent closings in a neighborhood in seconds. What it misses is the telephone pole behind one of those lots, or the mold in another one's basement, the actual reason that home sold at a discount. The data is public. The story behind it isn't.

## SLIDE 4 -- WHAT TO DO DIFFERENTLY MONDAY (screenshot payload)
**Numbered list:**
1. **Stop letting AI write your listing descriptions.** Carrie says clients can already spot the tell: the same phrasing, the same hyphens, every time.
2. **Post the story the data can't see.** The mold, the pole, the reason a comp sold low. That's the value no AI search replicates.
3. **Sell the lifestyle before the square footage.** Luxury buyers are already asking AI for walkability and school quality before they ask for a listing. Answer that in your content first.
