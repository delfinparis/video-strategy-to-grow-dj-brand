## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
95 percent of his first year came from two free things.

**Body:**
Chris Wolfe moved to El Dorado Hills, California with no sphere of influence and no past
clients to call. Ninety five percent of his 32 to 33 deals that first year came from just two
sources, split about 50/50: cold calling expired listings and for-sale-by-owners, and hosting
open houses. He says plainly that he hated the cold calling part, and did it anyway. "It was
that ingredient of desperation," he told D.J. on the podcast. "I need now business." Desperation
is not a strategy anyone recommends. The two activities it pushed him into are the strategy, and
they cost nothing but time.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What built the first $510,000, in order:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Cold call expired listings and for-sale-by-owners, and circle prospect around them.** He
   memorized scripts and still found it hard. He made the calls anyway, every day.
2. **Host open houses on a regular cadence, including the slow season.** He started in winter,
   hauling signs through the rain. It was the fastest way to meet people already in the market.
3. **Know your own split.** He can name the exact ratio, about 50/50 between the two sources,
   because he tracked it. You cannot double down on what you have not measured.
4. **Put your daily non-negotiables in a system you check every morning.** He started on a paper
   checklist and later built it into software, Activate Daily, because seeing the boxes get
   checked kept him consistent on the days motivation did not show up.
