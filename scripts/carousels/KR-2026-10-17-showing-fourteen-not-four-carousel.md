## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to agree on before the sign goes up:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Set the price checkpoint at the listing meeting.** Tell the seller up front: "We'll judge the price at showing fourteen, not showing four."
2. **Name the number before the panic call.** A seller who already knows fourteen is average does not spiral after four.
3. **This isn't "never cut the price."** The same agents told HomeLight that 57 percent favor pricing slightly below market to drive offers in 2026. The checkpoint is about timing, not about holding a number too long.
