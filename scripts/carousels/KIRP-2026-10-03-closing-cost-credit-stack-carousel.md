## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
His fix: stack a lender credit on top of a seller credit.

**Body:**
Maldonado works with a lender who offers lender credits, then writes a seller credit into the
same contract. Combined, the two cover a gap a down payment alone never does. "We're getting them
lender credits while at the same time writing into the contract that the seller gives the seller
credits," he says. "That's basically what we do to get them covered."

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Maldonado's exact sequence on every credit-heavy offer:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Ask the lender for a credit first.** It closes part of the gap before the seller is even in
   the conversation.
2. **Write the seller credit into the offer as a term, not a request.** Price the offer a little
   higher to cover it, so the seller's net doesn't drop.
3. **Add 3% earnest money if the credit makes the offer look weak.** It shows the commitment the
   credit alone doesn't.
4. **Offer a faster escrow.** Maldonado's lender can close in 21 days, which matters most to a
   seller who needs the cash fast.
