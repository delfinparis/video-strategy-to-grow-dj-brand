## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The equity is real. The system built to use it is not.

**Body:**
Home equity held by homeowners age sixty-two and older climbed to a record $14.66 trillion in
the third quarter of 2025, up nearly two percent from the prior quarter, according to the
NRMLA/RiskSpan Reverse Mortgage Market Index. Most of that wealth sits behind a mortgage system
built around a paycheck, and retirement income does not fit that shape. A seller who looks
"equity rich, income light" on paper is often just a seller the standard underwriting model was
not built to read.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Bring this into your next conversation with a seller sixty-two or older:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Ask what they've already looked into.** A straight sale is one option among several. Some sellers have priced downsizing against a reverse mortgage or a HELOC and never said so out loud.
2. **Name the equity, specifically.** "Homeowners your age are holding a record amount of home equity right now" opens a different conversation than "are you ready to list?"
3. **Bring a lender who reads retirement income.** Standard debt-to-income underwriting often misreads Social Security, pensions, and investment draws. A referral to a lender fluent in that gap keeps you useful even if the client's best move is not a sale at all.
