## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The numbers, and who is counting them.

**Body:**
Compass studied more than 70,000 of its own closed sales from March 2025 to March 2026 and found
homes marketed in phases, first shown privately or to Compass's own network, then moved to the
full public listing site, sold for 4.6 percent more and closed 34 percent faster than homes listed
straight to the MLS. That is real money across a real number of closings. It is also Compass
grading its own private-listing program, the one it profits from every time an agent uses it, so
the number is worth naming out loud before pitching it to a seller as settled fact.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Try phased marketing on your next listing:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Start private for about a week.** Show it to your own network or brokerage before it goes to the full public listing site.
2. **Use that week for real feedback.** Price and condition reactions, without the public showing-count clock already running.
3. **Move it to the full public listing after that week.** It shows up looking wanted, not sitting.
4. **Track your own numbers next time, not just one company's study.** Compass has a stake in this answer. Your own closings are the receipt that actually applies to you.
