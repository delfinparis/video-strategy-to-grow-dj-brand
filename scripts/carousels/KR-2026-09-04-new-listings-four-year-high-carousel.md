## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The market didn't slow down. It got more crowded.

**Body:**
New listings of U.S. homes for sale rose 2.1 percent week over week on a seasonally adjusted
basis for the four weeks ending August 30, 2026, according to Redfin, the highest level since
August 2022. Total homes for sale grew far slower, up just 0.4 percent in the same window, and
buyer demand stayed flat. That gap is the story: every new listing this month is landing in front
of buyers who already have more to compare it against than they did in July.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What changes at your next listing appointment:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pull the current active competition before you talk price, not last month's comps.** Show the seller exactly what else is on the market in their band right now.

3. **Get it showing-ready before the sign goes up.** A buyer with more options skips the listing that still needs imagination.
