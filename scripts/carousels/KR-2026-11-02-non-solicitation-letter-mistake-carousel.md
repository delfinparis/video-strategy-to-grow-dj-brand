## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Before you sign anything with a non-solicitation clause:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Find the actual definition of "solicit" in your own agreement.** Some contracts count any service to a past client as a violation, not just an invitation.
2. **Check the time window.** Two years is common. Know the exact date it starts and the date it ends.
3. **Ask what happens to clients you brought with you.** A clause written to protect the company's clients can get applied to yours too.
4. **Get a real answer before you leave, not after a letter arrives.** A lawyer reads the clause once. A cease-and-desist letter reads it for you.
