## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Google ranks the name on file. Not the name on your business card.

**Body:**
Alex Maldonado, a REALTOR at Circle Real Estate in Los Angeles, told D.J. on Keeping It Real that
most agents lose local search visibility over one fixable detail: their Google Business Profile
name, address, and phone number don't exactly match their state license. Google treats a
mismatch as a weaker, less trustworthy listing and ranks it lower. Maldonado also keeps his
specialty written directly into the profile's title and category, not buried in a bio paragraph,
so the exact words a buyer searches are already on the page.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Fix your profile this week:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pull up your state license.** Compare it to your Google Business Profile name, character for character. No nicknames, no "Team," no extra words either side adds on its own.
2. **Put your specialty in the title, not the bio.** "First-time buyer specialist, [your city]" belongs in the business category or services field, where Google reads it, not in a paragraph nobody opens.
3. **Match your name, address, and phone everywhere.** Same spelling and the same suite number on your website, your brokerage page, and every social profile. One mismatch and Google treats you as a different business.
4. **Recheck it every quarter.** A team change or a new office address breaks the match again, and the profile never flags it for you.

Open your Google Business Profile tonight. Check the name against your license.
