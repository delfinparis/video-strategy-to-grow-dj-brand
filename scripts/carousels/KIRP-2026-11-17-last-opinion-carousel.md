## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Here's why that happened.

**Body:**
Williams says sellers already check their home's estimated value, run a number through an AI
tool, or request an actual cash offer before they ever pick up the phone. By her count, **150,000
homeowners a month** are requesting an online cash offer alone. By the time an agent gets the
call, the seller has already formed an opinion. You're not informing them anymore. You're
confirming or correcting what they already decided.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
What Vija tells agents to do about it:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Put a home value tool on your own site.** It gives a seller a reason to run into you during
   the research phase instead of after it.
2. **Watch who keeps checking their number.** A lead who returns to a valuation report more than
   once is getting close to selling, before they've told anyone.
3. **Walk into every appointment with four or five exit options, not one.** A cash offer is a
   tool in the belt now, next to listing it, renting it, or waiting.
