being told to be careful. They remember who never gave them a number to call first.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Hand this to every buyer at onboarding:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Don't rely on a spoken warning at the start of the transaction.** A verbal "watch out for scams" is forgotten before the first wire ever moves.
2. **Do put it in writing at onboarding.** "I will never send wiring instructions by email or text. If you get any, call me first at this number before you move money."
3. **Do give them the number on paper, not just in a text thread.** A saved contact survives a spoofed inbox. A buried text does not.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
The written callback rule is the protocol. The warning was never enough.

**Subhead:**
Hand it over before the first wire, not after the first scare.
