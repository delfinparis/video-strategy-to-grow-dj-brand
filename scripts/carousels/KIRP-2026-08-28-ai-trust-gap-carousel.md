rather than trusting the brief's paraphrase, per Rule 1: fetched
`cotality.com/insights/analysis/trust-but-verify-ai` directly. Cotality's AI in Housing Survey
(published April 15, 2026) found trust in AI to help find a home fell to 16% of U.S. prospective
buyers, down from 30% in 2025 (a 14-point drop), while the share who prefer working with a person
to secure a mortgage rose to 55%, up from 46% in 2025 (a 9-point increase). Survey covered
recent buyers (purchased within 5 years) and prospective buyers (planning within 2-5 years)
across the U.S., Canada, U.K., and Australia, fielded January 29 to February 9, 2026.

worried about being replaced by AI search tools. Rule 0 criterion 4, a surprising, sourced

## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The AI they trust least is the one closest to their money.

**Body:**
Cotality's 2026 AI in Housing survey found trust in AI for home search fell from 30 percent to
16 percent in a year, while the share of buyers who prefer a human for their mortgage rose from
46 percent to 55 percent. Buyers will let AI suggest a neighborhood. They won't let it near the
number that decides what they can actually afford. The agents who lean into that gap, instead of
competing with the AI on search, are the ones buyers still call first.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What this changes about how you market yourself:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Stop competing with the portal.** Buyers already trust AI more than you for the first scroll through listings. Fighting that is a losing position.
2. **Lead with the mortgage conversation, not the listing tour.** That's the exact moment buyers say they want a person, not a model.
3. **Put your name next to a lender's, not a search feature's.** Trust transfers between people. It does not transfer between apps.
