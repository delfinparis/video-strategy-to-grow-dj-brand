## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to do instead of skipping the call:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Don't skip the call because it feels awkward.** That discomfort is exactly why your competition won't dial either.
2. **Call within the hour, and don't pitch.** Say this: "I'm not calling to relist your home today. I'm calling to find out what went wrong, because if nobody names it, the next agent just repeats it."
3. **Lead with the diagnosis, not the sale.** Now you're the diagnostician, not the vulture, the one person who called to help instead of to sell.
4. **Pull this week's expireds and call the first five today.**
