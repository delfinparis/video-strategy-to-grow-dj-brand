**Body:**
Zillow's 2025 Consumer Housing Trends Report for Agents found that about two-thirds of sellers covered some or all of the buyer's closing costs. Most listing presentations quote one number: the sale price minus commission. The seller plans around that number, then finds out at the table that a chunk of it just went to the buyer's side instead.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to add before the sign goes in the yard:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Put a buyer-closing-cost line on every net sheet.** Show it before the seller asks why the check is smaller than they expected.
2. **Run the estimate two ways.** One if the buyer asks for nothing, one if you cover part of their closing costs. Plan on the second number.
3. **Say it out loud at the listing meeting.** "Here's what you walk away with either way. Plan on the smaller one."

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Show them the smaller check first.

**Subhead:**
The number on the net sheet should already be the number they get at the table.
