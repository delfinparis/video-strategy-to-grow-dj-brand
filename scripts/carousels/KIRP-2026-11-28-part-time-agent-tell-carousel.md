## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Here's the gap that explains it.

**Body:**
NAR's 2026 Member Profile puts a number on it. Among agents working fewer than 20 hours a week,
**57%** earn under $25,000 a year. The median REALTOR, counting everyone, grossed $58,100 in 2024.
Part-time isn't a smaller version of the job. It's a different income bracket, and a transaction
deadline doesn't check which bracket your agent is in.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
The fix:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Say it at the first meeting.** If you hold another job, tell the client before they find out
   by losing a house to it.
2. **Build real coverage.** A licensed partner or a transaction coordinator who works business
   hours, not a voicemail that resets the clock.
3. **Put your real response window in writing.** State your actual turnaround time in the
   agreement instead of letting a client assume nine-to-five.
