## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The code has a **number.** Most listings skip it.

**Body:**
The 2024 International Residential Code, Section R310, sets the exact test for a legal bedroom: an
emergency escape window with a net clear opening of at least 5.7 square feet, a minimum opening
height of 24 inches, and a minimum opening width of 20 inches. Sections R304 and R303 add a
minimum floor area of 70 square feet, with at least 7 feet in any horizontal direction, and a heat
source capable of holding 68 degrees (a space heater does not count). A finished-basement fourth
bedroom with no window, or a converted attic nook with a door and nothing else, fails that test
before an appraiser or a lender ever gets involved. Calling it a bedroom anyway doesn't change the
code. It just moves the correction from the listing to the closing table.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Before you list the "extra bedroom":

**Numbered list:**
1. **Measure the window opening yourself.** Under 5.7 square feet, or under 24 by 20 inches, it fails egress. No exceptions for a nice room.
2. **Check the floor area and the heat source.** Under 70 square feet, no permanent heat, or no 7-foot dimension anywhere in the room, it isn't a legal bedroom either way.
3. **If it fails, use the honest word.** Den, flex room, office, or bonus room. If it's close, say "easily converted to a fourth bedroom" instead of miscounting now.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Call it a bedroom, and the buyer finds out at **inspection.**

**Subhead:**
Use the room's real word and let the number do the selling instead.
