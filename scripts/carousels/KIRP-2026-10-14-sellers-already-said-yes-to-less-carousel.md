**What you do differently on the next offer:**
1. **Pull the price history before you write anything.** A cut once means there's room to ask

2. **Point your buyer toward homes that already reduced.** Those sellers already said yes to
   less, in writing.
3. **Bring up the rate, not just the price.** At 7.28%, a lower purchase price can offset a
   higher monthly payment.
4. **Tell your buyer a full-price offer isn't required right now.** More than one in four
