## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The silence is the mistake. Not the deadline.

**Body:**
Closing week is the highest-anxiety stretch of the whole deal. The buyer is wiring their life
savings, the lender wants one more document, and the walkthrough isn't confirmed yet. That's
exactly the week some agents go quiet, assuming no news means good news. The buyer doesn't read
it that way. They read it as the deal falling apart.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What you do during closing week:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Send a one-line update every day, even with no news.** "Lender has everything, waiting on clear-to-close, expect Thursday." Silence reads as trouble, a short update doesn't.
2. **Confirm the walkthrough time in writing.** Don't leave it to float until the day before.
3. **Verify wire instructions by phone, every time.** Never trust an email alone. This exact week is the one wire fraud targets.
4. **Say it up front.** "I'll check in every day this week, even if nothing's new." Said at the start, it turns silence from scary into expected.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
The deal isn't done until they have the keys.

**Subhead:**
A daily check-in costs two minutes. Going quiet costs the relationship.
