## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
What that changes about your next buyer conversation:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Run the payment math before the showing.** If affordability is what kills deals, the
   conversation that saves one happens before the home search starts, not during it.
2. **Know every buydown and down-payment program cold.** The agents closing in this market are
   the ones who can talk through real options on the spot, not refer out and lose momentum.
3. **Say the real number out loud, early.** A buyer who hears their actual monthly payment in
   week one makes fewer offers they can't really afford.
