## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
That number isn't locked up. It's just undiscussed.

**Body:**
Most of that equity sits in homes that are paid off or close to it, owned by people who haven't
run the math on what it's actually worth to them today. Some are ready to downsize and don't know
what that unlocks. Some want to help a kid with a down payment and haven't connected their own
equity to that goal. Some are staying put and have never heard the case for a reverse mortgage or
a home equity line done right. None of that gets discussed because nobody brings it up first.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Three conversations this number opens with clients 62 and older:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Downsizing math.** Ask what their current home would sell for today, then what that number actually funds. Most haven't run it since they bought.
2. **Helping the next generation.** If a kid or grandkid is house-hunting, ask whether a gift or a loan against equity has ever come up. It often hasn't, because nobody framed it as an option.
3. **Aging in place.** For clients who aren't moving, this is the opening to mention a reverse mortgage or HELOC conversation with a trusted lender, not to sell one yourself.
