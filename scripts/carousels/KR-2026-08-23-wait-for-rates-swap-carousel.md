## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The seller isn't choosing "now" versus "later." They're choosing this competition versus the next one.

**Body:**
A rate drop lowers a buyer's payment, but it also brings the sellers who were waiting for the
same thing back onto the market at the same time. More listings competing for the same pool of
buyers tends to mean more competition for that seller's home, not less. "Let's wait for rates"
sounds like patience. It's actually a bet that fewer people will be selling six months from now,
and that bet usually loses.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
The exact response:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Acknowledge it first.** "That's a fair thing to think about, and I want to walk through what actually happens if rates drop."
2. **Name the competition that comes back with it.** "A lower rate brings other sellers off the fence too. You'd be selling into more competition, not less."
3. **Anchor to what you both control today.** "The price and the terms are the two things we can set right now. The rate is the one thing neither of us controls."

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Sell into today's competition, not next season's.

**Subhead:**
The rate might drop. So might the number of buyers willing to pay what this listing is worth right now.
