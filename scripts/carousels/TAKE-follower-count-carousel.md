**Body:**
The algorithm never checks your follower count before it decides who sees your next video.
It checks whether the last batch of strangers it showed the video to stopped scrolling and
watched. The follower-growth services selling engagement pods, comment-for-comment ladders,
and "gain the next thousand" challenges get paid the same whether that number ever turns
into a client.

## SLIDE 4 -- SCREENSHOT PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Check these three numbers before you check followers:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Completion rate.** The single heaviest ranking signal on Reels, Shorts, and Facebook video. It decides who sees tomorrow's post, not who followed last month's.
2. **Sends.** A private share to another agent is weighted three to five times a like. It is also the only one of these that means a specific person thought of you by name.
3. **Saves.** The number that means someone plans to come back and actually use what you posted.
