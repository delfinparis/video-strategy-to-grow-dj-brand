## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

On Saturday, August 22, the 30-year fixed rose fourteen basis points to 6.64%, and the 5/1 ARM

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What changes today:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Check the rate sheet before every buyer call.** Not once a week. The morning of the call. A stale number is worse than no number.
2. **Flag ARM volatility by name when you present loan options.** An ARM can move three or four times as hard as a fixed rate on the same news day. Say that out loud before a buyer picks one.
3. **Text on a real move. Don't wait to be asked.** "Rates jumped this weekend, here's what it means for you" beats a buyer finding out from a headline.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Be the one who called before they had to ask.

**Subhead:**
The rate moves either way. Whether you're the source of that news is the only part you control.
