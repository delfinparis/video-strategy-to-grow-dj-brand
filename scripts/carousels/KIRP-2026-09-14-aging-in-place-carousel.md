## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
This isn't a health story. It's a listing and buyer-demand story.

**Body:**
Alignment Health's 2026 Social Threats to Aging Well in America survey, run with Ipsos and
polling more than 2,200 adults 65 and older, found nearly 70 percent now face challenges that
affect their ability to remain safely and independently in their own home, up four percentage
points from 2025. It was the most commonly cited barrier in the survey, ahead of healthcare
access and economic instability, and the third year running that aging-in-place difficulty has
topped that list. Every one of those households is either a future seller, a future buyer of an
accessible home, or both.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to do with a senior client or lead this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Ask about the stairs before you ask about the budget.** Single-story, a step-free entry, and
   a main-floor bathroom often matter more to this buyer than square footage.
2. **Know what "accessible" actually means on a showing.** A no-step entry, wide doorways, and a
   walk-in shower are things you can point out, not just an MLS checkbox.
3. **Build one relationship with an aging-life-care professional or senior move manager.** One
   warm referral covers the logistics you're not trained to handle.
4. **Say it in your marketing.** "Right-sizing" and "single-level living" bring in leads that
   "downsizing" quietly pushes away.
