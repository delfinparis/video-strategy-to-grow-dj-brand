## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Two bureaus instead of three changes who gets approved.

**Body:**
FHFA Director Bill Pulte says the agency is moving Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac from the standard
three-bureau credit pull to a two-bureau model, "bi-merge," for loans the GSEs back. The formal
announcement is expected October 12, 2026, with lenders phasing it in over the following one to
three months. Pulling fewer bureaus changes the credit picture a lender sees, especially for a
buyer who looks worse on one bureau than the other two.
