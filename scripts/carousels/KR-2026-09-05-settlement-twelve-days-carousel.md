  deadline to exclude yourself or object is September 17, 2026, and the deadline to file a claim
  is October 27, 2026."
  - Source: OpenClassActions.com, "NAR Homebuyer Settlement: $120.3M, Claim by Oct 27"
    (https://openclassactions.com/settlements/real-estate/homebuyer-antitrust-class-action-settlement.php).
    Fetched directly: the page states the total settlement fund is "$120,334,500" pro rata after
    fees and costs, the exclusion/objection deadline is "September 17, 2026," and the claim filing
    deadline is "October 27, 2026" for both online submissions and mailed forms postmarked by that
    date.
  - Corroborated by web-search results independently reporting the same figures and dates:
    TopClassActions.com, "$120M NAR Real Estate Commission Class Action Settlement," and
    ClaimDepot.com, "NAR $120M Homebuyer Antitrust Class Action Settlement." Not independently
    fetched; used because multiple named, independent outlets report the identical figure and
    dates.
  - Status: primary figure and both deadlines confirmed via direct fetch.

- **Claim:** "NAR itself is contributing $52.25 million of that fund."
  - Source: OpenClassActions.com, "NAR $52.25M Tuccori Homebuyer Settlement: Claims Open"
    (https://openclassactions.com/news/nar-tuccori-buy-side-commission-settlement.php). Fetched
    directly: the article states NAR agreed to pay "$52.25 million to resolve nationwide homebuyer
    commission claims," roughly 43 percent of the combined $120.3 million fund across 26 settling
    parties, and names the case "Tuccori et al. v. At World Properties, LLC et al." (Case No.
    1:24-cv-00150, N.D. Ill.). The same article notes NAR's payments are structured over multiple
    years, with most funds distributed after June 2028, which is why this carousel does not imply
    an immediate payout timeline on any slide.
  - Status: confirmed, direct fetch of the source article.

- **Claim:** eligibility is limited to "anyone who purchased a home listed on an MLS where a
  commission was paid to any brokerage during the applicable class period," and sellers are
  excluded.
  - Source: same OpenClassActions.com settlement page, same direct fetch. The page states
    eligible claimants are individuals who purchased a home listed on a multiple listing service
    anywhere in the United States where a commission was paid to any brokerage in connection with
    the transaction, with class periods varying by state and all ending June 25, 2026, and
    explicitly excludes sellers.
  - Status: confirmed, direct fetch of the source article.
