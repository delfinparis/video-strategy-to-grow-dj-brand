## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The gap that makes this a buyer's market.

**Body:**
Redfin counted 57.9 percent more home sellers than buyers nationally in August, up from 52.1 percent in July, the widest gap in Redfin's records since 2013. Sellers in the market grew to about 1.53 million, up 3.9 percent in a single month, the biggest jump Redfin has recorded. Buyers barely followed, up a tenth of a percent. Redfin's senior economist Asad Khan put it plainly: "Even during a time when housing costs are elevated, the surplus of sellers over buyers makes it a good time to be a house hunter, in some respects."

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What that shift means for your next conversation:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Tell buyers to actually use the edge they have.** Khan's own advice: negotiate on price and ask for concessions like repairs or help with closing costs. Most buyers still don't ask.
2. **Set seller expectations before you sign, not after the first open house.** A seller pricing against last year's numbers is pricing against a market that no longer exists.
3. **Ask every seller about concessions up front.** Rate buydowns, repair credits, and closing-cost help are now a normal part of getting an offer, not a sign the deal fell apart.
4. **Watch your own local numbers, not just the national gap.** Redfin's 57.9 percent is a national average. Nashville alone ran 139 percent more sellers than buyers in August. Know where your own market sits before promising a seller a number based on someone else's metro.
