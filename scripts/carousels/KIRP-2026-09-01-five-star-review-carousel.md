## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Google doesn't count the stars. It reads the words.

**Body:**
On Keeping It Real, Bobby Kerr explained why "great experience, loved working with them" does
nothing for an agent's visibility: it doesn't prove to Google or the AI models citing local
businesses that a real person had a real transaction. Bobby also flagged the pattern Google
actively suppresses on the content side: "Google is quite literally suppressing those types of
profiles because they say I don't need to promote 10,000 mirrors of 10,000 other agents." Same
principle both places. Generic gets buried. Specific gets shown.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What Bobby tells clients to include, every time:

**Numbered list:**
1. **The business name, exactly as it appears on your profile.** "DJ was great" doesn't do it if your profile name is Kale Realty. Both need to show up.
2. **A real address anchor: city, state, and the neighborhood or street.** Real estate makes this the easiest ask in any industry, the transaction already happened at a specific address.
3. **One specific, provable result.** "Sold in 5 days, above asking" beats "highly recommend" because it reads as something only a real client could know.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Stop asking for five stars.

**Subhead:**
Ask for the name, the neighborhood, and the number.
