## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Shorter hours make the room feel busy, not slow.

**Body:**
A long open house spreads buyers out, so each one arrives alone and the rooms feel empty. A
tighter window, one to four in the afternoon instead of all day, puts more buyers in the house
at the same time. Buyers who see other buyers start to worry they could lose it.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Do this before your next listing:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Set the hours to one to four.** Not nine to five, not "all day." A tight window concentrates the people who show up.
2. **Post the same hours everywhere.** One schedule on every flyer and listing site, not a range that drifts by platform.
3. **Tell the seller why.** A shorter open house isn't less effort. It's a room that looks busier.
4. **Watch the first thirty minutes.** If it clears out fast, the window is still too long for the traffic you have.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
A busy-looking open house needs fewer hours, not more.

**Subhead:**
Set your next one for one to four, and see who shows up.
