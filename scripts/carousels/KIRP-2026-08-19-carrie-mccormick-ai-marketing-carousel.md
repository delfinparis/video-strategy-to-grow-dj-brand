## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
"When information becomes free, **judgment** becomes priceless."

**Body:**
Carrie's point: AI can already answer what a buyer used to ask an agent, the comps, the
neighborhood, the price range. What it can't do is know there was a telephone pole behind one
of those "closest five homes," or that a property sold at a discount because of mold in the
basement. That local, lived knowledge is what turns a data answer into a judgment call, and
it's the part of the job AI doesn't reach.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Her three predictions that change what you post:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Lifestyle is going to outperform square footage as a value driver.** Post the neighborhood paired with the buyer it fits, and say why, instead of another square-footage headline.
2. **Hyper-local expertise becomes one of the most valuable assets in the business.** When a buyer asks AI who the best agent for a neighborhood is, that answer needs your name already attached to it somewhere online.
3. **The best agents become small media companies and educators.** Carrie's own words: an agent who isn't building content isn't providing value, and AI surfaces the agents who are.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
AI can answer the market. It can't be the trusted advisor.

**Subhead:**
Be the one thing it can't replace.
