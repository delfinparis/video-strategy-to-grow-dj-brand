## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
This isn't your marketing underperforming.

**Body:**
Per HousingWire's report on Zillow's Q3 2026 earnings, Preferred connections generate **35% more
revenue per connection** than Zillow's old lead model. That's the actual reason the funnel is
narrowing. It's not that non-Preferred agents suddenly got worse at converting leads, it's that
Zillow makes more money routing buyers to the agents already inside the program, and it is
building toward sending every buyer connection that way.
