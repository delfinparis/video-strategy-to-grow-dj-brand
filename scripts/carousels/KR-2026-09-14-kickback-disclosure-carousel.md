## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The law doesn't ban the referral. It bans the secret.

**Body:**
Under RESPA Section 8, enforced at 12 CFR 1024.14, it's illegal to give or accept any fee,
kickback, or other thing of value for referring a client to a settlement service provider, a
lender, title company, or inspector included. The statute, 12 U.S.C. 2607(d), sets the penalty:
a fine up to $10,000 or a year in prison, and separately, the agent becomes liable to the client
for three times whatever changed hands. The rule doesn't stop you from recommending someone good.
It stops you from getting paid for the recommendation without saying so.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Before you hand a client "my guy":

**Numbered list:**
1. **Ask yourself if money moves.** A referral fee, a marketing co-op, or a stake in the company
   all count. If yes, disclosure comes before the referral, not after.
2. **Put any affiliated-business arrangement in writing.** A verbal mention after the fact does
   not satisfy the rule.
3. **Hand over three names, not one.** An independent option list is the fastest way to prove the
   recommendation was on merit.
4. **Say the sentence out loud.** "I get nothing if you use them. I recommend them because
   they're good." If you can't say it, don't send the referral.
