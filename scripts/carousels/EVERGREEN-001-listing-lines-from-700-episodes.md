## SLIDE 3 -- LINE 1
**Headline:**
1. When they are price-shopping you.

**Body:**
"I can be the cheapest agent you talk to, or the one who nets you the most. Those are usually not the same person. Which one are we solving for?"

---

## SLIDE 4 -- LINE 2
**Headline:**
2. When they want to "try it themselves first."

**Body:**
"Totally fair. Can I ask what your plan is if the first two weekends come and go with no offer? Because that is the moment that costs sellers the most, and it is the part I am built for."

---

## SLIDE 5 -- LINE 3
**Headline:**
3. When they compare you to Zillow or a portal.

**Body:**
"The portal shows your house. I position it. One of those gets you traffic. The other gets you the offer you actually want. You are hiring me for the second one."

---

## SLIDE 6 -- LINE 4
**Headline:**
4. When they go quiet at the end.

**Body:**
"You do not have to decide tonight. But if you did, what is the one thing still holding you back? Let's just name it out loud."
