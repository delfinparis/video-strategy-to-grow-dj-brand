## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Nobody told the client what normal looks like.

**Body:**
During the search, you answered in minutes because you wanted the business. The day the contract is signed, response time quietly drifts to a day or two, and nobody agreed to that change. The client can't tell whether two hours or two days is normal, because you never said. So they stop asking real questions, they start every text with an apology, and a client who apologizes to reach you is not a client who refers you.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Say this at the signing table, before anyone leaves:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Publish the number out loud.** "You'll hear from me every Friday, and inside four hours on anything urgent." Say it before the ink dries, not after the first slow reply.
2. **Send it again in writing.** Text the same window right after the meeting, so it's not just a thing they half-remember.
3. **Hold the number you set.** If a Friday update slips twice, the client stops trusting the sentence you said at the table, and you're back to the apology texts.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
A published response window turns silence into a **promise**, not a guess.

**Subhead:**
Say the number once, at the table, and your client never has to wonder what normal is.
