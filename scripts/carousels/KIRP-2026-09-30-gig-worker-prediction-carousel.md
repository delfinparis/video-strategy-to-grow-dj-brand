**Numbered list:**

2. **Get people hooked on you as a person, not just your listings.** Show the work, the wins, even the behind-the-scenes days.
3. **Stop leading with access.** AI can already open a lockbox app and pull comps. Lead every conversation with what only you know about this deal.
