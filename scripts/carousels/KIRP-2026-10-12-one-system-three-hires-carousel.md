## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Build your own version this week:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pick the role costing you the most hours.** Miebach started with transaction coordination,
   the task eating the most time for the least judgment it actually requires.
2. **Put your templates in one shared folder.** Listing descriptions, follow-up scripts, closing
   checklists, all in a Google Drive folder, not scattered across your laptop and your phone.
3. **Point one AI tool at that folder.** Let it write from your own documents instead of a blank
   prompt with no context behind it.
4. **Keep the system portable.** Miebach built his to work across AI platforms, not locked to
   one app that could change its price or shut down tomorrow.
