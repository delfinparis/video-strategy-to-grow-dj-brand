## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
It was never about the **percentage.**

**Body:**
The commission conversation is about value perception, not price. Ask a buyer to define what
they want you to do less of, and they're forced to look at the actual work instead of the
number. Most can't name a single thing, because they never thought past "opens doors."

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
The swap for the commission-cut ask:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Don't defend the number, list your tasks, or fold out of fear of losing them.** All three are the trap.
2. **Ask this instead.** "What part of what I do do you want me to do less of?"
3. **Then stop talking.** Let the silence sit. Don't fill it for them.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
You don't defend the number. You ask them to **define the work.**

**Subhead:**
Try the exact question on your next buyer consult.
