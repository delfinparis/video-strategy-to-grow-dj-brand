## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Denver listings that cut price between day eight and fourteen averaged $2,321.
  Listings that waited until day fifty to fifty-six averaged $28,278, more than a 12-times

    published 2026-07-29 (https://coloradohorseproperty.com/blog/denvers-q2-2026-price-reductions-and-seller-concessions/).
    Underlying data is REcolorado's analysis of closed residential listings across 11 Greater

  promise of a specific dollar saving.
