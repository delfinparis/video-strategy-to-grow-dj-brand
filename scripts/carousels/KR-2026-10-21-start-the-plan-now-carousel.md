## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to do the next time a homeowner says "I'm thinking about selling":

**Numbered list:**
1. **Book the walk-through this week, not when they call back.** The conversation is the trigger,
   not a future "I'm ready."
2. **Set a list date together, on the spot.** A date on the calendar is a plan. "Whenever you're
   ready" is not.
3. **Hand them dated steps working backward from that date.** Repairs, photos, and listing, each
   with its own month.
