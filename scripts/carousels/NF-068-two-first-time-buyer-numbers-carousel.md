## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
**21%** is a survey. **29%** is who actually closed.

**Body:**
The 21 percent comes from NAR's November Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, released 2025-11-04, a survey that asks buyers if this was ever their first home. NAR calls it a record low and has tracked the series since 1981. The 29 percent comes from NAR's July 2026 Existing-Home Sales report, released 2026-08-11, using mortgage data: anyone who hasn't owned a home in the past three years. That includes someone buying alone again after a divorce or the death of a spouse.
