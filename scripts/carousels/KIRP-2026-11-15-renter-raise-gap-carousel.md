## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
It isn't a rate problem. It's an income gap.

**Body:**
Zillow's October 2026 Rent Report found a household that can comfortably afford rent on a
typical single-family home, **$2,243** a month, would need a **$14,408** raise to comfortably
afford owning one at **$2,603** a month with 20 percent down, before saving anything toward that
down payment. Using the standard 30 percent of income guideline, that is **$89,724** a year to
rent comfortably versus **$104,132** to own. The monthly gap is $360. The income gap behind it is
much wider.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
What to do with a renter this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Stop leading with "rates will come down."** The real blocker most renters face is income,
   not the rate on the day they call you.
2. **Ask what they pay in rent before you talk price.** A renter paying close to $2,600 a month
   is already closer to owning than they think. Anchor the conversation there, not on listing
   price.
3. **Hand over a short list of down payment assistance and dual-income qualifying programs** before
   the renter has to ask for one.
4. **Target renters with a lease renewal in the next 90 days.** That is the one moment they are
   already doing this exact math on their own.
