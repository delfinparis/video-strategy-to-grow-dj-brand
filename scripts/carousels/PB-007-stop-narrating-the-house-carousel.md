## SLIDE 1 -- HOOK (carries ~80% of the weight)

You walk a buyer into a house and start narrating: "And this is the kitchen."

**Subhead (smaller, below):**
Stop. They already know it's a kitchen. Here's what to do instead.

---

## SLIDE 2 -- STANDALONE SECOND HOOK
*(Must work alone. Instagram re-serves this slide to anyone who doesn't swipe past slide 1.)*

**Headline:**
Ask this one question before you say a single word about the house.

**Subhead:**
"What does your current place not have that you wish it did?"

---

## SLIDE 3 -- THE PROBLEM

**Headline:**
Every feature you point out, they can already see.

**Body:**
You're filling the silence with noise, and learning nothing about the person you're supposed to be helping.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- THE MOVE

**Headline:**
Ask it at the front door, before the tour starts:

**Body:**
"What does your current place not have that you wish it did?"

---

## SLIDE 5 -- THEN DO THIS

**Headline:**
Go quiet.

**Body:**
Watch their faces room to room. The kitchen they linger in, the closet they open twice, the yard they walk back to. That's your data.

---

## SLIDE 6 -- WHY IT WORKS

**Headline:**
You can't sell a home you don't understand someone's reason for wanting.

**Body:**
Their answer tells you which rooms to slow down in and which to walk past.

---

## SLIDE 7 -- SAVEABLE RECAP

**Headline:**
The showing script:

**Numbered list:**
1. Ask the question at the door.
2. Say nothing until they answer.
3. Stay quiet during the tour and watch, don't narrate.
4. Let their reactions tell you which rooms matter.

---

## SLIDE 8 -- CLOSE ("here's what you do now" action)

**Line 1 (here's what you do now):**
Next showing, ask the question at the door. Then say nothing until they do.

**Line 2 (loop-back, smaller, below):**
The agent talking is performing. The agent listening is closing.
