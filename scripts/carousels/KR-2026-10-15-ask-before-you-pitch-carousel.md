## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The number that predicts who books more isn't call volume.

**Body:**
Patel's leading indicator is how many conversations it takes an agent to book one appointment.
Agents with a high ratio "talk far too much, ask far too little and pitch their services before
they understand how they can help the consumer." Agents with a low ratio do the opposite: they
ask questions first, slow down, and let the other person talk.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**What you do differently on your next call:**
1. **Open with a question, not your pitch.** Find out what they're trying to do before you say
   a word about yourself.
2. **Slow your pace on purpose.** Patel's low-ratio agents talk less and listen more, not the
   other way around.
3. **Track your own ratio.** Count how many conversations it takes you to book one appointment.
   That number predicts your close rate better than your call volume does.
4. **Pitch last, not first.** Once you actually know what they need, the pitch writes itself.
