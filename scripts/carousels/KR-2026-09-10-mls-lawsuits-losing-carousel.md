## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
If you're waiting on a court to change your dues bill, stop waiting.

**Body:**
On September 9, 2026, a three-judge Fifth Circuit panel rejected Texas broker Lou Eytalis's
antitrust challenge to NAR's MLS access rules, ruling the "conclusory facts" in her suit were
"insufficient to state an antitrust injury." Eytalis had called the membership requirement "an
anti-competitive monopoly" and disputed roughly $5,000 in 2023 and 2024 fees her local
association charged inactive agents. Her case joins a pattern: almost every similar lawsuit has
been dismissed or dropped, with only a few, including a Michigan brokers' case now before the
Sixth Circuit, still working through appeals. The fee structure hasn't had to change, because the
courts keep agreeing it doesn't have to.
