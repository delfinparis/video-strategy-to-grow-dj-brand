## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Body:**
Inman's 2025 Real Estate Technology Survey put the average agent's lead response time at 917
minutes, over fifteen hours. NAR's 2025 Home Buyers and Sellers Generational Trends Report found
most buyers work with the first agent who responds, not the most experienced one or the one with
the best marketing. The lead didn't disappear. Somebody else picked up the phone while it was
still warm.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What changes today:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Set a five-minute alarm on every lead source.** Portal leads, CRM leads, website leads. The alarm is the system. Your memory isn't.
2. **Send a text before you dial.** A short "got your inquiry, calling in two minutes" message holds their attention while you're getting to the phone.
3. **Check last week's response times once.** The fifteen-hour average includes agents who think they're already fast. Look at your own number before assuming you beat it.
