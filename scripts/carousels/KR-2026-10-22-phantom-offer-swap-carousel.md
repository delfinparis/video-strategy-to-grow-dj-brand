## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
That's not a tactic. It's a code violation.

**Body:**
NAR's Code of Ethics, Article 2, bars agents from exaggeration, misrepresentation, or
concealment of pertinent facts related to a property or a transaction. Standard of Practice
1-15 says an agent may disclose that a real offer exists, and only with the seller's approval.
Neither article leaves room for inventing one that isn't there. Saying it to speed up a buyer
isn't hustle. It's the exact behavior those two rules exist to stop.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to say instead of the bluff:

**Numbered list:**
1. **"I can't disclose other offers without my seller's approval."** True, defensible, and it
   still signals real interest without inventing anything.
2. **Give a real deadline, not a manufactured one.** "All offers are due Thursday at 5pm" only
   works as urgency if your seller actually set that date.
3. **Be ready to produce what you claim.** If a buyer's agent asks to see the other offer and
   you can't show one, you've already answered their question.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Say it straight. Real offer, or none at all.

**Subhead:**
The bluff risks your license. The truth only risks the sale.
