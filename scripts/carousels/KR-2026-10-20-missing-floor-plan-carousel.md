Floor plans beat almost everything else on your listing.

**Body:**
NAR's 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers asked online buyers to rate how useful different
listing features were. Photos ranked highest, at 81 percent. Detailed property information came
next, at 77 percent. Floor plans came third, at 57 percent, ahead of the agent's own contact

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Add this to every listing before it goes live:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Book the floor plan in the same visit as the photos.** One appointment, not a second trip.
2. **Ask for room sizes on the plan.** A buyer judging the layout wants to know if the furniture
   fits.
3. **Put it second in the photo order, right after the front of the house.** Buyers who click
   past the first photo are already judging the layout.
4. **Print it for every showing.** A buyer walking the rooms in person still wants the plan in
   their hand.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Pull up your next listing and check the photo order for a floor plan.

**Subhead:**
If it's not there, that's the first call to make today.
