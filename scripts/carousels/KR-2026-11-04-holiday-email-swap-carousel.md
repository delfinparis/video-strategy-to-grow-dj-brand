## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Most agents either go quiet over the holidays or buy an ad to stay visible.

**Body:**
Neither is the move. Lemons-Ryhal built a six-touch calendar running from late October through
January and opened it with a two-question survey asking her list what winter task was on their
mind and what they wanted covered in January. Their answers decided her next three emails. About
one percent of her list unsubscribed, roughly 130 people out of 13,000, which is the real cost of
staying visible this way. Compare that to what the same reach costs in ad spend.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to do differently this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Build a six-email calendar now, not in December.** Two in November, two in December, one in
   January, and one more if you are starting late. Don't wait for the holiday card to be the
   whole plan.
2. **Open with a two-question survey.** Ask what winter home task is on their mind and what they
   want covered in January. Let the answers write your next three emails.
3. **Give something in every email, never just a greeting.** A winterizing checklist, a tax
   document list, a year-end maintenance guide. The list stays on the list because every email
   is useful on its own.
