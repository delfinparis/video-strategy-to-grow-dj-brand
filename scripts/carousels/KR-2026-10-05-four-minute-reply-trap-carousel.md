---

## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
You didn't pick a policy. You picked a precedent.

**Body:**
Every reply time you've ever sent trains your client on what to expect next. Answer fast enough,
often enough, and the client stops reading it as responsiveness. They read it as your baseline.
The fix isn't replying slower on purpose. It's deciding the policy before the client decides it
for you.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Set this before your next new client, not after:

**Numbered list:**
1. **State your hours out loud, on day one.** "8am to 8pm, I respond within two hours," said once, before they need anything.
2. **Name your stop time.** Pick the hour you go dark for family time, and tell them that's the line, not an accident they'll discover later.
3. **Tell them the weekend plan too.** Whatever you actually do Saturday and Sunday, say it out loud so it's a policy, not a surprise.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Clients don't mind a policy. They mind being surprised by one.

**Subhead:**
Say your hours before the first fast reply sets them for you.
