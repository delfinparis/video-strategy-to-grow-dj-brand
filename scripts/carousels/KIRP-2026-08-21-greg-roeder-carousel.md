## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
His best friend asked him that once. He forgot it for years.

**Body:**
A quarter century ago, Greg was cold-calling strangers off a Cutco knife script. At a family
friend's graduation party, he pitched a room full of people he'd actually grown up with instead,
and sold **$3,900** in one afternoon. That's when his best friend Jack asked him why he'd been
selling to strangers at all. Years later, Jack was diagnosed with a brain tumor, and in the time
Greg spent sitting with him, that old question came back to the surface. That's when Greg
finally built a business around the answer.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
His system for working people who already know you:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Mobilize your people.** Host one recurring gathering built on something you actually care about, not a sales pitch with snacks. Greg's is a pickleball league. His rule at every one: "we're playing pickleball. I do not talk about business."
2. **Make content for your dream client.** Get permission first. His newsletter is opt-in and arrives on a schedule people signed up for, never a surprise in someone's inbox.
3. **Multiply through systems.** He spends about an hour a month building content that gets spread across the rest of it, and says no to the random obligations that don't build the system, so it doesn't require him to be reachable every hour of every day.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
You already know your next client. Stop chasing the ones you don't.

**Subhead:**
The list was never the problem. The follow-up was.
