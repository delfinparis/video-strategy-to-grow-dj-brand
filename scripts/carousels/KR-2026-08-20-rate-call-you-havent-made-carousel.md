theirs. The agent who calls today and says "this is the drop we talked about" owns that
conversation. The agent who waits for a rounder number loses the buyer to whoever calls first.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
The list to work today:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pull every buyer who mentioned rates in the last six months.** CRM notes, texts, voicemail follow-ups. The list is smaller than it feels.
2. **Lead the call with the number, not the pitch.** "Freddie Mac's average just hit six sixty-seven, the lowest in about a year" is a fact, not a sales line.
3. **Ask what payment they were waiting to see, not what rate.** Most buyers think in monthly payment. Translate 6.67% into their actual number before you hang up.
