## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
More homes listed. Fewer of them going under contract.

**Body:**
HousingWire's weekly tracker put active inventory at **895,988** for the week ending November
14, 2026, up **7.11%** from the same week last year. Pending sales for that same week were
**50,623**, down from **60,722** a year ago. Price cuts ticked up too: **43%** of listings versus
**42%** last year (Logan Mohtashami, HousingWire, 2026-11-14). The seasonal slowdown in new
listings is normal, it happens every November. The softer conversion from listed to pending is
the part that isn't just the calendar.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
What to do with a seller this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Don't blame the calendar for a slow showing schedule.** Inventory drops every November.
   That part repeats every year and isn't news.
2. **Explain the real number: fewer listings are turning into pending sales than a year ago.**
   That's the part that should change how you price, not the seasonal dip.
3. **Price sharp from day one.** With price cuts already ticking up year over year, a seller who
   tests high this month gets punished faster than usual.
4. **Use thinner active competition as the pitch to list now**, and pair it honestly with the
   slower buyer follow-through, so the seller hears both halves.
