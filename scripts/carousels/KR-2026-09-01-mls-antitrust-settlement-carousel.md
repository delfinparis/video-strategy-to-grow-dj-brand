## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The MLS rule you assumed was permanent just lost in court.

**Body:**
Compass sued Northwest MLS over fines its agents faced for using "coming soon" pre-marketing.
The settlement creates a new "First Look" status, launching September 4, letting a seller market a
home for up to 21 days without a public days-on-market clock or price-change history. Reffkin
called the old fine structure "the textbook definition of an antitrust violation." He did not call
this a one-time fight. Every MLS with a similar rule just watched one of them lose.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to check in your own market this week:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pull your own MLS's actual pre-marketing policy, not the summary version.** Read the fine schedule for coming-soon or delayed-marketing listings word for word.
2. **Ask your broker if that policy resembles the one that just lost.** A rule that fines a brokerage for following a seller's own marketing choice is the exact structure Reffkin's quote is describing.
3. **Track this, don't assume it's settled.** A rule that survives this year is not guaranteed to survive next year's lawsuit.
