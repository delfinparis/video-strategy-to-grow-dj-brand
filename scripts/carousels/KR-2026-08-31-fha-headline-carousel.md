## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
One headline. Two unrelated stories, stitched together.

**Body:**
The Wall Street Journal's "UWM Is a Government Mortgage Canary" tied United Wholesale Mortgage's
$603 million loss on a rate hedge to broader fears about FHA-insured lending. The Mortgage Bankers
Association's own president, Bob Broeksmit, published a rebuttal calling it what it was: "conflating
a single firm's hedging misstep with FHA's program-wide performance makes for an eye-catching
headline," one that "neither informs readers about the health of the FHA program nor the strength
of the independent mortgage bank sector." Your buyer's FHA pre-approval didn't get riskier this
week. The headline just made it sound that way.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to do this month if you have FHA buyers active:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Send a one-line note before they see the headline.** "Saw the FHA story going around. It's about one lender's hedging bet, not your loan. Nothing about your approval changed."
2. **Have the lender confirm it in writing, not you.** A two-line note from their loan officer carries more weight than reassurance from their agent alone.
3. **Bring it up yourself at the next touchpoint.** A buyer who reads the headline before you mention it starts wondering what else you're not telling them.
