## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Every plan gets demoed at twenty deals. Most years aren't twenty deals.

**Body:**
The Consumer Federation of America found that 49% of agents sold one home or zero over a
12-month period, based on a sample of 2,000 agents at large firms nationwide. A percentage-based
plan costs you nothing in a dead month and a lot in a great one. A fixed-cost plan does the exact
opposite. Neither is automatically wrong, but only one of them matches the year you're actually
about to have, and nobody shows you that version at the recruiting table.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Do this instead of taking the split at face value:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Don't run the plan at twenty transactions.** Run it at two, and run it again at zero.
2. **Don't take a verbal fee list.** Get every fee, desk, tech, E&O, transaction, in one email, in writing.
3. **Don't ask what the split is first.** Ask what you owe in a month you close nothing.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
The plan that wins your best year rarely protects your worst one.

**Subhead:**
Ask for the math before you ask for the split.
