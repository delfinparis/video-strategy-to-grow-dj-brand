## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The industry's own members named the blocker, and it isn't the one most agents lead with.

**Body:**
NAR's 2026 Member Profile asked REALTORS what most limits their clients from buying. Housing
affordability topped the list at 27 percent, more than double lack of inventory at 12 percent and
difficulty finding the right property at 11 percent. Rates never top that list. If the people
doing the deals say affordability is the real objection, a buyer conversation built around "let's
wait for rates to drop" is answering a question nobody asked.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to change about this month's buyer conversations:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Open with the payment, not the price.** Put the total to close and the monthly cost as line one of the CMA, before the listing photos.
2. **Bring a lender to the first buyer call.** Rate buydowns and down-payment assistance are the actual affordability levers, not a rate cut you can't control.
3. **Recalibrate the search criteria before the tour.** Have the affordability conversation before three rejected offers force it.
