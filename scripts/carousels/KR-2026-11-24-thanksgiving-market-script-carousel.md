## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Here's what the question actually means.

**Body:**
Nobody at the table wants a mortgage rate lecture. They want to know if the decision they're
scared to make, or scared they already missed, is as bad as they think. Answer the number and
you lose the room. Answer the fear underneath it, and you're the relative who actually helped,
not the one who ruined dinner with a spreadsheet.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
Four scripts for the table:

**Numbered list:**
1. **"Is now a bad time to buy?"** Say this: "There's no perfect month. There's the month your life needs a house. That's the only timing that matters."
2. **"Rates are insane, right?"** Say this: "They're higher than five years ago and lower than twenty years ago. You marry the house and date the rate. You can refinance later."
3. **"Should I just wait for the market to crash?"** Say this: "People have been waiting for that since 2022. Waiting has a cost too. You just don't see the bill."
4. **"Is my house even worth what I paid?"** Say this: "Let's actually look at your block instead of guessing from the news. I'll run it for you this week."
