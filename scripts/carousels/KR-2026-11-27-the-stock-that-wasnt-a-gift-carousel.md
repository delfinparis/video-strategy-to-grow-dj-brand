## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Here's what actually got penalized.

**Body:**
Pennsylvania's Attorney General found that two mortgage companies gave real estate agents and
brokers underpriced, nonvoting stock in their affiliated lenders. The agents who took it then sent
clients back to those same lenders. The state didn't need a conviction to act. It called the stock
a referral-based kickback, fined the companies $65,000, and left a $100,000 penalty standing if
they violate the settlement again.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
The fix:

**Numbered list:**
1. **If you hold any ownership stake in an affiliated lender, title company, or inspection firm,
   pull the paperwork this week.** Know exactly how that stake was priced and whether referrals
   were ever part of the conversation.
2. **Ask in writing whether your profit share is tied to referral volume.** If the answer is yes or
   nobody can answer clearly, that's the exact structure Pennsylvania just fined.
3. **Disclose every affiliated-business relationship to your client before you recommend it.** A
   disclosed relationship is a disclosure. An undisclosed one is the case above.
