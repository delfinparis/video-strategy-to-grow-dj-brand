## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
Nick's four pillars, in order:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Mind, body, spirit first.** A workout, a walk, whatever your version is. This is the one
   activity that gives energy instead of spending it.
2. **Prospect generation, immediately after.** Calls, letters, DMs, content, whatever your
   method is. Do it while your energy is highest, not whenever it fits.
3. **Prospect conversion next.** Buyer consultations and the follow-through that turns a lead
   into a client, once the heaviest lift of the day is already done.
4. **Client and customer service last.** Service work can run on lower energy. Prospecting
   cannot.
