**Body:**
Barry Jenkins of the Legacy Home Team, with Marissa Canario, had an AI tool read his team's
lead database at Zillow Unlock 2026 and found that **one in four** of their most expensive

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Run this before you buy another lead:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Pull every paid lead from your CRM.** Any platform that tracks call logs works. No new
   tool required.
2. **Flag every one with zero calls logged.** That's the leak, whatever the percentage turns out
   to be on your own list.
3. **Work those first, before spending on new leads.** You already paid for them once.
4. **Make this a monthly audit, not a one-time check.** The gap reappears the moment nobody's
   watching for it.
