## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
You didn't lose them. You taught them what your name means.

**Body:**
Call a past client only when you want a referral or a listing, and their phone learns your
pattern faster than you do. They see your name, they know what's coming, and they let it ring.
The fix isn't calling less. It's changing the ratio. Three calls that hand them something, for
every one call that asks them for something. Give first, long enough that your name stops
meaning "here it comes."

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Run this ratio before you ask for anything:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Call with something useful, three times.** A nearby sale, a tax deadline, a contractor
   worth using. No ask attached to any of them.
2. **Open differently.** Skip "how's the market." Ask about the thing you actually know about
   them.
3. **Say "no strings" out loud.** "A house on your street just sold. Want me to send you what it
   went for? No strings. I just thought you'd want to know."
4. **Then ask once.** Clearly, and stop there.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Pick three past clients tonight. Call with something useful, not an ask.

**Subhead:**
Give three times, then ask once. That's the whole ratio.
