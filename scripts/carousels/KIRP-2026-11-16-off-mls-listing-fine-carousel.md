## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
Before you promise a seller a private listing:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Read your own MLS's public marketing rule before the listing appointment**, not after a
   seller asks about going private. Yours may not match the one in the lawsuit.
2. **Say the risk out loud.** "I can try to keep this quiet, but my MLS can fine me for marketing
   it that way, and a pending lawsuit doesn't make that cost go away."
3. **Know the number, not just the rule.** Some MLS penalties reach $15,000 per violation. Find
   out yours before you make the promise.
4. **Treat "pending lawsuit" as "current rule."** Nothing about Compass's suits has changed what
   your own MLS can enforce today.
