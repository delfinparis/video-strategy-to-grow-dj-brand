## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The split is the easiest number to compare. It's also the one that moves the least.

**Body:**
Two agents on the exact same 70/30 split can have completely different years, because the split
doesn't generate a lead, train a script, or follow up with a client. **It just divides whatever
already landed.** The number that actually matters is how many extra transactions a brokerage's
training, systems, and support put in front of you this year, not the number printed on your
commission statement.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What to ask before you compare splits:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Ask what it does, not just what it pays.** Before comparing percentages, ask exactly what
   training, leads, or systems come attached to that number.
2. **Count last year's actual leads from your brokerage.** Not promised, not available on a
   portal you never open. The number that reached your phone.
3. **Price the split against production, not against itself.** A 60/40 split that hands you ten
   extra closings beats an 80/20 split that hands you none.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Here's what you do now.

**Subhead:**
Ask your broker this week how many real leads their systems put in front of you last year. Then decide if the split is the number that matters, or just the easiest one to compare.
