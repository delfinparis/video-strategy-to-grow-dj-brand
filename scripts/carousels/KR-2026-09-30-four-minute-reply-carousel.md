## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
You didn't set a schedule. You set a **precedent.**

**Body:**
Every fast reply is a lesson. Answer a question in four minutes on day one, and the client
learns that's your speed, not just today's speed. Six months later, a Sunday-night text gets
the same expectation, whether or not you meant to promise it. The fix isn't to slow down. It's
to say the policy out loud before the habit sets it for you.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Say this before the relationship sets the rule for you:

**Numbered list:**
1. **State your hours in the first conversation.** "From eight a.m. to eight p.m., Monday through Friday, I respond to every text within two hours."
2. **Name your stop time.** "I stop texting at eight p.m. so I can spend time with my family."
3. **Cover the weekend up front.** Tell them exactly what to expect on Saturday and Sunday, before they test it themselves.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Set the right expectation from the start.

**Subhead:**
A policy you say once beats a habit you defend for years.
