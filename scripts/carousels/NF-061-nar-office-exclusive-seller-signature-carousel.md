  - Source: NAR, "MLS Clear Cooperation Policy." CCP (adopted 2020) requires a listing broker to submit a listing to the MLS within one business day of marketing the property to the public. Retained in 2026.
  - Status: confirmed
- **Claim:** "They also gave your seller two ways to stay off the MLS. The office exclusive, and delayed marketing."
  - Source: NAR, "Multiple Listing Options for Sellers," effective March 25, 2025, implementation deadline September 30, 2025.
  - Status: confirmed
- **Claim:** "You have to file it with the MLS, and you have to get your seller to sign a certification."
  - Source: NAR, "Multiple Listing Options for Sellers." Both exempt listing types must be filed with the MLS pursuant to a seller certification disclosing the professional relationship and the seller's written acknowledgement of the MLS exposure they're waiving or delaying.
  - Status: confirmed
- **Claim:** "This drops the same month a Chicago courtroom put private listings on every headline in the country."
  - Source: Zillow v. MRED and Compass preliminary injunction hearing, U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, July 1-2, 2026. See NF-057, NF-058, NF-060.
  - Status: confirmed
- **Claim (editorial):** "NAR didn't ban the private listing. It made your seller sign for it."
  - Source: D.J. Paris's shareable moment / framing of the Multiple Listing Options for Sellers certification requirement.
  - Status: editorial framing
