## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
You didn't lose her. You never **asked** her.

**Body:**
Leave voicemails for a client who only reads texts, and she never calls back. You decide she
went cold. She didn't. She read every text and wondered why you disappeared. Neither of you did
anything wrong. Nobody asked which channel the other one actually uses, so the relationship ran
on a guess until it quietly stopped.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Ask these three questions at the first meeting:

**Numbered list:**
1. **"Call, text, or email?"** Ask before you assume. Don't wait for a missed call to tell you.
2. **"What hours are okay?"** Write the actual window down, not just the answer.
3. **"Do you listen to voicemail, or should I skip it?"** Most people say skip it.
4. **Write it in their contact record.** Not a sticky note. Somewhere you'll see it again.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY

Ask the three questions, then talk to them the way they actually listen.

**Subhead:**
Thirty seconds of questions. A whole deal's worth of less friction.
