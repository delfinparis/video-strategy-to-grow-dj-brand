**Headline:**

**Body:**
A 2026 Clever Real Estate survey of home sellers found that just one in three who used an agent
ever tried to negotiate the commission, but 93 percent of the ones who asked got a reduction.
Most agents wait for that question and get defensive when it lands. The agents who don't lose the
conversation never make the seller work up the nerve to ask. They bring the fee up themselves, in
the same breath as what it buys.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What changes at your next listing appointment:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Stop waiting for the ask.** Bring your fee up yourself, in the same sentence where you explain what it buys the seller.
2. **Trade defense for plain math.** If they ask "is that negotiable," don't retreat. State exactly what's included at that rate and let them decide.
3. **Watch which sellers refer you.** The ones who never had to ask are the ones who come back. The ones who had to fight for a number remember the fight, not the deal.
