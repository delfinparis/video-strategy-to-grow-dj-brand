## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**

**Body:**
The FTC's own settlement lays it out: in February 2025 Zillow paid Redfin $100 million, and
Redfin agreed to shut down its rental-listings business and hand its customers to Zillow for up
to nine years. Five states joined the case. The order announced August 24 forces Redfin back

listings to every day. This settlement is the reminder that the answer isn't neutral.

**Numbered list:**

3. **Put one hour a week into a channel you own.** Past clients, referral partners, your own site. Nothing that depends on someone else's algorithm.
