## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
The swap, listing by listing:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Don't write "open floor plan."** It reads as a selling point and Zillow's own data says it sells for less.
2. **Do walk the house and name what it's made of.** White oak floors, walnut cabinets, soapstone counters, whatever is actually there.
3. **Don't name a material the house doesn't have.** Only list what you can point to in the room. A wrong claim costs more than a vague one.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
Write down what the house is made of, not what the layout looks like.

**Subhead:**
The description that names materials is the one that sells for more.
