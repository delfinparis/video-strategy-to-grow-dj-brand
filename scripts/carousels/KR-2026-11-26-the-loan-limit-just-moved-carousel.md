**The one idea:** FHFA's new 2027 baseline conforming loan limit is $855,400, a $22,650 (2.71%)
increase from 2026's $832,750. Loans at or below that number get conforming pricing (lower rates,
easier qualifying) through Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac; loans above it need jumbo financing, which
usually costs more and underwrites harder. Every buyer who was quoted jumbo terms somewhere in
that $22,650 gap this year may not need jumbo financing once lenders adopt the new number for
2027 -- a concrete, actionable thing to go back and tell clients this month, which is what makes
this a `market-tip` rather than a plain `stat` deck.

## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Conforming and jumbo aren't the same loan. The line between them just **moved.**

**Body:**
A loan at or under the conforming limit qualifies for Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac pricing:
generally lower rates, easier underwriting, smaller down payments. Go a dollar over that limit
and the loan becomes jumbo, with its own stricter terms and usually a higher rate. FHFA just
moved that line up $22,650, to $855,400 for 2027. Any buyer whose loan amount sat in that
$22,650 gap was paying jumbo terms for a loan that's conforming next year.
