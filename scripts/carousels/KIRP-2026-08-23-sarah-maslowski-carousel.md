**Headline:**

**Body:**
Sarah Maslowski has closed over $150 million in real estate from YouTube, with no paid ads and no
cold calling. Then her video editor had a family emergency the same week she had no video bank
and no backup editor lined up. "I had nothing in the bank, no backup video, no buffer to speak
of," she said on Keeping It Real. The channel that runs through one person is one emergency away
from going dark, and the algorithm remembers the gap even after it's fixed.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
The fix she's putting in place:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Build a video bank of two to three fully edited videos.** Ready to post before you need them. That's the buffer that makes a bad week invisible to your audience.
2. **Name a backup editor before you need one.** Someone already briefed on your style who can step in the day your regular editor can't.
3. **Separate your planning week from your filming week.** Research and scripting pull a different kind of energy than filming. Doing both the same day burns out both.
