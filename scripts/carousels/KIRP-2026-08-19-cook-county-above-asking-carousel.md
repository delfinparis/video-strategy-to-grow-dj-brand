## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Two different markets, one headline.

**Body:**
NAR's July report put the national Pending Home Sales Index at 71.2, the lowest reading since
January 2026, down 2.3% month over month. Cook County ran the opposite direction: 51.3% of
homes sold above list price, and the median sale price climbed to $398,875, up 6.4% year over
year. Chicago proper is doing its part too, with only 2.8 months of available supply. The
national story is a slowdown. The local story is still short inventory and buyers competing.
