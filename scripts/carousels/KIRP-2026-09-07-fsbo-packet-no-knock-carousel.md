## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**

**Body:**
Megan Walters, co-founder of the Walters Team at eXp in Columbia, Missouri and NAR's 2026 30
Under 30, pulls her area's FSBO, expired, and withdrawn listings every Monday and Wednesday. On
