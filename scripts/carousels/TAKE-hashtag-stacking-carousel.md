## SLIDE 6 -- SCREENSHOT PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What actually earns the post reach now:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Cut to three to five tags.** Specific and realtor-first. Past that number you are working against the post, not for it.
2. **Put the real search phrase in the first line.** Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube all read captions like search engines now. The words do the work the tags used to.
3. **Judge the post on sends, not tags.** A private send to another agent carries three to five times the distribution weight of a like. That's the number worth chasing.
