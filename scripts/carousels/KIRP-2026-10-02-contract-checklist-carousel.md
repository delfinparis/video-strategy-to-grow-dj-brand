## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The contract is a signal, not paperwork.

**Body:**
A misspelled name, a blank line where an initial goes, an old version of the form: the other
agent reads all of it as a preview of how the whole transaction will go. When her seller is
weighing two offers, she points to the cleaner one. You never find out that's why you lost.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Read it out loud before you send it. Then check:

**Numbered list:**
1. **The names match the lender's letter.** A misspelled name is the first thing an underwriter
   catches too.
2. **The address has the unit number.** Not just the building.
3. **The price matches in numbers and words.** Both have to say the same figure.
4. **The date is right.** A wrong date is the fastest tell that a form got recycled.

---

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
