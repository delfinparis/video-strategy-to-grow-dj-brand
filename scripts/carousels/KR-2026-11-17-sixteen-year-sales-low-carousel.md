## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
Fewer sales plus more inventory is a buyer's market, even unlabeled.

**Body:**
NAR reported existing-home sales at an annual rate of 3.75 million in October, the slowest pace
since August 2010 and down 8.8% from a year earlier, while the number of homes for sale rose to
1.59 million units, up 4.6% from a year ago. Fewer buyers chasing more homes is the textbook
definition of leverage. Most buyers walking into a showing right now don't know they have it.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
What to do with that in your next buyer conversation:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Lead with the inventory number, not the rate.** A buyer who hears supply is up 4.6% acts
   differently than one who thinks they're fighting ten other offers.
2. **Put contingencies back on the table.** Inspection and financing contingencies quietly came
   back as sales slowed. Use them instead of assuming a waiver is required.
3. **Ask for concessions as the default, not the long shot.** In a market with sales at a 16-year
   low, asking for a credit is normal, not a risk to the offer.

---

Here's what you do now.

**Subhead:**
