## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
Buyers didn't lose trust in AI. They lost trust in AI doing this **alone**.

**Body:**
A Cotality survey found homebuyer trust in AI to help find a home fell from 30% in 2025 to 16%
in 2026, a 14-point drop in a single year. At the same time, 55% of buyers said they would prefer
working with a person to secure a mortgage, up from 46% the year before. Three in four buyers
still assume AI is somewhere in the process. They just want a person checking its work.

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What that means for your marketing this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Put your name and face on every listing description.** An auto-generated blurb is the exact thing buyers say they trust less this year.
2. **Call before you text-blast.** A real check-in beats an automated drip in the same year buyers said they want a person, not a system.
3. **Say out loud what you catch that AI doesn't.** The number that doesn't match the comps, the line item a buyer would gloss over. Name the specific save, not "I add value."
