## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The wrong sentence turns a deadline into an argument.

**Body:**
Most agents open the day-thirty call with "we need to talk about the price." The seller hears you
quitting and starts defending a number that was never the actual problem. Realtor.com's June 2026
analysis, from senior economist Joel Berner, found homes that close around the four-week mark sell

Nobody has to admit they were wrong.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Say this instead, word for word:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Open with the number, not your opinion.** "Realtor.com ran the numbers. Homes that close at four weeks do 1.8 percent better against asking than average. At eighteen weeks, worse."
2. **Name the exact week you're in.** "We're at week four. I'd rather move while we still pick the number."
3. **Make the call today.** Find the listing on your desk closest to day thirty and have this conversation before week six, not after.

**Headline:**
Day thirty isn't when you lose the listing.

**Subhead:**
