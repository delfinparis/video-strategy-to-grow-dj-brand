an agent's own website. They cannot read Instagram or Facebook, because those platforms block AI
crawlers from indexing what gets posted there. Years of social content do nothing for how an
agent shows up when someone asks an AI tool "find me a realtor in this area." The fix lives on
the website, not the feed.

## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
AI tools read your **site.** They can't read your feed.

**Body:**
Facebook and Instagram don't let AI crawlers in, so every post an agent has ever made is
invisible to ChatGPT and Claude. McCarthy's point, on his own show: "The more these answer
engines know about you and can read your site and learn about you, the more likely you are to
be recommended." Your website is the one place these tools can actually open and read. If it has
nothing specific on it, there's nothing for them to find.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD

**Headline:**
Three things to do about it:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Write about your own market, on your own site, in your own words.** A generic listing feed
   looks like every other agent's. A page about your specific neighborhood doesn't.
2. **Let AI write and publish the pages for you.** Tools built for this, like Real Geeks' AI
   Connect, turn a prompt into a published page, so "no time" stops being the excuse.
3. **Ask your website provider this tomorrow: "Do you allow AI training bots to crawl my
   website?"** McCarthy's own line, and he calls it table stakes.
