## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The agents who cut their rate to survive gave up money nobody was taking from them.

**Body:**
Average buyer agent commission dropped to 2.58% right after the settlement, then climbed back
past where it started, to 2.82% by February 2026 (Clever Real Estate's national survey of 533
agents). The agents who assumed clients would only accept a discount preemptively lowered their
own fee. The agents who kept explaining what they actually do, and what it's worth, are the
ones getting paid the higher number two years later.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
The value conversation that replaces the discount:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Say your fee out loud, early.** Waiting for the buyer to ask makes it feel negotiable before you've said what it buys them.
2. **Name what the fee pays for.** Contract negotiation, inspection management, appraisal gap coverage, being reachable at every step. Specific, not general.
3. **Don't discount before anyone asks you to.** If a buyer never brought up the settlement, the settlement isn't the reason to lower your number.
