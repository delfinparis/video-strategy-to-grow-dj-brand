## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The question isn't rude. It's just an easy door out.

**Body:**
A closed yes/no question hands a cold lead a frictionless no, and the call ends before you learn
anything about where they actually stand. Swap it for a question that assumes they're still in
the process and needs a real answer: "I'm keeping an eye on the market for you. Has your
timeline or price range shifted at all?" That question can't be answered in one word, so the
lead has to actually think about where they are instead of reaching for the exit.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Say this the next time you dial a cold lead:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Don't ask "are you still looking?"** It's a yes/no question with a built-in exit, and most leads take it.
2. **Ask about their timeline instead.** "Has your timeline or price range shifted since we last talked?" requires a real answer.
3. **Reference something specific.** "I've been keeping an eye on [neighborhood or price range] for you" shows the lead you didn't forget them between calls.

## SLIDE 5 -- TAKEAWAY
**Headline:**
The swap isn't the words. It's making the lead answer instead of exit.

**Subhead:**
Pull five cold leads from your database tonight and try the new question on each one.
