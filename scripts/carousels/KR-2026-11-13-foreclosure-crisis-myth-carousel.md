## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN

**Headline:**
The 2008 comparison doesn't hold up to the numbers.

**Body:**
HousingWire tracked the same week of new foreclosure-related listings every year: 280,400 in
2009, 353,457 in 2010, 352,030 in 2011, and 54,478 in 2026. Loan-to-value ratios that reached
nearly 86% in 2011 now sit at 44.2%. Mortgage delinquency is sitting inside the 1% to 4% range
that is always present, crisis or not. The FHA foreclosure moratorium ended in October 2025, and
foreclosures take 9 to 18 months to turn into new listings, so even that doesn't explain a wave
that isn't in the data yet.
