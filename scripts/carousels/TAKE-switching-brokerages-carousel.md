4. Stands with the agent: yes. The friction points at brokerages that trade on the fear of

## SLIDE 4 -- SCREENSHOT PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What actually keeps your book if you move:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Call your top twenty before anyone else does.** A personal call beats a rumor every time. They stay because you told them, not because a brokerage kept them.
2. **Say where and why, in one line.** Clients don't need a pitch. They need the fact and the reason, from you, first.
3. **Keep doing the thing that earned the referral.** The move doesn't reset the relationship. Stopping the behavior that built it does.
