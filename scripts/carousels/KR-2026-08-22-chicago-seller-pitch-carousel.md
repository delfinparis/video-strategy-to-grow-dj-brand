## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The national buyer drought is the strongest listing pitch you have this month.

**Body:**
Redfin's analysis, covered in Bloomberg's August 19, 2026 newsletter, found 39 of the 49 major
US metro areas are now buyer's markets, with sellers outnumbering buyers nationally. Chicago
isn't one of them: 0.72 months of supply, homes moving in an average of 47 days, and sale prices
averaging 102.12% of asking (Houzeo, citing MRED MLS-IL data, July 2026). A seller who reads the
national headlines assumes their market softened too. It didn't, and the contrast is the opening
line.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
How to use the contrast in the appointment:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Open with the national number, not the local one.** "Buyers are vanishing almost
   everywhere else in the country. Not here." Let the seller feel the exception before you name it.
2. **Show the 0.72-months figure next to whatever they think their competition looks like.**
   Most sellers are pricing against a market that doesn't exist anymore, in either direction.
3. **Price to the 102% reality, not to fear borrowed from national headlines.** A seller
   spooked by the news will underprice a home that doesn't need it.
