**Body:**
Denver's Q2 2026 data: listings that cut price between day eight and day fourteen averaged a $2,321 reduction. Listings that waited until day fifty to fifty-six averaged $28,278, more than twelve times as much, the report's own characterization. ## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Four things to do instead of waiting:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Put a price-review date in the listing agreement on day one.** No offer by day fourteen, the number moves. Agreed while it's still cheap to be right.
2. **Track showings, not weeks.** Thin showings by day fourteen is the signal that matters, not a feeling that it's still early.
3. **Say the quiet part at the appointment, not at day fifty.** "I'll tell you before it gets expensive to fix" is easier to hear on day one than on day fifty.
4. **Watch inventory, not just the listing.** Active inventory has held above 1.1 million for seven straight weeks. The window for an easy correction is tighter than it was a year ago.

## Data Source

- **Claim:** "Listings that cut price between day eight and day fourteen averaged a $2,321 reduction. Listings that waited until day fifty to fifty-six averaged $28,278, more than twelve times as much."
  - Source: Colorado Horse Property, "Denver's Q2 2026 Price Reductions and Seller Concessions," published 2026-07-29. https://coloradohorseproperty.com/blog/denvers-q2-2026-price-reductions-and-seller-concessions/ Underlying data is REcolorado's analysis of closed residential listings across 11 Greater Denver Metro counties, April through June 2026. Report language: "Listings on the market for 8 to 14 days averaged a $2,321 price cut. By 50 to 56 days, the average price cut had grown to $28,278, more than a 12-times increase."
  - Who was measured: closed residential listings, 11-county Greater Denver Metro, Q2 2026.
  - Status: confirmed. Carried forward from `scripts/inside-the-industry/NF-067-late-price-cut-costs-12x.md`, sourced 2026-08-13.

- **Claim:** "Active inventory has held above 1.1 million for seven straight weeks."
  - Source: Realtor.com data dated 2026-08-06, reported in Carrington Wholesale, "Weekly Housing Headlines," published 2026-08-07. https://www.carringtonwholesale.com/2026/08/07/weekly-housing-headlines-91/
  - Who was measured: national active listing inventory.
  - Status: confirmed. Carried forward from NF-067.

- **Causation caveat:** The report shows cuts taken later were larger. It does not show that an early cut prevents a later large one. Homes still sitting at day fifty are plausibly the most mispriced at list. This deck's claim stays inside what the data supports, that the correction gets more expensive the longer it waits, and the close is a timing mechanism, not a promise of a specific dollar saving.

- **Scope caveat:** The $2,321 and $28,278 figures are Denver metro only, and every slide that carries them names Denver. The national inventory figure is what extends the relevance outside Colorado.
