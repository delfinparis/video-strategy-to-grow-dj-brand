## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
42.10 percent of listings took a price cut last week, statistically the same rate as a year ago.

**Body:**
HousingWire's Logan Mohtashami tracks this every week. Typically about a third of homes see a price
reduction before they sell, and this year's share, 42.10 percent, is running essentially even with
last year's 42 percent. The market gets called "orderly" at the macro level. Listing by listing,
that just means the same number of sellers are still starting too high and correcting later, and a
late cut spends the attention an accurately priced listing would have kept from its first week.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What changes before you take the listing, not after:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Build the number from the last three closed comps, not the seller's target.** A CMA that has to stretch to justify their number is the cut you're pre-scheduling.
2. **Say the range out loud before they push back.** "Based on what's closed nearby, we're between X and Y" moves the anchor before they set one of their own.
3. **Be willing to pass on a listing that won't price at the top of the range.** A house that sits and cuts costs you more than the listing you turned down.
