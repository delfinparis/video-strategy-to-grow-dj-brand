## SLIDE 3 -- THE TURN
**Headline:**
The ask doesn't build reach. It suppresses it.

**Body:**
Meta's Transparency Center defines engagement bait as a post that explicitly requests comments,
shares, tags, or likes for no reason other than the engagement itself, and says accounts using
the tactic should expect reach to drop, with bigger drops for repeat use. Most agents keep
asking anyway, because a comment count is the easiest number to put on a coaching slide. Nobody
has to prove what that comment was actually worth.

---

## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
What changes in your next three posts:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Delete "comment below," "tag a friend," and "double tap if."** Every version of the ask trips the same policy.
2. **Replace it with one sentence you'd actually send another agent.** Not a request. A line worth forwarding on its own.
3. **Watch sends and saves for two weeks, not comments.** That's the number the swap is actually built to move.
