## SLIDE 4 -- PAYLOAD
**Headline:**
Protect the follow-up hour, especially this month:

**Numbered list:**
1. **Block one hour of follow-up calls every weekday.** Keep it on the calendar in your busiest
   closing month, not just your slow one.
2. **Put every live lead on a standing follow-up, not a one-time call.** The leads that go quiet
   in a busy season are the ones nobody called back.
3. **Check your own numbers every quarter.** If your appointments always drop after March, the
   calendar is the problem, not the market.
