# Why You Don't Follow Up With Leads (It's Not Discipline)

> **WOW: Reframes the #1 agent self-sabotage (no follow-up) as a trigger problem, not a willpower problem, and hands over the exact research-backed fix. Beachhead category: prospecting/discipline.**

## Script (45-60 seconds)

### HOOK / PROBLEM (0:00-0:12)
You know you should follow up with your leads. So you tell yourself to be more disciplined. And tomorrow, you don't do it anyway. Here's the thing nobody tells you: discipline was never the problem. "Be more disciplined" is the problem. It's a wish, not a trigger.

### MECHANISM (0:12-0:35)
There's 20 years of research on this. A psychologist named Peter Gollwitzer found that when people write a plan in one very specific form -- "when this happens, I will do that" -- they follow through two to three times more often than people who just intend to. They're called implementation intentions. The cue carries the action, so you don't have to find the willpower in the moment.

### APPLICATION (0:35-0:50)
So stop writing "follow up with leads" on your list. That never fires. Write this instead: "When I pour my coffee at 8am, I call the first five names in my CRM." Now you're not relying on motivation. You're relying on the coffee. The trigger does the work.

### ARCHIVE TIE-IN (0:50-0:58)
The top producers I've interviewed who never lose a lead aren't superhuman. They've just hooked the work to something that already happens every single day.

### CLOSE / SOFT CTA (0:58-1:05)
