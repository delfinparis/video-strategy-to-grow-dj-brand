# The Text You've Been Avoiding Sending

> **WOW: Names the exact fear that kills agent prospecting (I'm bothering them) and uses social-psych research to prove the fear is a measurement error. Beachhead category: prospecting/outreach.**

**Pillar:** What Actually Works | **Type:** Research-backed | **Beachhead:** yes
**Primary Platform:** LinkedIn | **Post Date:** TBD

> **Strongest Line:** "You're the only one in the conversation calling it an imposition."

## Script (45-60 seconds)

### HOOK / PROBLEM (0:00-0:14)
There's a text on your phone you've been meaning to send for a month. A past client. Something like "just thinking of you, hope the house still feels like home." And you keep not sending it. Because some quiet part of you is sure you'd be bothering them.

### MECHANISM (0:14-0:38)
Here's what the research actually says about that. Nicholas Epley and his colleagues at the University of Chicago ran the studies. People consistently, dramatically underestimate how much others appreciate hearing from them. The person on the other end is happier to get your message than you predict. Not sometimes. Every time they tested it. You are the only one in that conversation calling it an imposition.

### APPLICATION (0:38-0:52)
So send three of them today. Not a pitch. Not "hey, know anyone looking to buy." Just the human one. "Thought of you. Hope you're well." Three texts, before you talk yourself out of it.

### ARCHIVE TIE-IN (0:52-0:58)
The agents I've interviewed who built entire businesses on referrals aren't more talented. They just never decided that staying in touch was annoying.

### CLOSE / ACTION (0:58-1:04)
Three texts. Today. Watch what comes back.
