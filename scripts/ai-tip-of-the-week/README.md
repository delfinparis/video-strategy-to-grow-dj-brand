# AI Tip of the Week

The Friday weekly slot in the post-pivot cadence. One AI prompt or tactic per week, drawn from the curated library, drives traffic to **tapthis.co** to fire 5-pixel retargeting for Kale recruiting.

---

## When + where

- **Every Friday**, all 6 retained crosspost surfaces
- **Frame:** "What I teach my agents at Kale"
- **CTA:** "Full prompt plus 569 more at tapthis.co" (or a similar variant)
- **Target runtime:** 45-55 seconds

---

## Script structure

| Beat | Duration | What it does |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| HOOK | 3-5s | Scroll-stopper. Usually from the source AIAM script. |
| FRAME | 3-5s | "My agents at Kale..." — positions this as insider sharing, not AI evangelism |
| TIP | 20-30s | Exact tool + exact prompt + how to run it |
| REFRAME | 5-10s | The one-liner that makes it memorable |
| CLOSE | 3-5s | "Full prompt plus 569 more at tapthis.co" |

---

## Source library

Scripts are drawn from the 100 audited AI Agent Minute scripts in [`../ai-agent-minute/`](../ai-agent-minute/). Condensed to ~55 seconds from the AIAM format's ~75-100 seconds. The goal is a tight, tapthis.co-funneled version of each tip.

Overlap between the 100 audited AIAM and the 620 at tapthis.co is partial — some tips are at both; some at only one. When filming, the spoken script should come from the audited 100 (they're camera-ready + editorially compliant). The CTA always points to the full tapthis.co library.

## Retargeting mechanics

```
AI Tip of the Week post
  ↓ viewer clicks "tapthis.co" in caption
Lands on tapthis.co (620-prompt library)
  ↓ 5 retargeting pixels fire simultaneously
  ├── Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
  ├── Google Ads
  ├── LinkedIn Insight Tag
  ├── Reddit
  └── TikTok
  ↓ 30-90 days of Kale recruiting ads across all 5 platforms
Viewer clicks a retargeting ad
  ↓
joinkale.com
  ↓
Webinar signup (You're Doing AI 100% Wrong)
  ↓
Book a call → Kale recruiting conversation
```

**What to track as leading indicators:**

1. **tapthis.co click-through rate** per AI Tip (Metricool link tracking or Google Analytics)
2. **Retargeting audience size** on each pixel platform (Meta Audiences, Google Ads audiences, etc.)
3. **joinkale.com traffic attributed to tapthis.co referrer**
4. **Webinar signups tagged from tapthis.co funnel**
5. **Book-a-call volume tagged from AI Tip source**

Full funnel is documented in [`../../docs/strategy/2026-04-18-pillar-pivot-decision.md`](../../docs/strategy/2026-04-18-pillar-pivot-decision.md).

## Filming notes

- One AI Tip = one Friday post across all 6 surfaces
- Aim to film 4 AI Tips in a single recording session (month's worth)
- Use the same studio setup as Inside the Industry posts for visual consistency (orange mic, Kale Realty background)
- Spoken script should follow the 5-beat structure above; don't ad-lib the CTA — the tapthis.co reference is the entire funnel

## File naming convention

`NNN-short-topic-slug.md`

E.g.:
- `001-i-want-to-think-about-it-claude-roleplay.md`
- `002-re-engage-ghosted-leads-claude-sequence.md`
- `003-write-listing-descriptions-with-ai.md`

Keep numbering sequential (001, 002, ...). Makes it easy to reference "Tip #N" in captions ("Tip #42 of 620. All free at tapthis.co.").
