# The 10-Minute Claude Shutdown Ritual That Gives You Your Evenings Back

## Full Script (Spoken)

**HOOK (0:00-0:05)**
The most productive agents I've ever interviewed don't work harder than you. They stop better.

**FRAME (0:05-0:10)**
My agents at Kale carry thirty open loops into dinner every night. Here's the prompt we use to close them.

**TIP (0:10-0:40)**
Ten minutes before you shut down. Open Claude. Tell it what you got done today, what's still open, and what tomorrow needs.

Then type: "Sort my open items by priority. Tell me what's actually urgent versus what my brain is just looping on. Give me my top three priorities for tomorrow morning."

Close the laptop. The loops are closed. Your brain can actually rest.

**REFRAME (0:40-0:50)**
You're not just carrying work home every night. You're carrying the feeling that you're always behind. How you end today determines how you start tomorrow.
