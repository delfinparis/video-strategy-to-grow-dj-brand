# Stop Sounding Like a Robot -- The 5-Minute Claude Voice Training That Fixes It

## Full Script (Spoken)

**HOOK (0:00-0:05)**
People can tell when AI wrote your content. And they trust it less when they can.

**FRAME (0:05-0:10)**
My agents at Kale constantly ask me why their AI posts sound robotic. Here's the fix -- five minutes, done once.

**TIP (0:10-0:40)**
Before asking Claude to write anything, paste three to five examples of content you've actually written. Captions, emails, texts -- doesn't matter.

Then type: "Analyze my writing style. My tone, my vocabulary, my sentence length, my personality. Now write a post about [topic] in that exact voice. Don't sound like AI. Sound like me."

Save it as a Claude Project. Every piece of content you write after that actually sounds like you.

**REFRAME (0:40-0:50)**
Most agents use AI to replace their voice. Top producers use it to amplify it. AI is a force multiplier for your voice. Not a replacement for it.
