# Wake Up Ghosted Leads With Claude -- The Three-Touch Value Sequence

> **WOW: Tactical specificity + mirror moment -- every agent has ghosted leads in their CRM. The fix is a Claude prompt that stops chasing and starts giving them reasons to come back.**

**Series:** AI Tip of the Week | **Post Date:** Friday, May 1, 2026
**CTA variant (this tip):** *"Tip #2 of 570. All free at tapthis.co."*

## Shareable Moment
> "Leads don't ghost agents they find valuable. They ghost agents they feel pressure from."

---

## Full Script (Spoken)

**HOOK (0:00-0:05)**
A lead going quiet doesn't mean they're gone. It means your last message gave them no reason to respond.

**FRAME (0:05-0:10)**
My agents at Kale have ghosted leads in every CRM. Here's the prompt we use to wake them up.

**TIP (0:10-0:40)**
Give Claude the lead's original profile and type: "Write a three-touch re-engagement sequence built entirely on value. No asks. Touch one: a relevant market update for their target area. Touch two: a specific property matching what they told me they wanted. Touch three: a low-pressure check-in asking if their timing has changed."

Every message gives them a reason to respond. None of them makes them feel guilty for going quiet.

**REFRAME (0:40-0:50)**
Most agents chase ghosted leads. Top producers give them a reason to come back. Leads don't ghost agents they find valuable. They ghost agents they feel pressure from.

Tip #2 of 570. All free at tapthis.co.
