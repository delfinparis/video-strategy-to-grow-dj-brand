## Full Script (Spoken)

**HOOK (0:00-0:04)**
"I want to think about it." Those five words have cost you more commission than any market downturn ever will.

**FRAME (0:04-0:10)
My agents at Kale get this objection every week. Here's the prompt I share with them.

**TIP (0:10-0:40)**
Open Claude. Type this: "Act as a seller who just said 'I want to think about it' after my listing presentation. Stay fully in character. Push back like a real seller would." Then hit send. Run it five times.

By the time you hear those words for real, you've been there a hundred times. Your brain stops defaulting to "take your time" and starts responding on muscle memory.
