## Shareable Moment

## Full Script (Spoken) (~46 seconds)

### SCENARIO (0:00-0:08)
You walk a buyer into a house and start narrating. "And this is the kitchen." Stop. They know it's a kitchen.

### DON'T (0:08-0:17)
Every feature you point out is one they can already see for themselves. You're filling the silence with noise, and learning nothing about them.

### DO (0:17-0:30)
Before you say one word about the house, ask this. "What does your current place not have that you wish it did?" Then go quiet, and watch their faces room to room.

### WHY (0:30-0:41)
You can't sell a home you don't understand their reason for. Their answer tells you which rooms to slow down in. The agent talking is performing. The agent listening is closing.

### TRY THIS (0:41-0:48)
Next showing, ask it at the front door. Then say nothing until they do.

## Social Copy

### Personal LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
You walk a buyer into a house and you start narrating.

"And this is the kitchen. And here's the primary bedroom."

Stop. They can see it's a kitchen.

Every feature you point out is a feature they were going to notice on their own. You're filling the silence with noise, and you're learning nothing about the person you're supposed to be helping.

Here is the move. Before you say a single word about the house, ask one question at the front door. "What does your current place not have that you wish it did?"

Then go quiet. Watch their faces room to room. The kitchen they linger in, the closet they open twice, the yard they walk back to. That is your data.

You cannot sell a home you do not understand someone's reason for wanting. Their answer tells you which rooms to slow down in and which to walk straight past.

The agent talking is performing. The agent listening is closing.

Next showing, ask the question at the door. Then say nothing until they do.
